## Summary
This sermon explores how believers put on the armor of God through the renewal of their minds rather than physical action. Pastor Simpson emphasizes that spiritual strength comes through a process of growth and change, not a one-time event, drawing parallels to being filled with the Spirit and bearing fruit. The key insight is that believers put on spiritual armor with their minds, choosing to serve the law of God rather than yielding to the law of sin.

## Scripture
Ephesians 6:10-14 — Paul instructs believers to be strong in the Lord and in the power of His might, to put on the whole armor of God to stand against the wiles of the devil, acknowledging that the battle is not against flesh and blood but against spiritual forces.

## Key Themes
- **Source of Strength**: Believers are to be strong in the Lord's power and authority, not through strength that comes upon them but through recognizing His power and might
- **Putting On the Armor**: The armor of God is put on through the mind, not as a physical garment, involving a process of renewal rather than a one-time event
- **Spiritual Warfare**: The battle is not against flesh and blood but against principalities, powers, rulers of darkness, and spiritual wickedness in high places
- **The Mind's Role**: The mind is the instrument through which believers put off the old man and put on the new man, serving the law of God

## Notes

### Introduction
- Isaac Phillips and Kim had trees down and power out from weather
- Becky and Chris's son Nathan also affected but okay
- Continuing study in Ephesians chapter 6

### Being Strong in the Lord (Verse 10)
- "Finally my brethren be strong in the Lord and in the power of his might"
- Three words dealing with strength, force, and power
- "Be strong" - relates to our will, decision-making, internal fortitude
- "Power" - references authority in Paul's writings
- "Might" - has to do with force and God's attributes of strength
- Difference between the strength God has versus the strength He provides for us

### Old Testament vs. Grace Perspective
- Under kingdom program, Spirit came upon people (like Samson)
- Spirit could leave them (Samson lost power when hair was cut)
- Today believers are sealed by the Spirit - He doesn't leave
- Our job is to be led, directed, and guided by the Spirit we already have
- Strength comes differently for us today than in Old Testament

### Putting On the Armor (Verse 11)
- "Put on the whole armor of God that you may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil"
- The phrase "put on" in Old Testament typically refers to putting on clothing or garments
- Something we can physically relate to doing

### Romans 13:12-14 Example
- "Let us therefore cast off the works of darkness and let us put on the armor of light"
- Armor of light is not tangible - cannot put hands on it
- Yet supposed to put it on like a garment that stays until taken off
- "Put ye on the Lord Jesus Christ and make not provision for the flesh"
- There is both a casting off and a putting on

### The Process, Not an Event
- Most preaching on armor of God tells what it is and what it does
- But doesn't specifically explain how to put it on
- Similar question: Are you filled with the Spirit or not?
- In the kingdom, signs and wonders manifested being filled with Spirit (tongues, healing)
- For us today, it's the fruit of the Spirit
- Fruit requires a process: seed, planting, growth before bearing fruit
- Being filled with Spirit and putting on armor of God is a process, not an event

### 1 Corinthians 15:51-53
- "Behold, I show you a mystery, we shall not all sleep, but we shall all be changed"
- At rapture or death, the body of Christ will get new bodies (adoption)
- "In a moment, in the twinkling of an eye at the last trump"
- "This corruptible must put on incorruption"
- "This mortal must put on immortality"
- A change literally takes place - you become something else
- This happens at glorification (completion of salvation)

### 2 Corinthians 5:17 Connection
- "If any man be in Christ, he's a new creature. Old things are passed away, behold all things are become new"
- Context deals with someone already saved
- Difference between being in the flesh versus being in the spirit
- Can be saved and not be in Christ because walking in the flesh
- When in Christ, you are a new creature - you have been changed

### Growth Requires Nourishment
- For something to grow and continue growing, it must be fed and watered
- Must receive light
- When you stop, growth stops (example: grass not growing as fast in cold weather)
- God's creation teaches us spiritual truths
- Must bring nature and science back to God and relate it spiritually
- Change requires certain things to take place on a regular basis

### Ephesians 4:20-24
- "But ye have not so learned Christ"
- "That ye put off concerning the former conversation the old man, which is corrupt according to the deceitful lusts"
- "And be renewed in the spirit of your mind"
- "And that ye put on the new man"
- Cannot physically put this on
- You put things on and put things off with your mind

### Romans 7:25
- "I thank God through Jesus Christ our Lord"
- "So then with the mind I myself serve the law of God, but with the flesh the law of sin"
- Chapter discusses different laws: law of God, law of my members, law of my mind, law of sin
- Law of sin: never content, deceptive, leads from one thing to another, brings control
- Law of the mind: garbage in, garbage out - whatever you program it to do
- "With the mind I myself serve the law of God"
- Able to yield yourself to God, give yourself to God through the mind
- Strength and fortitude to do this is done in the mind

### The Mind as the Battle Ground
- Sin wars against the law of the mind (verse 23)
- Brings into captivity to the law of sin
- Sin puts people in bondage and control
- Fight this with the mind by serving the law of God
- Connection to being filled with Spirit, bearing fruit, and the growing process
