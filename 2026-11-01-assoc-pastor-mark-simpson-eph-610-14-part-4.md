## Summary
Pastor Simpson explores what it means to practically "put on" the armor of God by examining biblical passages about putting off the old man and putting on the new man. He emphasizes that putting on the armor is not a physical act but a spiritual transformation that begins in the mind and heart, requiring continual reinforcement of truth to remain strong in a sin-cursed body. The message clarifies that spiritual strength comes through consistent engagement with God's Word, similar to how physical strength requires ongoing exercise.

## Scripture
Ephesians 6:10-14 — Paul commands believers to be strong in the Lord and put on the whole armor of God to stand against the devil's schemes.

## Key Themes
- **Putting On and Putting Off**: Christians must actively choose to put off the old man with sinful deeds and put on the new man created in righteousness and holiness.
- **Spiritual Transformation**: True change happens not just in outward actions but through internal transformation of mind and heart, moving truth from knowledge to belief.
- **Source of Strength**: Spiritual strength comes through continual engagement with Scripture, not as a one-time event but as an ongoing process necessary for living in a sin-cursed body.
- **Standing Firm**: To stand means to take a position on truth and not relinquish it, establishing firm doctrinal foundations that cannot be moved.

## Notes

### Introduction: The Armor of God
- Familiar passage from childhood Sunday school lessons
- Different to study what it really means to put on the armor
- Must discern when to take Scripture literally versus relatively
- The armor pieces represent spiritual truths, not physical items

### The Source of Strength (Ephesians 6:10)
- Three similar but distinct words: strong, power, might
- **Strong**: Internal fortitude and will God has given
- **Power**: God's authority and dominion
- **Might**: Actual force and power being used
- Strength differs from Old Testament times to how Christians receive it today

### Understanding Putting On and Putting Off

#### Ephesians 4:20-25
- Put off the former conversation (old way of life)
- Put off the old man which is corrupt
- Be renewed in the spirit of your mind
- Put on the new man created in righteousness and true holiness
- Putting away lying, speak truth with neighbors
- Change begins in the mind, not with physical hands

#### Colossians 3:8-14
- Put off: anger, wrath, malice, blasphemy, filthy communication
- Anger management classes are wrong approach—don't manage anger, put it off
- Stop lying by telling the truth—identify right from wrong and choose
- Put on the new man renewed in knowledge after God's image
- Put on as elect of God: bowels of mercies, kindness, humbleness, meekness, long-suffering
- Natural body doesn't show mercy—must choose to put it on
- Forbearing one another means putting up with one another
- Standard for forgiveness: if Christ died for it, you're required to forgive it
- Put on charity (love) which is the bond of perfectness

#### Romans 13:12
- Cast off works of darkness
- Put on the armor of light
- Armor referenced beyond just Ephesians 6
- Truths more important than the illustration itself

### The New Creation

#### 1 Corinthians 15:51-54
- God does the changing when we put on immortality
- Corruptible body will put on incorruption
- When mortal puts on immortality, death is swallowed up in victory
- Same phrase used for our choices and what God does
- Part of security for believers—glorification is promised

#### Galatians 3:27
- As many baptized into Christ have put on Christ
- Holy Spirit placed believers into the body of Christ
- Holy Spirit didn't come down upon us but placed us in Christ's body
- Sealed until the day of redemption

#### Ephesians 2:8-10
- Saved by grace through faith, not of works
- We are God's workmanship created in Christ Jesus unto good works
- Became created as members of the body of Christ

#### 2 Corinthians 5:17
- If any man be in Christ, he is a new creature
- Becoming new creature means becoming member of Christ's body
- Christ is the head, parts fitly joined together to bring glory to God

### The Heart of the Matter
- Youth retreat discussion about giving your heart to the Lord
- Difference between serving God with mind versus heart
- Many go through motions of Christianity without true belief
- Knowledge in head versus belief in heart creates different outcomes
- Priorities, purpose, desires, bucket list, view of eternity all change with heart transformation
- Song lyric: "The things of this earth grow strangely dim in the light of his glory and grace"

### Where Transformation Begins (Romans 7:21-25)
- Multiple laws (principles) dealing with how person operates under grace
- Delight in law of God after the inward man
- Law in members (sin-cursed body) wars against law of mind
- "O wretched man that I am, who shall deliver me from the body of this death?"
- With the mind I serve the law of God, but with the flesh the law of sin
- Putting on armor begins in the mind and ends in the heart

### Standing Firm (Ephesians 6:11-14)
- Verses 11, 13, and 14 all reference standing
- To stand means to not relinquish a position
- Take a position and don't move off it
- Much of armor relates to doctrine, truth, light, righteousness, instruction
- Need established positions of truth that cannot be moved
- Truth must move from knowledge to understanding to wisdom
- Stand against the wiles (trickery) of the devil

### Examples of Standing
- Christ fired scripture back at Satan during temptation—quick process
- Genesis 3: Eve and Adam compromised truth and position, caved in
- Need strength to be rooted with firm foundation

### The Nature of Spiritual Strength
- Psalm 1: Like tree planted by rivers of water with root system
- Building requires digging hole and pouring concrete foundation
- But we don't have physical roots or concrete
- **Strength comes by continual confirmation of truth in life**
- Roots grow, concrete sets up and becomes firm—for us it's continual process
- Why continual? Because we continually live in sin-cursed body
- Like pouring water into cup with holes—fills up, leaks out, must refill

### Building and Maintaining Strength
- Strength doesn't come upon us automatically
- Get strength similar to physical body strength
- Build up bodies through work, endurance, taking care
- As soon as you stop, you start losing it
- When in Word of God and studying Bible, will have strength
- When stop studying, strength leaks out the bottom
- Cup becomes empty before you know it
- Satan uses wiles to make you believe cup is still full
- Truth makes you stop pointing at others and deal with yourself
- Be transparent, sincere, real, genuine
- Call yourself what you are and deal with yourself
- Take in truth, plug holes, stay strong, be new creation for Christ

### Our Trial Period
- Outer man is sin-cursed
- When saved, circumcision made without hands separated body from soul and spirit
- God purposely wants us to live in sin-cursed body yet yield to Him
- Get to do this for a season, for a little while
- Trial period determines what rest of eternity will be like
- Different from marketing trial periods—this one matters eternally
- Christ gives grace, salvation, hope freely
- Then we get choice: Will you live for me or not?
- Will you put this on or put that on?
- Will you put away this or keep this?
