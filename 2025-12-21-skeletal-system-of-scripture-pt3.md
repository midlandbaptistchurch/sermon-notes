## Summary
Pastor Payne continues exploring the skeletal structure of Scripture by examining God's blueprint for creation and the succession of worlds. Drawing from 2 Peter 3 and Genesis 1, he traces how God established His throne in heaven and His footstool on earth, how Lucifer's rebellion corrupted the original world, and how Scripture reveals three distinct world systems: the world that then was (destroyed by the flood), the present evil world (from Noah to Christ's return), and the world to come (the millennial kingdom and eternity).

## Scripture
2 Peter 3:1-7 — Peter addresses scoffers who deny Christ's coming and ignore the judgment that came upon the world through the flood.

Genesis 1:1 — God's original creation of heaven and earth, establishing His throne and kingdom.

## Key Themes
- God's sovereignty and throne established over creation
- The original world corrupted by Lucifer's rebellion and Adam's sin
- God's judgment on creation through successive worlds
- The three worlds: old world, present evil world, and world to come
- The foundation of the earth laid by God with purpose and wisdom
- Satan's ongoing rebellion (mystery of iniquity) throughout human history
- The believer's role in God's plan from before the foundation of the world

## Notes

### God's Throne and Authority
- God created heaven as His throne and earth as His footstool (Isaiah 66:1)
- The order of creation: God's throne → God's chambers → angels → earth
- God established dominion and authority over all creation

### Lucifer's Rebellion
- Lucifer, the greatest of angelic creation, sought to become "the most high" and possess heaven and earth
- He drew one-third of the angels into rebellion with him
- When he corrupted his wisdom, he became the serpent—an enemy of God's domain
- Lucifer's fall demonstrates God's inevitable judgment on rebellion

### The Succession of Worlds

**The World That Then Was** (Genesis 1-3 through Noah)
- Created by God in the six days of creation
- Had different conditions: people lived 700-900 years, the Lord walked on earth, cherubim guarded the garden
- Corrupted when Adam and Eve sinned
- Judged and destroyed by God's flood in Noah's time

**The Present Evil World** (Noah through Christ's return)
- Established after the flood (second world system)
- Extends from Noah to the Second Coming of Christ
- Will end with the tribulation period and Christ's return
- Characterized by Satan's ongoing "mystery of iniquity" (2 Thessalonians 2:7)

**The World to Come** (Millennial Kingdom and Eternity)
- The millennial kingdom where Christ reigns for 1,000 years
- God will fulfill His covenant with the animal kingdom
- People will live extended lifespans again
- Followed by the new heaven, new earth, and the world without end (eternity)

### God's Plan and Wisdom
- Paul prayed that believers would be filled with knowledge of God's will—not just surface understanding but deep inner knowledge (Colossians 1:9)
- God's hidden wisdom in mystery, long concealed but now revealed through Christ
- The body of Christ has a role to play in God's plan from before the foundation of the world (Ephesians 1:4)
- One day the body of Christ will subdue the world to come, taking authority over principalities and powers

### Creation Details
- The earth was originally covered with water (Genesis 1:2)
- God separated the waters: waters above the firmament and waters below
- God spoke those waters back and prepared dry land
- The earth today is 70% covered by water—a legacy of God's careful ordering

### The Skeletal System of Scripture
- Just as bones provide structure to the human body and protect vital organs, Scripture has a skeletal structure
- This structure spans from eternity past through eternity future
- Understanding creation, worlds, and God's sovereignty reveals the framework of all biblical truth
