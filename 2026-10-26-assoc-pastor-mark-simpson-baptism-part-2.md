## Summary
This teaching distinguishes between water baptism practiced under the law and in early Acts, and Spirit baptism that places believers into the body of Christ under grace. Pastor Simpson argues that when Paul wrote "Christ sent me not to baptize" (1 Corinthians 1:17), he was indicating a transition away from water baptism. The baptism for believers today is Spirit baptism—being identified with and placed into Christ's body at salvation—not a water ritual. Despite not practicing water baptism, the church retains "Baptist" in its name because baptism (Spirit baptism) remains central to their doctrine.

## Scripture
**1 Corinthians 12:13** — "For by one Spirit are we all baptized into one body, whether we be Jews or Gentiles, whether we be bond or free; and have been all made to drink into one Spirit." This passage teaches that the Holy Spirit places believers into the body of Christ at salvation.

**Colossians 2:11-13** — Describes spiritual circumcision, being buried with Christ in baptism, and being raised with Him through faith. This baptism is identification with Christ's death, burial, and resurrection, not a water ceremony.

**Romans 6:3-4** — "Know ye not, that so many of us as were baptized into Jesus Christ were baptized into his death? Therefore we are buried with him by baptism into death: that like as Christ was raised up from the dead by the glory of the Father, even so we also should walk in newness of life." This passage applies baptism to how believers should live after salvation.

## Key Themes
- **Spirit Baptism vs. Water Baptism**: Water baptism was practiced under the law and in early Acts with repentance and confession of sins; Spirit baptism places believers into Christ's body at salvation under grace.
- **Paul's Transitional Ministry**: Paul baptized some early on but later emphasized that Christ sent him not to baptize but to preach the gospel, indicating a shift away from water baptism.
- **What Happens at Salvation**: When a person believes the gospel, they are baptized into Christ's body, sealed by the Holy Spirit, circumcised spiritually, and forgiven of all sins (past, present, future).
- **Romans 6 Application**: The baptism picture in Romans 6 teaches believers to die to self and walk in newness of life, not a command to be water baptized.
- **Why "Baptist" Church**: The church retains the Baptist name because baptism (Spirit baptism into the body of Christ) is central to their doctrine, and they "got it right" while maintaining a welcoming outreach to those from Baptist backgrounds.
