## Summary
Pastor Simpson explores the meaning of being "risen with Christ" from Colossians 3:1-3, challenging believers to examine their spiritual condition and set their affections on heavenly things rather than earthly concerns. The message emphasizes the importance of spiritual discernment and understanding what it means to be spiritually alive versus spiritually dead.

## Scripture
Colossians 3:1-3 — Paul's instructions to believers about their new identity in Christ and the call to seek heavenly perspectives.

## Key Themes
- **Spiritual condition assessment**: Understanding whether we are spiritually "risen" or "dead"
- **Heavenly-mindedness**: Setting affections on things above, not earthly things
- **Romans 6 connection**: The foundational teaching of dying and rising with Christ in baptism
- **Throne room vision**: Drawing from Revelation 4 to understand heavenly realities
- **Bible study methodology**: The importance of deep, thoughtful engagement with Scripture
- **Spiritual discernment**: Living with awareness of spiritual realities and conditions

## Notes

### Bible Study and Spiritual Influence
- "He that has the most influence wins" - the amount of biblical influence in our lives determines spiritual victory
- Bible reading alone isn't enough; we need study, writing, memorization, and application
- The goal isn't just time spent in Scripture but the level of influence it has on our thinking

### "If Ye Then Be Risen with Christ" (v.1)
- Key question: Are you actually risen with Christ?
- Connection to Romans 6: baptized into His death, buried with Him, raised to walk in newness of life
- This isn't just about salvation but about ongoing spiritual condition
- Requires understanding the difference between being spiritually alive vs. spiritually dead

### "Seek Those Things Which Are Above" (v.1)
- Must know what heavenly things actually are before we can seek them
- Revelation 4 description of the throne room: jasper, sardine stone, rainbow like emerald, 24 elders with golden crowns, lightning, thunder, seven lamps of fire, sea of glass, four beasts
- Includes: angels, kingdoms, the throne room, colors, sounds, judgment seat
- Seeking these things brings glory to God when we acknowledge His majesty

### "Set Your Affection on Things Above" (v.2)
- This is not natural - requires deliberate choice
- Our sin-cursed flesh won't naturally do this
- Must actively seek after heavenly things through Scripture study
- Future glorified bodies where "nobody's going to be stupid no more" - everyone will have discernment and wisdom

### "Ye Are Dead" (v.3)
- If risen, then also dead (to sin, self, flesh)
- Need to understand what spiritual death and resurrection look like practically
- "Your life is hid with Christ in God" - our true life source

### Spiritual Discernment
- Many people live without discernment - unaware of spiritual realities
- Need wisdom and sensitivity to know God's leading and others' spiritual needs
- Lack of discernment often stems from lack of caring about spiritual matters

### The Test of Influence
- Are you more concerned about what people think or what God thinks?
- God knows our condition even when others don't
- The Word of God should have "free course" in our lives
