## Summary
This sermon explores the husband-wife relationship as a picture of Christ's relationship with the church, focusing on what Christ gave to the body of Christ. Pastor Simpson emphasizes that husbands are to love their wives as Christ loved the church, not just by being willing to die for them, but by understanding four specific things Christ gives: closeness (indwelling of the Spirit), confidence (sealing of the Spirit), communication (the Word of God), and contemplation/hope (the rapture). The key principle is that husbands must first receive these gifts from Christ before they can effectively give them to their wives.

## Scripture
Ephesians 5:25-27 — Husbands are commanded to love their wives as Christ loved the church and gave himself for it, that he might sanctify and cleanse it with the washing of water by the word, and present it to himself a glorious church without spot or wrinkle.

## Key Themes
- **Headship**: Christ as head of the church serves as the model for the husband's role, requiring submission, exaltation, and developing the mind of Christ through study of principles rather than just commands
- **Receiving Before Giving**: Husbands must first receive Christ's love (closeness, confidence, communication, contemplation) before they can effectively give these to their wives
- **Four Gifts Christ Gives**: Indwelling of the Spirit (closeness), sealing of the Spirit (confidence/security), the Word of God (communication), and hope of the rapture (contemplation)
- **Sanctification as Choice**: While salvation is secure, believers choose whether to receive Christ's love and present themselves sanctified and cleansed before him

## Notes

### Review of Headship
- Emphasized the word "as" used throughout Ephesians 5:22-33 for comparisons
- 1 Corinthians 11:3 shows chain of headship: wife to husband, man to Christ, Christ to God the Father
- Headship involves more than submission - includes exaltation and thinking like Christ
- Having "the mind of Christ" comes from studying principles under grace, not just commands
- This prepares believers for the judgment seat of Christ where he will be our head

### What Christ Gave to the Body of Christ

**1. Closeness - The Indwelling of the Spirit**
- Believers are the temple of the Holy Ghost at salvation (1 Corinthians 3, 6)
- Romans 8 speaks of Christ dwelling with believers in their walk and sanctification
- Romans 8:1 - "no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus who walk not after the flesh, but after the Spirit"
- Romans 8:8-9 is about walking in the Spirit versus the flesh, not about salvation security
- This is a conditional indwelling related to sanctification and service
- Husbands must recognize and receive this closeness from Christ to give it to their wives

**2. Confidence - The Sealing of the Spirit**
- Ephesians 1:13 - "after that ye believed, ye were sealed with that Holy Spirit of promise"
- Order of salvation: hear, understand, trust, believe, then sealed
- The prayer is not what saves - understanding and belief are critical
- Many Christians are confident in salvation but lack confidence in their walk
- Need security and confidence in relationship with the Lord, not just in salvation
- This confidence allows believers to serve, make mistakes, and keep going

**3. Communication - The Word of God**
- Every word is pure and given so believers can understand
- Husbands need Bible reading, prayer time, and acknowledgement of the Lord in decision-making
- One entire premarital counseling session is devoted to communication
- Communication with the Lord enables effective communication with spouse
- Quality communication comes from quantity - need enough in-depth conversations
- Builds on closeness and confidence

**4. Contemplation - Hope of the Rapture**
- Looking forward to future hope
- These four things (indwelling, sealing, Word, rapture hope) are given in this age
- Husbands receive these from Christ and exemplify them to their wives

### Christ's Desire for His Church (verses 26-27)

- "That he might sanctify and cleanse it with the washing of water by the word"
- "That he might present it to himself, a glorious church, not having spot or wrinkle or any such thing, but that it should be holy and without blemish"
- The word "wrinkle" is notable - used only one other time in Job
- Christ presents the church to himself at the judgment seat, then to the Father
- This is Christ's desire, but he doesn't force believers to do it
- Not based on fear or law alone, but on receiving his love
- Believers choose to receive Christ's love and reciprocate by presenting themselves sanctified and cleansed
- At the rapture believers get glorified bodies and there will be unity of the body of Christ
- Christ wants that spirit of unity here on earth before we get there

### Practical Application for Marriage

- Lost or carnal men can be nice to wives, but that doesn't bring God glory
- The goal is deeper - bringing God glory through the marriage relationship
- Requires right motivation, not just checking boxes
- Paul was "constrained by his love" (2 Corinthians 5) and called himself "prisoner of Jesus Christ"
- Sin brings bondage, but cleansing and grace bring freedom and liberty
- Paul took that freedom and made Christ his head, the Word his authority
- This creates a walk and relationship bringing glory at judgment seat and blessing on earth

### Marriage Counseling Insights

- Every young married couple will take each other for granted
- Get comfortable and stop putting in effort like during dating
- Must prepare for this and work at it
- Similar to Christians who get excited at salvation then drop off
- People forget what the Lord has done for them
- Many adults think the prayer is more important than understanding in salvation
- Never tell someone they're saved - let the Word of God tell them
