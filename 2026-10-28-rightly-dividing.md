## Summary
Pastor Jim Payne teaches on understanding when "time past" ended and "but now" began in God's program, focusing on God's covenant relationship with Israel as a circumcised people separated from the nations. He traces God's work from calling Abram through establishing the Davidic covenant, emphasizing that God severed Israel from all other nations as His unique inheritance on earth. This study helps explain the "gospel of the circumcision" versus the "gospel of the uncircumcision" mentioned in Galatians 2:7-8.

## Scripture
Genesis 12-17 — God calls Abram out of Ur, promises him land and seed, and establishes the covenant of circumcision as a sign that separates Abraham's descendants from all other nations.

2 Samuel 7:11-17 — God establishes the Davidic covenant, promising David that his throne and kingdom will be established forever.

Deuteronomy 32:8-9 — When God divided the nations, He set Israel apart as His special inheritance while giving other nations over to principalities and powers.

Leviticus 20:22-26 — God declares that He has severed Israel from other peoples to be holy unto Him.

## Key Themes
- **Time Divisions in Scripture**: Understanding when "time past" ended requires tracing God's covenant program with Israel from Genesis through the establishment of the kingdom promises.
- **Israel's Separation**: God severed Israel from all nations, not counting them among the nations, making them His unique inheritance on earth with the land as their promised possession.
- **Circumcision as Covenant Sign**: Circumcision was the token/sign of the Abrahamic covenant, representing putting away the flesh and marking Israel as God's circumcised people distinct from uncircumcised Gentile nations.
- **Gospel of Circumcision vs. Uncircumcision**: Galatians 2:7-8 references good news for the circumcised (Israel) and good news for the uncircumcised (Gentiles), representing different aspects of God's program.
- **No Favor for Jews Today**: In the current dispensation of the body of Christ, circumcision avails nothing; God is not showing respect to Jews over Gentiles but dealing with all individuals alike.

### Genesis 1-11: Three Rebellions
- Covered approximately 1,800 years in 11 chapters
- Three rebellions: Adam and Eve, Genesis 6 (sons of God), Tower of Babel with Nimrod
- God scattered nations at Babel into 70 different nations (Genesis 10)
- God gave up these nations, placing principalities and powers over them as gods to lead them
- God chose to inherit just one nation for Himself

### God's Call of Abram
- Abram introduced in Genesis 11:26
- Genesis 12:1 is past tense: "the Lord had said" (already said before)
- Journey: from Ur of the Chaldees, stopped at Haran, then into land of Canaan
- Emphasis throughout on "the land" God would give to Abram and his seed

### The Abrahamic Covenant
- Genesis 13:14-17: God tells Abram to walk through the land (legal ownership by placing feet)
- Genesis 14:17-24: Abram meets Melchizedek; acknowledges God as "most high God, possessor of heaven and earth"
- Genesis 15:18: God makes covenant with Abram regarding land and seed
- Genesis 17: Covenant confirmed; Abram becomes Abraham ("father of many" instead of "high father")

### Circumcision Established
- Genesis 17:9-14: Circumcision commanded as token/sign of covenant
- Romans 4:11: Circumcision is a sign
- Jews require a sign; all four great covenants with Israel confirmed by signs
- Circumcision represents putting away the flesh
- Context: Abram tried to help God through flesh (Hagar/Ishmael) rather than waiting on God's promise
- Reminder that promised seed comes by God's promise, not human effort

### Covenant Passed to Isaac and Jacob
- Genesis 26:1-5: Covenant passed to Isaac
- Genesis 28:12-15: Jacob's ladder vision; covenant passed to Jacob
- Angels ascending and descending (not descending first) indicates God's government ultimately established on earth
- Jacob's name changed to Israel ("prince with God")
- His descendants called "children of Israel"

### Israel at Mount Sinai
- Exodus 19:3-8: Approximately 2-3 months after leaving Egypt
- God says: "You have seen what I did to Egyptians and how I brought you to myself"
- "If you obey my voice and keep my covenant, you shall be a peculiar treasure unto me above all people"
