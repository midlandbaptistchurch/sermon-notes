## Summary
Pastor Payne continues his study on creation by examining Genesis 1:1-2 and the gap between these verses. He explains how the Bible focuses on the earth from Genesis 1:2 onward, discusses Lucifer's rebellion and fall, and shows how Genesis 1:2 uses language of divine judgment rather than original creation.

## Scripture
Genesis 1:1-2 — The opening verses of creation, with particular focus on understanding the gap between God's initial creation and the subsequent judgment described in verse 2.

## Key Themes
- Rightly dividing Scripture into time past, but now, and ages to come
- The gap theory between Genesis 1:1 and 1:2
- Lucifer's creation, reign, and rebellion
- The Bible's focus on the earth versus the heavens
- Language of judgment in Genesis 1:2
- Subsequent revelation in Scripture

## Notes

### Rightly Dividing Creation
- Scripture divides into three periods: time past (Old Testament), but now (church age), and ages to come (future)
- Creation follows this same pattern: original heaven and earth (Genesis 1:1), current heavens and earth (now), and new heaven and earth (future)
- Genesis 1:1 describes the original creation "in the beginning"
- Between Genesis 1:1 and 1:2 exists an undetermined gap of time

### The Angelic Creation
- God created angels between creating heaven and earth (Genesis 1:1)
- Job 38:6-7 shows angels were present and rejoicing when God laid earth's foundations
- Lucifer was given dominion over the original earth
- The angelic world existed for an unknown period before rebellion occurred

### Lucifer's Rebellion and Fall
- Satan's rebellion happened in the gap between Genesis 1:1 and 1:2
- This rebellion brought God's judgment upon the earth
- The earth became "without form and void" due to this judgment
- Genesis 1:2 describes the aftermath of judgment, not original creation

### The Bible's Focus on Earth
- From Genesis 1:2 through the end of the Gospels, Scripture focuses on the earth
- God gave the earth to mankind, specifically to Israel as His chosen people
- The church age (Romans through Philemon) shifts focus from earth to heavenly things
- Colossians 3:1-2 instructs believers to set affections on things above, not earthly things

### Language of Judgment in Genesis 1:2
- "Without form and void" (tohu wa bohu) appears only in Genesis 1:2 and Jeremiah 4:23
- Jeremiah 4:23-26 describes judgment and destruction, showing this phrase indicates judgment
- Darkness, the deep, and waters in Genesis 1:2 all represent divine judgment
- The Spirit of God "moved" (brooded) over the waters, preparing for restoration

### Subsequent Revelation
- God conceals some matters that are revealed later in Scripture (Proverbs 25:2)
- Example: The names of Pharaoh's magicians (Jannes and Jambres) revealed in 2 Timothy 3:8
- Understanding of Lucifer's rebellion comes from piecing together various Scriptures
