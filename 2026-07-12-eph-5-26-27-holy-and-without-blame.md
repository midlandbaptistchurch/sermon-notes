## Summary
Pastor Mark continues teaching through Ephesians 5:22-33, focusing on verses 26-27 where Paul transitions from husband-wife instruction to describe Christ's presentation of the church. He emphasizes a spiritual cycle: husbands learn to experience Christ's love and headship, implement it with their wives, and learn from their wives' godly submission how to better exalt Christ. The sermon highlights that Christ eagerly anticipates the day when believers receive glorified bodies and are presented "holy and without blame," free from sin-cursed flesh, which should motivate believers to live that way now.

## Scripture
Ephesians 5:26-27 — Christ sanctifies and cleanses the church with the washing of water by the Word, that He might present it to Himself a glorious church, not having spot or wrinkle or any such thing, but that it should be holy and without blemish.

## Key Themes
- **Spiritual Cycle in Marriage**: Husbands receive Christ's love and headship, give it to their wives, and learn from their wives' submission how to better exalt Christ
- **Future Presentation**: Christ looks forward to the day when the church is presented holy and without blame in glorified bodies
- **Present Application**: Believers should strive to be holy and unblameable now (Colossians 1:22-23) while still in sin-cursed bodies
- **Sanctification Process**: The washing of water by the Word applies both to salvation and ongoing Christian growth

### Review: The Spiritual Cycle (from verse 25)
- A man must first experience Christ's love and see Him as head
- Reverence and exaltation of Christ can be a struggle for men
- Submission and obedience are somewhat easier, but exalting someone else is difficult
- As the husband understands Christ's love, he implements it with his wife
- As the wife receives this love, she gives reverence, submission, exaltation, and obedience to her husband
- **The Missing Part**: The husband learns from his wife's example of submission and exaltation
- Wives can naturally reciprocate exaltation and reverence better than men
- The husband observes his wife's godly submission and learns to give the same back to Christ
- This creates a cycle: Christ → husband → wife → husband → Christ
- This is not natural for men and must be consciously processed

### Study Over Service
- Many churches emphasize service over study
- Study should create a foundation for service
- Without study, believers cannot know why they do what they do
- Many will be disappointed at the judgment seat because they served without understanding
- Living under grace means understanding why you do what you do
- Most people don't think of Christ as head or understand that headship role

### Verses 26-27: Paul's "Rabbit Trail"
- While teaching husband-wife relationships, Paul diverts to discuss the church's presentation
- He does this to show the significance and importance to Christ
- Some exalt the husband-wife relationship but nullify other things important to Christ
- The wedding day is important, but there's a greater "wedding day" coming

### Christ's Anticipated Day of Relief
- How much relief will Christ receive when believers get glorified bodies?
- He won't have to deal with us in the flesh anymore
- This will be a relief even to deity: God the Father, Christ, and the Holy Spirit
- Believers who battle their flesh get weary from it
- Looking forward to the day of no more fighting sin-cursed flesh
- No more dealing with other people's "stupid and their stuff"
- At the judgment seat: glorified bodies, adoption of sons, works judged by fire
- Collectively presented before God the Father, then positioned in the angelic kingdom
- During tribulation and millennium on earth, the church will be with angels in the heavenly kingdom

### The Balance: Ephesians 1 vs. Colossians 1
- **Ephesians 1:4**: "That we should be holy and without blame before him in love"
- Context (verse 5): "Having predestinated us unto the adoption of children"
- Adoption refers to receiving glorified bodies (Romans 8, Galatians 4)
- This describes our future state when presented before God
- **Colossians 1:22**: "Holy and unblameable and unreproveable in his sight"
- Verse 23: "If ye continue in the faith grounded and settled"
- The word "if" indicates this is about present life, not guaranteed future state
- Not about continuing to stay saved (we're sealed to the day of redemption)
- About choosing to live as we will be before we actually get there
- Denying the flesh and walking in the Spirit now

### Application to Ephesians 5:26-27
- Verse 26: "Sanctify and cleanse it with the washing of water by the word"
- The Word is essential to salvation and ongoing spiritual life
- Believers take in the Word daily to grow and be where they should be
- Verse 27: "Present it to himself a glorious church"
- A church that brings glory, without spot, wrinkle, or blemish
- Holy and without blemish

### Reality for Husbands
- A husband cannot make his wife holy or right
- He can do what Christ does: give grace and liberty
- Reality: Christ's grace and liberty are often rejected
- Christ is worthy of exaltation, submission, obedience, and headship
- Yet He receives so little of it
- A husband doing right doesn't guarantee automatic reciprocation
- Not dealing with an if-then equation, but with people and free will

### Grace vs. Law
- When believers understand what Christ has done, it is motivating
- It makes us want to exalt, honor, and obey Him
- But we're dealing with grace, not law
- Grace and liberty mean there's no guarantee of reciprocation
- If a wife sees her husband and Christ as head, does that guarantee he'll reciprocate love? No.
- But doing what we're supposed to do, regardless of temporal reactions, guarantees reward at the judgment seat
- When we stand without sin, those times we denied flesh and did right will be rewarded
- God rewards us for doing right regardless of what anyone else was doing

### The Marriage Cycle
- When both do it right and the cycle is created, it's a great thing
- It makes it easier to do what you're supposed to do
- But it's not just about getting what we want
- It's about Him getting glory

### Conclusion
- Remember how big of a day it's going to be for Christ
- If we want to give Him glory, and it's going to be a big day to Him, it must be a big day to us
- We must understand and get ahold of this truth
