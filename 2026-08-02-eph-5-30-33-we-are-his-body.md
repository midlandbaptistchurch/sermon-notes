## Summary
Pastor Simpson examines Ephesians 5:22-33, emphasizing that while this passage addresses husband-wife relationships, its primary focus is on Christ's relationship with the church. The profound mystery that believers are members of Christ's body—His flesh and bones—means we represent Him on earth as His hands, feet, mouth, and mind. This identity requires understanding our role in the body without thinking more highly of ourselves than we ought, maintaining unity under Christ as our head.

## Scripture
Ephesians 5:22-33 — Paul uses the marriage relationship as an illustration of the greater mystery: Christ as head of the church, with believers as members of His body. The passage emphasizes submission, sacrificial love, sanctification, and the profound oneness between Christ and the church, concluding that this mystery speaks more about Christ and the church than about earthly marriage.

## Key Themes
- **Christ as Head**: The passage emphasizes headship more than the husband-wife relationship, with Christ being the head of the church as the eternal, primary relationship being illustrated
- **Members of His Body**: Verse 30 reveals the profound truth that believers are members of Christ's body, His flesh and bones—representing Him on earth as His physical presence
- **The Great Mystery**: Paul identifies this as a great mystery (verse 32) concerning Christ and the church, not primarily about marriage
- **Oneness in the Body**: Believers are to have unity and oneness under Christ's headship, functioning as different members but one body
- **Proper Self-Assessment**: Understanding our identity in Christ's body means not thinking more highly of ourselves than we ought while recognizing our value

## Notes

### The Emphasis on Christ and the Church
- Popular interpretation focuses on husband-wife relationships (verses 22-33)
- The passage mentions the church, body, and Christ's relationship to the church repeatedly throughout
- Every instruction to husbands and wives includes "as" comparisons to Christ and the church
- Verse 24: "as the church is subject unto Christ, so let the wives be to their own husbands"
- Verse 25: "husbands love your wives even as Christ loved the church and gave himself for it"
- Verses 26-27 step away from marriage to focus exclusively on Christ sanctifying and cleansing the church
- The relationship between Christ and the church is eternal, while earthly relationships (marriage, parent-child, master-servant) are temporal

### Understanding Our Identity as His Body
- Verse 30: "For we are members of his body, of his flesh and of his bones"
- This is more than being in His hand—we ARE His hand
- We are ambassadors because our home is literally in heaven with spiritual blessings in heavenly places
- We represent Christ as His body, bone, and flesh on earth
- This creates a sobering responsibility—we are His hands, feet, mouth, and mind
- The challenge: we are His body while still being sin-cursed in this life
- Gospel songs rarely emphasize Christ as "head"—He's called King, Lord, Savior, but headship is not emphasized

### The Great Mystery Revealed
- Verse 31: "For this cause shall a man leave his father and mother, and shall be joined unto his wife, and they two shall be one flesh"
- Verse 32: "This is a great mystery: but I speak concerning Christ and the church"
- The oneness in marriage is beautiful, but not greater than believers being made Christ's body
- This was hidden and is now revealed
- This truth needs to be meditated upon and memorized—it's profound and difficult to fully comprehend

### Cross-Reference with Romans 12
- Romans 12:1-2: Present bodies as living sacrifice, transformed by renewing of mind
- Romans 12:3: "not to think of himself more highly than he ought to think, but to think soberly"
- Context shows this relates to functioning in the body of Christ, not just general pride
- Verses 4-6 list seven gifts to be given within the body
- We have value as members of Christ's body, but must not think more highly of ourselves than we ought
- Our greatest value is proving what is the good, acceptable, and perfect will of God

### Cross-Reference with 1 Corinthians 12
- 1 Corinthians 12:14-18: The body is not one member but many
- Different members have different functions (foot, hand, ear, eye)
- Some members are more comely (desirable) than others
- God has set every member in the body as it pleased Him
- We don't get to choose which member we want to be—we function where God places us
- Whatever the body needs, that's what we are to become so needs are met

### Application to Church Life
- Christ is the head, we are members of the body
- We must be "fitly joined together" with Christ and with one another
- Different personalities, preferences, genders, cultures—but one head brings unity
- The umbrella we come under: Christ being the head puts us on level ground
- Whatever role God assigns in the body is where we should function
- Our function should bring glory to God and Christ our head

### The Three Relationships in Ephesians 5-6
- Head and body (eternal relationship)
- Husband and wife (temporal)
- Parents and children (temporal)
- Masters and servants (temporal)
- The eternal relationship is the primary focus being illustrated through the temporal ones

### Meditation on Profound Truths
- Verse 30 is the kind of verse that needs to be memorized
- Such profound statements need to be meditated upon and thought on regularly
- When encountering verses hard to comprehend, recognize them as requiring deeper study
- Paul in Philippians spoke of being apprehended by Christ while still trying to apprehend
