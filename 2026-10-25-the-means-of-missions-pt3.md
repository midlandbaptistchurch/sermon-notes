## Summary
The final message in the series focuses on the seventh point: the worldwide movement God expects in missions. This movement must be Spirit-empowered, church-generated, multiplying, and worldwide in scope. Beginning at the church in Antioch, early missionaries were sent forth by the Holy Ghost, and their testimony became that they had "turned the world upside down." The mission's challenge is not merely counting missionaries, but reproducing churches that multiply themselves across America and around the world.

## Scripture
**Acts 13:1-6** — The church at Antioch sends out Barnabas and Saul as the Holy Ghost directs them after fasting and prayer. They depart to Salucia and Cyprus, preaching the Word of God.

**Acts 17:6** — Paul and his companions are described as those who "have turned the world upside down."

**2 Timothy 2:1-2** — Paul instructs Timothy to commit what he has learned to faithful men who will be able to teach others also, demonstrating the principle of multiplication.

## Key Themes
- **Spirit-Empowered Movement**: Missions is ultimately powered not by money, technology, or personalities, but by the Holy Ghost working through believers
- **Church-Generated Movement**: God accomplishes His work in the world through the local church, whether large or small, established or meeting in homes
- **Multiplying Movement**: The biblical pattern is one church planting another, which reaches another, training faithful men who can teach others also
- **Worldwide Movement**: The goal is turning the world upside down with the gospel of Jesus Christ, reaching neighbors across the street and across the sea

## Notes

### Review of the Series (The Magnificent Seven)
- **Motive**: God's will is for all men to be saved and come to the knowledge of the truth (1 Timothy 2:4)
- **Message**: Christ died for our sins, was buried, and rose again the third day according to the Scriptures—the glorious gospel
- **Messenger**: The type of man God sends to preach; how shall they hear without a preacher, and how shall they go unless they be sent
- **Method**: God's approach to evangelizing the world
- **Mandate**: The Great Commitment—God has committed unto us this ministry of reconciliation
- **Means**: The provision God wants us to provide through prayer, people, and resources
- **Movement**: The worldwide movement God expects (current message)

### The Movement God Expects (Acts 13:1-6)

#### Spirit-Empowered Movement
- Barnabas and Saul were sent forth by the Holy Ghost from Antioch
- The use of "Holy Ghost" indicates a personal relationship with the Holy Spirit
- Missions is not ultimately powered by money, technology, or personalities
- The Holy Spirit takes information and brings transformation to lives
- The movement is empowered by the Holy Spirit of God

#### Church-Generated Movement
- The sending occurred at the local church at Antioch
- The expression of the universal church is found in the local church
- God does His work in the world through the local church
- This includes churches with buildings, churches in homes, or Bible studies in the jungle
- After COVID, the need to emphasize the local church has increased because the devil's goal was to destroy it

#### Multiplying Movement
- Acts shows one church planting another, which reaches another
- 2 Timothy 2:1-2 outlines God's plan for multiplication
- Training occurs: commit to faithful men who shall be able to teach others also
- Example: 48 works scattered across an area the size of Texas in Myanmar
- Trained leaders find faithful men, train them, and send them to start churches

#### Worldwide Movement
- Testimony in Acts 17:6: "These that have turned the world upside down are come hither also"
- The world in the 21st century needs to be turned upside down
- Politics in America, Europe, Burma, Philippines demonstrate the world's need
- The Philippines calls democracy "demo-crazy" because it is crazy
- Filipino pastors were trained to be missionaries in other countries

### The Mission's Challenge
- Not merely how many missionaries are on the wall
- How many churches are reproducing themselves in America and around the world
- We have a message worthy of duplication
- We have instructions from Scripture worthy of duplicating in other men and places
- We have all the equipment needed; God is waiting on the desire to get it done

### God's Invitation
- God invites us to give
- God invites us to go
- God invites us to pray
- God invites us to send
- God invites us to reach our neighbor across the street and across the sea with the gospel
