## Summary
Continuing a study on the armor of God, Pastor Mark explores Ephesians 6:12 and the meaning of "we wrestle not against flesh and blood." He emphasizes that Satan's primary goal is to pervert and neglect truth through deception, lies, fear, doubt, and false values. The warfare Christians face is not physical but spiritual, centered in the mind and heart. Understanding what "flesh and blood" represents—temporal, earthly things resulting from the sin curse—helps believers recognize they are not fighting against people or circumstances, but against spiritual forces through maintaining their position in truth.

## Scripture
Ephesians 6:12 — "For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places."

## Key Themes
- **Satan's Goal**: Satan seeks to pervert and neglect truth through deception, lies inserted into truth, fear, doubt, false assumptions, and establishing false values.
- **Standing in Truth**: To "stand" means establishing a position in truth and not relinquishing it; believers grow from knowledge to understanding to wisdom, maintaining absolutes that cannot be moved.
- **Understanding Flesh and Blood**: "Flesh and blood" refers to temporal, earthly things resulting from the sin curse, not spiritual realities revealed by God.
- **Spiritual Not Physical Warfare**: The wrestling described is not physical combat but spiritual battle in the mind and heart concerning truth versus deception.

## Notes

### Review of Wiles of the Devil (from previous week)
- Satan's primary method: perverting and neglecting truth
- Deception and lies to seek corruption
- Inserting truth into lies and lies into truth
- Fear, doubt, and worry leading to false assumptions
- False portrayal claiming something true that is not
- Establishing false values—making unimportant things seem important

### Biblical Relations vs. Reality
- The Bible often uses relational language (running races, warfare, armor) to illustrate spiritual truths
- People often focus on the relation (the metaphor) and miss the actual truth being communicated
- Must take the relation back to the reality and understand it in context
- Many take Ephesians 6 out of context, thinking they should physically fight demons
- The warfare relates to something real but is figurative

### Standing Against the Wiles (Ephesians 4:14-15)
- Ephesians 4:14: "That we henceforth be no more children, tossed to and fro, and carried about with every wind of doctrine, by the sleight of men, and cunning craftiness, whereby they lie in wait to deceive"
- Answer: "But speaking the truth in love...grow up unto him in all things"
- Standing means establishing a position and not relinquishing it
- Not about never growing, but about maintaining absolutes in truth
- Truth is the kryptonite to all the wiles of the devil
- With truth and understanding, believers can see through anything Satan brings

### Walking in Vanity of Mind (Ephesians 4:17-19)
- Ephesians 4:17: Walk not "as other Gentiles walk in the vanity of their mind"
- Vanity = empty, without worth or value
- Their thoughts, values, and priorities are vain
- Understanding darkened, alienated from the life of God
- Through ignorance—not just not knowing, but ignoring what you know to be truth
- Many Christians have knowledge without understanding; never gets to their heart or values
- Blindness of heart, being past feeling
- Given themselves over to lasciviousness, uncleanness with greediness (sexual sins but also spiritual unfaithfulness)

### Spiritual Lasciviousness
- Paul emphasizes sexual sins because believers are members of Christ's body
- When married, you become one flesh
- Committing spiritual lasciviousness = cheating on God through the body
- Doing what? With who? With the world, the flesh, the vanity of one's mind

### Good Words and Fair Speeches (Romans 16:17-20)
- Romans 16:17-18: Mark those who cause divisions contrary to doctrine; they serve their own belly "by good words and fair speeches deceive the hearts of the simple"
- Satan uses good things to deceive God's people
- Things that are good in life, if exalted above God, become idols
- Need to humble ourselves and ask: "Lord, is there anything I'm missing? Any place I'm deceived?"
- Cannot tell anything to someone full of pride

### Wise and Simple (Romans 16:19-20)
- "Yet I would have you wise unto that which is good, and simple concerning evil"
- With wisdom, we cannot be deceived
- Evil things that deceive others will be simple to those with understanding
- Want temptation to be simple, like Christ's response to Satan in the wilderness
- Contrast with Genesis 3 where Eve was deceived
- Romans 16:20: "And the God of peace shall bruise Satan under your feet shortly"
- We bruise Satan when his wiles and evil are simple because we've given ourselves to truth

### The Need for Self-Examination
- Pastor Mark wondered: "Is there any place in my own life that I am deceived?"
- "Is there something I'm missing while living my life and thinking how I think?"
- Critical to understand the enemy and humble ourselves
- Ask God to teach us and keep us open

### What is Flesh and Blood?

**Matthew 16:17**
- Peter's confession: "Thou art the Christ, the son of the living God"
- Jesus: "Blessed art thou Simon Barjona: for flesh and blood hath not revealed it unto thee, but my Father which is in heaven"
- First use of "flesh and blood" phrase in Bible
- Means: man didn't reveal this, something temporal didn't reveal this—God Himself did
- God revealed truth, communicated it, allowed Peter to see it

**1 Corinthians 15:50**
- "Now this I say, brethren, that flesh and blood cannot inherit the kingdom of God; neither doth corruption inherit incorruption"
- Flesh and blood cannot go to heaven
- Our bodies will be changed, putting on incorruption
- Speculation: Before sin, Adam and Eve may have had "living water" instead of blood
- When they sinned, blood entered (blood represents the sin curse)
- At rapture, not just piles of clothes but complete transformation—blood changed back to living water
- Flesh and blood = earthly, temporal, result of sin curse

**Galatians 1:16**
- Paul called by God's grace "to reveal his son in me that I might preach him among the heathen; immediately I conferred not with flesh and blood"
- Didn't go talk to people, went to God who revealed truth to him
- Direct communication with God versus earthly/temporal consultation

### Summary of Flesh and Blood
- Dealing with earthly, temporal things
- Sometimes sin-curse related things
- Versus direct communication with God and eternal things
- When Ephesians 6:12 says "we wrestle not against flesh and blood," it means our battle is not against temporal, earthly, human things
- Our warfare is spiritual, in the mind and heart

### The Nature of Our Warfare
- Not physical like Jacob wrestling with God (Genesis)
- This is spiritual—begins with the mind, goes to the heart
- That's where we deal with it
- Our battle is about truth versus deception in our minds and hearts
