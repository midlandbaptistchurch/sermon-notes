## Summary
Pastor Mark Simpson continues the study on headship from Ephesians 5:22-33, emphasizing that true headship involves not just submission but also exaltation. He argues that before wives can properly submit to husbands as heads, believers must first understand what it means for the church to submit to Christ as head. The sermon illustrates that headship requires using one's mind to study, discern, and understand, not mindless obedience, and that the relationship provides benefits like comfort, security, peace, and purpose.

## Scripture
Ephesians 5:22-33 — Paul instructs wives to submit to husbands as to the Lord, for the husband is head of the wife as Christ is head of the church. Husbands are commanded to love wives as Christ loved the church. The passage uses the marriage relationship as a picture of Christ's relationship with the church.

## Key Themes
- **Headship Includes Exaltation**: True submission to a head involves not just obedience but actively exalting and making much of the one who is head.
- **Christ as Model for All Headship**: Before understanding marital headship, believers must first understand Christ's headship over the church and God the Father's headship over Christ (1 Corinthians 11:3).
- **Headship Requires Thinking**: Showing proper headship requires using one's mind to study, discern, and understand, not cutting off one's head to let another do all thinking.
- **Benefits of Proper Headship**: Submitting to Christ as head provides comfort, security, peace, hope, joy, and purpose that cannot be found elsewhere.

## Notes

### Introduction - Father's Day and Headship Context
- Father's Day officially instituted in 1972 by President Nixon
- Originated in early 1900s Washington when a woman raised by her father alone heard a Mother's Day sermon and advocated for fathers
- This study continues examining headship from Ephesians 5, which has been the focus for the past two weeks

### The Importance of Recognizing Christ as Head
- People commonly refer to Christ as Savior, God, King, but rarely as "head"
- If Scripture calls Christ "head," this makes it automatically significant and worthy of attention
- Many emphasize husband as head of wife more than Christ as head of church, creating imbalance
- Cannot have right understanding when knowledge is concentrated in one area and diluted in another

### 1 Corinthians 11:3 - The Chain of Headship
- "The head of every man is Christ and the head of the woman is the man and the head of Christ is God"
- Christ currently submits to God the Father while seated at the right hand of the Father
- This demonstrates headship functioning even in the Trinity
- Christ models both being under headship and being a head

### Philippians 2 Connection
- Christ humbled himself, took form of a man
- Associated with common people (shepherds, fishermen) not religious elite
- Used John the Baptist as forerunner - rugged outdoorsman contrasting with religious Pharisees
- After becoming sacrifice, Christ was exalted to right hand of Father
- Even in exalted position, continues to submit to God as head

### What Headship Is NOT
- Not cutting off your own head so the other person does all thinking
- Not stopping being a complete whole person
- Not just mindless obedience without engagement
- Not just a list of do's and don'ts
- Not about one person becoming superior and the other inferior

### What True Headship Requires
- Using your mind, thoughts, ability to study
- Ability to discern and make decisions
- Active engagement rather than passive compliance
- Understanding and wisdom, not just knowledge
- Learning to think and know God's will
- Studying to understand the whole plan and program of God

### Two Components of Headship
1. **Submission** - Obedience and being subject to authority
2. **Exaltation** - Making much of, lifting up, glorifying the one who is head
- Both must be present for proper headship
- Example: Queen of Sheba to Solomon - "Happy are thy servants" showed joy in serving

### Benefits Received from Christ's Headship
- **Comfort** - Not just being comfortable, but security
- **Peace** - Peace that passes understanding
- **Security** - Confidence in eternal destiny and purpose
- **Hope** - Living with expectation of life to come
- **Joy** - Delight in the relationship
- **Love** - Unconditional love that cannot be undone
- **Knowledge and Wisdom** - Submitting to One who knows everything
- **Purpose** - Understanding why we do what we do

### The Problem of Missing the Relationship
- Some view Christianity as just do's and don'ts (can't do this, can't do that)
- They miss the relationship and benefit package that comes with Christ as head
- Grace gives principles, not just rules - supposed to learn how to think
- People with lots of knowledge (internet age) but little wisdom or understanding
- Knowledge without understanding becomes stagnant pool rather than living water

### Application to Marriage
- The "as" comparisons in the passage are critical
- Verse 24: "As the church is subject unto Christ, so let the wives be to their own husbands"
- Verse 23: "For the husband is the head of the wife, even as Christ is the head of the church"
- Before a wife submits to husband, must first understand what it looks like for church to submit to Christ

### Premarital Counseling Application
- Marriage follows three C's through Scripture:
  1. Chapter 1-2: **Created** by God
  2. Chapter 3: **Corrupted** by Lucifer
  3. Ephesians 5/Today: In **Christ**
- Teaching couples about the relationship and how things practically function
- Books used emphasize understanding how God designed the relationship

### Understanding Male and Female Design
- For men: Important to believe "he's the man" - simple need that brings contentment
- Women's needs are more complex and require more understanding
- Not by accident - God designed these differences with purpose
- The design points back to Christ's relationship with the church
- Husband desires exaltation from wife just as Christ desires exaltation from church

### The Danger of Ego vs. Confidence
- Word "ego" often seen negatively, especially by women
- Man controlled by ego becomes insecure, cocky, arrogant
- Nobody wants to follow arrogance
- Fine line between cockiness and confidence
- Teenage boys especially struggle with insecurity that worsens around girls
- Security should be found in Christ and who we are in Him
- Don't have to be better than everyone else to be what you're supposed to be

### Competition vs. Exaltation
- Some in Christian circles are more competitive than unified
- Want to be better than others to feel better about themselves
- Looking down on others brings Christ no glory
- Doesn't show biblical wisdom or proper headship
- God desires exaltation and submission together

### For Wives Specifically (Ephesians 5:22-24)
- Command to submit to own husbands as unto the Lord
- "In everything" - comprehensive scope with proper understanding
- If Christ is head of a wife, she will be much better at showing husband as head of home
- Can properly submit even though husband is not perfect, makes mistakes, is sin-cursed
- Getting hold of this brings forth blessing

### For Husbands Specifically (Ephesians 5:25)
- "Husbands love your wives, even as Christ also loved the church and gave himself for it"
- Must exemplify the love Christ showed to the church
- To truly show love to wives, must receive, understand and discern the price Christ paid
- Must understand the love Christ shows to us
- In role of headship, husband must provide a benefit package just like Christ does

### Security in Marriage
- One of the most important foundational things in marriage relationship
- Also foundational in relationship with God
- Asking: "If I get tired or mess up, what else am I going to do?"
- Still have to stand before Him, give account, receive glorified body
- Becomes matter of love AND logic - "This is just what I do"
- Security comes from understanding this is the only way that makes sense

### Receiving Christ's Love to Give Love
- To love wife, must receive and understand Christ's love
- Must understand the giving and sacrifice that goes with it
- Marriage relationship is temporal picture of eternal reality
- Roles in heaven will be different but similar
- Thought about angelic world and how angels came to be vs. how humans reproduce

### The Temporal Picture of the Eternal
- Marriage is temporary, pointing to something eternal
- How it will be in heaven will be different yet similar
- Understanding our roles and what we do
- Need to see Christ in His current position as head
- Time coming when He will rule over all things

### Conclusion
- Want to see Christ as head in His current position
- Want to understand our roles in marriage
- Don't want to just be submissive to Christ - want to exalt Him
- Want to lift Him up and see Him for who He is
- Want to enjoy and be reminded of all He gives
- Security in Christ is not just eternal security but security of purpose
- Challenge to see Christ as our head and exalt Him in that position
- Through submission, obedience, being subject, but also seeing who He is and making much of it
