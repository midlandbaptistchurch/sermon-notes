## Summary
Tim Faulk challenges Christians to turn from modern forms of idolatry that hinder God's mission movement. Using Paul's response to idolatry in Athens as a starting point, he identifies three primary idols among professing Christians: loving pleasures more than God, loving the praise of men more than God, and serving the creature more than the Creator. The sermon calls for repentance and turning to God to enable a great missions movement at the end of this age, citing Adoniram Judson's ministry in Burma as an example of what God can accomplish through those who put aside idolatry.

## Scripture
**Acts 17:16** — Paul's spirit was stirred when he saw Athens wholly given to idolatry, just as Christians should be stirred by the blatant idolatry in places like the Philippines and Burma.

**Colossians 3:5** — Identifies covetousness as idolatry, calling believers to mortify (kill) these earthly members.

**2 Timothy 3:1-7** — Describes last days characteristics including being "lovers of pleasures more than lovers of God," having a form of godliness but denying its power.

**John 12:42-43** — Chief rulers believed but wouldn't confess Christ because "they loved the praise of men more than the praise of God."

**Romans 1:24-27** — God gave them up because they "worshipped and served the creature more than the Creator."

**1 Thessalonians 1:9** — The Thessalonians turned to God from idols to serve the living and true God.

**Romans 5:20** — Where sin abounded, grace did much more abound.

## Key Themes
- **Idolatry Among Christians**: Modern idolatry is often less blatant than statues but equally damaging, including anything loved more than God
- **Three Primary Idols**: Loving pleasures more than God, loving praise of men more than God, and serving the creature more than the Creator
- **Hindering the Movement**: These forms of idolatry prevent Christians from experiencing God's power and participating in a great missions movement
- **Turning from Idolatry**: Like the Thessalonians, Christians must actively turn from idols to serve the living and true God
- **Grace Abounds**: God's grace is sufficient to overcome whatever idolatry stands in the way of serving Him

### Paul's Response to Idolatry (Acts 17:16)
- Paul's spirit was stirred seeing Athens wholly given to idolatry
- Blatant idolatry in the Philippines: Santo Niño on every shelf, exaltation of Virgin Mary
- Basilica in Cebu City where they mechanically change clothes on a doll they call God
- Buddhist idolatry in Burma: Shwedagon temple with various Buddha statues (skinny, pointing, fat)
- Intelligent people wholly given to idolatry
- If you're saved, seeing idolatry should stir your heart

### Vision for Revival and Movement
- Rejects the idea that we must be in the Laodicean age
- "I don't see anything in this Bible that tells me there can't be a revival down here toward the end of this age of grace"
- God wants a movement, a missions movement
- Devil knows Christians are prone to idolatry, though less blatant than in Athens

### First Idol: Loving Pleasures More Than God (2 Timothy 3:1-7)
- Characteristics of last days: lovers of own selves, covetous, boasters, proud, blasphemers
- Key phrase: "lovers of pleasures more than lovers of God"
- These are apostate Christians, not unbelievers
- They have "a form of godliness" but deny the power
- Cannot get hold of God's power because they love pleasures more than God
- Examples: Rather be fishing than at church, watching NFL than at church
- Taking two-week holiday to watch Alabama Crimson Tide play
- "They love God. They just love stuff more than God"
- Result: Can't come to the knowledge of the truth (verse 7)
- Their calendar is full of pleasures, not the things of God

### Second Idol: Loving Praise of Men More Than God (John 12:42-43)
- Chief rulers believed on Christ but wouldn't confess Him
- Feared being put out of the synagogue
- "They loved the praise of men more than the praise of God"
- Not that they didn't want God's praise—they just wanted men's praise more
- Results in form of godliness but denying the power
- Christians doing things for praise of men more than praise of God
- Love the praise of scholarship and titles behind their name
- Example: Young man from Burma leaving for five years to get doctorate degree
- Men wither when told the Bible is perfect as it is
- Would rather stand before Judgment Seat believing the book too much than too little
- "I'm betting that he won't" hold it against believing the Bible too much

### Third Idol: Serving the Creature More Than the Creator (Romans 1:24-27)
- Changed the truth of God into a lie
- How they do this: "Oh, it says this, but a better rendering would be that"
- "It says this, but it really means that"
- Speaker's response: "I'm going to believe what it says"
- Worshipped and served the creature more than the Creator
- Not that they didn't serve the Creator—they served the creature MORE
- God's response: "God gave them up unto vile affections" (verse 26)
- Led to homosexuality and other unnatural affections
- "I don't want God to give up on me"
- Must not give more credit to man than to God
- "The spirit of man that's in me bears witness with the spirit of man that's in you, and that is that we're all dirty rotten sinners"
- Must give credit to "Him who loved me and gave Himself for me"

### The Solution: Turn from Idols (1 Thessalonians 1:9)
- The Thessalonians "turned to God from idols to serve the living and true God"
- Can turn from whatever idolatry has infested you
- Self-examination questions presented:
  - Do I have more pleasure loving pleasures more than God?
  - Do I love the praise of men more than the praise of God?
  - Am I serving and worshiping the creature more than the Creator?
- Must ask Philippinos to turn from Santo Niño and Virgin Mary
- Must ask Buddhists to turn from Buddha statues
- Must ask Christians to turn from loving pleasures more than God

### Grace for the Turning (Romans 5:20)
- "Where sin abounded, grace did much more abound"
- True for salvation: grace abounds past our sin
- Also true for sanctification: wherever sin stops, grace abounds past it
- Can bring idolatry to the Lord and receive grace to overcome
- Don't have to let these things stand in the way

### Vision: The Adoniram Judson Example
- Judson left Massachusetts for India, ended up in Burma
- Goal: plant a church with 100 members
- Translated the Bible (the Judson Bible)
- By 1850: hundreds of churches with over 100,000 Christians in Burma
- Result of someone getting hold of the movement
- Someone put aside pleasure and served the Lord
- Put aside service to creature and served the Creator
- Put aside praise of men and sought praise of God

- Desire to see great missions movement at the end of this age
- "I really believe we're standing at the end of this age"
- Preparing hearts for movement involves:

  - Contrite spirit
  - Apologetic spirit
