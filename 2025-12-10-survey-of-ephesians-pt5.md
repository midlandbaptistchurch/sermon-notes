## Summary
Pastor Payne examines Ephesians 3, focusing on the mystery that Paul reveals about God's eternal plan for the body of Christ. He emphasizes how this mystery differs from the prophetic program for Israel and explains Paul's unique role as the apostle to the Gentiles.

## Scripture
Ephesians 3 — Paul explains the mystery of Christ and his commission to reveal God's plan for the Gentiles.

## Key Themes
- The mystery of the body of Christ
- Paul as prisoner and apostle to the Gentiles
- Heavenly places and future glory
- Distinction between mystery and prophecy
- God's eternal purpose in creation

## Notes

### The Importance of Ephesians 3
- Entire chapter devoted to "the mystery" - like other crucial doctrines given full chapters (1 Cor 13 on charity, 1 Cor 15 on resurrection)
- Most unknown important chapter in the Bible
- Breaks down into Paul's explanation (vv. 1-12) and prayer (vv. 13-21)

### Paul's Unique Position (3:1)
- "Prisoner of Jesus Christ for you Gentiles"
- Imprisoned because of his ministry to Gentiles
- References his arrest in Jerusalem and appeal to Caesar
- Called "the apostle" (not "an apostle") of the Gentiles in Romans 11:13

### The Mystery vs. Prophecy
- **Prophecy**: Gentiles saved through Israel's exaltation (Isaiah 60, Romans 15:8-12)
- **Mystery**: God's plan to save Gentiles apart from Israel's program
- Israel stumbled (Gospels) then fell (Acts 7 - Stephen's stoning)
- Through Israel's fall, salvation came to Gentiles (Romans 11:11)

### Christ's Different Ministries
- **Romans 15:8**: Christ was minister to circumcision (Israel)
- **Romans 15:16**: Paul is minister to Gentiles
- Two different programs with different purposes

### The Body of Christ
- Hidden from ages and generations (Colossians 1:26)
- "Christ in you" - the hope of glory
- CRIBS acronym: Crucified, Regenerated, Indwelt, Baptized (into body), Sealed
- Purpose: to fill heavenly places and eventually dislodge Satan

### Heavenly Places
- Not just "dying and going to heaven"
- Organized hierarchy of authority and responsibility
- Body of Christ will fill these places based on faithfulness to truth
- Connected to God's eternal purpose and new creation
