## Summary
Pastor Payne concludes the series on the Judgment Seat of Christ by examining specific areas of accountability believers will face: wealth and stewardship, fruit-bearing and soul-winning, family relationships, and finishing well. He emphasizes that believers must give an account for how they used their resources, whether they won souls to Christ, how they fulfilled their family roles, and whether they remained faithful to Pauline doctrine throughout their lives.

## Scripture
2 Timothy 4:6-8 — Paul's final words before execution, declaring he has fought the good fight, finished his course, and kept the faith.

## Key Themes
- Accountability for wealth and stewardship (1 Timothy 6:17-19)
- Soul-winning as fruit and crown (Proverbs 11:30, 1 Thessalonians 2:20)
- Family relationships and roles (Ephesians 5)
- Finishing well and keeping the faith
- Continuing in Pauline doctrine

## Notes

### Review of Previous Points
1. **Foundation** - Building upon Paul's ministry, not the twelve apostles
2. **Faith** - God rewards those who diligently seek Him
3. **Faithfulness** - Being found faithful as stewards
4. **Fidelity** - Doing all to God's glory, not man's praise
5. **Fealty** - Inner devotion manifested in godly living
6. **Fitness** - Keeping the body under subjection
7. **Favor** - Living with charity/love in action

### Accountability for Wealth (1 Timothy 6:17)
- Every American believer is rich by world standards
- Money represents time spent in life
- Four uses for money:
  1. Provide for family
  2. Help other people
  3. Support local church ministry
  4. Support missions work
- "Laying up in store for themselves a good foundation against the time to come"
- Under grace, believers should give "much more" than Israel's tithe

### Soul-Winning and Fruit-Bearing
- "The fruit of the righteous is a tree of life; and he that winneth souls is wise" (Proverbs 11:30)
- "The crown of the wise is their riches" - souls won to Christ
- Paul called the Thessalonians his "hope or joy or crown of rejoicing"
- "They that sow in tears shall reap in joy" (Psalm 126:5-6)
- Challenge: "Who are you taking to heaven with you?"

### Family Accountability (Ephesians 5)
- **Husbands**: Provide, protect, preserve wives; give spiritual leadership; rear children in the Lord
- **Wives**: Submit to husband's leadership; strengthen and support him; make him the best man he can be
- Both will give account to Christ for their stewardship of family relationships

### Finishing Well (2 Timothy 4:6-8)
- Paul's three declarations before execution:
  1. "I have fought a good fight" - against world, flesh, and devil
  2. "I have finished my course" - completed God's predetermined path
  3. "I have kept the faith" - maintained the body of Pauline doctrine
- Warning against departing from "the faith" (1 Timothy 4:1)
- Must "continue in the faith" to be presented "holy and unblameable" (Colossians 1:22-23)

### The Fight Against Three Enemies
1. **The World** - crucified to us through the cross (Galatians 6:14)
2. **The Flesh** - crucified with its affections and lusts (Galatians 5:24)
3. **The Devil** - seeks to take believers captive and blind them to truth
