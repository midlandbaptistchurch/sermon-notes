## Summary
Pastor Payne continues the series by examining the rise and fall of Lucifer, revealing how this creature's pride and rebellion against God brought conflict and warfare into creation. Using Ezekiel 28 and Isaiah 14, he shows how Lucifer's transformation from an anointed cherub to Satan is the root cause of all spiritual warfare believers face today.

## Scripture
Ezekiel 28:12-15 — God's description of Lucifer as the anointed cherub who was perfect until iniquity was found in him.

## Key Themes
- The origin of spiritual conflict in Lucifer's rebellion
- Life as warfare for believers - "onward Christian soldiers"
- Lucifer's original beauty, wisdom, and anointed position
- The holy mountain of God and stones of fire
- Pride as the root of all rebellion against God
- The Christian life characterized as spiritual battle

## Notes

### Review of Seven Indicators
Before addressing Lucifer's fall, Pastor Payne reviewed seven indicators from Genesis 1-2 showing trouble existed before Adam and Eve's sin:
1. Darkness not declared good
2. The word "replenish" 
3. "Subdue" indicating enemies to put down
4. "Dominion" given because it was taken from angels
5. Adam told to "dress and keep" the garden (protective language)
6. Tree of knowledge of "good and evil" - evil already existed
7. "Ye shall be as gods" - other gods already present

### Life as Warfare
The Christian life is fundamentally warfare:
- 2 Corinthians 10:3-4 - "weapons of our warfare"
- 1 Timothy 1:18 - "fight a good warfare"
- 2 Timothy 2:3 - "endure hardness as a good soldier"
- Ephesians 6:12 - "we wrestle"
- God Himself is "a man of war" (Exodus 15:3)

### Lucifer's Original State (Ezekiel 28:12-15)
- **Full of wisdom** - given the sum of wisdom (not wisdom itself like Christ)
- **Perfect in beauty** - adorned with precious stones for beautification
- **Musical abilities** - pipes and tabrets created within him
- **Anointed cherub** - highest position covering God's presence
- **Set on the holy mountain of God** - like Moses at Mount Sinai
- **Perfect until iniquity was found** - pride led to his downfall

### The Holy Mountain of God
The mountain represents God's kingdom where He meets with His creatures:
- Parallels between Lucifer's position and Moses' calling
- Both had authority over God's people
- Both encountered God in "stones of fire"
- Satan later disputed over Moses' body (Jude 9)

### The Root Problem: Pride
1 Timothy 3:6 warns against pride "lest he fall into the condemnation of the devil" - pride was Lucifer's downfall and remains dangerous for all believers.
