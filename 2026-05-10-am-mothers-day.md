## Summary
Pastor Jim Payne examines the life of Deborah from Judges 4-5 as an example of godly motherhood and womanhood. Through Deborah's example as a prophetess, faithful wife, leader, and mother in Israel, he encourages mothers to be spiritual women who honor their husbands and raise godly children.

## Scripture
Judges 4:4-9, 5:7 — The account of Deborah, a prophetess and judge who led Israel during a time of oppression.

## Key Themes
- Spiritual motherhood and being a "prophetess" in the home
- Honoring marriage and identifying with one's husband
- Righteous leadership and strength in difficult times
- The lasting legacy and influence of godly mothers

## Notes

### Why We Honor Mothers
- National holiday established by President Woodrow Wilson
- Our "reasonable service" as God's people (Romans 12)
- The fifth commandment: "Honor father and mother" (Ephesians 6:2)
- Because we want to, from our hearts

### Deborah's Character Traits

**1. A Spiritual Woman (Judges 4:4)**
- She was a prophetess - one of only five women in Scripture given this title
- Prophets brought God's message to the people (Hebrews 1:1, Amos 3:7)
- She walked with God, heard from God, and spoke for God
- Mothers need to be "prophetesses" who translate God's Word into their lives

**2. A Faithful Wife (Judges 4:4)**
- Called "the wife of Labidoth" - she identified with her husband
- Even though she was famous, she honored her covenant with her husband
- She reverenced him and his role as head of the home
- Never let career or success usurp the priority of home and family

**3. A Righteous Leader (Judges 4:5)**
- She judged Israel - both legislated law and served as a liberator
- Sat under "the palm tree of Deborah" - palm trees symbolize righteousness (Psalm 92:12)
- People came to her for judgment and guidance
- She was a role model for family and nation

**4. A Mother in Israel (Judges 5:7)**
- She arose as "a mother in Israel" - raising up a godly seed
- Motherhood should be seen as the most important role
- The first 5-7 years are especially impressionable for children
- "God couldn't be everywhere, so He created mothers"

**5. A Fighter and Warrior (Judges 4:6-9)**
- When Barak was afraid to fight without her, she went to battle
- She inspired courage in weak male leadership
- Great men often have great mothers (Samuel/Hannah, Timothy/Eunice and Lois)
- Mothers have opportunity to influence the world for Christ

### The Legacy of Motherhood
The sermon concluded with a reading about a mother's journey through life, showing how she taught her children courage, strength, and faith in God, leaving a living presence that extends beyond death.
