## Summary
Pastor Payne continues his series on the skeletal system of Scripture, examining Genesis 1:1-2 and arguing that the word "and" in verse 2 represents an extended period of time during which Lucifer reigned and fell, resulting in divine judgment that left the earth "without form and void."

## Scripture
Genesis 1:1-2 — The opening verses of creation, with focus on the gap between God's initial creation and the earth's subsequent state.

## Key Themes
- The skeletal system of the Bible provides structure from beginning to end
- God's will is to reconcile heaven and earth under Christ's authority
- The word "and" in Scripture can represent extended periods of time
- Darkness in Scripture is associated with divine judgment
- Genesis 1:2 describes judgment, not creation

## Notes

### The Skeletal System Framework
- Physical aspect: "the heaven and the earth" → "the heavens and earth" → "new heaven and new earth"
- World systems: Four distinct periods from creation through eternity
- All of creation traces back to one person (Lucifer) and one event (his fall)

### Genesis 1:1-2 Analysis
- Verse 1: "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth" (complete statement)
- Verse 2: "And the earth was..." (the word "and" represents extended time)
- Examples of "and" representing time gaps: Luke 4:15-16 with Isaiah 61:1-2 (2,000 years between Christ's first and second coming)

### The Fall of Lucifer
Three key questions about Lucifer:
1. When was he created? (Job 38:7 - when angels rejoiced at earth's foundation)
2. When did he fall? (Before Genesis 3:1, as he appears as the serpent)
3. When did he reign? (Between Genesis 1:1 and 1:2)

### Divine Judgment in Genesis 1:2
- "Without form and void" appears only twice in Scripture: Genesis 1:2 and Jeremiah 4:23
- Jeremiah 4:23-26 describes this phrase as resulting from God's "fierce anger"
- God didn't create the earth "in vain" (Isaiah 45:18) but perfect
- Darkness represents divine judgment (Exodus 10:21, Matthew 27:45)

### The Body of Christ Connection
- The body of Christ was chosen "before the foundation of the world" (Ephesians 1:4)
- Kept secret "from the foundation of the world"
- Part of God's eternal plan to reconcile all things
