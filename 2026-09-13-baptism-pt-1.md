## Summary
Bill Baughman leads Sunday School through a survey of the twelve baptisms found in Scripture, arguing that only the twelfth — spiritual baptism into Christ — is the one baptism that applies to believers in the current age. He then explores the mechanics of that spiritual transformation by examining the tripartite nature of the human person (spirit, soul, and body) and how God's indwelling Spirit helps believers resist worldly corruption.

## Scripture
Romans 6:3-5 — Paul's declaration that believers are baptized into Christ's death and resurrection, serving as the theme verse and anchor for the lesson's treatment of what baptism means for the believer today.

## Key Themes
- The twelve baptisms of Scripture and their dispensational contexts
- Spiritual baptism into Christ as the one baptism for the current age (Ephesians 4:5)
- The tripartite nature of humanity: spirit, soul, and body (1 Thessalonians 5:23)
- Spiritual circumcision: the removal of the body of sin at salvation (Colossians 2:11-12)
- The indwelling Holy Spirit as a tool for balancing spiritual and worldly influences
- The ongoing war of the flesh (Romans 7) and the believer's responsibility to yield members to righteousness

## Notes

### The Twelve Baptisms Surveyed
Bill works through twelve baptisms identified in Scripture, noting that most are tied to water, specific dispensations, ceremonies, or historical events:

1. **The Flood** — God's judgment on all flesh; Noah and his family saved through water (Genesis 6; 1 Peter 3:20-21)
2. **Baptism unto Moses** — Israel passing through the sea under the cloud (1 Corinthians 10:1-2)
3. **Divers Washings under the Law** — Ceremonial priestly washings (Hebrews 9:10)
4. **Ceremonial Washings (tradition of men)** — Rebuked by Jesus as man-made tradition (Mark 7)
5. **John's Baptism of Repentance** — Public sign of readiness for the coming kingdom at the Jordan River (Acts 13:24)
6. **Jesus' Water Baptism by John** — The Holy Ghost descended as a dove; the Father's voice affirmed the Son (Luke 3:21-22)
7. **Baptism of Suffering and Death** — Jesus' identification with sin and the cup of wrath (Luke 12:50)
8. **Baptism of the Holy Ghost** — Pentecost; cloven tongues of fire, speaking in other tongues (Acts 2:1-4)
9. **Baptism with Fire** — Tied to Malachi's refiner's fire; purification at Christ's return (Matthew 3:11; Malachi 3:2)
10. **Water Baptism in the Name of Jesus** — Kingdom-focused baptism under Philip's preaching (Acts 8:12); connected to signs and miracles (Mark 16:16-18)
11. **Baptism for the Dead** — A false doctrine practiced by some Corinthians; also practiced by Mormons today — Paul corrects this (1 Corinthians 15:29)
12. **Baptism into Christ** — Spiritual baptism into Christ's death and resurrection; the one baptism for the present age (Romans 6:3-5; Ephesians 4:5; Colossians 2:12)

### The One Baptism That Matters Now
Bill argues that Hebrews' reference to "the doctrine of baptisms" (plural) acknowledges all eleven prior baptisms, but Ephesians 4:5 establishes that there is now **one baptism** for the body of Christ. This twelfth baptism is not water-based but spiritual — occurring at the moment of salvation, uniting the believer with Christ's death, burial, and resurrection.

### The Mechanics: Spirit, Soul, and Body
Drawing on 1 Thessalonians 5:23, Bill outlines the tripartite human person:

- **Spirit** — The point of connection with God. Contains God's Spirit, man's spirit, and the mind. God communicates only at the spirit level. The lost person is spiritually blind (Ephesians 4:18) and cannot perceive God's instruction.
- **Soul** — Contains the **mind** (thinking), **emotions** (feeling), and **will** (believing). Transformed by the renewing of the mind (Romans 12:2).
- **Body** — Contains **emotions** (linking back to the soul), **senses** (sight, hearing, smell, taste, touch), and **members** (physical actions).

**Key connections:**
- Spirit connects to soul via the **mind**
- Soul connects to body via **emotion**
- Input flows from God → spirit → soul → body → action (members)
- The world works in reverse: body (senses) → soul → man's spirit, potentially corrupting it

### Spiritual Circumcision
At salvation, the believer is spiritually circumcised — the body of sin is cut away (Colossians 2:11). Though the believer still lives in a fleshly container, the body of sin has been removed. The indwelling Holy Spirit (Romans 8:9-11) now equips the believer to resist worldly input coming through the senses and to walk in newness of life.

### The Balancing Act
Bill describes the Christian life as a balancing act: godly input (Scripture, preaching, teaching) flows spirit → soul → body; worldly input (lust of the flesh, lust of the eyes, pride of life — 1 John 2:16; James 3:15) flows body → soul → spirit. The baptism into Christ and the indwelling Spirit provide the tools to balance and overcome worldly corruption — the same war Paul describes in Romans 7.

### Bill's Personal Baptism Testimony
Bill shares that he was baptized by full immersion at an independent Baptist church after being saved, understanding it as an act of **identification** rather than salvation. He gathered his family under the pretense of a fishing trip so they could witness his public declaration of faith.
