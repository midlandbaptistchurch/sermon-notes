## Summary
Pastor Jim Payne explores the significance of the temple veil being torn from top to bottom at Christ's death, revealing three profound changes: the abolition of human priesthood, the elimination of animal sacrifices, and the consecration of a new way to approach God directly through Christ.

## Scripture
Mark 15:33-39 — The account of Christ's death and the supernatural tearing of the temple veil, demonstrating God's satisfaction with Christ's sacrifice.

## Key Themes
- The torn veil abolished the need for human priests as mediators
- Animal sacrifices were eliminated by Christ's perfect sacrifice
- God consecrated a new and living way to approach Him through Christ's flesh
- Believers now have bold access to God's presence
- Christ serves as our only mediator and high priest

## Notes

### Historical Context of the Temple Veil
- The veil was 60 feet tall, 30 feet wide, and 9-10 inches thick
- Woven with four threads: fine linen (righteousness), purple (kingship), blue (heavenly), scarlet (sacrifice)
- Only the high priest could pass behind it once per year on the Day of Atonement
- Herod's temple where this occurred was destroyed in 70 AD

### Seven Witnesses to Christ's Innocence
Before the crucifixion, seven different people/groups declared Christ's innocence:
1. Pilate - "I find no fault in this man"
2. Herod - "Nothing worthy of death in this man"
3. Judas - "I have betrayed innocent blood"
4. Pilate's wife - "Have nothing to do with this just man"
5. The thief on the cross - "He has done nothing wrong"
6. The centurion - "Truly this was the Son of God"
7. Roman soldiers - Acknowledged He was righteous

### Seven Sayings from the Cross
Three sayings (9am-12pm) focused on others:
- "Father, forgive them"
- "Today you will be with me in paradise"
- "Woman, behold your son; behold your mother"

Four sayings (12pm-3pm) during darkness:
- "My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?"
- "I thirst"
- "It is finished"
- "Father, into your hands I commend my spirit"

### Three Victories of the Torn Veil

**1. Abolish** - The intercessory work of human priesthood
- Christ is now our only mediator (1 Timothy 2:5)
- No need for human priests to confess sins to
- Direct access to God through Christ alone

**2. Eliminate** - The animal sacrificial system
- No more daily slaughter of animals at the brazen altar
- Christ's sacrifice was "once for all" (Hebrews 10:10)
- The gruesome, repetitive system was replaced by Christ's perfect sacrifice

**3. Consecrate** - A new and living way to God
- Bold access to the Holy of Holies through Christ's blood
- Christ's flesh was the veil that was torn
- God is satisfied with Christ's sacrifice (Isaiah 53:11)
- Believers can approach God with confidence

### The Berlin Wall Illustration
Just as the Berlin Wall (1961-1989) separated East and West and represented oppression versus freedom, the temple veil represented separation between holy God and sinful humanity. When Christ died, God "tore down" the veil from top to bottom, opening access to His presence.
