## Summary
Pastor Simpson challenges believers to develop intentional plans for spiritual growth and accountability before the judgment seat of Christ. Drawing on business planning principles (SMART goals), he outlines seven areas of personal responsibility toward God's Word: reading, studying, writing, memorization, meditation, teaching, and preaching. The sermon emphasizes that without a deliberate plan, believers risk neglecting their most important preparation.

## Scripture
2 Timothy 2:15 — "Study to show thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth."

Colossians 3:16 — "Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly in all wisdom; teaching and admonishing one another in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing with grace in your hearts to the Lord."

## Key Themes
- The importance of deliberate planning for spiritual preparation
- Stewardship of God's Word and personal accountability
- Seven foundational areas of engagement with Scripture
- The SMART goals framework applied to eternal priorities
- Time management and the mathematics of spiritual investment
- Overcoming procrastination through disciplined systems
- The warning of "suffering loss" at the judgment seat

## Notes

### The Need for a Plan
- Pastor Payne's seven-point series on the judgment seat (Foundation, Faith, Faithfulness, Fidelity, Fealty, Favor, Fitness) provides theological framework
- The judgment seat should inform daily thinking and decision-making
- Most people have detailed plans for temporal matters (finances, retirement, home remodeling) but lack plans for eternal matters
- "To fail to plan is a plan to fail" — a true principle not explicitly biblical but biblically aligned

### The SMART Framework Applied to Eternity
**S - Specific:** Choose one clear area of focus (e.g., stewardship of God's Word)
**M - Measurable:** Create goals with concrete metrics you can track
**A - Attainable:** Set objectives within your genuine ability and circumstances
**R - Relevant:** Ensure all actions align with your ultimate goal of standing approved before the Lord
**T - Time-bound:** Assign specific times and seasons to your spiritual disciplines

### Seven Areas of Responsibility to Scripture
1. **Reading** — Regular, deliberate engagement with the biblical text
2. **Studying** — Deep investigation requiring effort and discipline
3. **Writing** — Taking notes to transition reading into active study
4. **Memorization** — Hiding God's Word in your heart (Psalm 119:11)
5. **Meditation** — Pondering, musing, and dwelling on Scripture
6. **Teaching** — Sharing biblical truth with others
7. **Preaching** — Receiving and retaining what is proclaimed from the pulpit

### The Mathematics of Spiritual Investment
- 15 minutes daily of Bible reading = only 1% of 1,440 daily minutes
- Attending every church service (4 hours weekly) = only 2.4% of 168 weekly hours
- Most believers are unknowingly operating at minimal spiritual saturation
- "Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly" requires a definition of "richly" beyond minimal compliance

### Practical Implementation
- Write down your goals and keep records to track progress
- Choose a specific time and place for Bible reading (Pastor Simpson reads early morning in his basement)
- Use accountability tools: phone alarms, written schedules, daily reflection
- Build flexibility into your plan without abandoning the plan itself
- Compare your time investment in spiritual matters against your investment in temporal pursuits

### The Gravity of the Judgment Seat
- God will audit not just intentions but actions and time stewardship
- "Suffer loss" (1 Corinthians 3) awaits those who build carelessly despite having Christ as foundation
- The judgment is righteous and based on the Word of God (John 1:1)
- Disappointment and loss are real consequences, distinct from salvation but eternally significant

### Overcoming Resistance
- The sin-cursed flesh will not naturally pursue spiritual discipline
- What feels "hard" temporally is insignificant compared to standing before the Lord unprepared
- When God's Word begins to dwell richly, your values and perspective fundamentally shift
- Motivation follows action; begin with the plan, and spiritual hunger will develop
