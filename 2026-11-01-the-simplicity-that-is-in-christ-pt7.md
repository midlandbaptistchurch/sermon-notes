## Summary
Pastor Payne teaches on the simplicity of Christian service, emphasizing that serving God encompasses every arena of life—not just church activities. He explains that believers will receive "the reward of the inheritance" for their service to the Lord Christ, and that true worship is submission and service, not merely emotional expression. Service extends to the family, workplace, local church, and social interactions, all done heartily as unto the Lord.

## Scripture
Colossians 3:23-24 — Believers are to do everything heartily as unto the Lord, knowing they will receive the reward of the inheritance for serving the Lord Christ.

Psalm 100:2 — Commands believers to serve the Lord with gladness and come before His presence with singing.

## Key Themes
- **The Reward of Service**: There are two inheritances for believers—one of grace and glory, and one of reward based on service (Romans 4:4).
- **The Expectation of Service**: Believers have been begotten into God's family, baptized into the body of Christ, and bettered (translated from darkness to light), creating an expectation of service.
- **The Extension of Service**: Service encompasses all areas of life—family (fulfilling God-ordained roles), work (working as unto the Lord), local church (using gifts to serve), and social interactions (living godly before others).
- **Worship as Service**: Biblical worship is not emotional expression but falling down in submission and serving God; submission without service is empty, and service without submission will wear you out.

## Notes

### The Exhortation to Serve
- Colossians 3:23-24: Whatever we do, do it heartily as unto the Lord
- There is a "reward of the inheritance" for serving the Lord Christ
- This reward is not of grace but of debt (Romans 4:4)—God owes us for our service
- Psalm 100: Serve the Lord with gladness because He is God, He created us, and we are His people
- Joshua 24: Choose whom you will serve
- Romans 12:1: Presenting our bodies as a living sacrifice is reasonable service
- Revelation 1:1: The book is given to show servants what must come to pass

### The Expectation of Service
- **Begotten**: We've been birthed into God's family through the gospel (1 Corinthians 4:15), not adopted
- **Baptized**: We've been baptized by one Spirit into the body of Christ; the body serves the head
- **Bettered**: We've been translated from the power of darkness into the kingdom of God's dear Son (Colossians 1:13)
- Election deals with service, not salvation—Christ was God's elect servant (Isaiah 42:1)
- Christ came to serve, not to be ministered unto (Matthew 20:28)
- Angels are elect to serve as fellow servants (1 Timothy 5:21; Revelation 22:8-9)

### The Extension of Service: Four Arenas

#### 1. Family Service
- The family unit pictures the relationship between Christ and the believer/church
- **Husbands**: Love wives sacrificially (Ephesians 5:23, 25, 28); nourish and cherish them; provide and protect; make them feel secure and cherished
- **Wives**: Come alongside husbands in godly submission; complete them, not compete with them; make them better men
- **Children**: Obey parents in the Lord; honor them by walking with God and caring for them in old age (Mark 7:10-13)
- Proverbs 20:20; 30:17 warn of severe consequences for dishonoring parents

#### 2. Work Service
- Ephesians 6:6: Serve not with eye service as men pleasers, but as servants of Christ
- All ground is sacred ground for believers; God dwells in us wherever we go
- Work is holy activity when done unto the Lord
- We do our work enabled by God's strength, acknowledging Him
- Proverbs 21:4: The plowing of the wicked is sin because they don't acknowledge God

#### 3. Local Church Service
- The church is both an organism (spiritual body) and an organization (with tasks to accomplish)
- Various ministries: preaching, teaching, choir, cleaning, yard work, nursery, kitchen
- Try different areas of service; find what fits your gifts and abilities
- Never a failure until you quit trying

#### 4. Social/Public Service
- Galatians 5:13: Use liberty not for the flesh, but by love serve one another
- Romans 14:7-8: None of us lives or dies to ourselves; we live unto the Lord
- Romans 14:13: Don't put stumbling blocks in others' way
- Our lives affect others—fellow believers, pastors, and the lost world
- Romans 14:18: Serving Christ in these things makes us acceptable to God and approved of men

### Worship Defined
- Matthew 4:8-10: Worship is bowing down in submission and serving God
- Daniel 3 repeatedly connects falling down, worshiping, and serving
- Biblical worship is always a posture of submission (bowing, falling, kneeling)
- Worship = submission + service
- Submission without service is empty platitude
- Service without submission will wear you out
- Willie Byrd's example: cooking pancakes at men's prayer breakfast, saying "serving the Lord with gladness"
