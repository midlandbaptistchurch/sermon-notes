## Summary
In this second installment of his series on Christian experience, Pastor Jim Payne argues that there are essential spiritual experiences every believer should undergo before leaving this earth. Building on the foundation of regeneration and redemption covered in Part 1, he walks through three additional experiences: the revelation of biblical truth through the Holy Spirit, the resorting to the local church as a place of spiritual refreshment and community, and the rescue of the lost through personal evangelism.

## Scripture
Romans 5:1-5 — Paul's description of the chain from justification through tribulation to experience and hope serves as the theological anchor for the entire series. Ephesians 1:15-18 provides the basis for the experience of revelation, with Paul's prayer that believers would receive "the spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of Him." Mark 2:13 and John 10:41 illustrate resorting to Christ, while Jude 1:21-23 grounds the call to rescue the lost.

## Key Themes
- The Christian life is defined by real, personal spiritual experience
- Regeneration and redemption are the foundation of all subsequent Christian experience
- Revelation — having one's spiritual eyes opened to rightly divide the Word — is a necessary and transformative experience
- The local church is God's ordained "resort" for spiritual refreshment, community, and mutual ministry
- Every believer should experience the joy of pulling someone out of the fire through evangelism
- Dispensational understanding of Scripture (distinguishing Paul's ministry, the mystery, and right division) is central to the experience of revelation

## Notes

### Introduction
- Title drawn from the old American Express slogan: "Don't leave home without it" — adapted to "Don't leave earth without these experiences."
- Romans 5:1-5 is the anchor text: justification, faith, peace with God, access, hope, tribulation, patience, **experience**, and hope that does not disappoint.
- Experience is defined as "a particular instance of personally encountering or undergoing something" — it is subjective and personal.
- The Christian life, like natural life, is fundamentally shaped by experience.

### Review: Experience 1 — Regeneration
- Titus 3:5 — not by works but by the washing of regeneration.
- Regeneration = being given spiritual life; Ephesians 2:1 — "you hath he quickened, who were dead in trespasses and sins."
- Caution against hollow religious exercises without true regeneration.
- True salvation produces fruit and a knowing of "the grace of God in truth" (Colossians).
- Pastor Payne's testimony: upon salvation he told his wife Tracy something had happened to him, even before he fully understood what regeneration meant.

### Review: Experience 2 — Redemption
- Redemption is closely tied to regeneration — being bought by the blood of Christ.
- The Holy Spirit is the earnest (down payment) of redemption.
- God saves us to be vessels of honor in His great house (2 Timothy 2).
- Redemption renews the mind and reorients values, goals, and direction entirely.

### Experience 3 — Revelation
- Key text: Ephesians 1:15-18 — Paul's prayer that God would give believers "the spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of Him" and that "the eyes of your understanding" would be enlightened.
- Also Ephesians 3:1-5 — the mystery hidden in other ages but now revealed, made known to Paul by revelation and then to the apostles and prophets by the Spirit.
- This is not a second blessing of tongues (a charismatic error described as "a lie out of the pit of hell") — it is the Holy Spirit opening the mind to understand Scripture rightly.
- Central to this revelation is right division: understanding the difference between Paul's ministry and the Twelve, between "time past," "but now," and "ages to come," and between the Gospels' perspective and Paul's epistles.
- When this revelation comes, apparent contradictions in Scripture resolve and the Bible "makes sense."
- Congregants Adam and Willie are cited as personal examples of people who experienced this eye-opening moment.
- Pastor Payne's own testimony: early confusion as a new believer studying Scripture resolved when someone pointed him to Paul's ministry; God opened his eyes and he underwent a "revolution" in his understanding.
- This experience must be sought in prayer: "Lord, please open my eyes and give me the spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of you."

### Experience 4 — Resorting (The Local Church)
- Key texts: Mark 2:13 and John 10:41 — multitudes "resorted" to Jesus for teaching and truth.
- Matthew 11:28 — "Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest."
- A resort is a place one frequents for refreshment, renewal, rest, and pleasure — the local church is God's designed resort for believers.
- Daily personal devotion is a spiritual resorting to Christ; the local church is the physical dimension of that resorting.
- The local assembly is God's mechanism for the betterment of the believer's life — an institution, organism, and organization.
- Paul's consistent "one another" refrain across his epistles (Romans 15:5; 1 Corinthians 12:25; Galatians 6:2; Ephesians 4:2-3, 32; Colossians 3:13; 1 Thessalonians 4:18; 5:11) shows that involvement in one another's lives is central to God's design.
- The local church should become the **culture** of a believer's life — not merely a Sunday obligation but a people, a place, a pastor, and a purpose.
- Resistance to the local church is described as rebellion against the Word of God.
- Challenge to the congregation: Who are you "one anothering" with? Who are you serving? What is your purpose here?

### Experience 5 — Rescue (Evangelism)
- Key text: Jude 1:21-23 — "of some have compassion, making a difference; and others save with fear, pulling them out of the fire."
- Every believer should experience at least once the act of sharing the gospel and seeing someone come to faith.
- The experience of rescue: sitting with a lost person, presenting the gospel, and watching regeneration take place before your eyes — eyes opening, ears unstopped.
- God does not need us, but He has designed it so that He works through us; He has entrusted believers with the treasure of the gospel.
- Nothing surpasses the opportunity of leading someone to understand Christ and call upon Him for salvation.
- Challenge: No believer should live and die without making the effort to share the gospel and pull someone out of the fire.

### Conclusion
- The five experiences: Regeneration, Redemption, Revelation, Resorting, Rescue.
- These are not optional extras — they are the experiences God has ordained for every believer to enjoy.
- The Christian life is not mundane; it is defined by these encounters with the living God.
- Closing prayer: that every believer would examine where they may be falling short in these experiences and press in to all that God has ordained.
