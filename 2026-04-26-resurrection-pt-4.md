## Summary
Bill Baughman examines what Jesus did and said to demonstrate he cannot be merely a "good teacher" but must be God. By analyzing Jesus' "organically outrageous" actions—including cleansing the temple, performing miracles, and making divine claims—Baughman contrasts how humanity past, Jewish leadership, disciples, and modern people have viewed Jesus. The sermon emphasizes that Christ's resurrection was uniquely permanent and transformative, different from all other biblical resuscitations, and calls believers to live with honest, authentic witness to their faith.

## Scripture
Luke 24:1-12 — The account of the women discovering Jesus' empty tomb and the angels announcing his resurrection.

## Key Themes
- Jesus' divinity demonstrated through his words and actions
- The concept of being "organically outrageous"—genuinely shocking without being manufactured
- Contrast between how the world views Jesus and what Scripture reveals
- The uniqueness of Christ's resurrection compared to other biblical resuscitations
- Living as authentic witnesses to the resurrection

## Notes

### What Jesus Said
- Jesus claimed to be the Son of Man (which means God)
- His teachings were shocking and extreme: cutting off offending body parts, radical spiritual demands
- He presented himself with divine authority and claims that no mere teacher would make

### What Jesus Did
- Cleansed the temple during Passover, overturning tables and driving out merchants with a scourge
- Declared the temple sign: "Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up" (referring to his body)
- Withered the fig tree for lack of fruit, demonstrating divine judgment
- Cast demons into swine, controlling supernatural forces
- Healed the blind using methods that seemed outrageous (spitting on eyes, making clay)
- These actions transcended what any good teacher could accomplish

### How Different Groups Responded
- **Past humanity**: Said Jesus was John the Baptist, Elijah, Jeremiah, or one of the prophets
- **Jewish leadership**: Claimed he was the Son of David (rejecting his divinity), but couldn't answer his questions about Scripture
- **Disciples**: Peter correctly identified him as "the Christ, the Son of the living God," but later rebuked him when he spoke of his suffering, revealing incomplete faith
- **Modern people**: Surveys show 52% of U.S. adults see Jesus as a good moral teacher rather than divine; 30% of evangelicals similarly deny his divinity

### Jesus' Resurrection vs. Other Resuscitations
Ten other biblical resuscitations occurred (widow of Zarephath's son, Sunamite woman's son, widow of Nain's son, Jairus' daughter, Lazarus, Tabitha/Dorcas, Eutychus, and others), but they differ fundamentally from Christ's resurrection:

| Aspect | Other Resuscitations | Jesus' Resurrection |
|--------|----------------------|---------------------|
| Nature | Temporary revival to physical life | Permanent transformation to eternal life |
| Outcome | All died again later | Never dies, lives forever |
| Body Type | Same original mortal earthly body | Glorified, immortal, spiritual body |
| Power Source | Performed by Jesus or apostles | Jesus' own power |
| Ability After Rising | Normal human limitations | Could eat, be touched, appear/disappear, pass through objects |
| Purpose | Show God's power, comfort families, point to Jesus | Defeat death and sin permanently; first fruits of resurrection for believers |

### Living "Organically Outrageous"
- "Outrageous" = shocking, extreme, unconventional
- "Organically" = genuinely arising from the person, honest, not manufactured or scripted
- Not about being stupid or weird, but about being visibly authentic as a believer
- Living in a way that shows Christ is active and real in your life
- Examples: dressing distinctly for church, carrying a Bible visibly, praying openly
- The goal is for others to see and recognize: "That person is a believer in Christ"

### Key Scripture on the Resurrection
1 Corinthians 15:42-45 — The contrast between the natural body (sown in corruption, dishonor, weakness) and the spiritual body (raised in incorruption, glory, power). Jesus became "the last Adam...made a quickened spirit."

1 Corinthians 15:20-22 — Christ is "the firstfruits" of those who have slept; through his resurrection comes the guarantee of resurrection for all believers.

## Application
- Study Scripture seriously—even 10 hours of Bible study can transform understanding of the resurrection
- Recognize that calling Jesus merely a "good teacher" contradicts what he actually said and did
- Live visibly as a believer—let your authentic faith be evident to those around you
- Understand that your future resurrection will be like Christ's: permanent, bodily, glorified, and eternal
- Be bold but not weird in your witness; let genuine faith shine through honest living
