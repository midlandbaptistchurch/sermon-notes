## Summary
Hugh Taylor introduces a multi-part Sunday School series on why Midland Baptist Church believes the King James Bible is the Word of God in the English language. Drawing on personal testimony, he traces his own journey from using multiple Bible versions interchangeably to becoming fully persuaded that the KJV alone is the authoritative English Scripture. This introductory session orients the class to the course structure and Hugh's research methodology before diving into the subject proper in future sessions.

## Scripture
Romans 14 — Hugh references verses 1, 5, and 12 to establish that every person must be fully persuaded in their own mind and will give account of themselves to God, applying this principle to one's conviction about the Bible.

## Key Themes
- The King James Bible as the Word of God in the English language
- Personal testimony and spiritual journey toward KJV conviction
- The danger of selecting Bible versions to fit one's own purposes
- Reconciliation to God in everyday life, dependent on having the true Word
- The importance of credible, validated research when presenting the KJV issue
- Approaching those who disagree with grace while remaining convicted

## Notes
**Church's Position**
- Midland Baptist Church holds that the King James Bible is the Word of God in the English language.
- Previous teaching on the subject includes a series by Brother Tim Falk and extensive manuscript-focused teaching by Brother Bill Boffman.

**Hugh's Personal Disclaimer**
- Unlike his usual teaching style (primarily Scripture-driven), this series draws heavily on outside reference materials, which Hugh has cross-referenced and validated as thoroughly as possible.
- Key reference material includes Doug Stauffer's book on the King James Bible and works by Peter Ruckman, among others.
- Hugh acknowledges the material is largely a repackaging and synthesis of other men's research.

**Personal Testimony Highlights**
- Around 1974, fellow airman Tom Stanley challenged Hugh with "the Bible says" regarding occult involvement — Hugh's first serious encounter with Scripture as authority.
- Hugh describes a period of using multiple Bible versions and picking whichever suited what he wanted to say — a practice he now recognizes as spiritually backward.
- In late summer/fall 1981, at Angelic Air Base in Turkey, fellow believer Mark Powell introduced Hugh to the KJV-only position, bringing Peter Ruckman's commentary on Revelation as a reference point.
- Hugh and his wife Regina fairly rapidly came to the conviction that the KJV is the Word of God in English.

**Romans 14 Application**
- "Let every man be fully persuaded in his own mind" (v.5) — Hugh uses this to extend grace to those who disagree, while noting that "every one of us shall give account of himself to God" (v.12) drives his own pursuit of truth.
- He argues that one's reconciliation to God — not just eternal salvation, but daily alignment — is distorted without the King James Bible as the true standard.

**Red Dot Sight Illustration**
- Hugh illustrates the KJV issue using a red dot optic on a handgun: using the wrong Bible is like misreading the sighting system — you may hit something, but you will not hit the bullseye of truth. Correct sight alignment (the KJV) puts the red dot on the target.

**Course Structure (Two Parts)**
- *Part One — Precursors:* The backdrop of the KJV issue; God's interaction with man in producing Scripture; development of the English language; earlier English translations that led up to the KJV.
- *Part Two — Three Aspects of Integrity:* The integrity of the **men** (the translators), the **manuscripts**, and the **message**. Hugh notes the message aspect will be the most practically useful for believers when discussing Bible versions with others, as doctrinal corruptions in other versions can be demonstrated by direct comparison.
