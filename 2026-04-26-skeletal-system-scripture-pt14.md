## Summary
Pastor Jim continues his examination of Genesis 1, focusing on days 2-6 of creation to understand God's will. He explains how God created the firmament to divide waters, established three heavens, caused dry land to appear, and created life culminating in mankind's creation with dominion over the earth.

## Scripture
Genesis 1 — The six days of creation, distinct from the original creation of verse 1.

## Key Themes
- The firmament divides waters above from waters below, creating the universe
- Three heavens: God's throne realm, the firmament (space), and earth's atmosphere
- Dry land appeared when God gathered waters into ocean basins
- All creation reproduces "after its kind" - species remain distinct
- Mankind created in God's image with dominion to subdue the earth
- The cosmic battle between God and rebellion spans from Lucifer's fall to Adam's fall

## Notes

### Day Two - The Firmament (Genesis 1:6-8)
- God places firmament "in the midst" of waters to divide them
- The firmament is what we call the universe or space
- Creates separation between heaven (God's realm) and earth after Lucifer's rebellion
- Results in multiple heavens: original heaven, firmament heaven, and open firmament (atmosphere)

### Day Three - Dry Land and Vegetation (Genesis 1:9-13)
- Dry land "appears" (not created new) when waters gather into designated places
- God "broke up" parts of earth to create ocean basins
- Waters from the great deep fled into these basins, allowing land to stretch out above
- Vegetation appears with seed "after its kind" - no evolutionary change between species

### Days Four and Five - Lights and Sea Life (Genesis 1:14-23)
- Sun, moon, and stars created to give light upon earth - earth was not made for sun
- Marine life "created" (first use of word since verse 1) because water never existed on original earth
- Fish told to "fill" waters, not "replenish" - they had never existed before
- Birds created from ground, fish from water

### Day Six - Land Animals and Mankind (Genesis 1:24-31)
- Three categories of land animals: cattle (domesticated), creeping things, beasts of field
- All reproduce "after their kind" - species boundaries maintained
- Mankind created in God's image as Trinity-like beings (body, soul, spirit)
- Given dominion over earth that Lucifer had lost through rebellion
- Told to "replenish" earth - suggesting previous habitation by angels
- Commanded to "subdue" earth - meaning to conquer enemies (Satan's forces)

### The Cosmic Battle
- Rebellion exists both in heaven (Lucifer's fall) and on earth (Adam's fall)
- Garden of Eden was a fort from which to fight and subdue Satan's influence
- Adam's fall brought corruption and death through disobedience
- Sets stage for ongoing spiritual warfare between God's kingdom and Satan's rebellion
