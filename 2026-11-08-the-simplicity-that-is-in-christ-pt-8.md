## Summary
Pastor Jim Payne preaches on the responsibility of every believer to be a soul winner. He emphasizes that soul winning is not a gift but a duty and act of obedience for all Christians. Using the acronym BPO (Burden, Preparation, Opportunity), he outlines the essential elements of soul winning. He shares his own conversion story through soul winner Howard Bebout and challenges the congregation to examine whether they have a burden for lost souls, arguing that the value of a soul is demonstrated by Christ's sacrifice on the cross.

## Scripture
Mark 8:36-37 — Jesus asks what profit there is in gaining the whole world but losing one's own soul, and what can be given in exchange for a soul.

Proverbs 11:30 — The fruit of the righteous is a tree of life, and he that wins souls is wise.

## Key Themes
- **Soul Winning as Responsibility**: Soul winning is not a spiritual gift but a responsibility, duty, privilege, and indebtedness for every believer.
- **The Value of a Soul**: The worth of a soul is determined by what was paid for it—the blood of Christ at Calvary.
- **BPO Framework**: Soul winning requires three elements: Burden (caring for lost people), Preparation (knowing the gospel and praying), and Opportunity (seeking and praying for chances to share).
- **Believers as Conduits**: Christians serve as go-betweens, conduits between God and sinners, carrying the word of reconciliation.
- **Excuses for Not Winning Souls**: Common reasons include feeling unprepared, being thoughtless or spiritually lazy, fear of failure or rejection, and fear of being seen as fanatical.

## Notes

### The Value of a Soul
- Jesus emphasizes the tragedy of gaining the whole world but losing one's "own soul" (Mark 8:36)
- Value is determined by what someone will pay for something
- The value of a soul is demonstrated by Christ's death—His flesh ripped, beard torn out, brow pierced, suffering hellfire on the cross
- Every soul created at conception is as eternal as God is eternal
- Souls that go to hell experience degradation, devolving into worm-like existence in eternal conscious torment

### Soul Winning is Not Optional
- Proverbs 11:30 states "he that winneth souls is wise"—implication is that those who don't win souls are foolish
- Romans describes those "professing themselves to be wise" who "became fools"
- Soul winning is not a spiritual gift (not listed in 1 Corinthians 12, Romans 12, or Ephesians 4)
- It is a responsibility, act of obedience, duty, privilege, and indebtedness

### Believers as Saviors and Conduits
- 1 Corinthians 9:22 - Paul says "I might by all means save some"
- 2 Corinthians 5:18-20 - God has committed to us the word of reconciliation; we are ambassadors for Christ
- Romans 10:14 - "How shall they hear without a preacher?"
- Every believer is called to be a preacher of the gospel

### Excuses People Make
- Outright disobedience and refusal
- Feeling unprepared or unqualified
- Thoughtlessness—too busy with life
- Not being spiritually minded about people
- Spiritual laziness
- Fear of failure
- Fear of rejection
- Fear of being seen as a fanatic

### BPO: The Three Essentials

**Burden**
- Soul winning requires seeing people as eternal souls, not just in their humanity
- Psalm 126:5-6 - "They that sow in tears shall reap in joy...bringing his sheaves with him"
- The question: "Who are you taking to heaven with you?"
- 1 Thessalonians 2:19-20 - Paul describes the Thessalonians as his "hope, joy, and crown of rejoicing"
- Without natural affection is a mark of the last days
- Must pray for God to give a burden for souls

**Preparation**
- Must know the gospel: it's a gift received by believing/trusting
- Need to know key verses: Romans 6:23, Ephesians 2:8-9, Acts 16:31, 1 Corinthians 15:1-4
- Must have 1 Corinthians 15:1-4 as the gospel is found in Pauline literature, not the four Gospels
- Prayer is essential preparation—binding the strongman (Matthew 12:29)
- Satan keeps people captive and blinded to the gospel
- Must pray for souls and for the power of the gospel
- The power is not in the soul winner but in the Word of God (1 Corinthians 2:4)

**Opportunity**
- Must pray for opportunities to share
- Seek out opportunities intentionally
- Jerry Cloutier's example: prayed in the morning for opportunity, then asked "Lord, is this the one?" when encountering people
- Paul went to synagogues seeking opportunity
- Can be as simple as giving someone literature to read about salvation

### Personal Testimony
- Pastor Jim was led to Christ by Howard Bebout in Okinawa, Japan at age 18
- Howard befriended him, had him in his home for holidays
- Later stationed together in North Carolina
- Howard asked: "If you died right now, do you know for sure you'd go to heaven?"
- Showed him from the Bible in seven minutes that he could know for sure
- Pastor Jim asked if he'd still go to heaven if he killed someone after getting saved
- Howard answered: "Yes, but if you really get saved, you won't want to kill anyone"
- Got saved behind a building, then led Tracy (his wife) to Christ

### Examples of Soul Winners
- Jesus came to seek and save that which is lost
- Peter preached Acts 4:12 - no other name under heaven whereby we must be saved
- Paul was indebted with the gospel (Romans 1), not ashamed of the gospel
- Howard Bebout led Pastor Jim to Christ
- Jerry Cloutier prays for and finds opportunities daily

### The Urgency
- Nine people died in a plane crash in Florida, entering eternity in moments
- People die every second of the day
- We cannot judge individual eternal destinations, but we know people need the gospel
- If believers don't share the gospel, it won't get done

### What We Take to Heaven
- We come into the world naked and leave the same way regarding earthly possessions
- What we take to heaven are the souls we've won—our "sheaves"
- Psalm 126:6 - bringing sheaves with rejoicing
- 1 Thessalonians 2:19-20 - believers we've won are our crown of rejoicing at Christ's coming
