## Summary
Pastor Simpson begins the study of spiritual warfare in Ephesians 6:10, emphasizing that the command to "be strong in the Lord" requires understanding our position in Christ. He explains that spiritual strength comes not from occasional supernatural empowerment but from daily dependence on God through understanding our spiritual blessings in heavenly places. Using extensive Scripture, he reveals that believers will one day judge angels and participate in Christ's eternal kingdom, showing that earthly spiritual warfare prepares us for our eternal purpose.

## Scripture
Ephesians 6:10 — "Finally my brethren, be strong in the Lord and in the power of His might."

## Key Themes
- **Spiritual Strength**: Believers need daily spiritual strength that comes from understanding their position in Christ, not just occasional supernatural empowerment as in Old Testament times.
- **Heavenly Places**: The spiritual blessings in heavenly places (Ephesians 1:3) include both present spiritual realities and future roles in judging angels and reigning with Christ.
- **Eternal Purpose**: Present spiritual warfare is preparation for eternal responsibilities, making earthly battles seem small in comparison to eternal realities.
- **Position in Christ**: Understanding who we are in Christ and what we have in Him is a primary source of spiritual strength for daily living.

## Notes

### Introduction to Spiritual Warfare (Ephesians 6:10)
- The word "finally" connects all previous teachings in Ephesians to this spiritual warfare passage
- Charismatic groups often mishandle spiritual warfare concepts
- The passage must be studied verse by verse, word by word to understand God's expectations

### Understanding "Be Strong in the Lord"
- This is a principle, not a specific command with detailed instructions
- Questions to consider: What does it mean? What does it look like? How do you get strong? How do you maintain strength? Why do you need strength?
- Spiritual strength is not needed if there is no spiritual activity
- Those living carnally don't need spiritual nourishment because they're not expending spiritual energy
- The devil will make carnal believers feel comfortable

### Difference Between Old and New Testament Empowerment
- Old Testament/Early Acts: Holy Spirit came upon people for specific tasks (speaking in tongues, healing, miracles)
- Samson received strength when the Spirit came upon him
- Today: Believers are sealed with the Holy Spirit at salvation (Ephesians 4)
- Current believers need daily strength to endure ongoing battles
- We study to show ourselves approved rather than receive immediate divine words

### Source of Strength: Understanding Position in Christ
- Philippians 4: Prayer should result in peace that passes understanding
- Many pray but still worry, remain anxious, receive no peace
- Lack of peace indicates weak faith and insufficient spiritual activity
- No spiritual nutrition, no spiritual exercise, no spiritual strengthening
- Prayer life often strongest when something goes wrong
- Believers should be able to walk away from prayer with peace regardless of outcomes

### Spiritual Blessings in Heavenly Places (Ephesians 1:3)
- "Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who hath blessed us with all spiritual blessings in heavenly places in Christ"
- Most teaching about heaven focuses on mansions, streets of gold, gates of pearl
- Pastor Simpson's childhood confusion about heaven being "one long church service" or "dancing around the rings of Saturn"
- Questions about what believers will actually do in heaven

### Heaven's Structure and Authority (Colossians 1:15-17)
- Things created in heaven and on earth
- Things visible and invisible
- Four categories listed: thrones, dominions, principalities, powers
- These structures exist both on earth and in heavenly places
- Angels dwell in heavenly places with organizational structures
- 1 Corinthians: Believers will judge angels
- Questions about angelic kingdoms, residences, and organization

### The Battle in Heavenly Places (Ephesians 6:12)
- "We wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places"
- High places refer to areas above the earth, in heavenly places
- Good angels and bad angels (demons) exist
- Satan is "the prince of the power of the air"
- Warfare exists not just on earth but in the heavenly realm

### War in Heaven (Revelation 12:7-9)
- Timing: During the tribulation period (after rapture, during judgment seat of Christ)
- "There was war in heaven"
- Michael and his angels fight against the dragon (Satan) and his angels
- Occurs at the midpoint of tribulation (1,260 days)
- Satan and his angels are cast out of heaven to earth
- This triggers the great tribulation (final three and a half years)
- After this, only good angels and believers remain in heavenly places

### Timeline of End Times Events
1. Rapture of the church
2. Judgment seat of Christ (believers judged for works)
3. Seven-year tribulation period on earth
4. War in heaven at midpoint (Revelation 12)
5. Great tribulation (final three and a half years)
6. Second coming of Christ
7. Battle of Armageddon
8. Thousand-year millennial reign
9. Satan loosed for a season
10. Battle of Gog and Magog
11. Great white throne judgment
12. New heaven and new earth
13. Dispensation of the fullness of times (Ephesians 1:10)

### Believers' Future Role
- Present during judgment seat of Christ while war in heaven occurs
- Will judge angels (1 Corinthians 6)
- Will have authority, thrones, principalities in heavenly places
- "Saints shall judge the world"
- Will rule and reign with Christ
- Interaction with angelic kingdom
- Service in Christ's eternal kingdom

### The Dispensation of the Fullness of Times (Ephesians 1:10)
- "That in the dispensation of the fullness of times he might gather together in one all things in Christ, both which are in heaven, and which are on earth"
- After all wars (in heaven and on earth) are complete
- Christ established on throne
- Satan and all sinners cast into lake of fire
- Remaining: good angels, believers, and Christ
- Christ ruling from earth throughout all God's creation (endless)
- All things in heaven and earth united under Christ

### Application to Present Spiritual Warfare
- Understanding future purpose gives perspective on present battles
- This life is preparation for eternal life and eternal purpose
- What we do here matters eternally
- Temporal battles are small; spiritual warfare is huge
- Being involved spiritually in spiritual warfare is everything
- Believers must see the significance of present spiritual activity

### Need for Spiritual Strength
- Peace of God keeps hearts and minds through Christ Jesus
- Spiritual strength comes from:
  - Understanding position in Christ
  - Knowing who we are in Christ
  - Understanding what we have in Christ
  - Grasping hope, security, and promises
  - Recognizing eternal purpose and value
- Daily dependence on God necessary
- Control of thoughts requires spiritual strength
- Doubt and fear indicate lack of God's intended strength
