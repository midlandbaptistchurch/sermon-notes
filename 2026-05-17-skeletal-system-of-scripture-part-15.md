## Summary
Pastor Jim Payne continues the Skeletal System of Scripture series with a graduate-level review of God's will and purpose. The sermon emphasizes that God's will is His deliberate, purposeful action to accomplish His plan—a direct response to Lucifer's rebellion. Through five sets of three (trilogies), Pastor Payne reviews foundational truths about God's physical creation, the structure of Genesis 1, and the judgment of God.

## Scripture
Ephesians 1:1-11 — Paul's apostleship and our adoption are according to God's will and purpose.

Colossians 1:9-10 — Paul's prayer for believers to be filled with knowledge of God's will in all wisdom and spiritual understanding.

Ezekiel 28:16-18 — God's five "I will" statements in response to Lucifer's rebellion.

Genesis 1:1-2 — The first stage of creation and the judgment upon the earth.

Revelation 21:1 — The third stage of creation: a new heaven and new earth.

## Key Themes
- God's will as His deliberate, purposeful action to accomplish His plan
- Understanding God's will extends beyond personal salvation to God's cosmic purposes
- Lucifer's rebellion prompted God's exercise of His will
- The three stages of physical creation: original creation, six days of restoration, and the new creation
- Genesis 1:2 as a verse of judgment describing the earth's desolation due to sin and rebellion
- The importance of spiritual wisdom and understanding in knowing God's will

## Notes

### God's Will Defined
- God's will is not merely what He wants for individual believers, but what God is determined to accomplish
- It is a deliberate action and process by God to accomplish His purposes
- Alternative term: God's purpose
- Believers are called to be filled with the knowledge of His will in all wisdom and spiritual understanding

### Why God Exercises His Will
- Lucifer, a created being, exercised his will in rebellion against God
- Lucifer declared five "I will" statements expressing his intention to overcome God
- God responded with His own five "I will" statements, declaring judgment and purpose
- God's will is His deliberate response to establish His purposes against all rebellion

### Five Trilogies Review

**First Trilogy: God's Purpose in Physical Creation**
- Stage 1: Genesis 1:1 — Original creation of heaven and earth
- Stage 2: Genesis 1:3-31 — Six days of creation, establishing the heavens (plural) and earth with atmosphere and life
- Stage 3: Revelation 21:1 — New heaven and new earth, the final restoration

**Second Trilogy: The Three Parts of Genesis 1**
- Part 1 (Verse 1): Original creation
- Part 2 (Verse 2): The earth's desolate state
- Part 3 (Verses 3-31): The six days of creation, each marked by "And God said" and "and the evening and the morning"

**Third Trilogy: Genesis 1:2 and God's Judgment**
- The earth was without form and void — indicates God's fierce anger
- Darkness covered the face of the deep — judgment associated with darkness, opposed to God's light
- This judgment resulted from sin and rebellion, specifically Lucifer's violence against God's throne
