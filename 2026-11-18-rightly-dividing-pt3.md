## Summary
Pastor Jim Payne continues his study on rightly dividing the Word of Truth, focusing on understanding the three major time zones in Scripture: time past, but now, and ages to come. This message reviews the structure of the Old Testament and examines how Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John are entirely focused on Israel and the fulfillment of Old Testament covenants, not the body of Christ. The sermon emphasizes that believers today have a responsibility to understand and teach the mystery revealed to Paul, not the Great Commission given to Israel in Matthew 28.

## Scripture
2 Timothy 2:15 — Study to show thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.

Matthew 15:24 — But he answered and said, I am not sent but unto the lost sheep of the house of Israel.

Romans 15:8 — Jesus Christ was a minister of the circumcision for the truth of God, to confirm the promises made unto the fathers.

## Key Themes
- **Rightly Dividing the Bible**: Understanding the three time zones (time past, but now, ages to come) is essential for proper Bible study and avoiding vain babblings
- **Israel's Central Role in Time Past**: From Genesis 12 through the Gospels, God's program focused on the nation of Israel and fulfilling covenant promises to Abraham and David
- **Our Commission Today**: Believers are called to the ministry of reconciliation and to make all men see the fellowship of the mystery, not to keep the Matthew 28 Great Commission given to Israel
- **Captivity to Error**: 2 Timothy 2:24-26 warns that believers can be taken captive by the devil through misunderstanding biblical truth and must be patiently instructed in meekness

### Biblical Structure Review

#### Old Testament Structure (39 Books)
- **Genesis 1-11**: Gentile world (approximately 2000 years), three rebellions (Adam/Eve, Genesis 6, Tower of Babel)
- **Genesis 12-Malachi**: Focus on Israel after God disinherits the Gentile nations
- **17 Historical Books**: 
  - 5 books: Pentateuch (basic law)
  - 9 books: Joshua through 2 Chronicles (pre-exile history)
  - 3 books: Ezra, Nehemiah, Esther (post-exile history)
- **5 Practical Books**: Job through Song of Solomon (poetical books for Israel's relationship with Jehovah)
- **17 Prophetical Books**: 
  - 5 major prophets: Isaiah through Daniel (basic prophecy)
  - 9 pre-exile prophets: Hosea through Zephaniah (correspond to 1-2 Kings, 1-2 Chronicles period)
  - 3 post-exile prophets: Haggai, Zechariah, Malachi

#### New Testament Structure (27 Books)
- **5 Historical Books**: Matthew, Mark, Luke, John, Acts
- **9 Body Church Epistles**: Romans through 2 Thessalonians (to seven churches)
- **4 Pastoral/Practical Epistles**: 1 Timothy, 2 Timothy, Titus, Philemon (bridge between church age themes)
- **9 Hebrew Church Epistles**: Hebrews through Revelation

### God's Dealings with the Gentile World

#### Deuteronomy 32:8 - The Division of Nations
- When God divided the nations, He set bounds according to the number of the children of Israel (12)
- Result: 12 natural land masses on earth (North America, Central America, South America, Greenland, Arctic, Antarctic, Africa, Europe, Asia, Australia, Greater Antilles, Lesser Antilles)
- God disinherited the Gentile world and placed spiritual authorities (principalities and powers) over them
- Verse 9: "For the LORD's portion is his people; Jacob is the lot of his inheritance"

#### Genesis 10:25 and the Days of Peleg
- "In his days was the earth divided"
- Peleg's name means "division"
- God physically divided the dry land during this time

#### Exodus 15:17 - God's Inheritance
- Moses prophesied that God would bring Israel into "the mountain of thine inheritance"
- "In the place, O Lord, which thou hast made for thee to dwell in"
- "The Lord shall reign for ever and ever"
- God chose a specific land for Himself where He would dwell and reign

### The 400 Years of Silence
- Amos 8:11 prophesied a famine "of hearing the words of the LORD"
- After Malachi, God did not speak for 400 years
- This makes the statement in John 1 significant: "There was a man sent from God, whose name was John"
- John the Baptist fulfilled Isaiah 40 and Malachi 3 as the forerunner to prepare the Messiah's way

### Jesus Christ's Earthly Ministry Focus

#### Romans 15:8 - Minister of the Circumcision
- Jesus Christ was "a minister of the circumcision for the truth of God, to confirm the promises made unto the fathers"
- Two primary promises: to Abraham (seed, land, great nation) and to David (throne, eternal kingdom)

#### Matthew 15:21-28 - The Syrophoenician Woman
- Jesus initially ignored the Gentile woman's request
- He stated clearly: "I am not sent but unto the lost sheep of the house of Israel" (verse 24)
- She had to acknowledge her position as a "dog" eating crumbs from the master's table
- Mark 7:27 adds: "Let the children first be filled" - Israel must be dealt with first before Gentiles

#### Luke 7:1-6 - The Centurion
- The centurion was deemed "worthy" because "he loveth our nation, and he hath built us a synagogue"
- Genesis 12 promise: "I will bless them that bless thee"
- The Gentile was blessed through blessing Israel
- Jesus went with them because the centurion had blessed Israel

#### Luke 19:1-10 - Zacchaeus
- Jesus knew from the foundation of the world about "the place" where Zacchaeus would be
- Verse 9: "This day is salvation come to this house, forasmuch as he also is a son of Abraham"
- Zacchaeus was saved because he was a son of Abraham, part of the Old Testament covenant people

#### Matthew 10:5-6 - Sending Out the Twelve
- Jesus commanded: "Go not into the way of the Gentiles"
- "But go rather to the lost sheep of the house of Israel"

#### The "Lost Sheep" Terminology
- Israel called "lost sheep" even though they are God's covenant people
- They are "sheep of his pasture" but lost due to disobedience to the covenant
- God had not yet cast Israel away - they remain His people throughout the Gospels

### The Great Commission Problem

#### Matthew 28:19-20
- Traditional teaching focuses on this as the church's commission
- Command: "teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you"
- Problem: Jesus commanded the disciples to obey those who "sit in Moses' seat" - the Judaic Law System
- It is literally impossible for the church today to keep this commission since we are not under the law

#### Our Commission Today
- 2 Corinthians 5: The ministry of reconciliation committed to us
- 1 Timothy 2:4: God's will that all men be saved and come to the knowledge of the truth
- Ephesians 3:9: "Make all men see what is the fellowship of the mystery"
- The truth we are to communicate is the mystery - the body of Christ revealed to Paul

### Stewardship and Accountability

#### Our Responsibility
- Believers are stewards over the Bible given to them
- We will give account for our responsibility as soul winners
- We will give account for discipling others in God's will for today
- Must help people understand the mystery and rightly divide the Word

#### 2 Timothy 2:24-26 - Patience in Teaching
- Verse 15 context: "Study to shew thyself approved...rightly dividing the word of truth"
- Verse 16: Anything not rightly divided is "profane and vain babblings"
- Verses 24-25: "The servant of the Lord must not strive; but be gentle unto all men, apt to teach, patient, in meekness instructing those that oppose themselves"
- People oppose themselves when they reject rightly divided truth
- They hold to tradition and teachings from respected preachers instead of embracing the mystery

#### The Enemy's Work
- Verse 26: Believers can be "taken captive by him [the devil] at his will"
- God's two purposes today: (1) people to be saved, (2) people to come to knowledge of the truth
- Satan opposes both: blinds lost people to the gospel (2 Corinthians 4:4) and takes believers captive to prevent them from seeing the mystery
- Verse 25: God may "give them repentance to the acknowledging of the truth"
- Verse 26: They may "recover themselves out of the snare of the devil"

### Ministry Observations
- Pastor has seen people attend for 10 years before suddenly understanding truths he's been teaching all along
- The importance of patience in teaching - people come to understanding in God's timing
- Many churches today focus preaching on the Gospels, suggesting it's God's word for the body of Christ
- This causes great damage because Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John are "nothing concerning us today"
- The body of Christ is not in focus in the Gospels - only Jews and Gentiles being dealt with

### Prophetic Promises Throughout the Prophets

#### Zechariah 9:9-10
- Verse 9: "Rejoice greatly, O daughter of Zion...behold, thy King cometh unto thee...lowly, and riding upon an ass" (first coming)
- Verse 10: Battle weapons cut off, "and his dominion shall be from sea even to sea, and from the river even to the ends of the earth" (second coming)

#### Zechariah 14:9
- "And the LORD shall be king over all the earth: in that day shall there be one LORD, and his name one"
- At the end of every prophet, God confirms His promise to give Israel their kingdom
