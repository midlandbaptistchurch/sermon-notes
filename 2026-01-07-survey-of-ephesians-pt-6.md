## Summary
Pastor Jim Payne explores Ephesians chapter 3, focusing on the mystery of Christ—God's plan to unite Jews and Gentiles as one spiritual body in the church age. Paul emphasizes his role as a steward and minister of this mystery, which was hidden in previous ages but is now revealed through apostles and prophets. The sermon highlights the significance of understanding this mystery for the church's maturity and eternal purpose.

## Scripture
Ephesians 3 — Paul's exposition of the mystery of the body of Christ and the dispensation of grace given to him for the Gentiles.

## Key Themes
- The mystery: God's plan to unite Jews and Gentiles in one body
- The body of Christ as a spiritual entity transcending local churches
- Paul's role as a prisoner and steward of the dispensation of grace
- Revelation and understanding of God's purpose
- The ministry of apostles and prophets after Christ's ascension
- Gentiles as fellow heirs and partakers of the promise
- The importance of discerning knowledge of the mystery

## Notes

### The Mystery Defined
- The mystery is not something superstitious or mystical, but rather something concealed and then revealed by God
- It is described as the most important yet most unknown chapter in the Bible
- The mystery concerns what God is doing today through the body of Christ

### Gentiles as Fellow Heirs
- The central revelation of Ephesians 3:6 is that Gentiles should be "fellow heirs" and "of the same body" with Jews
- This was hidden in previous ages but is now made manifest through the gospel
- Both Jews and Gentiles are "partakers of his promise in Christ by the gospel"

### The One Body
- There is one spiritual body of Christ that transcends the entire earth, not merely local church assemblies
- By one Spirit, all believers are baptized into this one body (1 Corinthians 12:13)
- The body is unified under Christ as its head

### Paul's Commission
- Paul is a prisoner for the sake of his ministry to the Gentiles
- He is a steward of the "dispensation of the grace of God" given to him
- His role is to distribute spiritual food (doctrine and teaching) "in due season"
- Paul received special revelation from Jesus Christ and later illumination by the Spirit

### Understanding Through Reading
- To truly understand the mystery requires deep reading and discernment, not merely a cursory glance
- Believers must gain knowledge of the mystery of Christ through careful study of Scripture
- The goal is full assurance and acknowledgment of God's eternal plan

### Apostles and Prophets After Ascension
- Christ, upon his ascension, gave apostles and prophets as gifts to the body of Christ
- These post-ascension apostles and prophets are distinct from the twelve apostles and Old Testament prophets
- They reveal the mystery by the Holy Spirit to bring special illumination to God's people

### The Purpose of the Mystery
- God's ultimate purpose is to present every believer perfect in Christ Jesus
- The mystery encompasses God's plan for reconciliation of all things under Christ's headship
- Believers are destined to be instruments of God's will and governmental agencies in eternity

### Paul's Sufferings
- Paul fills up in his flesh the sufferings of Christ—not for salvation, but in carrying the gospel to a God-rejecting world
- He labored more abundantly than others through the mighty working of God's grace in his life
- His ministry demonstrates the supernatural effectiveness of divine grace
