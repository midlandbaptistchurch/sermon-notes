## Summary
Riley Vasold presents six "absolutes" — guaranteed, unchanging truths about God — drawing from across Scripture to encourage the congregation. Moving from the sobering realities of God's jealousy and coming wrath to the comforting promises of grace, love, the rapture, and God's ultimate victory, the sermon aims to anchor believers in eternal certainties during difficult times.

## Scripture
Multiple passages across Exodus, Isaiah, Romans, Ephesians, 1 Thessalonians, and Revelation — collectively presenting a panoramic view of God's character and His redemptive plan from creation to the new heavens and new earth.

## Key Themes
- God is a jealous God who tolerates no rivals
- God's wrath against sin is real and will be fully executed
- God's grace is the only and sufficient answer to His wrath
- God's love is inseparable from believers
- The rapture is a certain, imminent hope for the church
- God will ultimately win the war over sin, death, and hell

## Notes

### 1. God Is a Jealous God
- Exodus 20:5 — God identifies Himself as a jealous God in the context of the commandment against idols.
- Exodus 34:14 — Remarkably, "Jealous" is given as one of God's actual names: "the LORD, whose name is Jealous."
- Vasold distinguishes biblical jealousy from its negative cultural connotation, likening it to a proper valuing of one's spouse.
- Application: Modern celebrity and athlete worship mirrors ancient idol worship and provokes God's jealousy.
- Proverbs 6:34 — Misplaced jealousy becomes destructive rage; God's jealousy is righteous and purposeful.

### 2. God's Wrath Will Be Fulfilled
- Isaiah 13:6-9 — The Day of the Lord is described as a day of cruel wrath and fierce anger; the sun, moon, and stars will be darkened.
- Revelation 14:17-20 — The harvest of the earth is cast into "the great wine press of the wrath of God"; blood flows for 1,600 furlongs.
- Romans 12:19 (referenced as Romans 13:19) — "Vengeance is mine, I will repay, saith the Lord."
- Romans 1:18-23 — God's wrath is revealed against those who suppress the truth; those without excuse who exchanged the glory of God for created things.
- Point of comfort: Awareness of God's wrath should magnify gratitude for His grace toward believers.

### 3. God's Grace Saves You
- Ephesians 2:8 — "For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God."
- Romans 5:15 — As one man (Adam) brought death, so the one man Jesus Christ brought abounding grace to many.
- Grace is described as boundless and immeasurable precisely because it must cover the full weight of God's wrath.
- Challenge to the congregation: Do not let earthly concerns (finances, politics, daily frustrations) crowd out appreciation for this "immeasurable gift."
- 2 Corinthians 12:9 (referenced in passing) — "My grace is sufficient for thee."

### 4. God Loves You
- Romans 8:35-39 — Nothing — tribulation, distress, persecution, famine, death, angels, principalities, height, nor depth — can separate believers from the love of God in Christ Jesus.
- Vasold notes a two-pronged attack: earthly trials try to separate us from Christ's love; spiritual forces try to separate us from the Father's love.
- Believers are declared "more than conquerors" — exceeding even what Adam was originally commissioned to be.
- John 3:16 cited as the foundational statement of God's love.
- God's love is described in terms of His eternal choice of believers before the foundation of the world.

### 5. God Will Rapture You
- 1 Thessalonians 4:13-17 — The dead in Christ rise first; then living believers are caught up together with them in the clouds to meet the Lord in the air.
- Qualifier for the rapture: believing that Jesus died and rose again (v. 14).
- Revelation 4:1 — John hears a trumpet voice saying "Come up hither" — offered as a preview of the rapture call.
- Vasold expresses anticipation of reunion with departed believers and with historical figures of faith.
- Affirms a pre-tribulation rapture; warns against teaching that places the church in the tribulation.
- The rapture is imminent — no prophecy must be fulfilled before it occurs.

### 6. God Will Win the War
- Revelation 20:11-12 — The Great White Throne Judgment: the dead, small and great, stand before God; books are opened; the dead are judged according to their works.
- Revelation 20 — Death and hell are cast into the lake of fire.
- Revelation 21:4 — In the new world, there will be no more death, sorrow, crying, or pain; the former things are passed away.
- The new heaven and new earth will be characterized by the presence of Christ as its light, and the complete absence of sin, the devil, and death.

### Concluding Challenge
Vasold closed with three personal questions for reflection:
1. Are you prepared to be among the company of those who will be there?
2. Have you given up temporal things in exchange for eternal ones?
3. Are you prepared to put on immortality?
