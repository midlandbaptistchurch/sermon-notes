## Summary
Riley Vasold presents six foundational, unchanging certainties about God drawn from across Scripture. Moving from God's jealousy and coming wrath through to his grace, love, the rapture, and his ultimate victory, the sermon is designed to anchor and encourage believers during difficult times. The closing challenge calls listeners to examine whether they are living in light of these eternal realities.

## Scripture
Multiple passages across Exodus, Isaiah, Romans, Ephesians, 1 Thessalonians, and Revelation — together tracing the full arc of God's character and his plan for humanity from creation to the new heaven and new earth.

## Key Themes
- God is a jealous God who tolerates no rivals
- God's wrath against sin is real, righteous, and will be fully executed
- God's grace is boundless and is the only escape from his wrath
- God's love is inseparable from his people
- The rapture is a certain, imminent hope for believers
- God will ultimately and completely win the war against sin, death, and hell

## Notes

### Absolute 1: God Is a Jealous God (Exodus 20:5; Exodus 34:14)
- The word "jealous" is not merely a negative trait; in the Bible it is closely connected to a rightful valuing of one's exclusive relationship.
- Exodus 34:14 reveals that "Jealous" is actually one of God's names — *the Lord whose name is Jealous*.
- Application: Modern culture elevates athletes and celebrities as functional gods; God's jealousy stands as a direct rebuke to this idol-making tendency.
- Proverbs 6:34 — jealousy misplaced is destructive rage; God's jealousy is righteous and protective.

### Absolute 2: God's Wrath Will Be Fulfilled (Isaiah 13:6-9; Revelation 14:17-20; Romans 1:18-23)
- Isaiah 13:6-9 describes the Day of the Lord as a day of fierce, destroying wrath — hearts melting, faces as flames, sun and moon darkened.
- Revelation 14:17-20 pictures the great winepress of the wrath of God; blood flowing as high as a horse's bridle for 1,600 furlongs.
- Romans 1:18-23 explains the reason for wrath: humanity suppresses the truth of God that is clearly evident in creation, becomes vain in imagination, and exchanges the glory of God for idols.
- God's vengeance is his own to execute (cf. Romans 12:19). This truth should produce gratitude in the believer who has been spared.

### Absolute 3: God's Grace Saves You (Ephesians 2:8; Romans 5:15)
- Ephesians 2:8 — salvation is by grace through faith, not of ourselves; it is the gift of God.
- Romans 5:15 — just as one man (Adam) brought death to many, so the grace of one man (Jesus Christ) has abounded to many.
- Holding God's wrath in one hand and his grace in the other reveals just how boundless grace truly is — grace must exceed the full measure of wrath.
- Challenge: Believers can lose sight of this immeasurable gift by being consumed with mortgages, politics, and daily frustrations.

### Absolute 4: God Loves You (Romans 8:35-39)
- Romans 8:35-37 — tribulation, distress, persecution, famine, nakedness, peril, and sword cannot separate us from the love of Christ; we are *more than conquerors*.
- Romans 8:38-39 — death, life, angels, principalities, powers, things present, things to come, height, and depth cannot separate us from the love of God in Christ Jesus.
- The enemy wages a two-pronged attack: earthly physical hardships against our relationship with Christ, and spiritual powers against our relationship with God — yet neither prong succeeds.
- God's love predates the foundation of the world; his choosing of his people is certain and permanent.

### Absolute 5: God Will Rapture You (1 Thessalonians 4:13-17; Revelation 4:1)
- 1 Thessalonians 4:13-14 — believers do not sorrow as those without hope; those who sleep in Jesus will God bring with him.
- 1 Thessalonians 4:16-17 — the Lord will descend with a shout, the voice of the archangel, and the trump of God; the dead in Christ rise first, then the living are caught up together with them in the clouds.
- Revelation 4:1 — John's experience of the open door in heaven with the voice like a trumpet saying *"Come up hither"* is a picture of what the church will hear at the rapture.
- The rapture is imminent — no prophetic event must precede it.
- Emphasized: the church's rapture is distinct from and prior to the events of the tribulation described in Revelation; believers are not appointed to endure God's wrath.

### Absolute 6: God Will Win the War (Revelation 20:11-14; Revelation 21:4)
- Revelation 20:11-14 — the Great White Throne Judgment: the dead, small and great, stand before God; books are opened; death and hell are cast into the lake of fire.
- The defeat of death and hell is total and final.
- Revelation 21:4 — in the new world, God will wipe away every tear; there will be no more death, sorrow, crying, or pain; the former things are passed away.
- Believers will live in a world lit by Jesus himself, free from sin, the devil, and all that corrupts.

### Closing Challenge
Vasold closed with three searching questions for the congregation:
1. Are you prepared to be among the company of those who will be in that new world?
2. Have you given up the temporal things of this world in exchange for the eternal?
3. Are you prepared to cast off mortality and put on immortality?
