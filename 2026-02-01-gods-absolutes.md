## Summary
Riley Vasold presents six "absolutes" — guaranteed truths about God — drawn from across Scripture. Moving from the sobering realities of God's jealousy and coming wrath, to the comforting promises of His grace, love, the rapture, and ultimate victory, the sermon aims to encourage believers during a dark season of life. The message closes with a personal challenge to live fully for God in light of these eternal certainties.

## Scripture
Multiple passages spanning Exodus through Revelation, anchoring each of the six absolutes in direct biblical text.

## Key Themes
- God is a jealous God who tolerates no rivals
- God's wrath against sin is real and will be fulfilled
- God's grace is the only sufficient answer to His wrath
- God's love is inseparable from His people
- The rapture is a certain, imminent, and comforting hope
- God will ultimately win — death, hell, and sin will be cast away

## Notes

### Absolute 1: God Is a Jealous God
- Exodus 20:5 — God identifies Himself as a jealous God in the context of idolatry
- Exodus 34:14 — God's very *name* is Jealous, underscoring how central this attribute is
- Jealousy in Scripture is tied to a proper valuing of covenant relationship, not mere envy
- Modern idolatry illustrated through the culture of athlete-worship, echoing ancient Rome's glorification of Olympic athletes
- Proverbs 6:34 — misplaced jealousy becomes destructive rage; God's jealousy is righteous

### Absolute 2: God's Wrath Will Be Fulfilled
- Isaiah 13:6–9 — vivid description of the Day of the Lord: melting hearts, darkened sun and moon, fierce anger
- Revelation 14:17–20 — the great winepress of God's wrath; blood flowing for 1,600 furlongs
- Romans 1:18–23 — wrath revealed against those who suppress the truth; creation leaves all without excuse
- Romans 12:19 (referenced as Romans 13:19) — "Vengeance is mine, I will repay, saith the Lord"
- God's wrath is not meant to discourage believers but to highlight the magnitude of the grace that spares them from it

### Absolute 3: God's Grace Saves You
- Ephesians 2:8 — "For by grace are ye saved through faith, and that not of yourselves, it is the gift of God"
- Romans 5:15 — the free gift of grace through one man, Jesus Christ, far exceeds the offense brought in through one man, Adam
- Grace is boundless because it must be sufficient to cover the full weight of God's wrath
- Challenge to the congregation not to let daily anxieties (finances, relationships, politics) crowd out the appreciation of this immeasurable gift

### Absolute 4: God Loves You
- Romans 8:35–39 — nothing can separate us from the love of Christ or the love of God
- Two-pronged attack identified: earthly/physical things try to separate us from Christ's love; spiritual forces try to separate us from God's love — both fail
- Believers are "more than conquerors" — recovering more than what Adam lost
- God's love is expressed as His eternal choice of His people before the foundation of the world
- Even death draws the believer closer to God rather than away from His love

### Absolute 5: God Will Rapture You
- 1 Thessalonians 4:13–17 — the rapture revealed; the dead in Christ rise first, then living believers are caught up to meet the Lord in the air
- Qualifier: belief that Jesus died and rose again (v. 14)
- Revelation 4:1 — "Come up hither" as a preview of the voice/trumpet call at the rapture
- The rapture is pre-tribulational; believers are not appointed to go through God's wrath
- Personal reflection: anticipation of seeing loved ones and great saints of history (Paul, Tyndale, George Washington); desire to have lived a full life for God before it comes
- The rapture is imminent — no prophecy must precede it

### Absolute 6: God Will Win the War
- Revelation 20:11–14 — the Great White Throne Judgment; death and hell cast into the lake of fire
- Revelation 21:4 — no more pain, sorrow, death, or tears in the new heaven and new earth
- The universe itself remade; Jesus is the light of the new world
- All that remains: the presence of God, reunion with loved ones, and an end to sin's pull

### Closing Challenge
Vasold closes with three questions for self-examination:
1. Are you prepared to be among those present in that eternal state?
2. Have you given up temporal things in exchange for eternal ones?
3. Are you prepared to cast off mortality for immortality?
