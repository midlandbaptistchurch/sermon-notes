## Summary
Riley Vasold presents six unchanging certainties — "absolutes" — about God drawn from across Scripture, intended to encourage the congregation during a dark and cold February. Moving from the sobering realities of God's jealousy and wrath to the uplifting promises of grace, love, the rapture, and ultimate victory, the sermon calls believers to hold fast to these guarantees as anchors through the trials of life.

## Scripture
Multiple passages spanning Exodus, Isaiah, Romans, Ephesians, 1 Thessalonians, and Revelation — each selected to illustrate one of the six absolutes about God's character and promises.

## Key Themes
- God is a jealous God who tolerates no rivals
- God's wrath against sin is real and will be fulfilled
- God's grace is the only and sufficient answer to that wrath
- God's love is inseparable from believers
- The rapture is a certain, imminent hope for the church
- God will ultimately win the war; death, hell, and sin will be defeated

## Notes

### Absolute 1: God Is a Jealous God
- Key texts: Exodus 20:5; Exodus 34:14
- "Jealous" is not merely a negative trait — in Scripture it is closely tied to God's rightful claim on his people, as a husband values a spouse.
- Exodus 34:14 reveals that *Jealous* is actually one of God's names: "the LORD, whose name is Jealous."
- Application: Modern culture elevates athletes and celebrities as gods; God's jealousy is a direct response to such idolatry.
- Proverbs 6:34 — jealousy expressed wrongly is destructive rage; God's jealousy is righteous.

### Absolute 2: God's Wrath Will Be Fulfilled
- Key texts: Isaiah 13:6-9; Revelation 14:17-20; Romans 1:18-23; Romans 12:19 ("Vengeance is mine, I will repay")
- God's wrath is stored up and will be poured out — illustrated by the image of the great winepress of God's wrath (Revelation 14).
- The Day of the Lord will bring cosmic upheaval: hearts melting, sun and moon darkened.
- Romans 1 explains the reason for wrath: humanity suppressing the truth of God in unrighteousness, becoming vain in their imaginations.
- Point of encouragement: understanding God's wrath makes his grace all the more precious.

### Absolute 3: God's Grace Saves You
- Key texts: Ephesians 2:8; Romans 5:15
- "For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves, it is the gift of God."
- Grace is the only escape from God's wrath — and because the wrath is so vast, grace must be boundless.
- Romans 5:15 — as one man (Adam) brought death to many, so one man (Jesus Christ) brought abounding grace to many.
- Challenge: believers can lose sight of this immeasurable gift by focusing on mortgages, politics, and daily frustrations.

### Absolute 4: God Loves You
- Key text: Romans 8:35-39
- Nothing — tribulation, distress, persecution, famine, nakedness, peril, sword, death, life, angels, principalities, powers, height, or depth — can separate believers from the love of God in Christ Jesus.
- The enemy uses a two-pronged attack: earthly/physical trials to separate us from Christ's love, and spiritual forces to separate us from God's love — but both fail.
- We are "more than conquerors" — surpassing even what Adam lost in the fall.
- God's love and choice of his people predates creation itself.

### Absolute 5: God Will Rapture You
- Key texts: 1 Thessalonians 4:13-17; Revelation 4:1
- The rapture is imminent — no prophecy must be fulfilled before it occurs.
- 1 Thessalonians 4:16-17: the Lord will descend with a shout, the voice of the archangel, and the trump of God; the dead in Christ rise first, then living believers are caught up together.
- Revelation 4:1 offers a preview: a trumpet voice saying "Come up hither" — the first audible words believers will hear from God.
- Personal reflection: the speaker's only concern about the rapture is that it would come before he has done more for God.
- Note: the church's rapture is distinct from events described later in Revelation; believers are not appointed to go through the Tribulation.

### Absolute 6: God Will Win the War
- Key texts: Revelation 20:11-14; Revelation 21:4
- The Great White Throne Judgment: all the dead stand before God; books are opened; death and hell are cast into the lake of fire.
- Revelation 21:4 — in the new world there will be no more death, sorrow, crying, or pain; all former things are passed away.
- The devil, sin, and darkness will be gone; Jesus himself will be the light of the new heaven and new earth.
- Closing challenge: Are you prepared to be among those who will be there? Have you exchanged temporal things for eternal ones?
