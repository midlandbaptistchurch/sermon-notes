## Summary
Pastor Mark Simpson continues teaching from "One Book Rightly Divided," focusing on reconciling seemingly contradictory passages by understanding who scripture is written to and when it applies. He demonstrates how Jesus' teachings in the Sermon on the Mount (Matthew 5-7) were kingdom instructions for Israel during the millennium, not grace-age instructions for the church. Using examples like Mark 16:16-18, he shows how forcing all scripture to apply to the church creates confusion and contradictions that can only be resolved by proper dispensational understanding.

## Scripture
Matthew 5:21-48 — Jesus contrasts law with kingdom standards, teaching how life will be under His millennial reign
Galatians 3:24-29 — Under grace there is no difference between Jew and Greek, bond and free, male and female
Ephesians 2:11-13 — Gentiles who were once without hope are now brought near through Christ
Mark 16:15-18 — Commission to preach to every creature with signs following believers

## Key Themes
- **Rightly Dividing Between Law, Grace, and Kingdom**: Jesus' Sermon on the Mount teaches kingdom standards for the millennium, not grace-age living, which explains seemingly impossible commands like calling someone a fool resulting in hellfire danger.
- **The Middle Wall of Partition**: Under grace, the distinction between Jew and Gentile is removed; there is no difference in how people come to God during the church age.
- **Understanding Commissions**: Matthew 10 (reach Jews only), Mark 16 (tribulation servants reaching every creature), and Matthew 28 (kingdom Jews reaching nations) are different commissions for different times.
- **Reconciling Baptism and Salvation**: Mark 16:16 requires belief and baptism for salvation because it's written to tribulation saints, not the church under grace where belief alone saves.

## Notes

### Review: Law vs. Kingdom Standards
- Previously covered differences between "eye for an eye" under the law versus kingdom teachings
- Under the law, if someone murdered a family member, you were obligated to kill them or you weren't right with God
- Joshua's job was to eliminate enemy personnel—killing was his God-given assignment
- Cities of refuge were required for accidental killings to protect from required vengeance
- Under the law, gathering sticks on Sabbath, blasphemy, and serving other gods were punishable by death

### Understanding the Sermon on the Mount (Matthew 5-7)
- These chapters are NOT written to the church—they are kingdom instructions
- Matthew 5:5: "Blessed are the meek for they shall inherit the earth" (we inherit the heavens, not the earth)
- Matthew 5:21-22: Being angry without cause puts one in danger of judgment; calling someone a fool puts one in danger of hellfire
- This confused Pastor Mark for years until he understood it as kingdom teaching
- In the kingdom, Christ will be the religious system, judicial system, and government
- Things will be "tightened up" with Christ ruling directly—He won't tolerate even wrong thoughts
- Satan will be bound, making it much easier to live righteously
- Matthew 5:27-28: Not only don't commit adultery, don't even think about it (kingdom standard)
- Matthew 5:38-42: Turn the other cheek, give your cloak also, go the extra mile—works in the kingdom because Christ is the righteous judge and no one can manipulate or take advantage

### The Rapture and Glorification
- At the rapture, Christ will give us glorified bodies and remove our sin nature
- "He ain't gonna deal with that stupid for eternity"
- Everyone who is saved will forgive everyone else once glorified
- If you can't forgive now, it's because you're sin-cursed—your sin keeps you from forgiving

### The Church Age: No Difference Between Jew and Gentile
- Romans 10:12: "For there is no difference between the Jew and the Greek"
- During Old Testament and Gospels, Jews were elevated; Gentiles had to get to God through them
- The "middle wall of partition" (Ephesians 2) has been broken down
- Now genealogy and nationality don't matter for salvation
- Galatians 3:24-29: The law was a schoolmaster to bring us to Christ; now we're justified by faith
- Verse 27: Baptized into Christ means placed into the body of Christ at salvation (not water baptism)
- Verse 28: "Neither Jew nor Greek, neither bond nor free, neither male nor female"—all one in Christ
- These are temporal roles that won't matter in eternity

### Master/Servant Relationships in Scripture
- When a master and servant both got saved, they became brothers in Christ
- But the next day at work, the master was still master and servant still servant
- All angels are male (according to Scripture)
- Pastor Mark doesn't know if we'll be male and female in heaven or just angelic

### Israel's Future Commission
- Israel rejected God as their king and demanded a human king (Saul)
- Israel never fulfilled their original purpose to reach other nations
- During the millennium, Israel under the new covenant will finally accomplish this
- Those who endure through the tribulation without taking the mark will enter the millennium
- They will have God's word written in their hearts and will reach the nations during the 1000 years
- The lion will lay with the lamb; children can safely handle poisonous snakes
- All people from Abraham's bosom (or heaven, depending on interpretation) will join this group

### The Three Different Commissions
- **Matthew 10:5-7**: Don't go to Gentiles or Samaritans; go only to the lost sheep of Israel
  - This is the tribulation commission for the 144,000
  - Their job: finish what was started, reaching Jews during the tribulation
- **Mark 16:15-18**: Go into all the world, preach the gospel to every creature
  - Written to servants (the 144,000 and two witnesses)
  - Verse 16: "He that believeth and is baptized shall be saved"—this IS water baptism for salvation (for them, not us)
  - Verse 17-18: Signs shall follow—cast out devils, speak with new tongues, take up serpents, drink deadly things without harm, lay hands on sick for recovery
  - Baptists love verse 15 for missions but ignore verses 16-18 because they don't fit the "casserole" approach
- **Matthew 28:19-20**: Go teach all nations, baptizing them
  - This is the kingdom commission for Jews who enter the millennium
  - They will reach Gentile nations during Christ's 1000-year reign

### Why Jesus Said "Don't Go to the Gentiles"
- It wasn't because Jesus didn't care about Gentiles
- He wanted the plan done His way: Jews reach Jews during tribulation, then Jews reach Gentiles in kingdom
- The program hadn't changed between Matthew 10 and Mark 16
- God wanted those Jews to fulfill His will the way He told them thousands of years ago

### The Casserole Approach vs. Rightly Dividing
- The "casserole" approach tries to mix all scripture together and make it all apply to the church
- This creates impossible contradictions that require ignoring or twisting verses
- Example: Baptists say baptism in Mark 16:16 doesn't really mean water baptism is required because verse 16b only mentions unbelief for damnation
  - "That doesn't even make sense"
  - The only way it makes sense is if you're "trying to cram Mark into Romans"
- "You get enough people, if people say it loud enough, long enough, and enough people say it, you just believe it"
- People don't prove it with logic God gives them

### The "Spiritual Israel" Error
- Some teach the church is "spiritual Israel," meaning all Old Testament promises now apply to the church
- This means the church has replaced Israel in God's program
- This approach misses that the church was given something fresh and new, not Israel's promises
- It confuses the rapture with the second coming
- It tries to make Matthew 24 about the rapture when it isn't

### Revelation 6:9-11 and Tribulation Saints
- Fifth seal: souls under the altar of those slain during tribulation
- They cry "How long, O Lord?" asking for vengeance
- Given white robes, told to rest until their fellow servants are also killed
- These are people killed during tribulation for refusing the mark
- They're in heaven during the "little season" and will return with Christ

### The Importance of Understanding God's Spokesmen
- God has always had spokesmen throughout different ages
- Adam, Noah, Moses, Samuel, John the Baptist, Jesus, Peter (Acts 1-14), Paul (Acts 15-28)
- Paul is the spokesman for the church age (this time)
- Understanding who is speaking to whom is critical

### Simply Who's Mail?
- If scripture isn't written to you and you try to make it written to you, you'll have problems and confusion
- Satan is the author of confusion
- The Bible isn't meant to be confusing when viewed from the right perspective
