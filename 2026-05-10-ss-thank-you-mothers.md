## Summary
Bill Baughman shares a heartfelt Mother's Day message expressing gratitude to mothers, particularly focusing on the power of prayer. He shares his personal testimony of how prayers from his grandmother, mother, and daughter for 30 years led to his salvation at age 50. The message includes humorous observations about mothers' endless work and examines biblical examples of motherhood.

## Scripture
Genesis 2:24 — The foundation of marriage and family by God's design.
Proverbs 31:10-31 — God's description of a virtuous woman and mother.

## Key Themes
- The power of persistent prayer for salvation
- Gratitude for mothers' endless service and sacrifice
- Biblical examples of godly motherhood
- Marriage as "one flesh" according to God's design
- Life-giving power and nurturing love of mothers

## Notes
- **Personal Testimony**: Bill shares how his grandmother, mother, and daughter prayed for him for 30 years (ages 20-50) before his salvation in 2007
- **Mothers' Endless Work**: Humorous reading about all the tasks a mother does after saying she's "going to bed"
- **Proverbs 31**: God gives the longest description of any family member to mothers, showing His understanding of their importance
- **Ten Attributes of Motherhood**: Life-giving power, unconditional love, sacrificial selflessness, protection, patience, faith, forgiveness, humility, virtue, and hospitality
- **Biblical Examples**:
  - **Eve**: "Mother of all living" (Genesis 3:20)
  - **Sarah**: Mother of nations at age 90
  - **Mary**: Obedient servant who bore Jesus
  - **Jochebed**: Moses' mother who hid him for three months
  - **Hannah**: Prayed for Samuel and dedicated him to the Lord
- **Marriage Wisdom**: The two biggest life choices are salvation and choosing a spouse - both can be "heaven or hell"
- **Encouragement**: Don't give up praying for unsaved loved ones - 30 years of prayer was answered
