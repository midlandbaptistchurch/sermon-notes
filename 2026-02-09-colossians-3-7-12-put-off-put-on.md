## Summary
Pastor Simpson teaches on putting off the old man and putting on the new man from Colossians 3:7-12. He emphasizes that anger is a choice that must be put off, not managed, and explains how believers become new creatures through being saturated with God's Word and walking in the Spirit.

## Scripture
Colossians 3:7-12 — Paul's instructions on Christian transformation and behavior.

## Key Themes
- Putting off the old man with its deeds
- Putting on the new man renewed in knowledge
- Anger as a choice, not something to manage
- Progressive revelation and illumination in Bible study
- Becoming a new creature through God's Word

## Notes

### Progressive Revelation and Illumination
- Progressive revelation: things revealed as you continue studying
- Progressive illumination: continuing to get light and understanding
- Certain books like Ephesians, Colossians require careful study

### Review: The Inheritance and Wrath of God
- There are two types of inheritance for believers
- One comes with salvation and cannot be lost
- Another is based on works and can be lost through sin
- The "wrath of God" in context refers to judgment of believers' works

### Put Off - Dealing with Anger
- The Bible says to "put off" anger, not "manage" it
- Anger is always a decision - you choose to allow it or tell it no
- When you lose your temper, you lose your temperance (strength)
- Anger is compared to temporary insanity - you say and do things you regret
- Foundation: Romans 6 - don't let sin reign in your mortal body

### Put On - The New Man
- Command to put on the new man "renewed in knowledge"
- This happens through letting "the word of Christ dwell in you richly"
- Richly means saturation - like something so thick you can only have so much
- When saturated with God's Word, you begin to think differently
- You gain the mind of Christ and eternal perspective

### Becoming a New Creature
- 2 Corinthians 5:17 - "if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature"
- This isn't automatic at salvation - it's a process
- You become a new creature by walking in the Spirit and studying God's Word
- It's continual work - you can fall back into old patterns

### Attributes of the New Man (v. 12)
- Holy and beloved as the elect of God
- Bowels of mercies - mercy from the depths of your soul
- Kindness - being nice and doing things for others
- Humbleness of mind
- Meekness
- Long-suffering

### Practical Applications
- Show mercy even when people might take advantage
- Be kind not just to be good, but to let God work through you
- Don't do good works expecting something in return
- Look for opportunities to strengthen the body of Christ
