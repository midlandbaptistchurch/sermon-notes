## Summary
Pastor Payne continues the series on understanding the Bible's skeletal structure, focusing on the gap between Genesis 1:1 and 1:2, Lucifer's rebellion, and God's judgment that resulted in the earth becoming "without form and void." He explains the first day of creation when God said "Let there be light," distinguishing this light from the sun and connecting it to God's glory dwelling in unapproachable light.

## Scripture
Genesis 1:1-2 — The creation of heaven and earth, followed by judgment and darkness upon the earth. Colossians 1:9-10 — Paul's prayer for believers to be filled with knowledge of God's will and walk worthy of the Lord.

## Key Themes
- The threefold physical creation: heaven and earth (past), heavens and earth (present), new heaven and new earth (future)
- Lucifer's rebellion and subsequent judgment between Genesis 1:1 and 1:2
- The distinction between God's creative light and the sun
- Christians called to walk as children of light
- The firmament declaring God's glory

## Notes

### The Skeletal System Overview
- Goal: To be "filled with the knowledge of his will" (Colossians 1:9)
- Physical creation has three phases: original heaven/earth, current heavens/earth, future new heaven/earth
- Understanding God's purpose for physical creation is essential to Bible comprehension

### The Gap Between Genesis 1:1 and 1:2
- Genesis 1:1 describes original creation in eternity past
- Genesis 1:2 describes judgment after Lucifer's rebellion
- The word "and" can represent any length of time
- "Without form and void" appears only in Genesis 1:2 and Jeremiah 4:23 (context of God's fierce anger)

### Lucifer's Rebellion
- Created as anointed cherub with authority over angelic world
- Became lifted up with pride, merchandised rebellion throughout heaven
- Planned to exalt his throne above God's stars and be like the Most High
- God judged this rebellion, resulting in darkness and flood described in Genesis 1:2

### The First Day: Let There Be Light
- This light is not the sun (sun created on day four)
- Light dwells where God dwells (Job 38:19, 1 Timothy 6:16)
- God dwells in "light which no man can approach"
- This is the brightness of God's glory manifested in creation
- Light is currently hidden behind clouds and the firmament

### Application: Walking as Children of Light
- Christians translated from kingdom of darkness to light
- Called to walk as children of light (Ephesians 5:8)
- Must avoid fornication, uncleanness, covetousness
- Our inheritance depends on how we live
- Hell should know our names because of our stand for truth
