## Summary
This sermon examines Titus 2:2, focusing on four key characteristics aged men should possess: sober, grave, temperate, and sound in faith, charity, and patience. Pastor Simpson emphasizes the importance of studying lists of words in Scripture together to understand their combined meaning. He illustrates "grave" by reflecting on standing at a graveside, realizing believers get "one shot" at life with no opportunity to redo decisions. The message stresses that aged men represent the foundation of the church and must exemplify these attributes to strengthen the body of Christ.

## Scripture
Titus 2:2 — "That the aged men be sober, grave, temperate, sound in faith, in charity, in patience."

## Key Themes
- **Sobriety**: Being rational, real, rooted, and responsible; understanding why we do what we do with eternal perspective
- **Gravity**: Taking spiritual things seriously; recognizing we have one unrepeatable opportunity at life
- **Temperance**: Bringing spiritual elements together with discipline and precision, like the Old Testament priests; exercising self-control
- **Soundness**: Being complete, dependable, and faithful in faith (doctrine), charity (love with attributes), and patience (endurance)

## Notes

### Four Rs of Biblical Sobriety (Review)
- Rational: Given to reason, making sense of things with discernment and eternal perspective
- Real: Genuine, sincere, transparent; spiritual things being reality not just religious experience
- Rooted: Having strong foundation, not being moved, possessing true spiritual strength
- Responsible: Taking personal ownership and accountability; understanding rewards and consequences

### The Meaning of "Grave"
- Definition: Serious or solemn
- Only used three times in Scripture with this meaning (once in Titus 2:2, twice in 1 Timothy 3)
- Connected to the grave (burial place) and graven images (permanent inscriptions in stone)
- Illustration: Standing at Sister Bobby Bouter's graveside, realizing "life was done. They got one shot at it. There's no redo. There's no going back and fixed it. There's no, there's no cult. There's no gorilla glue. There's no time travel. There's no redo in it. You get one shot at it"
- Like engraving on precious metals—must be done carefully because there's only one opportunity
- Being grave means understanding the seriousness and obligation of life; having precision in spiritual matters

### The Meaning of "Temperate"
- Related to "temperance" in Galatians 5 (fruit of the Spirit)
- More comprehensive than just self-control
- Old Testament examples show tempering as bringing elements together precisely:
  - Exodus 29:2: Unleavened bread "tempered with oil" (linked with "anointed")
  - Exodus 30:34-37: Sweet spices tempered together for holy perfume—exact composition required
- 1 Corinthians 12:24: "God hath tempered the body together"
- Involves discipline, precision, completion, separation, producing holiness
- 1 Corinthians 9:25: "Every man that striveth for the mastery is temperate in all things"
- Bringing spiritual principles together so believers have right and complete understanding

### The Meaning of "Sound"
- Definition: Entire, whole, right, undecayed, uncorrupted, complete, dependable, faithful
- 2 Timothy 1:7: "God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind"
- 2 Timothy 1:13: "Hold fast the form of sound words"
- Titus 2:1: "Speak thou the things which become sound doctrine"
- 2 Timothy 4:3: "The time will come when they will not endure sound doctrine"
- Titus 1:13: "Rebuke them sharply, that they may be sound in the faith"

### Sound in Faith
- Faith as both noun (what we believe, our doctrine) and verb (extent to which we believe and live it out)
- Must have soundness—strong foundation that cannot be moved

### Sound in Charity
- Charity is more than just love—it includes specific attributes
- 1 Corinthians 13:4-5 describes charity's characteristics:
  - Suffers long and is kind
  - Envies not
  - Vaunts not itself, is not puffed up
  - Does not behave unseemly
  - Seeks not its own
  - Is not easily provoked
  - Thinks no evil
- These are attributes believers should exemplify while showing love to others
- Requires being in the Spirit to execute, especially "going through difficulty and be kind at the same time"

### Sound in Patience
- If faith is strong, believers realize anything endured on earth, God will deal with
- Not our job to fix other people, but to deal with ourselves
- Must remember Christ's sacrifice and what Paul endured
- Our test is dealing with being provoked and passing that test

### The Role of Aged Men
- Represent the foundation of the church
- When church goes wrong, it's because men don't do their job
- Young people are the future; aged men are the foundation
- Must take drama, emotion, whining, and complaining out of spiritual life
- Called to represent Christ in this world before receiving glorified bodies
- Provide security and comfort to the body when they have these attributes together

### Application: Deactivating Satan's Buttons
- Satan knows buttons to push in our flesh and people to use
- Sobriety, gravity, and temperance deactivate those buttons
- Pattern: Like Christ responding to temptation with Scripture (Matthew 4) versus Adam and Eve giving in (Genesis 3)
- Understanding these principles removes need to ask "Why is this happening?"
- Either it's sowing and reaping from bad decisions, or it's opportunity to exercise principles and bring glory to God
