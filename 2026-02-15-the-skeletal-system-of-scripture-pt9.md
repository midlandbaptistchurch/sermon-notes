## Summary
This sermon explores the rise and fall of Lucifer, examining how pride led to rebellion against God and initiated the cosmic warfare that affects all of life. Pastor Payne demonstrates that all conflict and struggle in existence traces back to Lucifer's rebellion, providing the biblical foundation for understanding why life is characterized as spiritual warfare.

## Scripture
Ezekiel 28:12-15, Isaiah 14 — Passages describing the original creation and fall of Lucifer, the anointed cherub who was perfect in beauty and wisdom until iniquity was found in him.

## Key Themes
- The origin of all warfare and conflict in creation
- Lucifer's original glory as the anointed cherub
- Pride as the root cause of rebellion and fall
- Life as spiritual warfare for believers
- The difference between God creating Lucifer vs. Lucifer creating Satan through rebellion

## Notes

### Seven Indicators of Pre-Adamic Trouble (Review)
1. Darkness was not declared good (unlike light)
2. The word "replenish" indicates previous abundance
3. "Subdue" relates to putting down enemies
4. "Dominion" was given because angelic dominion was lost
5. Adam told to "dress" and "keep" the garden (protective language)
6. Tree of knowledge of "good and evil" implies evil already existed
7. Satan's reference to "gods" (Elohim) that Eve understood

### Life as Spiritual Warfare
- Ecclesiastes 8:8 - War between life and death for all humanity
- 2 Corinthians 10:3-4 - Christians fight with spiritual weapons
- 1 Timothy 1:18 - Ministry described as "war a good warfare"
- 2 Timothy 2:3 - "Endure hardness as a good soldier"
- Ephesians 6:12 - Wrestling against spiritual wickedness
- Wrestling engages the entire body, representing total spiritual engagement

### God as a Warrior
- Exodus 15:3 - "The Lord is a man of war"
- Joshua 5:13-15 - Captain of the Lord's host appears to Joshua
- Isaiah 13:4 - "The Lord of hosts mustereth the host of the battle"
- Revelation 19:11 - Christ returns "in righteousness he doth judge and make war"

### Lucifer's Original Glory (Ezekiel 28:12-15)
- **"Thou sealest up the sum"** - The ultimate expression of God's creative power
- **"Full of wisdom"** - Had the sum total of wisdom (not wisdom itself like Christ)
- **"Perfect in beauty"** - Adorned with precious stones and gold
- **Musical abilities** - Created with "tabrets" and "pipes" for worship leadership
- **"The anointed cherub that covereth"** - Supreme position covering God's presence
- **Position on the holy mountain** - Access to God's throne and presence
- **"Walked...in the midst of the stones of fire"** - Like Moses receiving God's law

### The Nature of Cherubim
- Angelic creatures (not angels) who face God
- Guard God's presence (Genesis 3, Exodus 25)
- Have four wings and cover God's glory
- Lucifer was anointed "above his fellows" (Hebrews 1:9)

### Lucifer's Fall
- **"Perfect in thy ways...till iniquity was found in thee"** (v. 15)
- Pride led to grasping for what wasn't rightfully his
- Became Satan ("the adversary/resistor") and the devil ("the accuser")
- Contrast with Christ who "thought it not robbery to be equal with God"
- 1 Timothy 3:6 - Pride leads to "condemnation of the devil"

### The Cosmic Impact
- All warfare and conflict in existence stems from this original rebellion
- Understanding this provides the correct worldview for comprehending evil
- Without this knowledge, one cannot understand why the world is characterized by struggle
- This rebellion initiated the cycle of worlds: world that was, old world, present evil world, world to come, world without end
