## Summary
Pastor Jim Payne examines Ephesians chapter 3, which he calls one of the most important chapters in the Bible. The chapter is structured around two instances of "for this cause" (verses 1 and 14), dividing it into teaching on the mystery of Christ (verses 1-12) and Paul's powerful prayer for believers (verses 14-21). Payne unpacks Paul's four-fold prayer that believers be strengthened in the inner man, that Christ dwell in their hearts by faith, that they comprehend the dimensions of God's wisdom, and that they be filled with the fullness of God.

## Scripture
Ephesians 3:1-21 — Paul reveals the mystery of Christ and prays for spiritual empowerment of all believers.

## Key Themes
- The mystery of Christ revealed to the Gentiles
- Prayer as spiritual discipline and necessity
- Inner strength through the Holy Spirit
- God's wisdom in four dimensions: breadth, length, depth, and height
- The love of Christ that surpasses knowledge
- Being filled with the fullness of God
- The unified family of God (both Israel and the Church)

## Notes

### The Structure of Ephesians 3
- Verses 1-12: Doctrinal teaching about the mystery of Christ
- Verses 14-21: Paul's intercessory prayer for believers
- Both sections marked by "for this cause," emphasizing the urgency and purpose of the prayer

### Paul's Two Major Prayers in Ephesians
- **Ephesians 1:15-23**: Prayer that believers' eyes be enlightened to understand (1) the hope of His calling, (2) the riches of His inheritance, and (3) the greatness of His power
- **Ephesians 3:14-21**: Four progressive petitions that build upon one another

### The Four-Fold Prayer (Ephesians 3:14-21)

**First Petition (verse 16)**: Strengthened with might by His Spirit in the inner man
- God's might is not demonstrated today through physical miracles but through spiritual empowerment
- The battle is always in the inner man
- Reference to 1 Corinthians 10:13: God provides a way to bear trials without succumbing to them
- Trials strengthen us through appropriating God's truth in our inner being

**Second Petition (verse 17a)**: Christ dwell in your hearts by faith
- Not merely indwelling (positional) but abiding and resting (relational)
- Christ dwells more comfortably where truth is not resisted
- Understanding the mystery of Christ allows Him to dwell more fully in our lives

**Third Petition (verse 17b-18)**: Comprehend the breadth, length, depth, and height
- Four-dimensional understanding (not three-dimensional like our physical world)
- This refers to the dimensions of God's wisdom, not just the love of Christ
- Connection to Job 11:5-8, where Job's friends acknowledge God's wisdom is higher than heaven, deeper than hell, longer than the earth, and broader than the sea
- Paul prays believers can comprehend what Job's friends said was incomprehensible

**Fourth Petition (verse 19)**: Know the love of Christ which passes knowledge and be filled with all the fullness of God
- The love of Christ appears three times in Scripture: Romans 8:35 (security), 2 Corinthians 5:14 (motivation), and Ephesians 3:19 (understanding)
- To be filled with the fullness of God is the ultimate goal

### The Importance of Prayer
- Prayer is the hardest Christian discipline
- Spiritual life requires both "breathing in" (God's Word) and "breathing out" (prayer)
- Paul prays with a cause—he understands the critical importance of believers appropriating truth
- Intellectual knowledge differs from practical application; Paul prays for application

### The Nature of God's Giving
- God grants blessings "according to the riches of His glory" (Philippians 4:19)
- Not merely "out of" His riches, but according to the measure of all His riches
- This is both a measure and a resource for blessing our lives

### The Family of God
- Ephesians 3:15: "Of whom the whole family in heaven and earth is named"
- Believers are part of God's unified family alongside Israel
- God has distinct programs: Israel for the earth, the Church (body of Christ) for the heavenlies
- These programs remain separate but fused to accomplish God's eternal purpose

### Truth as Foundation
- Emotional preaching and illustrations are good but temporary
- What remains with believers is the substance of truth
- Truth provides the root and foundation for facing life's difficulties
- Understanding God's eternal purpose strengthens the inner man
