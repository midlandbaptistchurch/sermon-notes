## Summary
Brother Tim Faulk addresses the question "How shall they preach except they be sent?" by outlining the characteristics of the messenger God wants to send to fulfill His will that all men be saved and come to the knowledge of the truth. Using the framework of "The Magnificent Seven" (all starting with 'M'), he examines six essential qualities of God's messenger, emphasizing that every believer's life is their ministry. He shares personal testimony from recent ministry in the Philippines and Burma, including emotional returns to early mission fields and divine appointments.

## Scripture
Romans 10:14-15 — Paul asks a series of questions about the necessity of sending messengers: how can people call on Christ without believing, believe without hearing, hear without a preacher, or preach without being sent? He concludes by quoting Isaiah about the beautiful feet of those who bring the gospel.

1 Timothy 2:4 — God's will is for all men to be saved and to come to the knowledge of the truth.

## Key Themes
- **Sound Doctrine**: The messenger must understand and expound on biblical doctrine, not just rush to practical application, following Paul as God's spokesman for this age
- **Surrender**: Ministry begins with raising your hand and saying "Here am I, send me" in response to God's call
- **Sanctification**: The messenger must be set apart positionally, practically, and ultimately perfectly for God's use
- **Lifelong Study**: Continuous growth in biblical understanding rather than claiming to preach the same thing for decades
- **Balance**: Walking circumspectly with wisdom between ministry/work and family, avoiding extremes in either direction
- **Boldness**: Faithfully and courageously declaring the gospel message that Christ died for our sins, was buried, and rose again

### The Messenger Must Be Sound in Doctrine
- Problem in independent fundamental Baptist movement: preachers find doctrine then "put on their nikes" to rush to practical application without expounding the doctrine
- This camouflages what Scripture really means
- God gave us a spokesman for this age: the Apostle Paul (not 13th apostle, but apostle to Gentiles)
- Must digest what Paul says and not be ashamed of it
- Titus 1:9 - holding fast the faithful word as taught in sound doctrine
- Titus 2:1 - speak things which become sound doctrine
- Sound doctrine means something that makes sense
- Tim's preaching goals: (1) Make sense (2) Don't embarrass his wife
- 1 Timothy 1:10 and 2 Timothy 4:3 address the importance of and resistance to sound doctrine
- Question for congregation: Are you parroting Pastor Payne's doctrine or do you have sound doctrine yourself?
- Illustration: Pastor Loxin's baptism class in Philippines - after three Sundays of teaching, asks if someone dies unbaptized will they go to heaven; when students look uncertain, he starts class over

### The Messenger Must Be Surrendered
- Missions begins with surrender
- Tim's pattern of surrender:
  - Raised hand to help start Grace Baptist Church in Winchester, Kentucky
  - Raised hand on hill in Philippines when God dealt with his heart about missions
  - Raised hand when door opened in Burma (need for native English-speaking teachers)
  - Raised hand to come stateside to Huntsville, Alabama
- Living off someone else's surrender (like parents who went to mission field) is insufficient
- 1 Corinthians 9:16 - Paul understood ministry as divine calling, not career or personal ambition
- Greatest obstacle in ministry is often not lack of ability but lack of surrender

### The Messenger Must Be Sanctified
- Must be set apart for God's use
- 1 Corinthians 6:11 - positionally sanctified when saved
- Practical sanctification - setting apart daily to be "meat for the master's use"
- Perfect sanctification - will occur at rapture or resurrection
- God will ultimately complete His work in us

### The Messenger Must Be a Lifelong Student
- Must continue to grow in understanding
- Tim's conversation with Pastor Payne in 2002 about baptism - both have different positions 24 years later because of continued study
- Rejects notion of "preaching the same thing for 40 years"
- By God's grace, has learned better and repented of some previous teaching
- Bible is eternal - cannot grasp it all in 3-4 years of Bible school or seminary
- Won't get it all until sanctification is perfected
- If Paul hadn't attained or apprehended, we certainly can't
- 2 Timothy 2:15 - "Study to shew thyself approved unto God"
- Word "shew" (S-H-E-W) connects to showbread in Tabernacle - bread for display showing God's provision
- David ate the showbread (used as lesson by Jesus)
- Are you displaying what you're studying?
- "Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman" - no place for laziness
- Tim used to hate waking early to pray and study, but now God wakes him up anyway
- "A workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth" - requires work
- Cannot continually pour into others if no longer allowing God to pour into you

### The Messenger Must Have Balance
- Ephesians 5:15 - walk circumspectly (carefully, accurately, wisely)
- Ministry requires more than zeal - requires wisdom and balance
- Filipino and Burmese pastors lack balance - 24/7 Bible focus
- Greatest danger: becoming so committed to serving everyone else that we neglect those God entrusted to us first
- Pastor's first ministry is his wife
- Tim's first ministry is Lisa
- Family is first ministry, then must balance with other ministry
- Same principle applies to work - some men are so unbalanced they only talk about work
- Why are you working if not for your family?
- 1 Timothy 3:5 - if a man doesn't know how to rule his own house, how can he care for God's church?
- Family should not receive leftovers of time, energy, and affection
- Tim acknowledges times he's gotten "out of whack" in this area
- Ministry begins at home
- Ephesians 4:1 - walk worthy of the vocation with which you are called
- Ministry should touch: family, church, community, then world (in that order)
- Should be: passionate but not reckless, bold but not foolish, committed but not careless, busy but not frantic, sacrificial but not irresponsible
- God doesn't call us to do everything - calls us to faithfully do what He's given us
- 1 Corinthians 4:2 - it is required in stewards that a man be found faithful
- Faithfulness is greater than wealth and fame

### The Messenger Must Speak Boldly
- Must faithfully deliver the gospel message in whatever people group
- Ephesians 6:19-20 - Paul requested prayer for boldness to make known the mystery of the gospel
- If Paul needed prayer for boldness, we should too
- 1 Corinthians 15:1 - "I declare unto you the gospel" - a bold, articulate declaration
- Need men who will know, live, love, teach, and boldly proclaim the truth

### Additional Qualities
- **Rooted and Grounded**: Must be rooted in Word of God, having Scripture for all actions
- **Endures Storms**: Must endure hardness without surrendering purpose (not rain showers or speed bumps, but real storms)
  - 2 Timothy 2:3 - endure hardness as a good soldier of Jesus Christ
- **Shepherd's Heart**: Heart of empathy, willingness to put yourself in someone's shoes before judging, hearing both sides when multiple parties involved
- **Beacon of Hope**: Should always end with hope, not leaving people defeated or beaten up
  - Hope is essential message of missions

### Closing Illustration: Napoleon's Trumpeters
- Napoleon was a military genius studied by generals like Stonewall Jackson and Robert E. Lee
- His troops often faced superior odds, were undersupplied, or ambushed
- Simple tactic: After infantry met infantry in chaos of battle, before sending cavalry, he sent trumpeters
- Trumpeters played exciting French anthem that stirred soldiers' hearts
- Sound of trumpet gave French soldiers second wind to win battle after battle
- Parallel to Christian hope: 1 Corinthians 15 - "Behold I shew you a mystery. We shall not all sleep, but we shall all be changed in a moment, in the twinkling of an eye, at the last trump"
- 1 Thessalonians 4 - archangel will sound trumpet, we'll be gathered to meet Lord in air
- In midst of chaos, that trumpet will blow (possibly soon, hopefully tonight)
- There is hope in that trumpet
- Napoleon won by grace of the trumpet; Lord saves us by grace and will catch us up by grace at sound of trumpet
- Goal: Leave conference with this kind of hope, determined to serve God fervently until trumpet blows
