## Summary
Pastor Jim Payne continues his two-part study on "The Seed War," examining why God commanded Israel to utterly destroy the nations of Canaan — including women, children, and animals. Drawing from Genesis 3:15 through the conquest narratives, he traces the cosmic conflict between the seed of the woman and the seed of the serpent, with particular focus on the Nephilim: the giant hybrid offspring of fallen angels and human women whose corruption necessitated God's judgment in the flood and whose post-flood presence in Canaan explains the severity of God's commands to Israel. The sermon closes with a practical application that just as giants were strategically placed to block Israel from the Promised Land, believers today face giants the enemy uses to rob them of the abundant life God has promised.

## Scripture
Genesis 6; Deuteronomy 7:1-2 — The primary texts anchor a broad survey running from Genesis 3:15 (the proto-evangelium and the first announcement of the Seed War) through Numbers 13–14 (the twelve spies and the Anakim), with supporting references in Galatians 3:16, Colossians 1:20, Ephesians 1:9-10, Amos 2:9-10, and Genesis 12–15.

## Key Themes
- The Seed War: Satan's two-pronged strategy of violence and corruption to destroy the human line through which the Redeemer would come (Genesis 3:15)
- The Sons of God and the Nephilim: fallen angelic beings who cohabited with human women, producing a race of giants that corrupted both humanity and the animal kingdom (Genesis 6:1-4)
- God's flood as a strategic reset to preserve the genetically pure line of Noah through whom the seed of the woman would continue
- Post-flood giants: multiple tribes of giant peoples (Anakim, Rephaim, Emim, Zuzim, Amorites, etc.) strategically surrounding the Promised Land to oppose Israel
- God's command to utterly destroy the Canaanite nations as a necessary act of war to protect the messianic line and ultimately secure redemption for humanity
- Facing personal giants in faith, trusting that God has already granted the victory

## Notes

### Review of the Broader Series
- This message is part of a concluding "Some More of the Story" study following a four-to-five month series called "The Skeletal Structure of the Bible."
- The series began at Genesis 1:1 and traced creation, the angelic world, Lucifer's rebellion, and the formation of humanity as a younger race placed on earth with dominion.
- God's ultimate will, per Colossians 1:20 and Ephesians 1:9-10, is **reconciliation** — of things in heaven and things on earth — accomplished through Christ and, mysteriously, through the body of Christ.

### The Seed War Introduced (Genesis 3:15)
- After the fall of Adam and Eve, God pronounced the *proto-evangelium* ("first gospel") in Genesis 3:15: the seed of the woman would deal a deadly wound to the seed of the serpent.
- Satan's two weapons against that seed: **violence** (Cain murdering Abel) and **corruption** (the Genesis 6 angelic intrusion).
- The seed line is traced through Seth → Enos → … → Noah, and later narrowed to Abraham → Isaac → Jacob → the Jewish people → ultimately fulfilled in Christ (Galatians 3:16).

### The Nephilim Crisis (Genesis 6)
- The "sons of God" in the Old Testament are identified as angelic beings. During the days of **Jared** (more than 1,000 years before Noah), a group of these angels descended, took human wives, and produced a hybrid race of giants known as the **Nephilim** (Hebrew *nephal* — "fallen ones" or "those who cause others to fall").
- Pastor Payne connects this event to ancient mythology (Greek gods, demigods such as Hercules, Perseus, Gilgamesh), suggesting those traditions preserve a corrupted memory of real events.
- The corruption was not merely moral but **genetic and physical**: the animal kingdom was also affected, possibly explaining biblical references to monstrous creatures (cockatrice, unicorn, satyr) and hybrid beings.
- God's response: grief, then judgment. He gave 120 more years (Genesis 6:3), then sent the global flood, destroying all flesh — man, hybrid giants, and corrupted animals.
- **Noah** was chosen because he was "perfect in his generations" — his genetic lineage was uncorrupted, essential for preserving the pure human line through which Christ would come.
- God Himself brought the uncorrupted animals to the ark because only He knew which animals had not been genetically defiled.

### Giants After the Flood (Genesis 12–15)
- Genesis 6:4 states giants existed "in those days and also after that" — signaling a post-flood reappearance.
- Even in Abraham's day, giant tribes are documented: **Rephaim, Zuzim, Emim** (Genesis 14:5), **Amorites** described as cedar-tree tall (Amos 2:9), and at least 10–15 named giant clans listed in Genesis 14–15.
- The origin of post-flood giants is debated; proposed explanations include: (1) a second angelic incursion, (2) a local rather than global flood (rejected by Pastor Payne as unscriptural), (3) genetic corruption carried through Ham's wife onto the ark, or (4) survivors.
- Most post-flood giant tribes are associated with **Canaan** (son of Ham), consistent with God's curse on Canaan.

### The Twelve Spies and the Giants (Numbers 13–14)
- When Israel reached the border of Canaan after the Exodus, twelve spies were sent in. At **Hebron** they encountered the **sons of Anak** (Ahiman, Sheshai, Talmai) — members of the Anakim, a giant tribe.
- Ten spies reported: "We were as grasshoppers in our own sight and in their sight" (Numbers 13:33). The word *Nephilim* appears again here.
- Only Caleb and Joshua urged Israel to trust God and advance. The nation wept and refused, resulting in 40 years of wilderness wandering.
- Forty years later, an 85-year-old Caleb requested and received Hebron as his inheritance, personally defeating those three giants.

### Why God Commanded Total Destruction
- The giants and their associated nations were not scattered globally after the flood — they were **strategically concentrated around the land of Israel**, positioned by Satan to block God's people from the Promised Land.
- For 400 years (Israel's time in Egypt), these giant kingdoms developed and entrenched themselves (cf. Genesis 15:16 — "the iniquity of the Amorites is not yet full").
- God's command to kill men, women, children, infants, and animals was not rash anger but a **strategic act of war** to eliminate the corrupted seed before it could permanently corrupt the messianic line or produce further hybrid offspring.
- Named giants referenced for next week's study: Goliath, Ishbi-benob, Lahmi, Saph, the unnamed giant with six fingers and six toes, and **Og of Bashan** (whose iron bed was approximately 14 feet long).

### Practical Application
- Just as Israel's giants were strategically placed to keep God's people from entering and enjoying the Promised Land, the devil places giants in believers' lives to rob them of the abundant life God has promised.
- The call is to face those giants in faith — God has already declared the victory; the people must claim it by stepping forward in trust rather than retreating in fear.
