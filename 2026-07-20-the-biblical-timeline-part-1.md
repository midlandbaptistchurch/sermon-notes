## Summary
Assoc Pastor Mark Simpson introduces a Bible study tool — a dispensational timeline drawing from the book *One Book Rightly Divided* — to help church members build a solid foundation in right division of Scripture. He walks through the major markers on a biblical timeline, emphasizing the distinct age of grace (Romans through Philemon), the transitional period in Acts, and upcoming prophetic events such as the Rapture, Tribulation, and Millennial Reign. The session is framed as Part 1 of a multi-week study, with a full completed timeline planned for the following week.

## Scripture
No single primary scripture text was assigned. The following passages were referenced during the sermon:
- **Galatians 1:1** — Paul's apostleship came directly from Jesus Christ, not from men.
- **Galatians 1:8–9** — A curse on anyone preaching a gospel contrary to Paul's.
- **Ephesians 3:3–5** — The mystery hidden in other ages, now revealed to Paul by revelation.
- **Romans 16:25** — The mystery kept secret since the world began, revealed through Paul's gospel.
- **Philippians 4:10–13** — Paul's contentment in all circumstances; context for "I can do all things through Christ."

## Key Themes
- The importance of a foundational understanding of dispensationalism and right division
- The biblical timeline as a teaching and discipleship tool
- The Age of Grace as a distinct, previously hidden mystery revealed to Paul (Romans–Philemon)
- The transitional nature of the book of Acts (Acts 7 / Stephen's stoning as a key marker)
- Distinguishing the Rapture from the Second Coming
- Why Paul is emphasized: he alone received the mystery gospel by direct revelation
- Finances in Scripture: Matthew–Acts context (Tribulation preparation) vs. Grace principles (Philippians 4)
- The danger of pride and ignorance in handling Scripture

**The Timeline — Key Markers Discussed**
1. **Eternity past (left arrow)** — God has no beginning; events before Genesis 1:1 include creation of angels, the planet, and the rebellion.
2. **Genesis 1–2** — Creation of the present world.
3. **Genesis 11 onward** — Israel elevated as God's chosen people; Jews elevated above Gentiles as the channel to God.
4. **The Cross** — Central marker on the timeline.
5. **Christ's birth → earthly ministry → death, burial, resurrection, ascension**
6. **Acts 7 — Stephen's stoning** — Transitional marker; Jews brought down to the level of Gentiles. The transitional period (roughly the book of Acts) is often misunderstood.
7. **The Age of Grace (parentheses on the timeline)** — Romans through Philemon. This period was *not* prophesied; it was a mystery hidden before the foundation of the world and revealed exclusively to Paul (Eph. 3:3–5; Rom. 16:25). Inserting Hebrews into Paul's grace epistles is a common error among those who do not rightly divide.
8. **The Rapture** — Believers caught up; coincides with the Judgment Seat of Christ.
9. **The Tribulation (7 years)** — The 70th week of Daniel. The Antichrist rules, particularly in the last 3.5 years. Believers cannot buy or sell. This period connects back to Jesus's teachings in Matthew–Acts, which were preparing a Jewish remnant for this time (e.g., sell possessions, don't take money or weapons, supernatural provision — cf. Matt. 10).
10. **The Second Coming (Second Advent)**
11. **Millennial Reign** — 1,000-year reign of Christ.
12. **Battle of Gog and Magog / Great White Throne Judgment**
13. **Eternity future (right arrow)**

**Doctrinal Points Emphasized**
- Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John are not written *to* the Grace-age believer; they prepared Israel for the Tribulation.
- The mystery of the Age of Grace was never prophesied and will appear to contradict Matthew–Acts passages if right division is not applied.
- Paul received his gospel by direct revelation — just as Moses was the sole recipient of the Law, Paul was the sole recipient of the mystery gospel.
- Calling Paul greater than Jesus is a mischaracterization of the position; the point is that Paul's epistles are the authoritative instruction for the current age.
- Those who do not rightly divide will label this position "hyper-dispensationalist" or heretical.
- The Rapture has no prophesied timing; claiming imminent return based on current world conditions reflects ignorance of history and Scripture.

**Finances Illustration**
- Jesus told the rich it was harder than a camel through a needle's eye — because he was preparing people for the Tribulation, not the Age of Grace.
- Paul in Philippians 4 models Grace-age financial principles: contentment in both abundance and need; "I can do all things through Christ" (v. 13) is in the context of financial contentment, not general empowerment.
- A youth group exercise asked which financial bracket best serves God — the answer was that none is inherently superior; the principles of grace apply regardless of wealth level.
