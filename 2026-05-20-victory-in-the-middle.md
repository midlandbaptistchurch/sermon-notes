## Summary
Craig McKinley explores how to find victory in the middle seasons of life between salvation and heaven, using Peter's experiences of walking on water, denying Christ, and being restored to illustrate both an avenue for gospel sharing and the path to restoration through repentance.

## Scripture
Matthew 14:22-31 — Peter walks on water but begins to sink when he takes his eyes off Christ.

## Key Themes
- Victory in the middle of life's trials
- Using Peter's story as a gospel-sharing avenue
- Repentance and restoration after failure
- God's personal care for individuals
- Moving past bitterness and blame

## Notes

### Paul's Gospel Passion (Philippians 1)
- Paul's passion for the Gospel evident throughout his writings
- Called for conversations to become the Gospel (Philippians 1:27)
- Challenge: Why don't we talk about the Gospel more?

### Gospel Avenue: Walking on Water Story
- Use familiar phrase "walking on water" to engage people
- Matthew 14:22-31 provides relatable entry point
- Peter steps out in faith but sinks when focusing on circumstances
- Creates natural transition to Romans passages about salvation
- "Lord, save me!" - immediate response from Jesus

### Peter's Middle Failure
- Between salvation victory and heaven victory lies the "middle"
- Peter's bold declaration: "I will never deny you" (Matthew 26:33-35)
- Three denials despite sincere intentions
- Often fail when we feel closest to God
- Personal illustration: Road rage after praising God

### The Denial Sequence
- First denial: To servant girl at door (John 18:17)
- Second denial: To group warming by fire (John 18:25)
- Third denial: To Malchus's relative who witnessed ear cutting (John 18:26)
- Jesus heard all three denials and looked directly at Peter
- Peter "went out and wept bitterly" (Luke 22:62)

### Path to Restoration
- Repentance is essential - not blaming others
- Many drift from God due to unresolved bitterness
- Need to take personal responsibility for our responses
- Stop blaming others for our spiritual condition

### God's Personal Care
- Angel given specific message: "Tell the disciples and Peter" (Mark 16:7)
- God knew Peter would be distraught and needed personal assurance
- Shows God's individual care and desire for restoration
- Peter later became powerful preacher with great ministry

### Warning Against Prolonged Defeat
- Story of 83-year-old neighbor who left ministry
- Blamed church people for 61 years
- Finally restored at age 90, died at 91
- Don't waste decades without victory
