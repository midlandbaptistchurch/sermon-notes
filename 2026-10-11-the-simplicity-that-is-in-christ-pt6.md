## Summary
Pastor Payne addresses what he calls Christianity's biggest "sacred cow"—the doctrine of water baptism. By applying the principle of rightly dividing the Word of Truth (2 Timothy 2:15), he demonstrates that water baptism was a command in "time past" (John the Baptist through Acts) but is not part of Paul's gospel message for today. Paul states "Christ sent me not to baptize" and "I thank God that I baptized none of you," contrasting sharply with Peter's command to "repent and be baptized every one of you." The baptism that matters today is Spirit baptism—being placed into the body of Christ by the Holy Spirit (1 Corinthians 12:13).

## Scripture
**John 1:33** — John the Baptist says, "he that sent me to baptize with water," showing he had a commission from God to baptize.

**1 Corinthians 1:17** — Paul states, "For Christ sent me not to baptize, but to preach the gospel," showing a different commission than John the Baptist.

**Acts 2:38** — Peter commands Israel, "Repent, and be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins, and ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost."

**1 Corinthians 1:14** — Paul says, "I thank God that I baptized none of you."

**Ephesians 4:4-5** — "One body, and one Spirit...one Lord, one faith, one baptism"—the one baptism for today is Spirit baptism, not water.

## Key Themes
- **Rightly Dividing Water Baptism**: Water baptism was essential in "time past" (gospels and early Acts) but is not part of Paul's gospel for the body of Christ today
- **John the Baptist's Commission vs. Paul's Commission**: John was sent to baptize; Paul explicitly was not sent to baptize
- **Spirit Baptism vs. Water Baptism**: The baptism that places believers into the body of Christ today is by the Holy Spirit, not water (1 Corinthians 12:13)
- **Time Zones of Scripture**: Time past (Old Testament through early Acts), but now (Paul's epistles), and ages to come (Hebrews through Revelation)
- **Another Gospel**: Teaching that water baptism is necessary for salvation today is "another gospel" that perverts the gospel of grace (Galatians 1:6-9)

## Notes

### Introduction: Christian Breathing
- Psalm 150: "Let everything that hath breath praise the Lord"
- Christian life requires breath: inhaling (Bible reading) and exhaling (prayer)
- Most Christians fail in these two areas, making them weak and anemic
- The simplicity that is in Christ (2 Corinthians) should not be corrupted

### Rightly Dividing the Bible
- One command to study: 2 Timothy 2:15 - "Study to show thyself approved...rightly dividing the word of truth"
- Three time zones in Scripture (Ephesians 2):
  - Time past (Old Testament and Gospels)
  - But now (Paul's writings, Romans through Philemon)
  - Ages to come (Hebrews through Revelation)
- Not contradictions, but differences in the Bible
- Don't spin the Bible or say "what this really means is"—let it say what it says

### The Biggest Sacred Cow: Water Baptism
- Sacred cow: something deemed so important it can't be touched or questioned
- The biggest sacred cow in Christianity is the doctrine of baptism
- 50 different ideas about why, how, when, and where to get baptized
- The Bible doesn't make it hard if you apply rightly dividing

### The Contrast: John the Baptist vs. Paul
- **John 1:33**: "he that sent me to baptize with water"
- **1 Corinthians 1:17**: "For Christ sent me not to baptize"
- **Acts 2:37-38**: Peter commands "Repent, and be baptized every one of you"
- **1 Corinthians 1:14**: Paul says "I thank God that I baptized none of you"
- Clear difference between time past mandate and but now practice

### Water Baptism in Time Past
- First mention of baptism: John the Baptist (after 400 years of silence)
- John was sent to baptize—it's why he's called "the Baptist"
- Mark 1:4: "John did baptize in the wilderness, and preach the baptism of repentance for the remission of sins"
- Luke 7:28-30: Those who were baptized "justified God"; Pharisees who rejected baptism "rejected the counsel of God"
- Water baptism was God's command, counsel, and mandate in time past

### The Gospel of the Kingdom vs. Gospel of Grace
- Mark 16:15-16: "He that believeth and is baptized shall be saved"
- This is the gospel of the kingdom, not the gospel of grace
- Acts 2:23: Peter indicts Israel—"ye have taken, and by wicked hands have crucified and slain"
- Peter wasn't preaching the cross as good news; he was charging Israel with murder
- They were identifying with the coming of the Holy Spirit, not with Christ's death, burial, and resurrection

### Paul: Apostle to the Gentiles
- Romans 11:13: "I am the apostle of the Gentiles"
- Acts 22:21: "Depart: for I will send thee far hence unto the Gentiles"
- Romans 15:8: "Jesus Christ was a minister of the circumcision"
- Romans 15:16: Paul is "the minister of Jesus Christ to the Gentiles"
- 1 Timothy 2:7: "I am ordained a preacher, and an apostle...a teacher of the Gentiles"
- 1 Timothy 1:11: "the glorious gospel of the blessed God, which was committed to my trust"
- Ephesians 3:1: "I Paul, the prisoner of Jesus Christ for you Gentiles"

### The One Baptism for Today
- 1 Corinthians 12:13: "For by one Spirit are we all baptized into one body"
- Spirit baptism happens at salvation—the Spirit puts believers into the body of Christ
- Not visible, not something you realize when it happens
- Ephesians 4:4-5: "one body, and one Spirit...one Lord, one faith, one baptism"
- Not two baptisms (Spirit and water), but one—Spirit baptism

### What Was John Identifying Believers With?
- John didn't know about Christ's death, burial, and resurrection when he baptized
- Joel 2:28: "I will pour out my spirit upon all flesh"
- John was identifying believers with the pouring out of the Holy Spirit
- Acts 2:38: "ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost"
- Likely baptized by pouring water on them, not immersion
- They were confessing sins against the old covenant and looking forward to the new covenant

### The New Covenant
- Exodus 24:7: The old covenant—"the book of the covenant"—Israel said "we will do and be obedient"
- Israel failed to keep the old covenant
- Jeremiah 31:31-33: God promises a new covenant—"I will put my law in their inward parts"
- The Spirit will be poured out when God establishes the new covenant with Israel
- This is not for the body of Christ today—the Spirit indwells believers, not poured out upon them

### Where Does Water Baptism Identifying with Death, Burial, Resurrection Come From?
- The idea of water baptism identifying with Christ's death, burial, and resurrection is not found in time past
- It's not found in Paul's teaching for today
- It's a tradition that makes void the Word of God
- No biblical basis for this practice when rightly dividing

### The Danger of Another Gospel
- Galatians 1:6-7: "I marvel that ye are so soon removed from him that called you into the grace of Christ unto another gospel"
- If someone says you must repent and be baptized to be saved, that's another gospel
- Your gospel: "by grace are ye saved through faith...not of works" (Ephesians 2:8-9)
- Galatians 1:8: "But though we, or an angel from heaven, preach any other gospel unto you than that which we have preached unto you, let him be accursed"
- Accursed means damned and going to hell
- God doesn't "pussyfoot" around—you must have the right message

### True Identification
- Colossians 2:9-10: "ye are complete in Him"—nothing to add
- Don't need to add works to completion in Christ
- Ephesians 2:10: "For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus unto good works"
- True identification is living a life of good works—that's your badge, how you put on Christ

### Conclusion: The Simplicity
- Water baptism: important in time past, not important in but now
- They were sent to do it there, not sent to do it here
- Rejoice in Christ and the gospel of grace
- The Spirit baptizes believers into Christ's death, burial, and resurrection
- Know truth to help people, not to be puffed up
- Give an answer to those who emphasize water baptism by rightly dividing the Word
