## Summary
Pastor Simpson continues the study of Ephesians 5, focusing on the command to "walk in love as Christ also hath loved us." He explores the seven biblical uses of the word "walk" in Ephesians, examining how believers should walk in their past, in good works, worthy of their calling, and separated from worldly pursuits. The sermon emphasizes that our walk reflects our identity as servants, stewards, and sons of God.

## Scripture
Ephesians 5:2 — The instruction to walk in love, modeled after Christ's sacrificial love for us.

## Key Themes
- Walking in love as Christ loved us
- The seven biblical "walks" in Ephesians
- Walking worthy of our calling

- Walking in the Spirit versus walking in the flesh
- Separation from worldly pursuits with compassion

## Notes

### The Seven Walks in Ephesians
Pastor Simpson highlights that the word "walk" appears seven times throughout Ephesians, each providing a different perspective on Christian living:

1. **Walk in Times Past (Ephesians 2:1-3)** — Walking according to the course of the world before salvation, in the lust of the flesh. The "But God" moment of salvation marks the transition.

2. **Walk in Good Works (Ephesians 2:10)** — Created in Christ Jesus for good works that God has ordained. The emphasis is doing good works for the right reasons—from walking in the Spirit, not from pride or seeking human approval.

3. **Walk Worthy of Your Calling (Ephesians 4:1)** — Walking worthy of the vocation to which we are called. This requires understanding our value in Christ and fulfilling our roles as servants, stewards, and sons.

4. **Walk in the Spirit (Romans 8)** — The security of walking after the Spirit rather than the flesh. Nothing can separate us from the love of God when we walk in the Spirit.

5. **Walk Separated (Ephesians 4)** — Separating ourselves from the blindness, alienation, and ignorance of the world, while maintaining compassion and discernment toward others.

### Reflection on Our Past
Pastor Simpson emphasizes the importance of remembering our life before Christ. Even after 35+ years in ministry, he finds it valuable to reflect on his former life to maintain perspective on God's grace and his current identity.

### The Right Condition for Good Works
True good works require three considerations:
1. Consider your own condition—walk in the Spirit, not the flesh
2. Acknowledge God's goodness and kindness toward you through Christ
3. Do good works motivated by gratitude for Christ's grace, not for human approval

### Walking Worthy
Walking worthy means recognizing that while we didn't deserve salvation through our own merit, we cannot deny the value God has given us through Christ. This understanding helps us see ourselves in three roles: as servants (obedient to God), as stewards (responsible managers of God's gifts), and as sons (beloved by the Father).

### Security in Romans 8
Romans 8 assures believers that those walking after the Spirit cannot be separated from the love of Christ by any circumstance—hardship, suffering, or external pressure. This security comes from understanding that we are saved and loved, not from our performance.
