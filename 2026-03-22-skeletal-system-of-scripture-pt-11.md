## Summary
Pastor Jim Payne continues the series on God's skeletal framework of scripture by examining Lucifer's rebellion against God. He traces how Satan, as a created cherub, became lifted up with pride and merchandised a plan to overthrow God's throne, deceiving a third of the angels to follow him in his insurrection against Almighty God.

## Scripture
Isaiah 14:12-14 — Lucifer's attempted coup against God's throne
Ezekiel 28:12-17 — Description of Lucifer's beauty, position, and fall
Colossians 1:9-10 — Prayer to be filled with knowledge of God's will

## Key Themes
- The skeletal system of scripture: multiple worlds and world systems
- Lucifer as the anointed covering cherub, leader of angelic worship
- Pride and corruption of wisdom leading to rebellion
- Merchandising lies and flatteries to gain followers
- Satan's failed coup: "I will be like the most high"
- God's ultimate will: reconciliation of all creation under Christ
- The rebellion continued from angels to humans in the Garden of Eden

## Notes

### The Framework of Understanding
- Understanding God's will requires knowing the skeletal system of scripture: creation worlds, the world that then was, this present evil world, and the world to come
- Without this framework, Christians cannot properly understand God's purposes
- God's will is not about us—it's about bringing all creation to glory and preeminence to Christ

### Lucifer's Position and Fall
- Lucifer was a created being, not born or human
- He was the anointed covering cherub—above all other angels
- He led the worship of God in sanctuaries in the pre-Adamic Luciferian world
- He was perfect until iniquity was found in him
- His corruption came through pride and lifted-up heart regarding his beauty and wisdom

### The Method of Rebellion
- Lucifer went throughout the angelic world merchandising and trafficking lies
- He was a tailbearer—a gossiper and deceiver going up and down
- He used flattery and deception to convince angels to follow him
- A third of the angels followed him in violent rebellion against God's throne
- He convinced them through false promises and misrepresentation of God's character

### Lucifer's Failed Coup (Isaiah 14:13-14)
1. **Introduction**: "I will ascend into heaven"
2. **Three Points**:
   - I will exalt my throne above the stars of God
   - I will sit upon the mount of congregation in the sides of the north
   - I will ascend above the heights of the clouds
3. **Conclusion**: "I will be like the most high"

### God's Response
- God declared He would cast Lucifer out of the mountain of God as profane (irreverent)
- God alone is reverend and worthy of awe
- Lucifer's pride led to his destruction
- He was cut down to the ground

### The Continuation of Rebellion
- After his fall from heaven, Satan became the serpent in the Garden of Eden
- He continued his deceptive tactics with Adam and Eve
- He promised wisdom and equality with God, just as he had with the angels
- Humanity joined the rebellion through disobedience

### God's Ultimate Will
- God created heaven and earth for His Son, Jesus Christ
- Christ is to be preeminent in all things
- God's will is to reconcile and restore all creation under Christ's authority
- The church (body of Christ) will be taken to heavenly places and will usurp fallen authority
- Satan and his forces will ultimately be cast down to the earth, then to the pit, then to the lake of fire
