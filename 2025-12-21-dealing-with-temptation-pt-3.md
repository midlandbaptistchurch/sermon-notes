## Summary
In this third installment on temptation, Hugh Taylor explores how believers in the Age of Grace experience trials and tests that strengthen their faith. Using 1 Corinthians 10:13 as the foundation, he examines temptation as both a trial that tests our belief system and a distraction from God's will, emphasizing the importance of remembering God's faithfulness through life's challenges.

## Scripture
1 Corinthians 10:13 — "For God is faithful, who will not suffer you to be tempted above that you're able; but with the temptation also make a way to escape that you may be able to bear it."

Additional passages:
- Genesis 22:1 — God's testing of Abraham
- Hebrews 11:17-19 — Abraham's faith through trial
- 2 Timothy 4:1-8 — Paul's charge to Timothy and reflection on finishing his course

## Key Themes
- Temptation as trial and testing strengthens faith
- God's faithfulness provides a way of escape from temptation
- The importance of remembrance and repetition of God's truths
- Fighting the good fight and finishing well
- Prayer as a powerful tool in spiritual warfare
- Staying the course despite life's distractions and pressures

## Notes

### The Three Aspects of Temptation in 1 Corinthians 10:13
Taylor emphasizes the "three butts" in this verse:
1. We're all in the same boat — temptation is common to all mankind, though not every temptation affects every person
2. God provides a way of escape
3. We must bear it with God's help

### Temptation as Trial (Genesis 22 / Hebrews 11)
Using Abraham's willingness to sacrifice Isaac, Taylor illustrates how temptation in the form of testing:
- Challenges our belief system and faith in God's promises
- Strengthens our faith when we hold firm
- Proves the reality of our commitment to God
- Works like an exercise program for the spirit

### Can Believers Be Tested in the Age of Grace?
Taylor affirms that yes, believers today experience trials and tests:
- Not necessarily dramatic commands like Abraham received, but obstacles and challenges
- Health crises, financial stress, family struggles all serve as tests of faith
- These trials prove whether our faith is genuine
- We recognize them without needing direct revelation

### Finishing the Course (2 Timothy 4)
Paul's exhortation emphasizes:
- Accountability before God at Christ's appearing
- Preaching truth and resisting false doctrine
- Enduring afflictions while continuing ministry
- Reflecting on past faithfulness
- Each believer has their own individual course to run
- Like physical exercise, spiritual life requires discipline and ongoing effort

### The Power of Prayer
Taylor stresses that prayer:
- Is not a "last resort" but a primary tool
- Allows us to speak directly to God about matters we cannot fix ourselves
- Leverages our existence and ministry effectiveness
- Demonstrates faith in God's power and willingness to help

### Personal Testimony
Taylor shares his own journey:
- Early years of spiritual meandering before committing to Scripture
- Meeting Tom Stanley, a security policeman who challenged him about occult practices
- The turning point when confronted with biblical teaching against spiritualism and reincarnation
- Years of growth in understanding and living according to God's Word
