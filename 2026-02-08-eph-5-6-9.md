## Summary
Pastor Simpson teaches believers about the practical application of separation from sin and the balance between walking in light and walking in love. He emphasizes that while Christians have eternal security, they can lose temporal rewards through disobedience, and we are responsible for proving what is acceptable to the Lord through our daily decisions.

## Scripture
Ephesians 5:6-10 — Paul instructs believers to avoid the works of the flesh and maintain a life of separation unto God, walking as children of light and proving what pleases the Lord.

## Key Themes
- **Spiritual Inheritance:** There exists an inheritance that cannot be lost (eternal salvation) and an inheritance that can be lost (temporal rewards)
- **Separation Principle:** Be not partakers with those who practice the works of the flesh; separation is unto God, not merely from the world
- **Walking in Light and Love:** Believers must balance intellectual knowledge (light) with passionate devotion (love)
- **Personal Responsibility:** Under grace, believers must prove what is acceptable to the Lord through informed decision-making based on biblical principles
- **Progressive Illumination:** Salvation is a one-time event, but believers experience ongoing growth in understanding and revelation

## Notes

### The Inheritance (Ephesians 5:5-6)
- There are two types of spiritual inheritance: one that cannot be lost (new body, rapture, eternal security) and one that can be lost (temporal rewards and position in the kingdom)
- The wrath of God mentioned in these passages may refer both to sowing and reaping consequences in grace and to judgment at the judgment seat of Christ
- God bought us with a price; rejection of Him after salvation is serious and has consequences

### Be Not Partakers (Ephesians 5:7)
- The command is to avoid participation in the "works of the flesh" listed in verse 5
- Living in a sin-cursed world means exposure to temptation is unavoidable, but exposure does not equal participation
- The difference between temptation and sin is choosing whether to "stick it in your mouth and chew it and swallow it"
- Separation is primarily unto God; as we separate ourselves to Him, separation from worldly sin naturally follows
- This principle requires daily application and decision-making

### Walking as Children of Light (Ephesians 5:8-9)
- Believers were once in darkness but are now light in the Lord
- There is both a salvation aspect (we saw the light at conversion) and an ongoing process of progressive illumination
- The fruit of the light is goodness, righteousness, and truth
- Believers must walk in both **light** (knowledge, understanding, soberness, seeing the whole picture) and **love** (passion, emotion, compassion)
- Walking in light alone creates legalism; walking in love alone creates emotionalism; both together create spiritual maturity
- The term "circumspectly" means 360-degree awareness—seeing the complete picture

### Proving What Is Acceptable (Ephesians 5:10)
- Under grace, the responsibility to prove what is acceptable to the Lord rests on the believer, not on fixed rules
- "Proving" means testing and discerning through biblical principles, not seeking a single proof verse for every decision
- Believers must gather biblical knowledge and create a mindset of understanding to guide decision-making
- The degree and application of separation must be proven by each believer in their specific circumstances
- Failure to prove these things indicates a lack of seriousness about one's inheritance and standing before the Lord
- Small compromises (like moldy food left in a refrigerator) gradually become larger problems; conversely, small acts of obedience build momentum
