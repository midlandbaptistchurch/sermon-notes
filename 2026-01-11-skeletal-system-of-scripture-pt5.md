## Summary
Pastor Payne continues examining the skeletal framework of Scripture by distinguishing between Genesis 1:1 (creation in eternity past) and Genesis 1:3 (beginning of the six-day creation). He demonstrates that Genesis 1:2 describes a scene of judgment between these two events, likely following Lucifer's rebellion and the destruction of the original angelic world.

## Scripture
Genesis 1:1-2 — The original creation of heaven and earth, followed by a scene of judgment. Colossians 1:9-10 — Paul's prayer for believers to be filled with knowledge of God's will and to increase in knowledge of God.

## Key Themes
- The gap between Genesis 1:1 and the six days of creation beginning in Genesis 1:3
- The word "and" representing variable lengths of time in Scripture
- Genesis 1:2 as a scene of judgment following Lucifer's rebellion
- The importance of being filled with knowledge of God's will
- Comparing Scripture with Scripture for proper interpretation

## Notes

### The Importance of Knowing God's Will
- Colossians 1:9-10 emphasizes being filled with knowledge of God's will through wisdom and spiritual understanding
- Believers should increase in knowledge of God, not just seek personal help
- We need to understand what God wills to accomplish, not just His will for us personally

### The Timeline of Creation
- Genesis 1:1 describes creation in eternity past - "In the beginning God created the heaven [singular] and the earth"
- The angelic world was created between the heaven and earth, as Job 38:4-7 shows angels rejoicing when earth's foundations were laid
- Lucifer ruled over this original angelic world on earth
- The six days of creation begin in Genesis 1:3 with "And God said" - each day marked by this phrase
- Genesis 2:1 mentions "heavens" (plural), showing expansion during the six days

### The Word "And" Represents Variable Time
- Every verse in Genesis 1 begins with "and" except verse 1 and part of verse 27
- The word "and" moves narrative forward but can represent any length of time
- Examples given: Genesis 3:21 to 4:1 (time between expulsion and conception), Genesis 4:1-2 (nine months between conception and birth, years until Abel becomes shepherd)
- Genesis 3:15 - the word "and" represents 2,000 years between first and second coming of Christ

### Genesis 1:2 as Judgment Scene
- "Without form and void" compared to Jeremiah 4:23-26, which describes judgment
- Jeremiah passage shows this phrase connected to God's "fierce anger" and divine judgment
- Genesis 1:2 describes the aftermath of judgment on the original angelic world
- Second Peter 3:5-6 references "the world that then was" being destroyed by water

### The Call to Spiritual Nobility
- Proverbs 25:2 - "It is the glory of God to conceal a thing, but the honor of kings to search out a matter"
- God conceals truth to test believers and make them spiritual nobility
- Believers should study Scripture comparing "spiritual with spiritual" (1 Corinthians 2:13)
- We will stand before our Creator and be held accountable for studying His creation account
