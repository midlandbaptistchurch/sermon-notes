## Summary
Pastor Jim Payne continues his series on the skeletal framework of Scripture, examining 2 Peter 3 to address scoffers who claim "all things continue as they were from the beginning of creation." He demonstrates how Scripture reveals multiple distinct "worlds" (systems of ideology and values with their associated peoples) that have existed throughout history, from the original creation through various judgments to the future new heaven and new earth.

## Scripture
2 Peter 3:1-13 — Peter's warning about last days scoffers and God's pattern of judgment through history.

## Key Themes
- The distinction between "earth" (physical planet) and "world" (ideological systems with their peoples)
- Multiple worlds throughout Scripture: the world that then was, the old world, this present evil world, the world to come, and the world without end
- God's pattern of cataclysmic judgments in response to rebellion
- The skeletal framework that gives Scripture its structure from beginning to end
- The original creation with angelic beings before mankind's creation

## Notes

### The Scoffers' Error (2 Peter 3:4-7)
- Scoffers claim uniformitarianism: "all things continue as they were from the beginning of creation"
- They willingly ignore that things have changed dramatically since Genesis 1:1
- Peter refutes this by showing the progression from "the heaven" to "the heavens" and from "the earth" to "the earth standing out of the water and in the water"
- The world that then was perished by flood, proving change has occurred

### Biblical Distinction: Earth vs. World
- **Earth**: The physical planet created by God
- **World**: A system of ideology, values, and the creatures/people who live by those systems
- "Things that are different are not the same" - these terms are used distinctly in Scripture
- The world incorporates people or creatures with their belief systems and economies

### The Multiple Worlds of Scripture
1. **The world that then was** (2 Peter 3:6) - The original world system that perished by flood
2. **The old world** (2 Peter 2:5) - Noah's world with different conditions (longer lifespans, no meat eating, different judicial system)
3. **This present evil world** (Galatians 1:4) - Our current world system from which Christ delivers believers
4. **The world to come** (Ephesians 1:21, Hebrews 2:5) - The millennial kingdom world
5. **World without end** (Isaiah 45:17, Ephesians 3:21) - The eternal state with new heaven and new earth

### The Original Creation Framework
- Genesis 1:1: "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth"
- The heaven is God's throne, the earth is His footstool (Isaiah 66:1)
- Angels (sons of God, morning stars) were present when God laid earth's foundations (Job 38:7)
- This establishes the skeletal framework for all subsequent Scripture
- Lucifer's rebellion and fall occurred in this original creation context

### God's Pattern of Judgment
- Lucifer's rebellion led to cataclysmic judgment
- The world that then was - destroyed by flood
- Noah's world - ended by another flood
- This present evil world - will end with fire at Christ's second coming
- Pattern shows God responds to systematic rebellion with world-changing judgment

### The Purpose of Human Creation
- Mankind was created after angelic rebellion
- God created a new species "after his own image" to replace the rebellious angels
- Unlike the fallen angels, God planned redemption for mankind
- The rebellious "sons of God" will "die like men" (Psalm 82:6-7)
