## Summary
Bill Baughman reflects on the theme of being "blessed" as the church sets spiritual goals for 2026. He emphasizes the rarity and value of sound biblical teaching, conducts a dispensational analysis of the word "blessed" across Scripture (time past, ages to come, and but now), and concludes that true blessing comes through faith in the gospel of Jesus Christ.

## Scripture
Ephesians 1:17-18 — Paul prays that Christians be filled with the knowledge of God's will and all wisdom; Psalm 21:6 — The psalmist speaks of the presence of God and fullness of joy; Romans 9:5 — Paul blesses God as Father of Christ; 1 Peter 1:3-4 — God blessed us again unto a lively hope through Christ's resurrection.

## Key Themes
- Spiritual assessment and goal-setting for 2026
- The value of sound, biblical teaching in a world of many churches
- Dispensational study of "blessed" throughout Scripture
- True blessing comes through faith in the gospel
- Gratitude for pastoral and spiritual leadership

## Notes

### Personal Reflection on Blessings
Baughman opens by sharing reflections on his winter break in Florida, including visits with old church friends (Fred and Nancy, Dennis and Jocelyn, Willie and Sharon) and observing the youth group serving. These experiences prompted him to count his blessings.

### Setting Spiritual Goals
Following Pastor Mark's outline from November, Baughman identifies seven spiritual goals for 2026:
- **Study**: Commit to listening to sermons multiple times and taking notes
- **Read**: Engage with Scripture daily (he and his wife are reading through Galatians and Ephesians)
- **Serve**: Dedicate time to serving others and assisting brothers and sisters
- **Pray**: Set aside dedicated prayer time
- **Be faithful**: Attend church services regularly
- **Give**: Support tithing and missions
- **Assist others**: Help fellow believers

Baughman calculates that dedicating 15-18% of one's life to God through these practices is a reasonable investment in response to His blessings.

### The Rarity of Sound Biblical Teaching
Baughman emphasizes the exceptional nature of receiving sound, biblical teaching. He notes:
- There are 20 churches within a 10-mile radius in his Florida visit area
- In Michigan alone, there are 9,614 churches
- When filtered by denomination and further by doctrinal clarity (teaching dispensational truth about time past, but now, and ages to come), very few churches remain
- Only about one in 2,000 churches ensures congregants might be filled with the knowledge of God's will in all wisdom and spiritual understanding

He expresses gratitude for Pastor Payne's teaching and the Sunday school teacher's instruction.

### Definition of "Blessed"
Baughman examines the 1828 Webster's definition: "made happy or prosperous or exalted." He illustrates this with biblical examples:
- Psalm 21:6 — The blessed man finds fullness of joy in God's presence
- Psalm 5:8 — The blessed man walks in God's paths

### Dispensational Analysis of "Blessed"
Baughman conducts a spiritual-to-spiritual comparison of the word "blessed" across three time periods:

**Time Past:**
- God blessed Adam and Eve
- God blessed Noah and his sons
- Abraham received the blessing that all nations would be blessed in him
- Isaac was blessed and enriched 100-fold
- Jacob and Esau were blessed by their father

**Ages to Come:**
- The same pattern continues—God blesses individuals
- Blessings are prophetically spoken of for the future age

**But Now (Church Age):**
- Ephesians 1:3 — "Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who hath blessed us with all spiritual blessings in heavenly places in Christ"
- This verse is unique in the New Testament—it's the only place where believers in the church age are explicitly called "blessed"
- Romans 4:7-8 — David speaks of those whose iniquities are forgiven and to whom the Lord will not impute sin
- 1 Timothy 1:11 — Blessed be God, the only wise potentate, King and Lord of lords

Baughman notes that God specifically wanted us to focus on Ephesians 1:3 as the defining blessing for the church age—our blessing is in heavenly places in Christ.

### True Definition of Being Blessed
Baughman contrasts worldly blessings (like a sailboat purchased through human effort) with spiritual blessing. He concludes:

True blessing is being joyful about having spiritual blessings in heavenly places in Christ more than anything else in the world. God can bless us with miracles and spiritual understanding, but the primary blessing for believers now is our position in Christ and knowledge of His will through the gospel.

### Closing
Baughman prays that the congregation will receive the pastor's words with open hearts, open ears, and open minds, trusting that every word of Scripture is true.
