## Summary
Pastor Jim Payne explores Ephesians 3, focusing on Paul's revelation of the mystery of the church as the body of Christ—something hidden before the foundation of the world but now revealed to believers through the Holy Spirit. He emphasizes that spiritual maturity requires understanding the deep things of God and living with our affections set on heavenly things rather than earthly pursuits.

## Scripture
Ephesians 3:1-21 — Paul describes his revelation of the mystery and prays for believers to be strengthened in their inner man so that Christ may dwell in their hearts.

## Key Themes
- The mystery: The revelation that Gentiles are fellow heirs and members of the body of Christ
- The dispensation of grace: God's unique economy for this age given through Paul
- Spiritual maturity: Growing from milk (basic doctrine) to meat (deep things of God)
- The inner man: Being strengthened by the Holy Spirit dwelling and ruling in our hearts
- Heavenly focus: Setting our affections on things above rather than earthly things
- The hidden wisdom: God's plan ordained before the world, now revealed to believers

## Notes

### The Mystery Revealed (Ephesians 3:1-12)
- Paul was imprisoned so God could isolate him and give him fuller revelation of the mystery for the church
- The mystery is that Gentiles are now fellow heirs and members of the same body—the body of Christ
- This is uniquely Pauline doctrine; the body of Christ is found only in Paul's writings
- Many believers today do not understand this mystery, mistakenly thinking the church is described in the gospels or Hebrews

### Paul's Prayer for Believers (Ephesians 3:13-17)
- Paul encourages believers not to faint at his tribulations, which he endures for their glory
- He prays that God would grant them according to the riches of His glory
- Believers will be given new bodies and taken to heaven as part of the riches of God's glory (1 Thessalonians 4)
- Our affections should be set on heavenly things, not earthly possessions (Colossians 3:1-4)
- We must reckon ourselves dead to sin daily and live as new creatures in Christ

### The Holy Spirit and Spiritual Growth (1 Corinthians 2)
- The Holy Spirit reveals the deep things of God that are hidden from natural man
- As believers, we have the mind of Christ through the indwelling Spirit
- The natural man cannot receive the things of God because they are spiritually discerned
- Spiritual maturity comes from comparing spiritual things with spiritual things throughout Scripture
- Growth from spiritual infancy (milk) to maturity (meat) is a lifelong pursuit

### The Hidden Wisdom Ordained Before the World (1 Corinthians 2:6-8)
- God ordained the mystery before the foundation of the world
- The princes of this world did not know this wisdom, which is why they crucified the Lord of glory
- Had Satan and the rulers of this age known Christ's death would lead to His resurrection and the establishment of the church, they would not have crucified Him
- The cross appeared to be their victory, but it became their defeat

### Practical Application
- Believers must be yielded to the Holy Spirit so He can dwell comfortably in us (not just reside, but be president)
- Understanding the deep things of God brings comfort to the Holy Spirit dwelling within us
- Daily discipline involves reckoning ourselves dead to sin and living according to our new nature
- Spiritual discernment comes from growing in the knowledge of Scripture and the deep things of God
