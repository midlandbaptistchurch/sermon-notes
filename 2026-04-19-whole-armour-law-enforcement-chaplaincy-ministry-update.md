## Summary
Missionary John Moe, longtime friend of Midland Baptist Church and integrated law enforcement chaplain serving in Clinton County, Michigan, presented a ministry update on Whole Armour Law Enforcement Chaplaincy. He described how God called him from a business background into chaplaincy roles spanning multiple agencies — including a township police department, the county sheriff's office, and a SWAT team — and shared specific accounts of gospel ministry, critical incident debriefings, and pastoral care among first responders. His wife Sue also shared her role on the county Victim Support Team (VST), illustrating how their two ministries work together to bring the gospel and comfort to officers, deputies, and families in crisis.

## Scripture
Romans 13:1-6 — John Moe focused especially on verse 6, highlighting the phrase "attending continually" as a description of the unrelenting burden borne by law enforcement officers and first responders, and connecting that calling to God's ordained authority structure.

## Key Themes
- The divine calling and authority of law enforcement (Romans 13)
- Integrated vs. ceremonial chaplaincy — ministry from the inside out
- Building trust as a foundation for gospel witness
- The weight of "attending continually" — the toll on first responders
- Justification by faith in Christ alone vs. works-based religion
- Connecting the called to the one who called them
- Husband-and-wife ministry partnership
- Critical incident stress debriefing and pastoral care
- Prayer and financial support needs for the chaplaincy

## Notes
- **John Moe's background:** Lifelong businessman with an agricultural business degree; wrote the Michigan State Police civil service exam in 1989 but took a civilian career path. Began civilian ride-alongs with a township agency in 2010, accumulating ~300 hours in patrol cars before becoming a sworn reserve officer in 2013.
- **Chaplaincy call:** Invited to become chaplain by a sergeant in 2013–2014; initially declined out of humility but sensed a clear call from God within the week.
- **Training and roles:** Attended a police academy and public services chaplain academy; appointed to county SWAT team as chaplain/quartermaster in 2017; completed basic SWAT school in 2022; began serving Clinton County Sheriff's Office in 2023; became a fully deputized Marine Patrol deputy in 2025. Also runs the sheriff's office Peer Assistance Team.
- **Agencies served:** Bath Township Police Department, Clinton County Sheriff's Office, Clinton County Special Operations (SWAT) Team, plus three or four additional police agencies, two fire departments, Clinton County Area Ambulance Service, and County 911 dispatch — over 350 individuals across these organizations.
- **Sue Moe / VST:** Sue serves as a volunteer on the Clinton County Victim Support Advocates Team, responding to scenes of completed suicides, accidental deaths, and other critical incidents. Her motivation came after her nephew died of a fentanyl overdose in Daytona Beach four years prior. On one scene involving a grandfather and five-year-old lost through ice, Sue led the entire room — including the sheriff, a sergeant, a deputy, and grieving family — in prayer at the family's request.
- **Bible study:** Conducted an eight-week Bible study for county personnel (January–mid-March of the ministry year); approximately eight deputies attended. Topics included the authority of Scripture, the story of Gideon, the divine calling of law enforcement, and a clear gospel presentation on the final night. A deputy who had attended church his whole life said he had never heard that justification is entirely Christ's work at the cross. Another deputy wrestled with the idea that his grandparents' murderer could be forgiven — John gave a clear, honest gospel response while acknowledging the deputy's pain. Four of the Bible study attendees later came to a Friend Day service at Solid Rock Baptist Church.
- **Integrated chaplaincy model:** Distinguished from "ceremonial" chaplaincy (a pastor listed on an org chart who may rarely engage). John's sworn status and embedded roles give him organic access and earned trust, enabling one-on-one ministry that a purely external chaplain could not achieve.
- **Critical Incident Stress Debriefing (CISD):** Certified debriefer through the International Critical Incident Stress Foundation (ICISF). Conducted a debrief for the SWAT team following the Michigan State University active shooter event. Has conducted multiple debriefs including after a plane crash in Bath Township (three fatalities, nearly on his property) and after a drunk-driver incident that killed two members of a family of sixteen.
- **Funeral ministry:** Conducted a standing-room-only funeral (~150 attendees) for a firefighter's son, a veteran who died by suicide. Despite the non-church family's choice of opening music, John delivered a clear gospel presentation to all in attendance.
- **Officer-involved shootings:** Three since becoming county chaplain; responds to scene once secure, escorts officers away to decompress.
- **Personal evangelism moments:** A deputy called John to report he had pulled over and accepted Christ while driving; John and Sue were present at his baptism that same morning as the ministry update was given.
- **Romans 13 application:** Verse 6's phrase "attending continually" describes law enforcement's unrelenting vigilance — responding to people's worst days regardless of weather, holidays, personal circumstances, or national crises. Less than 0.69% of the U.S. population serves as first responders (full-time, paid-on-call, and volunteer), yet they attend to the worst moments of the other 99.3%.
- **Ministry purpose statement:** "The job of a police officer/deputy is most often a calling. The job of a cop chaplain is to connect the called to the one who did the calling."
- **Prayer and support needs:** Seeking prayer partners; partial compensation now received from township (~15 hrs/week, community relations role) and part-time Marine Deputy role. Turned down a VP business development offer and a full-time police role to remain focused on chaplaincy calling. Urgently seeking additional cop chaplains to serve growing need — has been asked by a neighboring county agency to serve but has no capacity. Prayer card available at the visitors desk.
