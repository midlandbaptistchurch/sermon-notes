## Summary
John Moe, a former member of Midland Baptist Church and founder of the Whole Armour Law Enforcement Chaplaincy Ministry, shares an update on his integrated chaplaincy work in Clinton County, Michigan. He describes how God called him from a business career into law enforcement chaplaincy and how his ministry — alongside his wife Sue's volunteer work with the Clinton County Victim Support Advocates — brings the gospel and pastoral care directly into the law enforcement community. Drawing on Romans 13, John challenges the congregation to appreciate the unrelenting sacrifice of first responders and to pray for those who attend continually upon the needs of others.

## Scripture
Romans 13:1-6 — John focuses particularly on verse 6, which describes those in civil authority as "God's ministers, attending continually upon this very thing," connecting the divine calling of law enforcement officers to the God who authorized their role.

## Key Themes
- Integrated vs. ceremonial chaplaincy — being present from the inside out rather than merely holding a title
- Building trust with law enforcement officers as a prerequisite for gospel ministry
- The divine calling of law enforcement as rooted in Romans 13
- Sharing the gospel clearly, including in Bible studies, funerals, and crisis situations
- The husband-wife ministry partnership between John (chaplaincy) and Sue (victim support advocacy)
- The unrelenting weight carried by the 0.69% of the U.S. population who serve as first responders
- Connecting the called to the One who called them

## Notes
- John and Sue Moe are former longtime members of Midland Baptist Church who moved to Lansing in 2004.
- John's background includes a business career, 300+ hours of civilian ride-alongs beginning in 2010, becoming a sworn reserve officer in 2013, and being appointed chaplain in 2014.
- He serves Bath Township Police Department, Clinton County Sheriff's Office, and the Clinton County Special Operations (SWAT) Team as primary responsibilities, with additional ministry to several other agencies, two fire departments, Clinton County Area Ambulance Service, and County 911 Dispatch.
- He is a certified debriefer for Critical Incident Stress Management (ICISF) and SWAT-certified operator/chaplain/quartermaster.
- In 2025, he became a fully deputized Marine Deputy with the Sheriff's Office.
- He led an eight-week Bible study for county deputies in early 2026, culminating in a clear gospel presentation; four of those deputies attended Friend Day at Solid Rock Baptist Church.
- A deputy named Ryan pulled over on the road and accepted Christ; John and Sue witnessed his baptism the morning of this presentation.
- Sue serves as a volunteer with the Clinton County Victim Support Team (VST), responding to critical incidents alongside John's chaplaincy work.
- John described conducting a critical incident stress debrief after the MSU active shooter event, where his SWAT team was first tactical boots on ground.
- He described other incidents: a grandfather and five-year-old lost through pond ice, a veteran's death by suicide, a family of 16 struck by a drunk driver, and three officer-involved shootings.
- John led prayer for approximately 75 officers of multiple agencies before a coordinated arrest operation related to a cold-case hunter murder in Bath Township.
- He conducted a funeral service for a veteran and son of a firefighter, delivering a clear gospel presentation to a mixed and unchurched crowd.
- John noted frustration with the International Conference of Police Chaplains' discouragement of explicitly sharing one's faith, and expressed interest in the Christ-centered organization Heroic Deeds based in Ohio.
- Ministry needs highlighted: financial support (he turned down a VP role and a full-time police position to remain available), prayer for sustainability, and prayer for Bible-believing individuals called to law enforcement chaplaincy to come alongside him.
- Midland Baptist Church is noted as one of the first supporting churches for the Whole Armour ministry.
- Prayer cards for John and Sue's ministry are available at the visitor's desk.
