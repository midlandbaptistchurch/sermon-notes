## Summary
Pastor Jim continues his series exploring the "skeletal system" of biblical truth, focusing on how Genesis 1 describes God's response to Lucifer's rebellion rather than the original creation. The six days of creation represent God's restoration and judgment following the catastrophic fall of the angelic world under Lucifer's leadership.

## Scripture
Genesis 1:1-20 — The creation account showing God's division of waters and creation of the firmament.
2 Peter 3:3-13 — Peter's response to scoffers regarding God's past and future judgments.

## Key Themes
- Lucifer's original reign and subsequent rebellion in the angelic world
- The firmament created as a result of divine judgment, not natural process
- Three heavens: atmospheric, stellar firmament, and God's throne realm
- Humanity created as sons of God to replace the fallen angelic order
- The lake of fire prepared for the devil and his angels

## Notes

### The Skeletal System Framework
- Scripture has a "skeletal system" of truth running from beginning to end
- Like physical bones that give structure and support, biblical truth provides framework
- This series builds understanding through review and additional information

### Review of 2 Peter 3: Timeline of Worlds
- Scoffers claim nothing has changed since creation (v. 4)
- Peter corrects them: things have changed dramatically
- "The world that then was" - an angelic world that perished by water (v. 6)
- Current heavens and earth reserved for fire judgment (v. 7)
- Future new heaven and new earth (v. 13)

### Key Questions About Lucifer
1. When was Lucifer created, when did he reign as king, and when did he fall?
2. Would not God bring cataclysmic judgment on Lucifer's rebellion as He did with other rebellions?

### Lucifer's Identity and Fall
- Lucifer means "light bearer" - found only in Isaiah 14:12
- Was the "anointed cherub" who covered God's throne
- Reigned as supreme being over the angelic world
- Rebelled through pride, leading 1/3 of angels in insurrection
- Became Satan, the devil, the serpent after his fall

### Four Results of Lucifer's Rebellion

#### 1. The Firmament Created
- Genesis 1:1: "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth"
- Originally heaven (God's throne) and earth were in same realm
- After rebellion, God divided waters with firmament (universe)
- Earth pushed into the deep, became "without form and void"
- Firmament separates God's throne from fallen earth

#### 2. The Lake of Fire Prepared
- Matthew 25:41 - everlasting fire "prepared for the devil and his angels"
- Created as eternal judgment for Lucifer's rebellion
- Located in southern chambers beneath the earth and waters

#### 3. Three Heavens Established
- First heaven: atmosphere where birds fly
- Second heaven: firmament with sun, moon, stars
- Third heaven: God's throne above the waters
- Paul was caught up to third heaven (2 Cor. 12:2)

#### 4. Humanity Created as Replacement
- Angels were original "sons of God" as direct divine creations
- Only Adam called "son of God" in Old Testament (Luke 3:38)
- After fall, humans born in Adam's likeness, not God's
- Through Christ, believers become "sons of God" (John 1:12)
- Created to replace fallen angelic order and inherit their positions
- Man given dominion that Lucifer forfeited

### The Purpose of Man
- Made "a little lower than the angels" but redeemable
- Created in God's image as special divine workmanship
- Told to "replenish" the earth - implying prior occupancy
- Destined to rule over principalities and powers
- Will inherit positions in heavenly realms
