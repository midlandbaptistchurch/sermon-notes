## Summary
Pat Boulton teaches that true Christian joy comes from making Christ our confidant and gaining confidence in His work in our lives. Using personal testimonies from recent funerals and family experiences, he illustrates how understanding that "he which hath begun a good work in you will perform it" (Philippians 1:6) produces joy regardless of circumstances. The sermon emphasizes the difference between reading "somebody else's mail" (gospels written to Israel) versus claiming promises written to us through Paul, and calls believers to surrender completely to Christ as their personal confidant.

## Scripture
Philippians 1:4-6 — Paul writes about making requests with joy for the Philippians' fellowship in the gospel, being confident that God will complete the good work He began in them until the day of Jesus Christ.

Mark 11:24, 26 — Jesus teaches about prayer and forgiveness, illustrating how these verses are written to Israel under a different dispensation, not directly to the church.

Isaiah 30:18 — The Lord waits to be gracious and show mercy; blessed are all who wait for Him, describing both God waiting on us and us waiting on God.

## Key Themes
- **Confidence in God's Work**: True joy comes from being confident that God will complete what He started in believers' lives
- **Rightly Dividing Scripture**: Understanding the difference between reading "somebody else's mail" versus promises written to us through Paul the apostle to the Gentiles
- **Gains vs. Losses**: Godliness with contentment is great gain (1 Timothy 6:6); accumulating spiritual gains through obedience produces joy
- **Christ as Confidant**: Making Christ our personal confidant rather than relying solely on earthly advisors brings lasting joy
- **Waiting on the Lord**: Both God waiting to bless us and us actively serving while waiting on Him

## Notes

### Personal Testimonies of Confidence and Joy

**Geno Brimmer's Funeral**
- Geno was a Navy veteran (E-5 on SS Saratoga), 100% disabled, faithful Christian who never married
- When told Geno was dying, Bolton prayed he would die quickly since he was ready for heaven
- Geno died 8 hours after the prayer
- At the funeral, a 45-year-old woman approached Bolton saying she now understood and would see Geno again—indicating salvation
- Bolton experienced joy because he had confidence God was directing him

**Michael Paul's Death**
- Bolton's nephew died suddenly at age 50 from heart issues
- 38 years earlier, when Michael was 12 and Bolton's father was dying, Bolton led Michael to salvation
- Bolton has confidence in that childhood salvation decision
- Daughter Amy immediately drove to care for her aunt Kathy (Michael's mother)
- Bolton will speak at the memorial service about the Lord, not just football days

### Understanding Our Mail vs. Their Mail

**Mark 11 Example**
- Mark 11:24: "What things soever ye desire, when ye pray, believe that ye shall receive them, and ye shall have them"
- This doesn't work for church age believers because it's written to Israel
- Mark 11:26: Forgiveness based on our forgiving others—not our dispensation
- Brother Payne taught in 1992 that much of the Bible is for us to learn from but not written to us
- Jack Barber said of Payne: "He might be a hair tick, but it's ticking right along. We need to listen."

**Reading Bolton's Father's Letters**
- Bolton's father Joseph wrote letters to Barbara (Bolton's mother) during WWII
- Bolton learned about his father from reading those letters after his father's death
- But the promises Joseph made to Barbara were not Bolton's promises—he was reading somebody else's mail
- Same principle applies to reading gospels written under different dispensation

### The Key to Joy: Confidence

**Philippians 1:6**
- "Being confident of this very thing, that he which hath begun a good work in you will perform it until the day of Jesus Christ"
- This verse should be underlined, circled, and read often
- We shouldn't have confidence in the flesh, but confidence in Christ
- The word "confident" or "confidence" appears throughout Philippians (verses 14, 25)

**What Produces Gains**
- 1 Timothy 6:6: "Godliness with contentment is great gain"
- When God shows you something, you get it, and you practice it—that's a gain
- Don't want to be the biggest loser, want to be the biggest gainer
- Gains produce joy because you see God working and directing
- Experience at Geno's funeral was a gain—Bolton knew God was leading and saw fruit

### Christ as Your Confidant

**The Problem with Human Confidants**
- To get help from earthly confidants, you must explain yourself, win them over, determine if they have resources to help
- Example: John (doctor at Moffat's Cancer Center) can be confidant on medical issues
- Bolton calls him "the smart guy" who helped explain Don Perkins' serious condition

**Christ as Perfect Confidant**
- With Christ, you don't have to explain—He already knows where you're at
- Don't have to wait your turn or build rapport
- When Bonnie was going through cancer, didn't have to wait to talk to Christ
- He's ready when you're ready
- If Christ doesn't become your confidant, you'll never have the joy being discussed

**Casting Burdens**
- "Cast thy burden upon the Lord and he shall sustain thee"
- Go ahead and cast it over on His side—He can carry it
- When you do this, that's a gain
- When you're a gainer, you can tell others how you've had gains, bringing joy because you know you're not in charge

### Blessed Are They That Wait

**Isaiah 30:18**
- "And therefore will the Lord wait, that he might be gracious unto you, and therefore will he be exalted, that he may have mercy upon you: for the Lord is a God of judgment: blessed are all they that wait for him"

**Two Positions**
- Picture Christ standing before us as we make daily decisions about surrender, gaining, confidence
- He's watching and waiting—He's the blesser who doles out blessings
- Sometimes He's standing with hands on hips waiting on stubborn believers
- The word "wait" doesn't mean standing idle in corner
- It indicates a waiter—busy in the Lord's work, doing what you're supposed to, ministering
- Either He's waiting on you, or you're waiting on Him

### Application Points

**Make Christ Your Confidant**
- God, I need you to be my confidant
- I don't know how to handle this
- I'm way out of control, don't have answers
- Please guide me, direct me
- Lord, my grandchildren are living in the wrong direction and I need you to help me do something
- Lord, take charge of their life
