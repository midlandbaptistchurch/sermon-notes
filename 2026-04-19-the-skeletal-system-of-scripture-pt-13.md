## Summary
Pastor Payne explains the structure of God's creation, focusing on the firmament and the division of waters. He describes how God created the heavens as a throne, the angelic realm, and how Lucifer's rebellion led to judgment and the formation of the universe as we know it today.

## Scripture
Genesis 1:1-13 — The account of creation's first three days, establishing light, the firmament, and dry land.

## Key Themes
- The nature of divine light and God's glory
- The structure of the firmament and the universe
- Lucifer's rebellion and subsequent judgment
- The division of waters above and below the firmament
- God's throne in the heavens and the crystalline sea
- The tabernacle as a picture of heavenly realities

## Notes
- In the beginning, God created the heaven (singular) as His throne and the earth as His footstool
- The angelic world was created before the earth's foundations were laid
- Lucifer's rebellion led to God's judgment, covering the earth with the deep
- The firmament was created by dividing the waters above from the waters below
- The universe is structured like a tabernacle - rectangular with God's throne at the top
- The darkness in space came from the face of the deep when God separated light
- Satan now rules as prince of the power of the air in the firmament
- The waters above the firmament are frozen like crystalline glass
- God's throne sits above this crystalline sea in the New Jerusalem
- Believers will one day pass through these waters to enter God's presence
