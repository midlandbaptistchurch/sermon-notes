## Summary
In Part 4 of his series on the simplicity that is in Christ, Pastor Jim Payne addresses the simplicity of Bible study, arguing that God intends His Word to be understood rather than feared or avoided. Drawing from 2 Timothy 2:15, he teaches that the key to unlocking the Bible is rightly dividing it into three time periods — "time past" (Old Testament and the Gospels), "but now" (Paul's epistles, Romans through Philemon), and "ages to come" (Hebrews through Revelation) — and that believers today are primarily addressed in Paul's letters written to the Gentile body of Christ.

## Scripture
2 Corinthians 11:1-3 — Paul's foundational warning against having one's mind corrupted from the simplicity that is in Christ, used as the anchor text for the sermon series. 2 Timothy 2:15 — "Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth," the central command around which the message is built.

## Key Themes
- The simplicity of Bible study as God's intention for believers
- Rightly dividing the Word of Truth (2 Timothy 2:15)
- Three time periods: "time past," "but now," and "ages to come"
- The Old Testament and Gospels as written to and about Israel
- Paul's epistles (Romans–Philemon) as the primary scripture for the body of Christ today
- God is not the author of confusion; understanding is available to believers
- Laziness and fear as the two main reasons Christians fail to study

## Notes
**Two Reasons Christians Fail to Study the Bible**
- Laziness and spiritual indifference — making excuses and lacking discipline
- Frustration and fear — feeling the Bible is impossible to understand

**What God Gave Believers at Salvation**
1. Eternal life
2. The Holy Spirit
3. The Bible — described as a book of explanation, information, instruction, and survival

**The Command to Study — 2 Timothy 2:15**
- "Study" is an imperative, not a suggestion
- The word "study" appears only three times in the Bible
- The goal: to be a workman approved unto God, not ashamed
- The method: rightly divide the word of truth — to "cut straight"

**God is Not the Author of Confusion (1 Corinthians 14:33)**
- Understanding is available and promised to believers
- Proverbs 4:7 — get wisdom, and with it get understanding
- 2 Timothy 2:7 — the Lord gives understanding to those who consider His Word
- Colossians 1:9 — God desires believers to be filled with wisdom and spiritual understanding

**Rightly Dividing — Three Time Periods (Ephesians 2:11-13)**
- *Time Past* — Old Testament (Genesis–Malachi): God's dealings with Israel; the law, covenants, and promises made to the Jewish nation
- *Time Past* continued — The Gospels (Matthew, Mark, Luke, John): Jesus came as a minister of the circumcision (Romans 15:8) to confirm promises to the fathers; sent first to the lost sheep of Israel (Matthew 10:5-6; Matthew 1:1)
- *But Now* — Paul's Epistles (Romans–Philemon): The dispensation of grace; God's revelation for the Gentile body of Christ today; the "mystery" hidden in previous ages (Colossians 1:25-26); Paul identified as the apostle of the Gentiles (Romans 11:13; Ephesians 3:1)
- *Ages to Come* — Hebrews through Revelation: Future kingdom age, tribulation period, millennial reign, and eternal state

**Practical Examples of Rightly Dividing**
- Dietary laws (Leviticus 11) — not binding on believers today; 1 Timothy 4:4 and Colossians 2:16 clarify freedom under grace
- Sabbath (Leviticus 23:3) vs. first-day worship (1 Corinthians 16:2; Acts 20:7) — the church gathers on the first day of the week, not the seventh-day Sabbath given to Israel
- Joy vs. mourning — Philippians 4:4 instructs believers to "rejoice alway"; James 4:8-9 (written to the twelve tribes) instructs mourning — no contradiction when books are rightly divided
- James is addressed to the twelve tribes scattered (James 1:1), placing it in the "ages to come" context of the tribulation period, not the present body of Christ

**Assurance for Believers**
- Romans 8:38-39 — nothing can separate the believer from the love of God in Christ Jesus
- God loves the believer as He loves His Son, because the believer is in His Son

**Closing Application**
- Pastor Payne called the congregation to admit failures in personal Bible study
- Encouraged believers to approach the Bible without fear, understanding that rightly dividing it makes it simple
- Primary exhortation: spend time in Romans through Philemon — written to and for the body of Christ today
