## Summary
This message examines the command to "stand against the wiles of the devil" in Ephesians 6:11. Pastor Simpson explains that Satan's strategy is subtle—he attacks believers from behind through lies, deceit, and corruption of truth. The devil's goal is to make God look bad through His creation by causing Christians to bring shame rather than glory to God. Victory comes not through our own intelligence but through holding firmly to truth, as Satan cannot overcome truth even though he is more subtle and smarter than us.

## Scripture
Ephesians 6:10-14 — Paul instructs believers to be strong in the Lord's power, put on the whole armor of God to stand against the devil's wiles, and recognize that our wrestling is not against flesh and blood but against spiritual powers of darkness.

## Key Themes
- **Satan's Subtle Strategy**: The devil attacks from behind, not frontally, using lies, deceit, and corruption to slowly move believers off their position without them realizing they're under attack.
- **The Goal of Satan**: Satan's ultimate desire is to pervert truth so much that God would be ashamed of believers' actions and lives, using Christians to embarrass their Creator.
- **Truth as Our Weapon**: While Satan is more subtle and smarter than us individually, he cannot overcome truth—making consistent study and application of God's Word our primary defense.
- **Heavenly Accusation**: Satan accuses believers before God day and night (Revelation 12:10), making our lives a topic of discussion in heavenly places and increasing the importance of representing God well.

### The Wiles of the Devil

#### Satan's Methods
- He is a liar and the father of lies
- Uses deceit to make false things look true
- Inserts truth into lies and lies into truth
- Example: Genesis 3 with Eve and Adam, who chose to believe Satan over God despite being closer to creation
- Most Christians have no idea when they are under attack because Satan does not want them to know

#### Modern Idolatry
- Old Testament Israel's primary sin was idolatry
- Contemporary idolatry exists but in forms believers don't recognize
- Idolatry involves: bowing down, giving things to, serving, thinking about, collectively gathering around something
- Example: Temporal success—making this life's accomplishments the priority rather than preparing for eternity
- The principle: "We live this life in preparation for the life to come because the life to come is more important than this one"

#### Confusion Over Scripture
- Satan uses text without context to confuse believers
- Example: Misunderstanding wealth—taking Matthew, Mark, Luke, John passages about the tribulation period and applying them to church age believers
- The rich young ruler, rich man and Lazarus, and the man who tore down his barns were warnings for people going into the tribulation, not instructions against retirement planning
- Philippians 4:13 "I can do all things through Christ" is taken out of context—it's about contentment in giving and finances, not unlimited power
- Paul's statement means knowing "how to be abased and how to abound" in different financial circumstances

#### The Battle for God's Word
- Two battles: text (one true Bible) and context (reading what's written to us versus what's not)
- Many churches and religions base beliefs on the Bible but take it out of context
- Learning to distinguish literal from figurative, what's written to us from what's not written to us

### Satan's Weapons

#### Fear, Doubt, and Worry
- These lead to assumptions
- Result in lack of clarity
- Without clarity, there's no confidence
- Without confidence from clarity, people become confused
- Confusion leads to going through motions or doing what feels right rather than what is right

#### False Portrayal
- Claiming something to be true that is not, and vice versa
- People confused about money, finances, priorities
- Don't understand their own purpose
- Ask "Should I do this or that? Is this okay?" but have no principles to discern

### The Reality of Spiritual Warfare

#### Job's Example
- Angels met with God and Satan joined the meeting (Job 1-2)
- God asked Satan what he'd been doing: "Walking to and fro amongst the earth"
- God initiated: "Have you considered my servant Job?"
- God allowed Satan to take Job's possessions and health but not kill him
- Shows that discussions about believers occur in heavenly places

#### Revelation 12:10
- Describes the war in heaven at the three and a half year point of the tribulation
- "The accuser of our brethren is cast down, which accused them before our God day and night"
- Satan currently accuses God's people continuously before God
- Says "Look at your creation. Look at what's going on. Look at this one, look at that one"
- Believers' lives are topics in heavenly places
- This should motivate us to represent God well and not bring Him shame

### The Solution: Truth

#### Strength Through God's Word
- Strength comes from greater influence of the word of God than we presently have
- More truth leads to seeing what's happening in our own lives and around us
- Helps identify when priorities are messed up, when people don't understand their purpose

#### Discernment Between Good and Evil
- Solomon's request at Mount Gibeon: "Give me the ability to discern between good and evil that I might judge so great a people"
- Discernment comes from clear, concise understanding of God's Word
- Recognizing idolatry in our own lives and in others requires this understanding

#### The Counterfeit Bill Illustration
- Cashiers aren't trained by studying counterfeit bills
- They study genuine bills thoroughly
- When they know the genuine well, they spot the counterfeit immediately
- Similarly, studying what is true and genuine in Scripture enables recognition of false teaching
- Satan cannot do anything against truth because all he can do is lie

### Practical Application

#### Putting on the Armor
- Not just making decisions but becoming those decisions
- Must go from mind to heart to soul
- Becoming who you are, not just doing certain things
- We are Christians because of who we are, not just what we do

#### Personal Responsibility
- Can't rely on pastor's study alone
- Must have personal, consistent engagement with Scripture
- Need steady diet of truth so it becomes a greater influence than anything the world offers

#### Young People's Experience
- Example of Lincoln in Pensacola encountering false doctrine
- He knew what he believed but needed confirmation when challenged
- Did what was right: asked questions, confirmed answers, proved what he knew
- Didn't argue with those who didn't want truth
- Some people you don't waste words with if they don't want to learn

#### The Importance of Becoming Truth
- Satan enjoys Christians who go through motions
- Satan loves Christians who go to church and don't get anything out of it
- Those who are one way at church and another way elsewhere
- More concerned with what man thinks than what God thinks
