## Summary
Pastor Jim continues his study on rightly dividing Scripture by examining the transition between "time past" (Old Testament) and "but now" (Church Age). He traces God's covenantal promises to Abraham and David, showing how the Old Testament prophets consistently pointed to a coming kingdom. The sermon emphasizes that understanding these "time zones" in Scripture is essential for proper Bible interpretation and spiritual growth, helping believers avoid confusion and see how Christ came as a minister to the circumcision to confirm promises made to the fathers.

## Scripture
2 Samuel 7:13-16 — God promises David an eternal kingdom and throne through his seed, establishing the Davidic covenant that prophets would later reference throughout Israel's history.

Romans 15:8 — Jesus Christ was a minister of the circumcision for the truth of God, to confirm the promises made unto the fathers.

## Key Themes
- **Time Zones in Scripture**: The Bible divides into "time past" (Old Testament), "but now" (Church Age), and "ages to come" (future prophecy), requiring proper division for understanding
- **The Davidic Kingdom Promise**: God made unconditional covenants with Abraham (seed and land) and David (eternal kingdom and throne) that permeate Old Testament prophecy
- **Ministry of Reconciliation**: Today's commission includes both getting people saved and teaching them to rightly divide the Word, understanding the mystery of the body of Christ
- **Israel's Unique Position**: Israel was severed from all nations, given the sign of circumcision, and made God's inheritance and lot, distinct from the Gentile world

## Notes

### Introduction: The Commission Today
- Our commission is the Ministry of Reconciliation and making all men see the fellowship of the mystery
- 1 Timothy 2:4 - God will have all men saved and come into the knowledge of truth
- Cannot grow without learning and knowing the Bible rightly divided
- If the Bible is confusing, it's not God's fault - God is not the author of confusion
- Must divide the Bible: time past, but now, ages to come

### Two Gospels
- Galatians 2:7 references the gospel of the circumcision and gospel of the uncircumcision
- Gospel of circumcision involves cutting; gospel of uncircumcision has no cutting required
- Understanding these differences is key to rightly dividing Scripture

### Time Past: The Old Testament Foundation
- Old Testament covers approximately 4,000 years
- Two subjects: Gentiles and Jews (no body of Christ in view)
- Body of Christ was a mystery - not discussed in Old Testament

### God's Dealing with Gentiles (Genesis 1-11)
- First 11 chapters cover 2,000 years with Gentile world
- Three rebellions occurred:
  - Adam and Eve
  - Sons of God marrying daughters of men (Genesis 6), leading to Noah's flood
  - Tower of Babel with Nimrod (Genesis 10-11)
- God scattered nations and divided Gentile world into 70 nations
- God disinherited them, giving them over to a reprobate mind
- Placed cosmic powers and authorities over the nations

### God's Calling of Abraham
- God called Abram (High Father) out of all nations
- Genesis 12, 13, 15, 17 develop God's relationship with Abram
- Abram becomes Abraham (father of multitude/father of many)
- God promised to make Abraham father of a nation more than sand of sea or stars of sky
- Abraham's descendancy includes Ishmael

### Israel Made Unique
- God separated Abraham's seed from all nations
- They would not be counted among nations
- They became God's lot and inheritance
- Sign of circumcision was the token making them people of promise

### The Abrahamic Covenant
- God promised: seed, land, blessing
- Unconditional covenant (legal binding contract)
- Circumcision united them to God as miracle-working God
- Abraham tried to help God through Hagar (bringing promised seed through handmaid)
- God said He didn't need help

### Covenant Passed to Isaac and Jacob
- Genesis 26: Covenant passed to Isaac
- Genesis 28: Covenant reconfirmed with Jacob
- Rest of Genesis (28 onward): Tale of Jacob marrying Leah and Rachel
- Twelve sons developed to become nation of Israel and establish government on earth

### The Pentateuch and Israel's Identity
- Moses wrote Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy
- Leviticus 20: God severed Israel from all nations
- Numbers 23: God does not number Israel among nations
- Deuteronomy 32: Israel is God's inheritance and lot
- Israel is unique, distinct, separated, sanctified people unto God

### Joshua and the Promised Land
- Joshua led Israel into land promised to Abraham
- Israel went whoring after other gods instead of showing fidelity
- Law was added because of transgression
- First commandment: "Thou shalt have no other gods before me"
- Israel chased after gods appointed over seventy Gentile nations (under scope of Baal)

### The Period of Judges
- Joshua challenged: "Choose you this day whom you will serve"
- Book of Judges: 490 years of repeated cycle
- Israel repeatedly went after gods of surrounding nations
- God judged and chastised them over and over

### Transition to Monarchy
- Samuel was fourteenth judge
- People rejected God's rule, wanting king like other nations
- God had already prophesied they would do this
- Samuel complained to God; God said they rejected Him
- God instituted monarchy system

### The Three Kings as Types
- **Saul**: Picture of Antichrist to come (666)
- **David**: Picture of Lord Jesus Christ in battle (man of war)
  - Represents Battle of Armageddon
  - King after God's own heart
- **Solomon**: Established reign of peace
  - Able to build temple during peaceful reign
  - Picture of Christ's millennial kingdom of peace

### The Davidic Covenant (2 Samuel 7:13-16)
- "He shall build a house for my name"
- "I will establish the throne of his kingdom forever"
- "I will be his father, and he shall be my son"
- "Thine house and thy kingdom shall be established forever before thee"
- God's plan: bring His kingdom and king to earth
- All rebellion traces back to Lucifer's rebellion and Adam's rebellion

### The Broader Biblical Context
- God created earth, gave habitation to Lucifer, sons of God, morning stars
- They rebelled; God judged
- God created new creation in His image and likeness: Adam and Eve
- Told Adam: be fruitful, multiply, replenish earth, have dominion, subdue it
- Authority of earth taken from Lucifer, given to Adam
- Eve and Adam rebelled
- Whole story is about rebellion against God until true king returns

### The Divided Kingdom
- After Solomon: kingdom divided between Rehoboam and Jeroboam
- Jeroboam went north with 10 tribes
  - Set up false worship in Bethel and Dan
  - Capital in Samaria
- Judah and Benjamin remained south near Jerusalem
- Period of several hundred years
- 19 kings to north, 20 kings to south
- All kings were pictures/types of God's plan to bring His kingdom and king to earth
- Northern kingdom: all bad kings
- Southern kingdom: handful of good kings, many bad ones

### The Prophets' Ministry
- Northern kingdom went into captivity to Assyria
- Southern kingdom went into captivity to Babylon
- During all the rebellion, God sent prophets in mercy and grace
- Prophetic books come after 1-2 Kings and 1-2 Chronicles in Bible arrangement
- But prophets actually prophesied during time of 1-2 Kings and Chronicles
- Prophets like Hosea, Joel, Amos, Obadiah, Jonah, Micah, Nahum prophesied during reign of 39 kings

### Return from Captivity
- Southern kingdom returned from Babylon after 70 years (around 535 BC)
- Next 50 years: rebuilding period
  - Zerubbabel returned to build temple
  - Nehemiah returned to build cities
- Story told in Ezra, Nehemiah, Esther
- Things still not going well
- God raised up prophets Haggai and Zechariah during this period

### Malachi: The Last Prophet
- Book of Malachi has four chapters
- God lays down pitiful condition of His people
- Seven charges against nation of Israel
- Book ends with word "curse"
- Warning: "unless I come and smite the earth with a curse"
- Last book of Old Testament

### The Silent Period
- After Malachi, God became silent for 400 years
- No prophet, sign, vision, or dream for 400 years
- Inter-testament period with much historical activity
- 400 years comparable to entire history of America (back to Mayflower and Pilgrims)

### The Kingdom Promise Throughout Prophets
- **Isaiah 2:2-4**: Mountain of Lord's house established, all nations flow to it, people go to house of God of Jacob
- **Isaiah 9:6-7**: Child born, Son given, government on His shoulders, Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God, Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace, upon throne of David
- **Jeremiah 23:5-6**: Righteous branch raised to David, King shall reign and prosper, execute judgment and justice, Judah saved and Israel dwell safely, called "The Lord Our Righteousness"
- **Hosea 1:1**: Prophesied to four kings of Judah and one king (Jeroboam) of Israel
- **Hosea 3:5**: Children of Israel return, seek Lord and David their king in latter days
- **Joel 3:16-17**: Lord roars out of Zion from Jerusalem after Second Coming and Battle of Armageddon, Jerusalem becomes holy, no strangers pass through
- **Amos 9:11**: Raise up tabernacle of David, rebuild ruins
- **Obadiah 21**: Saviors come to Mount Zion, kingdom shall be the Lord's
- **Micah 4:1-3**: Similar to Isaiah 2 about mountain of Lord's house, word of Lord from Jerusalem
- **Zephaniah 3:15**: Lord taken away judgments, King of Israel (the Lord) in midst, no more evil

### Prophetic Pattern
- All minor prophets contain warnings of judgment and God's wrath
- But end with kingdom hope
- "Beautiful golden clasp" at conclusion of each

### The Coming Millennial Kingdom
- Jerusalem currently sits about mile above sea level (why Bible says "went up to Jerusalem")
- God will burn down all mountains on earth (Mount McKinley, Mount Everest, all of them)
- Will raise Mount of Lord very high with Jerusalem at top
- Light of Lord shining from Jerusalem
- River flows from underneath throne of Jesus Christ
- River runs into Dead Sea, healing its waters
- Dead Sea currently dead because too salty
- People will fish from healed Dead Sea
- Waters flow into Mediterranean Sea
- Desert and wilderness areas will blossom like Rose of Sharon
- Fir trees, cedar trees, and all trees will clap their hands
- Trees will worship the Lord

### Governmental Order in Kingdom
- David raised from dead to reign as regent king under Lord Jesus Christ
- Twelve apostles raised from dead as part of governmental order
- Twelve tribes of Israel become kingdom of priests on earth

### Knowledge in the Kingdom
- Isaiah 11: Knowledge of Lord covers earth as waters cover seas
- Every man shall know Lord from least to greatest
- Won't need to teach people because they'll already have knowledge
- No strangers in kingdom - everyone knows the Lord

### Christ's Ministry to Israel
- After 400 silent years, voice crying in wilderness
- Strange fellow (John the Baptist) dressing and eating weird
- Message: "Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand"

### Romans 15:8 - Christ as Minister of Circumcision
- Jesus Christ was minister of the circumcision
- Goes back to Abraham in Genesis 15 and 17
- Christ came to confirm promises made to fathers
- In earthly ministry, not talking about body of Christ (nowhere in view)
- Came as Israel's Messiah in Matthew, Mark, Luke, John
- Came to confirm Abrahamic covenant and Davidic covenant (seed, land, kingdom)
- Came to His own people

### The New Covenant
- Jeremiah 31: God makes New Covenant with Israel
- Law was first/old covenant
- New Covenant promises:
  - Forgiveness of sins
  - Atonement
  - Sins separated as far as east from west
  - Sins in sea of forgetfulness
  - God remembers sins no more
  - Pour out Spirit upon them
  - Prosperity, health, wealth, peace
  - Multitude of days, multiply seed
  - Head nation on earth, not tail

### Current Events and Israel
- President Trump withdrew billions of dollars of aid to Israel
- Other nations jumping on bandwagon
- Setting up for coming days

### Time Past vs. But Now
- Time past involved much work and ordinances
- Israel worked for relationship with God
- Israel worked for access to God
- Today we have liberty and rest in Lord
- Don't work for relationship or access to God today
- Have access to Holy Spirit by grace (Ephesians 2-3)
- Salvation is having access to God, relationship with God Almighty

### Our Position in Christ
- Ordinances that were against us taken out of way
- Nailed to cross
- We are put into Christ
- In Christ we are complete
- Don't have to go to place of perfection for access to God
- Should go on to perfection in sanctification, but not for access

### Israel's Requirement vs. Ours
- Matthew 5: Jesus told Israel "Be perfect as your Father which is in heaven is perfect"
- That's binding on Israel, not on us today
- We have such privilege today
