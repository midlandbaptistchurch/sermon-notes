## Summary
Associate Pastor Mark Simpson leads an evening communion service, focusing on the physical suffering of Christ and the proper observance of the Lord's Supper. He emphasizes the importance of self-examination and returning to a proper understanding of what Christ accomplished through His broken body and shed blood.

## Scripture
1 Corinthians 11:17-28 — Paul's correction to the Corinthian church regarding their improper observance of the Lord's Supper.

Additional passages:
- Matthew 27:26-31 — The physical suffering and mockery of Christ
- 2 Corinthians 5:21 — Christ made sin for us

## Key Themes
- The physical anguish Christ endured on the cross
- Proper observance of communion as a unified body
- Self-examination before partaking of the Lord's Supper
- The importance of remembering Christ's sacrifice
- Coming together for the better, not for the worse

## Notes

### The Physical Suffering of Christ
- Pastor Simpson begins by reading Matthew 27:26-31, describing the scourging, mocking, and crown of thorns
- Emphasizes that Christ didn't just die for our sins, but died as our sins (2 Corinthians 5:21)
- The physical anguish serves as a reminder of what He did in our place

### The Corinthian Problem
- Paul had to correct the Corinthian church because they were "coming together for the worse, not for the better"
- They had divisions and were conducting selfish "love feasts" instead of properly observing communion
- Some were eating separately while others went hungry, missing the unity that should characterize the Lord's Supper

### The True Institution of the Lord's Supper
- Paul returns to what he received from the Lord (1 Corinthians 11:23-26)
- The bread represents Christ's broken body
- The cup represents His shed blood
- "As often as you eat this bread and drink this cup, you show the Lord's death till He come"

### Self-Examination
- Before partaking, believers should examine themselves (verse 28)
- Questions to consider: Has the cross meant more to me before? Have I drifted from study and fellowship?
- The goal is to return fresh to the cross and God's goodness

### Corporate Worship
- When the body comes together in unity, it brings God glory in a unique way
- Individual believers functioning together as one body gives God glory He doesn't receive any other way
- The importance of attendance and participation in corporate worship
