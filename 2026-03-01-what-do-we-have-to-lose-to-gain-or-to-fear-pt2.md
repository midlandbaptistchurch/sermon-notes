## Summary
Pastor Simpson continues his examination of what Christians have to lose, gain, or fear after salvation. He distinguishes between the free inheritance we receive at salvation and the earned inheritance based on our works afterward, emphasizing that while we are forgiven, we must still be freed from sin's dominion and will give account at the judgment seat of Christ.

## Scripture
2 Corinthians 4:18 — "While we look not at the things which are seen, but at the things which are not seen: for the things which are seen are temporal; but the things which are not seen are eternal."

## Key Themes
- Two types of inheritance: given (salvation) vs. earned (works after salvation)
- The difference between being forgiven and being free from sin
- Accountability at the judgment seat of Christ for both good and bad deeds
- The importance of seeing eternal things through Scripture study
- Living resurrected lives that value eternal over temporal things

## Notes
### Seeing the Unseen Through Scripture
- Christians must learn to see unseen, eternal things through God's Word
- Clarity in biblical understanding leads to confidence in living out the message
- We walk by faith, not by sight, deriving that faith from Scripture

### Two Types of Inheritance
1. **Given Inheritance (Salvation)**: Free gift based on Christ's finished work, including forgiveness, eternal security, and guaranteed new body
2. **Earned Inheritance**: Based on works after salvation, earned through faithful service and being freed from sin's dominion

### Forgiveness vs. Freedom from Sin
- **Forgiveness**: Free gift that covers all sins past, present, and future
- **Freedom from sin**: Required work of dying daily to sin's dominion (Romans 6:17-18)
- Both are necessary but distinct - we can be forgiven yet still enslaved to sin

### The Judgment Seat of Christ
- All Christians will appear before Christ's judgment seat (2 Corinthians 5:10)
- We will receive for things done in the body, "whether it be good or bad"
- Some will suffer loss while others receive rewards
- This judgment is about acceptance and inheritance, not salvation

### Practical Application
- Time in God's Word should be our highest priority
- We must assess the value of eternal vs. temporal things
- Daily resurrection means dying to flesh and living for eternal purposes
- Our works and service will be evaluated by Christ
