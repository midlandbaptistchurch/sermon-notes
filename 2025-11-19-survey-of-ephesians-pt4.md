## Summary
This message examines Ephesians as a book written for mature believers who can receive the deeper revelations of God's eternal purpose. Pastor Payne contrasts the spiritual maturity needed for Ephesians with the carnality addressed in Corinthians, and explores how the Trinity works throughout Ephesians to accomplish God's mystery program.

## Scripture
Ephesians 1:13-14 — Paul describes believers being sealed with the Holy Spirit of promise as the earnest of their inheritance.

## Key Themes
- Distinction between saints and carnal Christians
- The mystery program vs. prophetic program
- The Trinity's work throughout Ephesians
- The Holy Spirit as seal and earnest
- God's eternal purpose before the foundation of the world

## Notes

### Spiritual Maturity Required for Ephesians
- Ephesians is addressed to "saints and faithful" in Christ Jesus
- Unlike 1 Corinthians, written to carnal Christians who couldn't receive deeper truths
- The material in Ephesians requires spiritual maturity to understand
- Paul contrasts natural man vs. spiritual man's ability to discern spiritual things

### The Gospel of Your Salvation vs. Other Gospels
- Ephesians 1:13 refers to "the gospel of your salvation" - specific to this dispensation
- Different from kingdom gospel preached to Israel (Acts 2:38)
- Paul's gospel of grace versus gospel of the kingdom
- Galatians warns against preaching another gospel

### The Holy Spirit's Ministry Today
- Believers are sealed WITH the Holy Spirit, not BY Him
- The Spirit indwells believers as earnest of inheritance
- This is the dispensation of the Holy Spirit
- We can grieve the Spirit because He dwells within us
- Sealed "unto the day of redemption" (moving toward that day)

### The Trinity Throughout Ephesians
- Chapter 1: Trinity working in God's purpose
- Chapter 2: Access to God through Trinity's work
- Chapter 3: Trinity working in prayer
- Chapter 4: Unity of the Spirit
- Chapter 5: Fullness through the Spirit
- Chapter 6: Trinity in spiritual armor

### Ephesians Outline
- **Our Wealth** (1:3-3:21) - requires enlightenment
- **Our Walk** (4:1-6:9) - requires edification
- **Our Warfare** (6:10-20) - requires empowerment

### Mystery Program vs. Prophetic Program
- Prophetic program: spoken by holy prophets since the world began
- Mystery program: kept secret before the foundation of the world
- Israel's program was prophesied; church's program was hidden in God
- God's eternal purpose was planned before the foundation of the world
