## Summary
Pastor Payne continues his series on simplicity in Christ by addressing two key points about rightly dividing the Word of Truth. He examines Acts 2 and 4, arguing that these chapters describe Jewish believers preparing for tribulation and kingdom life, not the birth of the body of Christ. He contrasts how God provided for Israel (supernaturally through manna and communal living) with how believers today are to provide for themselves through honest work. The sermon emphasizes that understanding dispensational distinctions helps clarify what God expects of believers in different time periods.

## Scripture
Acts 2:14-47 — Peter preaches on the Day of Pentecost to the house of Israel, calling them to repent and be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ. The early believers sold their possessions and had all things common, with no one lacking.

Acts 4:32-37 — The multitude of believers continued in one heart and soul, selling their possessions to meet needs, giving witness to the resurrection, with none among them lacking.

## Key Themes
- **Rightly Dividing the Word**: Acts 2 describes a Jewish feast day and kingdom preparation, not the birth of the body of Christ for today's church
- **Different Provisions for Different Times**: God provided supernaturally through manna and communal living for Israel entering the kingdom; believers today are commanded to work with their own hands
- **Biblical Communism vs. Work Ethic**: The early Acts believers practiced voluntary sharing under kingdom authority; believers today are to work honestly and avoid idleness
- **Time Past vs. But Now**: Understanding dispensational distinctions prevents misapplication of Scripture meant for Israel's kingdom program to the church today

## Notes

### Acts 2 - Not the Birth of the Body of Christ
- Acts 2 occurs on the Day of Pentecost, a Jewish feast day 50 days after Passover
- Israel had seven major feast days: Passover, Unleavened Bread, First Fruits, Pentecost, Trumpets, Atonement, Tabernacles (remembered as "puff patat")
- Three times yearly, all Jewish males were required to come to Jerusalem: Passover, Pentecost, Tabernacles
- Peter addresses "men of Judea and Jerusalem" (v.14), "men of Israel" (v.22), and "all the house of Israel" (v.36)
- The indictment: "that same Jesus whom ye have crucified" - focusing on their responsibility for Christ's death
- Traditional churches claim Acts 2 as their birth but selectively adopt only parts of it (a "buffet belief system")

### The Message of Acts 2
- Peter preaches the resurrection, not the cross as good news
- Verse 30: Christ raised up "to sit on his throne" - David's throne in Jerusalem
- This is not the gospel of grace (death, burial, resurrection for justification)
- Verse 36: God made Jesus "both Lord and Christ" - Messianic proclamation
- Verse 38: "Repent and be baptized...in the name of Jesus Christ" (not Father, Son, Holy Spirit as in Matthew 28:19)
- Matthew 28:19 is for Gentiles learning about the Trinity; Acts 2 is for Jews identifying with the resurrected Christ they had murdered
- Christ reduced the charge from murder to manslaughter ("Father forgive them, they know not what they do")
- He became their high priest after the order of Melchizedek (not Aaron), providing refuge

### The Cities of Refuge Parallel
- Old Testament had six cities of refuge (three east, three west of Jordan)
- Those guilty of manslaughter could flee there until the death of the high priest
- Christ became the refuge for Israel - they could flee into Him
- Hebrews: "able to save them to the uttermost that come unto God by him...he ever liveth"
- His priesthood is Melchizedek (without beginning or ending), not Aaronic

### Acts 2:39 - Not About Gentiles
- "The promise is unto you, and to your children, and to all that are afar off"
- This quotes Daniel 9:7: "to the men of Judah...to all Israel that are near and that are far off"
- Peter wrote to "strangers scattered abroad" (1 Peter, 2 Peter)
- "As many as the Lord our God shall call" - referring to scattered Israel, not Gentiles
- Verse 40: "Save yourselves from this untoward generation"

### 3,000 Souls Added
- Acts 2:41: "added unto them about 3,000 souls"
- Exodus 32:28: When the first covenant was given, 3,000 died at the golden calf incident
- When the new covenant possibility arrives with the Holy Spirit, 3,000 are saved
- This is not arbitrary - a synthesis of Old and New Testament

### Acts 2:42 - The Apostles' Doctrine
- "They continued stedfastly in the apostles' doctrine"
- The 12 apostles got their doctrine from Christ during His earthly ministry
- Christ taught about earthly things, the kingdom coming to earth, surviving tribulation
- He warned them: if on housetop, flee; don't take anything; flee to mountains
- This is preparation for the 70th week of Daniel (the final seven years for Israel)
- 69 weeks fulfilled when Messiah was "cut off" (Daniel 9)

### The Kingdom Given to a Nation
- Matthew 21:43: "The kingdom of God shall be taken from you, and given to a nation bringing forth the fruits thereof"
- Taken from the Pharisees and Sadducees (religious/political leadership)
- Given to the "little flock" who followed Christ
- Luke 12:32: "Fear not, little flock; for it is your Father's good pleasure to give you the kingdom"
- John the Baptist was the "porter" who opened the door to the sheepfold (water baptism)
- They developed into "a nation of fruit bearers unto God"

### Acts 2:43-45 - Biblical Communism
- Verse 43: "Fear came upon every soul; and many wonders and signs were done by the apostles"
- If Acts 2 is for us today, why aren't we doing wonders and signs?
- Verse 44-45: "All that believed were together, and had all things common; and sold their possessions and goods, and parted them to all men, as every man had need"
- This is biblical communism - collective sharing without private ownership
- Karl Marx studied this passage (can be found in his writings)
- Marxist communism: "You have, we'll take"
- Biblical communism: "We have, we'll give"
- No evangelical or Baptist church imposes this requirement today

### Acts 4:32-35 - No Lack Among Them
- Verse 32: "The multitude of them that believed were of one heart and of one soul"
- What caused this unity? The Holy Spirit upon them in a supernatural way
- This is not happening today - we must yield ourselves and be filled
- Verse 33: "With great power gave the apostles witness of the resurrection"
- Not witnessing to the cross, but to the resurrection
- Telling people: "You killed Him, but God raised Him from the dead"
- Verse 34: "Neither was there any among them that lacked"
- They sold lands and houses, brought the proceeds to the apostles' feet
- Example: Barnabas sold his land in Cyprus (v.36-37)

### Where Did This Teaching Come From?
- Luke 12:33: "Sell that ye have, and give alms"
- Matthew 19:21: To the rich young ruler - "If thou wilt be perfect, go and sell that thou hast, and give to the poor"
- The law was summarized: Love God with all your heart, love your neighbor as yourself
- Christ was testing: "You say you love God and kept commandments - now love your neighbor: sell everything and follow me"

### The Crisis Period for Israel
- Hebrews is written to Hebrew believers in a crisis period
- They needed to "go on to perfection," not draw back into perdition (Hebrews 10)
- The kingdom message was "better than angels, better than Moses"
- They were preparing to enter tribulation and then the kingdom

### Exodus 16 - No Lack with Manna
- Verse 16-18: Instructions for gathering manna
- "He that gathered much had nothing over, and he that gathered little had no lack"
- God provided supernaturally for 40 years, feeding 1-3 million people
- This prepared them for the Promised Land, which would be a time of bounty
- They would have houses built and ready, fruits, vegetables, prosperity
- Deuteronomy 28-29, Leviticus 26: "If/then" propositions - "If you obey, then I will bless"
- Throughout Old Testament, Israel disobeyed and experienced lack

### Christ as the "I AM"
- Matthew, Mark, John: Christ declared Himself "I AM"
- Not just "always existing" but "I am everything you need"
- John 6: "I am the bread of life"
- John 8: "I am the light of the world"
- John 10: "I am the door...I am the good shepherd"
- John 14: "I am the way, the truth, and the life"
- John 11: "I am the resurrection"
- John 15: "I am the true vine, ye are the branches"
- Meaning: "Abide in me, and I will supply and meet your need supernaturally"

### Kingdom Prayer Promises
- Matthew 21:22: "All things, whatsoever ye shall ask in prayer, believing, ye shall receive"
- 1 John 5:14-15: "If we ask any thing according to his will, he heareth us...we have the petitions"
- Question: Can we claim these promises today?
- Do we always know the will of God?
- Paul says we "know not how to pray as we ought" (Romans 8)
- The Spirit prays for us, but we don't know what to ask for half the time

### Matthew 6 - Kingdom Living vs. Today
- Verse 25: "Take no thought for your life, what ye shall eat, or what ye shall drink"
- Christ was saying: "I'll take care of you every day like I provided manna"
- "Don't think about tomorrow" - but we think about tomorrow, next year, 10 years from now
- Verse 28: Consider the lilies - their clothes didn't wear out in wilderness
- Verse 33: "Seek ye first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness"
- The kingdom will be a righteous kingdom
- He will "take out of his kingdom all things that offend"

### But Now - Instructions for Today
- 1 Thessalonians 4:11: "Study to be quiet, and to do your own business, and to work with your own hands"
- "That ye may walk honestly toward them that are without, and that ye may have lack of nothing"
- We are not to gather manna daily
- We are not to pray and expect to receive whatever we ask
- We are not to sell everything and pool resources
- We are to work with our hands

### Time Past vs. But Now
- Old Testament and Gospels and Acts 1-7: Time Past
- We are in "But Now" - a present evil world
- We don't live by signs, miracles, wonders, and "the power of the world to come"
- We haven't "tasted" that (Hebrews 6:5)
- "The just shall live by faith" - believing the book, taking promises and doctrine
- In prayer, take sound doctrine and apply it to life situations
- Don't run back to Gospels saying "Lord heal me, give me prosperity"

### Work, Not Welfare
- Get an education, work hard, use your hands
- Take care of yourself and your family
- 1 Timothy 5:8: "If any provide not for his own, specially for those of his own house, he hath denied the faith, and is worse than an infidel"
- Christians who live on welfare, don't pay bills, aren't "walking honestly toward them that are without"
- You're not being honest about Christianity if you don't do right financially

### The Constitution's Purpose
- Preamble: "We the People...to form a more perfect union"
- "Establish justice, ensure domestic tranquility"
- "Provide for the common defense"
- "Promote the general welfare" - not provide for it, promote it
- Everyone has equal opportunity to work in peace and safety
- Government promotes by providing justice and defense
- "Secure the blessings of liberty to ourselves and our posterity"
- Does not say "provide for general welfare"

### 2 Thessalonians 3:10-15
- Thessalonians thought the Lord was coming back immediately
- They quit their jobs and started hanging around
- "When's the Lord coming back, Paul?"
- Not working, getting into everyone else's business
- "Idle hands are the devil's workshop; an idle mouth the same"
- Started talking, gossiping
- Verse 10: "If any would not work, neither should he eat"
- Verse 11: "Some...walk among you disorderly, working not at all, but are busybodies"
- Verse 12: "With quietness they work, and eat their own bread"
- Verse 14: "If any man obey not our word by this epistle, note that man, and have no company with him"
- "We're not going to get around that dude - he's a lazy bum, always bumming from everybody"
- Have nothing to do with him; tell him to go to work

### The Creator's Accountability
- We are created beings
- God our Creator is watching how we live
- We have the Bible - the instruction manual
- God says: "This is what I'm telling you to do, person in 2026"
- "I'm going to hold you accountable as a created being"
- "Don't go to Exodus 16, the Gospels, Acts 2, Acts 4"
- "This is what you're supposed to do But Now"
- Don't have to have abundance, but don't have to lack
- Don't live above your means
- "If you don't make a lot of money, live in a trailer - it's not going to kill you"
- One day we'll stand before God Almighty
