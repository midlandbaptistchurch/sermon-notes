## Summary
Pastor Payne continues his series on the structure of Scripture by examining the second and third days of creation. He explains how God created the firmament by dividing the waters, establishing a rectangular structure that mirrors the Old Testament tabernacle and serves as a frame for God's glory.

## Scripture
Genesis 1:3-11 — The creation of light, the firmament dividing waters, and the appearance of dry land.

## Key Themes
- The firmament as God's creation dividing waters above and below
- The universe structured like a tabernacle - rectangular with heavenly patterns
- Satan's fall from heaven and establishment of his domain in darkness
- The firmament as a tent/curtain where God will dwell
- The ultimate destination of believers through the waters to God's throne

## Notes

### Review of Day One
- God said "let there be light" - this light is different from the sun
- The light comes from beyond the great deep and waters
- This light is God's glory that will illuminate the New Jerusalem

### Days Two and Three of Creation
- Day Two: God creates the firmament by dividing waters from waters
- The firmament (space) is created by separating waters above and below
- Day Three: Dry land appears and vegetation is created
- These days occur after the initial creation in Genesis 1:1-2

### The Structure of the Universe
- In the beginning, God created heaven (His throne) and earth (His footstool)
- Angels were created, including Lucifer who rebelled
- God judged the rebellion, casting Satan and his angels down
- The earth was placed in "the deep" as judgment
- God then creates the firmament by dividing the waters

### The Firmament as Tabernacle
- God "stretches out" the heavens like a curtain and tent
- The structure is rectangular, like the Old Testament tabernacle
- The tabernacle was a pattern of heavenly things
- The universe serves as God's dwelling place and display of glory

### The Waters Above and Below
- Waters exist above the heavens (Psalm 148:4)
- Waters under the earth (Deuteronomy 5:8)
- The firmament divides and contains these waters
- The top of the deep is frozen like crystal (Ezekiel 1:22)

### God's Throne and the Crystal Sea
- John saw God's throne with a sea of glass like crystal before it
- The throne is literal and physical, currently hidden by clouds
- Believers will one day pass through the waters to reach God's presence
- Michael the archangel will lead believers through Satan's domain
