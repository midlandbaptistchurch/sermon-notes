## Summary
Pastor Simpson explores the practical aspects of Christian living through the metaphor of putting off old clothes and putting on new ones. The battle for spiritual growth takes place in our minds, requiring intentional renewal and replacement of wrong thoughts with godly ones.

## Scripture
Ephesians 4:21-24 — Paul's instruction on putting off the old man and putting on the new man after God's image.

## Key Themes
- The battleground of the Christian life is in the mind
- Repentance involves replacing wrong with right, not just stopping sin
- Being renewed in the spirit of your mind is crucial for spiritual growth
- The new man is created after God's image in righteousness and holiness

## Notes

### Review: The Negative Walk (Ephesians 4:17-19)
- Paul describes the darkness and blindness of those not following the Lord
- Spiritual blindness often results from repeatedly refusing truth
- The process of hardening leads to being "past feeling" with seared consciences

### Putting Off and Putting On (Ephesians 4:22-24)
- "Put off concerning the former conversation, the old man" - requires intentional action
- The old man is corrupt according to "deceitful lust" - sin uses deception
- "Be renewed in the spirit of your mind" - the battle is in our thought life
- "Put on the new man" - created after God's image in righteousness and holiness

### The Battle for the Mind
- Romans 7:23 describes the war between the law of the mind and the law of sin
- 2 Corinthians 10:3-5 teaches casting down imaginations and bringing thoughts captive
- Taking control of thoughts is essential for spiritual freedom
- Sometimes we must attack wrong thinking directly through prayer and love

### Repentance as Replacement
- Example from Ephesians 4:28: the thief stops stealing and starts working to give
- Romans 12:14-21 provides the difficult but necessary approach to enemies
- Can't just put off - must put on the right thing in its place

### Colossians 3 Parallels
- "Seek those things which are above" (v. 1-2) - a memory verse for mind renewal
- "Set your affection on things above, not on things on the earth"
- "When Christ who is our life shall appear" - eternal perspective motivates present holiness
- "Mortify therefore your members" - practical steps in putting off sin

### Forbearing and Forgiving
- Colossians 3:13 and Ephesians 4:31-32 teach forbearing (putting up with) others
- We forgive as Christ forgave us - this is our basis for forgiveness
- God models forbearance with all mankind, including our church body

### Peace and the Word
- "Let the peace of God rule in your hearts" (Colossians 3:15)
- Philippians 4:6-7 connects prayer with the peace that keeps hearts and minds
- "Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly" (Colossians 3:16) - saturation with Scripture
