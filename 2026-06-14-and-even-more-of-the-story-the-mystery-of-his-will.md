## Summary
Pastor Jim Payne continues a review series exploring the overarching theme of the Bible — government and God's sovereign will — tracing the story of God's kingdom from creation through Israel's history to the mystery of the Church. He contrasts Matthew 25 (the kingdom prepared "from the foundation of the world" for Israel) with Ephesians 1 (the body of Christ chosen "before the foundation of the world"), explaining why the Church was a hidden mystery kept secret so the devil would not prevent the crucifixion. The sermon concludes with God's ultimate plan: to use the body of Christ to reconcile the heavenly places, followed by His return to earth to establish His kingdom through Israel.

## Scripture
Ephesians 1:3-4 — Paul's declaration that believers are chosen in Christ "before the foundation of the world," contrasted with Matthew 25:34 where the King invites the blessed to inherit the kingdom prepared "from the foundation of the world." Additional references include Colossians 1:20, Hebrews 4:3-4, Isaiah 9:6-7, Revelation 11:15, 1 Corinthians 2:6-7, and 2 Timothy 4:18.

## Key Themes
- The primary theme of the Bible is government — thrones, kingdoms, and God's sovereign rule
- The distinction between "from the foundation of the world" (Israel/earthly kingdom) and "before the foundation of the world" (the body of Christ/heavenly calling)
- God's will as His sovereign purpose and plan — illustrated as a business owner hiring workers to fulfill his vision
- Satan's rebellion as the catalyst for God's redemptive plan to reconcile both the heavens and the earth
- The Church as a mystery hidden from Satan, revealed only after the cross through the Apostle Paul
- The body of Christ's heavenly destiny — to occupy and reconcile the heavenly places currently under Satan's domain
- God's future return to dealing with the nation of Israel after the Rapture, culminating in Christ's earthly kingdom

## Notes
**Two Contrasting Passages — Matthew and Ephesians**
- Matthew is written to/about the nation of Israel and concerns God's plan for the earth; Ephesians is written by Paul about the body of Christ and concerns heavenly things.
- Matthew 25:34 — the kingdom prepared "from the foundation of the world" refers to the world system established during the six days of Genesis 1.
- Ephesians 1:4 — believers chosen in Christ "before the foundation of the world" means before Genesis 1; the body of Christ was in God's mind prior to the creation of the current world system.

**The Theme of the Bible: Government**
- Genesis 1:1 — heaven is God's throne (Isaiah 66:1).
- Isaiah 9:6-7 — the government shall be upon His shoulder; of the increase of His government there shall be no end.
- Revelation 11:15 — the kingdoms of this world become the kingdoms of our Lord.
- The main theme of Scripture is government: God's sovereign rule being established over all creation through His Son.

**God's Will Defined**
- Ephesians 5:17 — "understanding what the will of the Lord is."
- Ephesians 1:9 — "the mystery of His will."
- Colossians 1:9 — being filled with the knowledge of His will.
- God's will = His purpose, His counsel, what He has sovereignly chosen to do.
- Illustrated as a business owner: God has a plan and hires people (believers) to come in and fulfill it.

**Satan's Rebellion and God's Response**
- Lucifer said "I will" (Isaiah 14:13) and rebelled, leading to judgment on the pre-Adamic world.
- God responded: "I will" — His plan to ultimately destroy Satan and reconcile all things.
- God spoke light into the darkness (dispelling Satan's darkness), re-formed the earth in six days, and created humanity.
- Colossians 1:20 — God's plan is to reconcile all things — in earth and in heaven — through the blood of the cross.

**The Storyline of the Kingdom Through Israel**
- Adam given dominion (a king) — lost it through the Fall.
- Noah given dominion — compromised through drunkenness.
- Nimrod/Babel — nations disinherited; God chooses Abraham and the nation of Israel.
- Jacob's blessing on Judah (Genesis 49): the scepter shall not depart until Shiloh (Christ) comes.
- 400 years in Egypt (10 x 40: Gentile oppression/testing) → Moses leads them out as a king (Deuteronomy 33:5).
- Joshua leads them into the land (Joshua = Jehovah saves; equivalent to Jesus).
- Judges period — repeated rebellion; every man did what was right in his own eyes.
- Israel rejects God as King and asks for Saul — a picture of the Antichrist.
- David — a man after God's own heart; picture of Christ the true King.
- Solomon — multiplies horses and wives (Deuteronomy 17), heart drawn away; kingdom divided.
- Northern kingdom (Israel) taken to Assyria after ~250 years; southern kingdom (Judah) taken to Babylon after ~310-320 years.
- Return from Babylon under Zerubbabel; temple rebuilt; then 400 years of silence (Amos 8:11 — a famine of hearing the Word).

**John the Baptist and Christ's Ministry**
- After 400 years of silence, John the Baptist announces the kingdom of heaven is at hand.
- Jesus preaches the kingdom, is mocked, and crucified as "King of the Jews" (Matthew 27:37).
- Raised from the dead; apostles preach at Pentecost — but Israel rejects again.
- Stephen (Acts 7) — filled with the Holy Spirit, sees the Son of Man standing at the right hand of God; Israel rejects the Holy Spirit's testimony and stones him.
- Father rejected, Son rejected, Holy Spirit resisted — Acts 7 closes the door on the kingdom offer to Israel for this age.

**The Mystery of the Church**
- God pivots: saves Saul of Tarsus (the chief of sinners = the leader of the rebellion against Christ).
- Paul becomes the revealer of the mystery — the body of Christ.
- 1 Corinthians 2:6-8 — the hidden wisdom of God ordained before the world unto our glory; "had they known it, they would not have crucified the Lord of Glory."
- The Church was hidden from Satan so he would not prevent the cross, which is the power that forms the one new man (Jew and Gentile together in one body).
- The mystery was kept secret from eternity past, hidden in God (Ephesians 3), and only revealed through Paul after the cross.

**The Body of Christ's Heavenly Destiny**
- Job — the heavens are not clean (plural; referring to the firmament and the area around the earth where Satan reigns as prince of the power of the air).
- The body of Christ is chosen from before the foundation of the world because Satan's domain is in the heavenly places — and God needs a righteous people to go up there and displace him.
- Ephesians 6 — we wrestle against principalities and powers, spiritual wickedness in high places.
- 2 Corinthians 5 — we have a building of God, a house not made with hands, eternal in the heavens.
- 2 Timothy 4:18 — the Lord shall preserve Paul unto His heavenly kingdom (not earthly).
- At the Rapture, the body of Christ is taken up to the heavenly places to reconcile them under Christ's authority.

**God's Return to Israel and the Earthly Kingdom**
- After the Rapture, God turns His attention back to the earth and the nation of Israel.
- The tribulation period; Christ returns at the second coming; Israel recognizes and repents of their rejection of Him.
- Christ defeats the Antichrist at Armageddon (Valley of Megiddo/Jehoshaphat), casts him into the lake of fire.
- Christ establishes His earthly kingdom — the fulfillment of everything promised to Israel from the foundation of the world.
- Believers in the body of Christ will be in heavenly places, having reconciled those realms, while Christ reigns on earth.
