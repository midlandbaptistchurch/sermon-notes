## Summary
Pastor Jim Payne continues his series on the simplicity in Christ by focusing on the simplicity of Bible study. He addresses common excuses people give for not understanding Scripture—confusion, contradictions, difficulty—and demonstrates that these problems are resolved through rightly dividing the Word of Truth. Using 2 Timothy 2:15 as the foundation, he shows how the Bible divides into three main dispensational periods: "time past" (Israel, law, earthly kingdom), "but now" (the Body of Christ, grace, heavenly position), and "ages to come" (Israel's restoration). He emphasizes that believers must understand Paul's epistles (Romans through Philemon) as their doctrinal foundation for today, while recognizing that other Scripture is written to different people for different purposes without creating contradictions.

## Scripture
**2 Corinthians 11:3** — Paul fears that believers' minds should be corrupted from the simplicity that is in Christ, just as Eve was beguiled by the serpent's subtlety.

**2 Timothy 2:15** — The only command to "study" in Scripture, instructing believers to study to show themselves approved unto God, workmen that need not be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.

**Ephesians 2:11-13** — Describes three time periods: "time past" when Gentiles were without Christ, "but now" in Christ Jesus, and implies future "ages to come" (Ephesians 2:7).

## Key Themes
- **Rightly Dividing**: The Bible must be divided into dispensational sections to avoid confusion and properly understand God's different dealings with different people at different times
- **Paul's Unique Apostleship**: Paul's epistles (Romans through Philemon) contain the doctrine and instructions for the Body of Christ today; believers must "consider what Paul says" to understand all other Scripture
- **Identity in Christ**: Believers' identity is not in denominational labels or personalities but in being "in Christ" as members of His body
- **Time Past, But Now, Ages to Come**: Scripture divides into three major periods dealing with Israel's earthly kingdom (past and future) and the Church's heavenly calling (present)
- **No Contradictions, Only Differences**: Apparent contradictions in Scripture (like faith without works vs. faith with works) are resolved by recognizing they address different people in different dispensations

## Notes

### Introduction: The Simplicity Series
- Series examines simplicity in Christ under five headings: Salvation, Sanctification, Study, Service, and Soul Winning
- Pastor's goal is to have a "conversation" rather than deliver a traditional sermon
- All that believers are and have is found "in Christ" (1 Corinthians 1:30)
- Believers live and move and have their being in Him (Acts 17)

### Review: Simplicity of Salvation and Sanctification
- **Salvation**: Simple—grace through faith, not works; believing the gospel (Christ died, buried, rose again)
- **Sanctification**: Two-fold—God sanctifies believers positionally; believers sanctify themselves practically through confession, forgiveness, and moving forward

### The Simplicity of Study
- Common excuses people make:
  - "The Bible is so hard to understand"
  - "I get so confused"
  - "There are contradictions throughout the Bible"
- God commands believers to "get understanding" (Proverbs 4:7)
- God promises to give understanding: "Consider what I say and the Lord give thee understanding in all things" (2 Timothy 2:7)

### The Command to Study and Rightly Divide
- **2 Timothy 2:15**: Only verse commanding believers to "study"
- Study to be approved unto God, a workman not ashamed
- Method: "Rightly dividing the word of truth"
  - Must sectionalize and "cut straight" the Bible
  - Not a suggestion but an imperative for every believer
  - Not unique to one church but incumbent upon all believers worldwide
- **1 Corinthians 14:33**: "God is not the author of confusion but of peace"
  - If confused in Scripture, the problem is not God's
  - Confusion comes from failure to rightly divide

### Three Major Divisions of Scripture

#### Time Past (Genesis through early Acts)
- Focus: Nation of Israel, the Law, earthly kingdom
- **Ephesians 2:12**: "That at that time ye being in time past Gentiles in the flesh...without Christ, aliens from the commonwealth of Israel"
- Covers Old Testament (39 books) and the Gospels (Matthew, Mark, Luke, John)
- Key people: Abraham, Isaac, Jacob (Israel)
  - Abraham called out (Genesis 12:1-3)
  - Isaac born as son of promise (Genesis 21)
  - Jacob renamed Israel, father of 12 tribes (Genesis 32:28)
- **Deuteronomy 11:21**: Promise of days multiplied "in the land...as the days of heaven upon the earth"
- Earthly, kingdom focus with messianic promises through Judah
- **Romans 15:8**: "Jesus Christ was a minister of the circumcision for the truth of God, to confirm the promises made unto the fathers"
- Christ came to fulfill these promises, but Israel rejected and crucified Him

#### But Now (Romans through Philemon)
- Focus: The Body of Christ, grace, heavenly position
- **Ephesians 2:13**: "But now in Christ Jesus ye who sometimes were far off are made nigh by the blood of Christ"
- **1 Corinthians 12:18, 20, 27**: Believers are members of one body
  - Not Israel, not under law
  - "But now hath God set the members every one of them in the body"
  - "Now are they many members, yet but one body"
  - "Now ye are the body of Christ, and members in particular"
- Paul is the apostle to the Gentiles and the body of Christ
- Saved by grace through faith, not works (Ephesians 2:8-9)
- Believers are "in Christ Jesus" in heavenly position
- **Romans 3:27**: "Where is boasting then? It is excluded"—no works-based salvation
- This dispensation ends at the rapture

#### Ages to Come (Hebrews through Revelation)
- Focus: Return to Israel, last days, millennial kingdom
- **Ephesians 2:7**: "That in the ages to come he might show the exceeding riches of his grace"
- **Hebrews 1:1-2**: "God, who at sundry times and in divers manners spake in time past unto the fathers, hath in these last days spoken unto us by his Son"
- After the rapture, God turns attention back to Israel
- Pattern repeats: Hebrew (Hebrews) → 12 tribes (James 1:1)
- **Ecclesiastes 1:9**: "The thing that hath been, it is that which shall be"
  - Past: Abraham (Hebrew) → Isaac, Jacob → 12 tribes
  - Future: Hebrews → 12 tribes scattered abroad
- Not written to or about the Body of Christ today

### Resolving Apparent Contradictions
- **Example: Faith and Works**
  - **Paul (Ephesians 2:8-9)**: "For by grace are ye saved through faith...not of works, lest any man should boast"
  - **Romans 3:27**: "Where is boasting then? It is excluded"
  - **James 2:24**: "Ye see then how that by works a man is justified, and not by faith only"
- Not a contradiction but a difference in audience and dispensation
  - Paul writes to the Body of Christ (but now)
  - James writes to "the twelve tribes scattered abroad" (ages to come)
- Don't try to synthesize—separate by rightly dividing

### Practical Application
- Believers must study Paul's epistles (Romans through Philemon) as their doctrinal foundation
- "Consider what I say" (2 Timothy 2:7)—run all Scripture through the prism of Paul's teaching
- When reading Old Testament, Gospels, or Hebrews-Revelation, recognize it addresses different people with different purposes
- Don't claim promises made to Israel as if made to the Church
  - Example: God promised to heal Israel for the kingdom, not a universal promise to the Church today
  - Example: John 11:26 "whosoever liveth and believeth in me shall never die"—kingdom promise, not rapture promise
- Understanding divisions brings peace, not confusion
- Don't be manipulated by preachers who misapply Scripture (e.g., taking Acts' communal living as a command for today)

### Identity and Purpose
- Believer's identity: "in Christ" as members of His Body
- Not defined by denominations (Baptist, Lutheran, Catholic, etc.)
- Not defined by loyalty to personalities or institutions
- Everything believers have is in Christ (1 Corinthians 1:30): wisdom, righteousness, sanctification, redemption
- God's plan for earth: Israel and the millennial kingdom
- God's plan for heaven: the Church, the Body of Christ
- Believers should appreciate the riches and blessings of grace beyond comprehension

### Teaching Principle: Give a Fishing Pole
- Illustration: "Give a man a fish, feed him for a day; teach him to fish, give him a fishing pole, he feeds himself for life"
- Pastor's goal: teach people how to study the Bible themselves, not just spoon-feed them weekly
- Problem in churches: people come for one sermon, get "a piece of fish," then starve the rest of the week
- Believers need to learn to feed themselves from Scripture

### The Heart Behind Rightly Dividing
- Pastor preaches what he believes is right because he will answer to God
- Not about pride or bragging, but about responsibility to Scripture
- If he thought he was wrong, he would change
- Believes every believer should take this seriously—we're "dying men talking to dying people"
- One day believers will stand before God with the King James Bible and give account
- Don't run your life by feelings, emotions, or temporal success
- God desires peace, not confusion, in understanding His Word
