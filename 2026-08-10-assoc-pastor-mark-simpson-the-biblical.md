## Summary
Pastor Mark Simpson teaches how to construct and utilize a Bible timeline as a tool for rightly dividing Scripture. Using the book "One Book Rightly Divided" (expanded edition) as a foundation, he demonstrates how plotting events, time periods, spokesmen, and biblical books on a timeline helps distinguish what applies to different dispensations. The timeline reveals the mystery of the Age of Grace as a parenthetical period between Israel's fall and restoration, showing how clarity in Bible study comes from understanding what mail is addressed to whom.

## Scripture
Romans 16:25 — "Now to him that is of power to stablish you according to my gospel, and the preaching of Jesus Christ, according to the revelation of the mystery, which was kept secret since the world began"

## Key Themes
- **The Bible Timeline as a Teaching Tool**: A properly constructed timeline helps visualize God's plan through history and prevents doctrinal confusion by showing what belongs to different time periods
- **The Age of Grace as Mystery**: The current dispensation was hidden in God and not revealed to Old Testament saints, making it distinct from Israel's prophesied program
- **Right Division Prevents Contradiction**: Understanding to whom Scripture is written prevents apparent contradictions and brings clarity to Bible study
- **Study Before Service**: Knowing why you do what you do is essential before effective service can take place

### Constructing the Timeline

**Basic Structure:**
- Draw arrows showing God's eternal existence
- Approximately 7,000 years from Genesis 1:1 to Great White Throne Judgment
- Cross placed approximately 4,000 years from beginning (not required to start here, but helpful)

**Time Periods Marked:**
- Genesis 1:1 to Genesis 12: approximately 1,800-2,200 years
- Genesis 12 to Cross: approximately 2,000 years
- Cross to Rapture (Age of Grace): approximately 2,000 years
- Tribulation Period: 7 years
- Millennial Kingdom: 1,000 years

**Key Events on Timeline:**
- Birth of Christ
- The Cross
- Christ's Ascension
- Rapture (meeting in clouds)
- Judgment Seat of Christ
- Second Coming (Christ returns to earth)
- Millennium
- Great White Throne Judgment

### The Age of Grace (Parenthetical Period)

**Represented by parentheses on timeline:**
- Mystery hidden from foundation of the world
- Not prophesied in Old Testament
- Inserted between Israel's fall and restoration
- Like a parenthetical statement in a sentence—could be removed and the sentence (Israel's program) would still make sense

**Key Doctrines Unique to Grace:**
- The Body of Christ
- Eternal security
- Christ as Head of the Body
- Salvation by grace through faith, not works
- Mystery revealed to Paul
- Jews and Gentiles saved the same way (no national distinction)

**Biblical Books of Grace Period:**
- Romans through Philemon (Paul's epistles)
- Hebrews through Revelation (also apply to this period)

### Rise and Fall of Israel

**Exaltation (Genesis 12 forward):**
- God took Abram, changed name to Abraham
- Established nation of Israel
- Given the Law
- Given circumcision
- Other nations (Gentiles) had to come through Israel to reach God
- Israel elevated despite repeated failures
- Kingdom split between Israel and Judah
- Israel had no good kings; Judah had only a few

**Fall (at the Cross/Acts period):**
- Messiah came and was rejected
- Messiah ascended
- Stephen stoned (Acts 7)
- Paul's conversion introduced the mystery
- Middle wall of partition broken down
- National Israel set aside

**Future Restoration:**
- Israel exalted again in Tribulation Period
- Israel exalted in Millennial Kingdom
- What was stopped will be picked up again

### Transition Periods

**Between Israel's program and Grace:**
- Represented by angled line on timeline
- Not an abrupt change but gradual transition
- Book of Acts shows this transition
- First 14 chapters focus on Peter
- Last 14 chapters focus on Paul

**Speculative period between Rapture and Tribulation:**
- Based on Daniel 2
- Time for Antichrist to emerge (from fallen angels, like Genesis 6)
- Antichrist comes from that seed
- Exact length unknown

### Books of the Bible on Timeline

**Old Testament (before Cross):**
- Genesis through Malachi positioned before the Cross

**Transitional:**
- Matthew through Acts (written about events leading to and including transition)
- These books reference the Second Coming, not the Rapture
- Rapture was not revealed during this time

**Age of Grace:**
- Romans through Philemon
- Hebrews through Revelation

### Distinguishing Rapture from Second Coming

**The Rapture:**
- Church meets Christ in the clouds
- Judgment Seat of Christ follows
- Not described in Matthew, Mark, Luke, or John (was still mystery)

**The Second Coming:**
- Christ comes to earth
- Described in the Gospels
- What Jesus and the 12 taught about
- Cannot apply Matthew 24 to the Rapture

**Why the distinction matters:**
- Many Christians don't know the difference
- Some know there's a difference but misapply scriptures
- Matthew through John only knew about Second Coming
- The mystery (including Rapture) had not yet been revealed

### Different Preaching About Jesus

**During His Earthly Ministry:**
- Jesus as Messiah
- The coming King
- Would set up kingdom after tribulation

**According to the Mystery (Grace):**
- Christ as Head of the Body
- Salvation by grace through faith, not works
- Death, burial, and resurrection as means of salvation
- Revealed to Paul by the ascended Christ

**Doctrinal Distinctions:**

### Using the Timeline as a Teaching Tool

**For Others:**
- Visual way to explain God's program
- Shows distinctions clearly
- Prevents confusion
- Can be expanded for specific studies (e.g., Book of Revelation)

**For Personal Study:**
- Start with notebook paper, progress to legal paper, poster board
- Can make multiple smaller sections
- Test yourself by drawing from memory
- Set goals to explain sections for 10-15 minutes
- Add detail progressively
- Reference tool for Bible study

**Expandable Sections:**
- Can focus on one section and add detail
- Example: Blow up Tribulation/Kingdom section for Revelation study
- Can add artistic elements (like Clarence Larkin's timelines)
- Always prove and test what others teach

### Avoiding Contradictions

**Examples of apparent contradictions without right division:**
- Turn the other cheek (Sermon on the Mount) vs. defending your household
- Under Kingdom: can turn other cheek because Christ rules with rod of iron
- Under Grace: can defend yourself (Romans 12 - live peaceably as much as lies in you)
- Unpardonable sin: could be committed under Israel's program, not under Grace
- Mark of the beast: can be taken during Tribulation, not possible now
