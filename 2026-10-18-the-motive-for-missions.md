## Summary
Tim Faulk presents missions as "God's Magnificent Obsession," drawing from the 1929 book and Rock Hudson film to illustrate God's consuming passion for all people to be saved and come to knowledge of the truth. He argues that God's will is clearly stated in 1 Timothy 2:4 and challenges believers to share God's obsession by being motivated by Christ's love, His sacrifice, the lostness of sinners, and eternity—rather than being fixated on location and comfort.

## Scripture
**1 Timothy 2:4** — God "will have all men to be saved, and to come unto the knowledge of the truth"

**2 Corinthians 5:14** — "For the love of Christ constraineth us; because we thus judge, that if one died for all, then were all dead"

**Philippians 2:5-8** — Christ's sacrifice: made Himself of no reputation, took form of a servant, became obedient unto death on the cross

## Key Themes
- **God's Magnificent Obsession**: God's overarching will is for all people to be saved and come to knowledge of the truth, not just to be saved but to understand right division and biblical truth
- **Four Motives for Missions**: The love of Christ that constrains us, Christ's sacrifice for us, the lost condition of sinners, and the reality of eternity
- **Location versus Eternity**: Believers must be more concerned about eternity and the displaced than about their own comfort and location
- **Knowledge versus Action**: Knowing Bible truth is insufficient if believers don't share it with others through various means including social media and church planting

## Notes

### The Magnificent Obsession Illustration
- Based on 1929 book by Thompson and Rock Hudson/Jane Wyman film
- Plot: playboy indirectly causes doctor's death, becomes obsessed with helping the doctor's widow who went blind
- Goes to medical school to restore her sight and adopt the deceased doctor's philosophy of helping people
- Parallel: God the Father has a magnificent obsession with His Son Jesus Christ
- This obsession manifests in missions—getting all people saved to bring glory to Christ

### God's Will for All People
- 1 Timothy 2:4: God's will is that ALL men be saved
- Not just saved, but "come unto the knowledge of the truth"
- Can be saved without knowing the truth—many believers don't understand right division
- Story: Retired missionary (55 years in ministry) heard right division teaching for first time, joined the church
- Over half of 8+ billion people will never hear even a "Brad presentation" of the gospel

### Four Motives for Involvement in Missions

#### 1. The Love of Christ Constrains Us (2 Corinthians 5:14)
- John 3:16: God so loved the world He gave His Son
- Love of Christ motivates us to love fellow man
- Some people easier to love than others
- Filipino people described as easy to love: servant hearts, hospitable, defer to others, love the Lord
- Filipino pastors consumed with discussing Bible: mystery vs. prophetic program, King James vs. other versions
- Burmese people also kind, hospitable, gentle in teaching their culture to foreigners
- Over 90% of Philippines is Roman Catholic—headed to hell without gospel
- Over 90% of Burma is Buddhist—headed to hell without gospel

#### 2. The Lord's Sacrifice for Us (Philippians 2:5-8)
- Christ didn't merely tell us to preach gospel—He gave Himself totally
- Being in form of God, didn't consider equality with God something to be grasped
- Made Himself of no reputation
- Took form of servant
- Made in likeness of men
- Humbled Himself, became obedient unto death, even death of the cross

#### 3. The Lost Condition of Sinners
- Over half of 8-9 billion people went to bed with assurance they'll never hear gospel
- Should be motivated by lostness of others
- Should rehearse salvation testimony to share with neighbors
- More comfortable witnessing to strangers than neighbors/family
- Philippines hotel experience: witnessed to Australian, British, German, American, half dozen Filipinos
- Stayed near smoking area where people gather—"go where the fish are"
- American man (77) just had lung tumor removed, smoking, raised in "hardcore Baptist family"
- Man's testimony: too many rules, turned him away from faith
- Salvation not about rules but about person of Jesus Christ
- Handwriting of ordinances against us removed, nailed to cross

#### 4. Moved by Eternity
- Older you get, more you think about eternity
- Losing best friend (Pastor Paul Little, April 2020) makes you think about eternity
- Losing wife of 40 years suddenly and unexpectedly makes you think about eternity
- Realize life is just a vapor (as Bible says)
- Location doesn't matter as much as eternity

### The Challenge: Why Not You? Why Not Now?

#### The Problem of Location Fixation
- Midland Baptist Church members know more Bible than any preacher on Church Road
- Pastor Pat described as "best Bible teacher in America" and "best Bible teacher I've ever known"
- But doesn't do rest of world any good if knowledge isn't shared
- Social media available: Facebook, Instagram, YouTube (not TikTok)
- Can share what's learned with lost people
- Could plant churches in Michigan, Alabama, Burma that teach right division
- Burma: 48 churches currently supported, 75 ready to go, but 8.5 million in Yangon alone, 60 million in country
- Need more than 48 Bible-believing churches for 60 million people

#### Burma Update
- Before military overthrow (February 2021): 21 churches
- After overthrow: 48 churches
- Military leadership is Buddhist, oppressing Buddhist population
- Burmese people questioning whether Buddhism is worth having
- Buddhism supposed to mean peace, karma, tranquility
- Military's actions "enough to make Buddha roll over in his grave"
- Buddha has a grave (unlike Christ)
- Muhammad also has a grave

#### Three Reasons People Come to Church
1. Learn what to believe—Pastor Pat teaches/preaches what to believe
2. Learn how to behave—personal example: didn't know what to wear, learned not to sit in Mr. Sibley's seat on front row
3. Find a place to belong—Midland Baptist Church provides all three

#### The Displaced Need Churches
- Believers who can't find church home that believes right Bible
- Unbelievers searching for truth
- Should be more concerned about displaced than our own place

#### The Danger of Fixation on Wrong Things
- Nothing wrong with coffee and talking Alabama football
- Problem when that's ALL you do
- Can hoot and holler on Saturday (even "cuss every now and then" when Tennessee beats Alabama)
- But if you keep doing ONLY that, you have a problem

#### Time Wasted "Praying About It"
- Gospel happened "in due time"
- Revealed by Paul who was born "out of due time"
- People waste time praying about things they should just do
- "I'll pray about coming to missions conference Wednesday/Thursday/Friday/next Sunday"
- "I'll pray about coming to visitation"
- "I'll pray about it" when personally hindered from serving God
- Could completely surrender to God's will today—why wait for commitment card Thursday night?
