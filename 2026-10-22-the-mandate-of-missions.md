## Summary
Tim Faulk distinguishes between the traditional "Great Commission" given to the twelve apostles and the commitment to the ministry of reconciliation given to the Church through Paul. He emphasizes that while believers are not commissioned to baptize in the name of the Father, Son, and Holy Ghost or perform signs and wonders, they are committed to proclaiming the gospel as ambassadors for Christ. The sermon challenges believers to examine their level of commitment to this ministry in light of appearing before the judgment seat of Christ.

## Scripture
2 Corinthians 5:11-21 — Paul explains that believers have been given the ministry and word of reconciliation, serving as ambassadors for Christ who plead with the world to be reconciled to God through the finished work of Christ.

## Key Themes
- **The Great Commitment vs. The Great Commission**: Believers today operate under Paul's commission to proclaim reconciliation, not the twelve apostles' commission to baptize and perform signs.
- **Transformation Through Reconciliation**: Salvation produces a new creature with a new position (in Christ), new person (new man), and new perspective (old things passed away).
- **The Ministry of Reconciliation**: God has committed to believers the responsibility of proclaiming how people can be reconciled to God through Christ.
- **Ambassadorship for Christ**: Believers represent Christ as His ambassadors, pleading with the world on His behalf.
- **Commitment and Accountability**: The judgment seat of Christ will evaluate believers' level of commitment to the ministry they've been entrusted with.

### The Traditional Great Commission (Matthew 28:19-20)
- "Go ye therefore and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Ghost"
- "Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you"
- Many churches display this as a plaque and seek to fulfill it

### Problems with Applying Matthew 28 Today
- Matthew 23:1-3: Jesus commanded disciples to observe what the scribes and Pharisees taught (sitting in Moses' seat)
- This included keeping the Sabbath and the 613 laws of the Old Testament
- The command was to observe the law, though not to follow the Pharisees' hypocritical example
- If we take Matthew 28 literally for today, we must apply all Christ commanded them

### The Great Commission in Mark 16:14-18
- Context: Jesus appeared to the eleven and rebuked them for unbelief and hardness of heart
- Verse 15: "Go ye into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature"
- Verse 16: "He that believeth and is baptized shall be saved"—requires belief AND baptism for salvation
- Verse 17-18: Signs that follow believers:
  - Cast out devils
  - Speak with new tongues
  - Take up serpents
  - Drink deadly things without harm
  - Lay hands on the sick for recovery
- Many stop at "preach the gospel to every creature" (full stop) and ignore the rest
- Through right division, we know why we're not doing these signs today

### Our Commitment: 2 Corinthians 5:17-21
- Verse 17: "If any man be in Christ, he is a new creature"
- Verse 18: God "hath reconciled us to himself by Jesus Christ, and hath given to us the ministry of reconciliation"
- Verse 19: God "hath committed unto us the word of reconciliation"
- We don't have a "Great Commission" per se, but we have a great commitment
- Paul went into all the world (Colossians 1:6), preached to every creature (Colossians 1:23), and to all nations (Romans 16:25)
- We follow Paul's example (1 Corinthians 11:1)

### The Importance of Commitment
- We're in a day where people don't want to commit to anything
- The commitment card given last Sunday requires prayer and decision
- Examples of commitment:
  - Marriage: "I do" means commitment for better or worse, riches or poverty, sickness or health
  - Church membership: Coming even when it's raining or snowing
  - Story of the young man who would visit his girlfriend "if it ain't raining" on the muddy road

### Christ's Ultimate Commitment
- Jesus left heaven's glory
- Made Himself of no reputation
- Took the form of a servant
- Was obedient unto death, even death of the cross
- Endured the shame
- This is the greatest commitment in all history
- Based on this commitment, God has committed the ministry of reconciliation to us

### The Transformation of Salvation (2 Corinthians 5:17)
- **New Position**: "If any man be in Christ"—seated together with Him in heavenly places now
- **New Person**: "He is a new creature"—we have a new man to live for, not the flesh
- **New Perspective**: "Old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new"
- Speaker was saved at age 25; Brother Bill at age 50; Heath at age 32
- Coming to Christ later in life means "a heavy sack of rocks" from past sins
- Must forget those things which are behind (Philippians 3:13)
- The committed Christian no longer views the world through human eyes
- We see people as souls for whom Christ died—whether in Michigan, Philippines, Burma, or around the world
- We see people as souls regardless of appearance or stage of life

### The Ministry of Reconciliation
- God didn't save us simply to sit, soak, and savor in rich doctrine
- He wants us to come to the knowledge of truth, but not just to sit there
- He wants us to take what we know and share it with others
- **We've been reconciled**: God has brought us back into fellowship with Himself
- **We've been entrusted**: God has given us responsibility for this ministry
- The ministry belongs to God, but the responsibility has been entrusted to us
- We are God's hands, feet, and fingers
- God trusts sinners like us with this ministry—what grace and mercy!

### The Message of Reconciliation (2 Corinthians 5:19)
- "To wit, that God was in Christ, reconciling the world unto himself"
- This is the glorious gospel of Jesus Christ
- It is not:
  - Man's philosophy
  - Politics
  - Culture (American, Asian, or African)
- It is the gospel of reconciliation
- God has provided forgiveness: "not imputing their trespasses unto them"
- Romans 5:8: "God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us"
- God did not hold sins against people to prevent them from hearing the gospel
- He's given everyone a chance

### Ambassadors for Christ (2 Corinthians 5:20)
- "Now then we are ambassadors for Christ"
- One of the great missions verses in all Scripture
- An ambassador doesn't represent himself but someone else
- We represent:
  - The Lord Jesus Christ
  - His church
  - His person
  - His work
  - His message
- "As though God did beseech you by us"—God pleads with the world through His people
- Example: Brother Mario Lockson in the jungles of Cebu stands in Christ's stead, pleading with jungle people to be reconciled to God
- God wants all men and women everywhere to be saved and come to the knowledge of the truth
- He's entrusted this magnificent mission to us

  - This ministry of reconciliation that's been given to us
- Philippians 2:12: "Work out your own salvation with fear and trembling"—in light of the judgment seat of Christ
