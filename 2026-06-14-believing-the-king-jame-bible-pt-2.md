## Summary
Hugh Taylor continues his series on the King James Bible by establishing foundational precursors necessary for understanding why the KJV is the Word of God in English. He addresses Satan's strategy to complicate the Bible issue while God desires to keep it simple and straightforward. The sermon emphasizes that the hope of the entire human race hinges on having the word of truth, and believers need to understand and defend the integrity of Scripture.

## Scripture
2 Corinthians 11:3 — Paul warns that just as Satan beguiled Eve through his subtlety, believers' minds can be corrupted from the simplicity that is in Christ.

2 Timothy 3:16-17 — All Scripture is given by inspiration of God and is profitable for doctrine, reproof, correction, and instruction in righteousness, that the man of God may be perfect, throughly (through) furnished unto all good works.

John 8:31-32 — Jesus tells believers that if they continue in His Word, they shall know the truth, and the truth shall make them free.

## Key Themes
- **Satan's Complexity vs. God's Simplicity**: Satan wants to make the Bible issue complicated and confusing to deceive and dissuade believers, while God wants to make it simple and straightforward.
- **Integrity as Central Issue**: All three main reasons for believing the King James Bible relate to the issue of integrity - the entire unimpaired state, moral soundness, incorruptness, and uprightness.
- **Truth as Foundation of Hope**: The hope of the entire human race hinges on having the word of truth; faith is leveraged on the word of God to effect change in life.
- **Walking in Integrity**: The just man walks in his integrity, and his children are blessed after him (Proverbs 27).
