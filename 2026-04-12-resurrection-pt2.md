## Summary
Bill Baughman continues the Resurrection series by establishing that Jesus came from heaven and can be trusted about heavenly things. The sermon explores the gospel foundation of Christ's death, burial, and resurrection, examines the gifts believers receive through faith, and defends the resurrection against scientific skepticism using the scientific method itself and historical evidence.

## Scripture
1 Corinthians 15:1-8 — Paul presents the core gospel: Christ died for our sins, was buried, rose the third day, and appeared to many witnesses.

## Key Themes
- Jesus' heavenly origin and trustworthiness
- The gospel as verifiable historical fact
- Gifts received through believing in the resurrection
- The limitations of the scientific method in evaluating the resurrection
- Non-biblical historical evidence for Christ's death and resurrection
- Law of authenticity and historical certainty

## Notes

### Jesus Came from Heaven
- Jesus is from heaven and therefore can be trusted about heavenly things, just as we trust people about their own homes
- Jesus witnessed heavenly events (Lucifer's fall, celebrations in heaven) and speaks with authority about the kingdom of God

### The Gospel Foundation (1 Corinthians 15:1-8)
- Paul received the gospel directly from Christ through revelation, not from other apostles
- The complete gospel includes: death, burial, resurrection, and appearing
- Christ died to pay for our sins according to the Scriptures
- Christ rose the third day and appeared to Cephas, the twelve, 500 believers, James, the apostles, and Paul

### Gifts of Believing the Gospel
- Born again
- Past, present, and future sins forgiven
- Circumcised without hands (Spirit separated from flesh)
- Sealed with the Holy Spirit
- Eternal life in heaven
- Protected from spiritual powers—nothing can separate believers from salvation
- Part of the mystery and body of Christ
- New species—a fellowship different from Old Testament Israel or Gentiles alone
- Community and fellowship with other believers

### Two Faiths Based on Historical Evidence
- Judaism is based on the verifiable events of the Exodus
- Christianity is based on the verifiable death, burial, resurrection, and appearances of Jesus Christ
- Both require belief in specific historical events; without the resurrection, Christian faith is meaningless

### The Scientific Method Cannot Evaluate the Resurrection
- The scientific method requires: observation, background research, testable hypothesis, experiment/testing, data analysis, and communication of results
- The scientific method can only evaluate natural, observable, repeatable processes
- The resurrection lies outside these parameters—it is not repeatable or testable
- Scientists claiming the resurrection "is not scientifically possible" are exceeding the scope of their methodology
- Science has no tools to investigate the resurrection and should remain silent on the matter
- Those claiming to use scientific methodology while dismissing the resurrection are engaged in circular reasoning

### Historical Evidence for the Crucifixion and Death
Non-biblical sources confirming the crucifixion:
- **Tacitus** (116 AD): Roman historian reporting that Jesus (Christus) suffered extreme penalty during the reign of Tiberius
- **Josephus** (93-94 AD): Jewish historian stating Pilate condemned Jesus to crucifixion
- **Talmud**: Jewish source referring to crucifixion on the eve of Passover
- **Lucian** (2nd century): Greek satirist who mocked Christians for worshiping a crucified man

These sources are significant because they come from opposition—hostile witnesses whose own beliefs make their testimony more credible.

### Medical Evidence for Christ's Death
- The Mayo Clinic analysis shows death from the crucifixion resulted from multiple factors:
  - Extreme stress causing blood and sweat (hematohidrosis)
  - Severe trauma from Roman scourging with metal-tipped whips
  - Blood loss and hypovolemic shock
  - Mechanical effects of crucifixion: suspension, difficulty breathing, asphyxiation
  - Spear thrust into the side (piercing the heart)
  - Blood and water discharge indicates death had already occurred

### Law of Authenticity and Historical Certainty
- Just as a stolen car case uses the law of authenticity (bill of sale, title, witness testimony), the resurrection can be proven through:
  - Gathering facts and evidence
  - Focusing on those facts
  - Allowing facts to force a conclusion based on evidence
  - This produces historical certainty

### Reaching the Lost
- Believers who are saved can consider spiritual things; the natural man cannot receive spiritual things as he lacks the Spirit
- Using non-biblical evidence, historians, and philosophers can provide data to move lost people toward seeking truth from Scripture
- Opposition sources make evidence more credible to skeptics
