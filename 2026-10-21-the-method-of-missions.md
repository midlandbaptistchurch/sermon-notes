## Summary
Tim Faulk presents the biblical method for missions from Acts 14, arguing that God's blueprint for world evangelization begins with the local church and expands through duplicatable ministry. He updates ministry statistics from Myanmar, reporting growth from 21 to 48 churches planted from Hope Baptist Church, and challenges the inverted modern model that prioritizes facilities over sending. Using Paul's missionary journey as the pattern, he demonstrates that effective missions involves evangelizing the lost, establishing believers in truth, equipping disciples through tribulation, and entrusting leadership to faithful men who can teach others.

## Scripture
Acts 14:19-27 — Paul and Barnabas are stoned, recover, preach the gospel in Derby, then return to strengthen disciples, ordain elders in every church, and report back to their sending church in Antioch.

Acts 13:1-3 — The Holy Spirit calls Barnabas and Saul to missionary work from the church at Antioch, establishing the local church as the launching point for missions.

2 Timothy 2:1-2 — Paul instructs Timothy to commit what he has learned to faithful men who will be able to teach others also, creating a multiplication model for ministry expansion.

## Key Themes
- **The Local Church as Mission's Foundation**: All biblical missions begin and end at a local church, as demonstrated by Paul's journey starting and concluding at Antioch Baptist Church
- **Inverted Modern Model**: Contemporary churches have inverted the biblical priority triangle by placing facilities first, saints second, and sending last, rather than the scriptural order
- **Duplicatable Ministry**: Expansion happens through teaching simple, duplicatable truths—belief in the King James Bible and right division—that any faithful person can learn and pass on
- **The Five E's of Missions**: Evangelizing the lost, establishing believers through teaching, equipping disciples through tribulation, entrusting ordained leaders, and expanding the ministry
- **God's Glory Through Weakness**: God robs missionaries of personal glory through adversity and weakness so that all glory goes to His Son, the Lord Jesus Christ

### The Corrupted Model vs. Biblical Model
- Modern churches focus on "Great Commission" language, but Paul's mandate is the "great commitment" to the ministry of reconciliation
- Current inverted triangle: Facility (top priority), Saints (middle), Sending (bottom)
- Biblical triangle: Sending (top priority), Saints (middle), Facility (minimal emphasis)
- Paul says God dwells not in temples made with hands; Christ sent him not to baptize but to preach the gospel
- Myanmar and Philippine churches meet mostly in houses with minimal facilities but maximum emphasis on sending

### The Blueprint from Acts 14

**1. Evangelizing the Lost (v. 21)**
- "When they had preached the gospel to that city"
- Message: Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures, was buried, and rose again the third day
- Focus always on salvation by grace through faith, plus nothing, minus nothing
- Preached to Jews first, then turned to Gentiles when Jews rejected the message

**2. Establishing Believers (v. 21)**
- "And had taught many"
- Acts 20:20 - Paul kept back nothing profitable, taught publicly and house to house
- Acts 20:26-27 - "I am pure from the blood of all men, for I have not shunned to declare unto you all the counsel of God"
- Taught Jews about their fathers receiving the law and accepting Christ as King and Savior
- Taught Gentiles they were cut off at Tower of Babel but not forsaken; door now opened through grace
- Goal: "all men to be saved and come to the knowledge of the truth" (1 Timothy 2:4)

**3. Equipping Disciples (v. 22)**
- "Confirming the souls of the disciples, and exhorting them to continue in the faith"
- "We must through much tribulation enter into the kingdom of God"
- Not entering the tribulation period, but experiencing tribulation in ministry
- God uses trials to confirm and equip leadership
- Example: Bank account wiped out before first Myanmar trip in 2010; zero balance but God restored it and the trip happened
- Modern belief that adversity means God isn't in it is wrong; adversity often proves God's involvement
- Preparation and warning that much tribulation accompanies doing anything for the Lord

**4. Entrusting Leadership (v. 23)**
- "When they had ordained them elders in every church"
- More than one elder, and multiple churches receiving ordained leadership
- Objective: indigenous reproducing leadership to plant churches
- Missions are the responsibility of the local church regardless of location
- Moving from single-cell ministry (one person doing everything) to multi-cell ministry (entrusting others)
- Brother James Lova and Brother Mario as examples of faithful men doing the work

**5. Expanding Ministry (v. 24-26)**
- Ministry multiplied through Pisidia, Pamphilia, Attalia, Perga
- 2 Timothy 2:2 - "The things that thou hast heard of me among many witnesses, the same commit thou to faithful men, who shall be able to teach others also"
- Key to Paul evangelizing the world in first century: teaching faithful men to teach other faithful men
- This creates a movement that explodes in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ

### The Duplication Principle
- What is done must be duplicatable for ministry to expand
- Brother Mario and Brother James succeed because "if that dumb American can do it, I can do it"
- Simple foundation: everyone gets one book (King James Bible), believes every word is in the right place
- Everyone persuaded every punctuation mark is in the right place
- Once you believe that, the only way to make sense is through right division
- Understanding the parenthetical age: not prophesied in Old Testament, revealed through Paul
- Cannot find the rapture in Jeremiah or Matthew 24
- Paul reveals the mystery: "We shall not all sleep, but we shall all be changed"
- Right division: Genesis 11 (Tower of Babel - Gentiles set aside) → Abraham called → Israel blessed and tied to land → Parenthetical age (Gentiles saved by grace) → Rapture → Tribulation (Jacob's trouble) → Second Coming
- Draw circles on a map, draw an arrow, duplicate what we believe about rightly dividing the Bible
- Ministry expands when people have the guts to believe the book and rightly divide it
- Those leaving Hope Baptist Church may not be as intelligent as Brother James, but they don't have to be

### God's Glory in Weakness
- Men must be robbed of glory so it goes to God's Son, the Lord Jesus Christ
- Examples of being robbed of glory: zero bank account, sleep injuries, weakness with age
- "My grace is sufficient" - God's grace must be and is sufficient
- Getting older brings weakness and increased need for God's grace
- God delights in watching ministry expand so participants can only say: "God, in my infirmities, will you give me grace to persevere?"
- The weakest vessel gives the most glory to God

### Personal Testimony: Bank Account Incident
- February 2010, preparing to visit Myanmar for first time to meet Brother James
- Just bought tickets from Cebu City to Yangon
- Logged on to Bank of America account to pay bills - both checking and savings accounts showed zero
- Someone of different nationality obtained his Michigan driver's license, put their picture on it
- Went to Bank of America branch in New Jersey, checked balance on one account
- Went to another branch down the street and withdrew all money from both accounts
- Thought maybe God didn't want him to go to Myanmar because of the adversity
- Took six weeks to explain and get money restored
- Had to write letters and call people in states explaining bills would be late
- Christian bank investigator got the case and said "I don't care what it takes, we're going to fix this"
- Thankful he didn't give up - went to Myanmar in March, met James Lova
- Meeting happened "because I met him then through adversity"
