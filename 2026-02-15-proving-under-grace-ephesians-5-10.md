## Summary
Associate Pastor Mark Simpson explores the believer's responsibility under grace to "prove what is acceptable unto the Lord" from Ephesians 5:10. He emphasizes that we must actively test and approve God's will through Scripture rather than relying solely on circumstances, comparing this with Romans 12:1-2 and other supporting passages.

## Scripture
Ephesians 5:10 — Continuing from the command to walk as children of light in verse 8.

## Key Themes
- Proving and testing what is acceptable to God
- Walking as children of light vs. darkness
- The difference between perfect will and permissible will of God
- Bible study methods and taking time to "crawl" through Scripture
- Examining ourselves according to Scripture
- Making decisions based on principles rather than circumstances

## Notes

### Bible Study Approach
- Learn to "crawl" through Scripture - go slowly and spend more time
- Change your reading plan if it's not bearing fruit
- Take notes and leave question marks for later study
- Use Scripture context over dictionary definitions

### Review of Ephesians 5 Context
- Seven walks mentioned in Ephesians, including walk in love and walk as children of light
- Two types of inheritance: one secured through salvation, another that can be lost based on our walk
- Contrast between darkness (ignorance, blindness, alienation) and walking as children of light

### The Responsibility to Prove
- "Proving" means to test, approve, or try things
- Our job to prove what is good, acceptable, and perfect will of God
- Don't determine God's will based solely on circumstances or "open doors"
- Principles should override circumstances in decision-making

### The Proved Will vs. Perfect/Permissible Will
- Many speak of perfect will vs. permissible will of God
- The "proved will" is the perfect will discovered through our testing
- God gives grace believers responsibility to choose and prove His will
- Sometimes involves choosing between good options based on what brings most glory

### Supporting Passages
- **Romans 12:1-2**: Present bodies as living sacrifice; be transformed by renewing of mind to prove God's will
- **Philippians 1:9-10**: Approve things that are excellent, not just what gets you by
- **1 Thessalonians 5:21**: Prove all things, hold fast that which is good
- **2 Corinthians 13:5**: Examine yourselves whether you're in the faith

### Practical Application
- Make decisions with a spirit of excellence, not just adequacy
- Use discernment and judgment alongside Scripture
- Sometimes decisions require collection of principles creating a mindset rather than single verse
- Our responsibility to prove ourselves according to Scripture, not wait for God to prove us
