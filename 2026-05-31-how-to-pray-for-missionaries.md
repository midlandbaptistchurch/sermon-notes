## Summary
Jonathan Earnhart challenges the church to be faithful prayer partners for missionaries, sharing personal stories from his family's missionary work in Peru. He provides biblical instruction on specific ways to pray for missionaries, emphasizing that prayer is more essential than financial support and can make a tangible difference in the lives of those on the mission field.

## Scripture
Colossians 4:2 — "Continue in prayer and watch in the same with thanksgiving with all praying also for us that God would open unto us a door of utterance to speak the mystery of Christ."

## Key Themes
- The power and necessity of prayer for missionaries
- Specific biblical requests to pray for missionaries (boldness, protection, effective ministry, safe travels, refreshment)
- Personal testimony of prayer's impact during crisis situations
- The importance of regular communication and encouragement from supporting churches
- God's faithfulness through preparation and difficult seasons
- Generational impact of faithful ministry and gospel witness

## Notes

### Background and Personal Journey
- Jonathan Earnhart and his family have been in missionary work in Peru since 1999
- They spent nearly 15 years on the mission field and recently returned to the United States for family needs
- They are now preparing to return to Peru with their youngest daughter, Madison
- The Earnharts have seen significant growth in the churches they've helped establish in Tacna, Peru

### Five Key Areas to Pray for Missionaries

1. **Pray for Boldness** — Ask God to give missionaries courage to proclaim the gospel boldly (Ephesians 6:18, Colossians 4:3-4)

2. **Pray that God's Word Would Spread** — Intercede that the gospel message will have free course and be glorified in the communities where missionaries serve (2 Thessalonians 3:1)

3. **Pray for Protection** — Lift up missionaries' physical safety and deliverance from danger and persecution (2 Thessalonians 3:2)
   - Jonathan shared his own experience of being assaulted by robbers while his 12-year-old son witnessed
   - Missionaries face unique dangers that require prayer covering

4. **Pray for Effective Ministry** — Intercede that those who hear the gospel will grow, learn, change, and become disciple-makers (John 15:5)

5. **Pray for Refreshment and Safe Travels** — Ask God to sustain missionaries spiritually through God's word and Christian fellowship, and protect them during journeys

### The Impact of Prayer Communication
- Most missionaries have 35-40 supporting churches, but hear from only 2-5 regularly
- Simple encouragement—a letter, email, or prayer update—can sustain missionaries through difficult seasons
- Personal testimony: A friend prayed for Becky while buying asparagus at Walmart (she saw a Peruvian label). At that exact moment, Becky was trapped in a riot on a bus with their daughter. The prayer was instrumental in their protection and safe passage.

### Generational Impact
- One girl (Michelle) attended a Kids Club outreach and eventually came to faith, married a church member (Jacob), and now serves faithfully in ministry
- Faithful ministry produces lasting generational fruit

### Practical Encouragement
- Go to the church's mission board and select a missionary to pray for this week
- Contact missionaries via their prayer letters or email
- Prayer partnerships are more essential than financial donations
- A simple message saying "we prayed for you" can transform a missionary's perspective and perseverance

### The Earnhart Family's Current Vision
- Returning to Peru to strengthen existing churches and establish new ministry
- Focus areas include:
  - Establishing a Christian school in Habitat (building already in progress)
  - Purchasing land in the developing community of Señor de los Milagros for a church and leadership training center
  - Preparing leaders for sustainable ministry across southern Peru
- This season is marked by clarity and sustainability rather than survival mode
