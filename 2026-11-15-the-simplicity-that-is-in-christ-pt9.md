## Summary
Pastor Jim Payne concludes the "Simplicity That is in Christ" series by examining the Apostle Paul's life strategy found in Philippians 3. Paul's philosophy of Christian living can be summarized in three simple words: focus (this one thing I do - knowing Christ), forgetting (continually putting behind past failures and victories), and future (pressing toward the mark for the prize of the high calling). Like an athlete running a race to win, believers must have a clear strategy to successfully complete their Christian life and attain eternal glory.

## Scripture
Philippians 3:10-14 — Paul expresses his desire to know Christ, the power of His resurrection, and the fellowship of His sufferings. He declares he has not yet attained perfection, but focuses on one thing: forgetting what is behind and reaching forward to what is ahead, pressing toward the mark for the prize of the high calling of God in Christ Jesus.

## Key Themes
- **Paul as Pattern**: The Apostle Paul serves as the pattern believer, demonstrating tenacity and faithfulness that brought him to the end of life with the testimony "I fought a good fight, I finished my course, I kept the faith"
- **Focus on Christ**: The singular focus of the Christian life must be knowing Christ and being conformed to His image, bringing all activities under this one umbrella purpose
- **Forgetting the Past**: Believers must practice continual forgetting (present participle) of things behind - both failures and successes - because we keep remembering and must keep putting them behind us
- **Future Glory**: There is a "high calling" - a literal, physical position in the heavenlies with varying degrees of glory for believers, motivating us to press toward the mark

## Notes

### Introduction: Paul's Testimony and Strategy
- Paul's life demonstrates unmatched tenacity and steadfastness in ministry
- 2 Timothy 4: "I am now ready to be offered, the time of my departure is at hand. I fought a good fight, I finished my course, I kept the faith"
- Paul confidently affirmed he fought a good fight - not all biblical figures could say this (Moses, Elijah, David, Jonah all had moments of wanting to quit)
- In contrast, many today fall into discouragement, depression, and lack the will to stay in the race faithfully
- Young people today look at the future without much hope due to cultural wars, woke ideology, geopolitical tensions, political hatred, financial insecurity, misinformation
- Despite world conditions, believers are called to run our race to win, to be light in a crooked and perverse generation (Philippians 2:14-15)
- We don't race against each other but against the world, the flesh, and the devil

### Paul's Use of Athletic Metaphors
- Paul frequently uses sports metaphors: wrestling, boxing, weight lifting, running races
- 2 Timothy 2 contains multiple metaphors: children, soldiers, athletes, farmers, workmen, vessels, servants
- Believers are running a race on a course God has set, expected to run to win
- Paul's pattern provides the strategy for successful Christian living

### First Word: Focus (Verse 13 - "This One Thing I Do")
- Philippians 3:10: "That I may know him and the power of his resurrection and the fellowship of his sufferings, being made conformable unto his death"
- Paul did many things in life, but brought everything to a single point of focalization
- Paul's focus was entirely on Jesus Christ - knowing Him, His resurrection power, His sufferings, conformity to His death
- Philippians 1:21: "For to me to live is Christ" (emphasis on "to me" - this was Paul's personal commitment)
- Philippians 3:7: "But what things were gain to me, those I counted loss for Christ"
- Philippians 3:9: To be found in Him, having righteousness through faith of Christ
- Romans 13:14: "Put ye on the Lord Jesus Christ"
- Galatians 2:20: "I am crucified with Christ: nevertheless I live; yet not I, but Christ"
- Paul's goal was not fame, fortune, or fanfare, but to know Christ and be conformed to His image

### God's Purpose: Conformity to Christ's Image
- Romans 8:28-29: All things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to His purpose - predestinated to be conformed to the image of His Son
- God's goal for every believer is conformity to the image of Jesus Christ
- One day God will fill His creation with those conformed to Christ's image
- The noise and confusion of the world couldn't phase Paul because his focus was Christ

### The Problem of Compartmentalization
- Christians today struggle with establishing priorities
- Many compartmentalize their lives - putting things in separate boxes
- This approach lacks the "one thing I do" philosophy
- Need to have one focus: becoming more like Jesus Christ

### Matthew 11:28 - "Learn of Me"
- Jesus welcomed the oppressed people of Israel: "Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest"
- "Take my yoke upon you, and learn of me"
- The answer to oppression is to learn of Christ
- Learn of His deity, trinity, virgin birth, resurrection, redemptive power, healing power
- Learn of the love of God in Christ - unconditional love from which nothing can separate us
- Learn of His mercy and grace (John 1: grace and truth came by Jesus Christ)
- Learn of His coming - the catching away of the body of Christ before the day of wrath
- Learn of His second coming, His wrath against sin
- When people need counsel: "Learn of Him" - He is the answer

### Application of Focus
- Nothing wrong with success, toys, generational wealth, material possessions
- But all must be under the umbrella of life's focus: becoming more like Jesus Christ
- All worldly things will pass away, but Christ will not
- One day we stand before Him
- Questions to consider: What's the focus of your life? What's the one thing you do? What are you moving toward? What are you living for? What gets you out of bed in the morning?

### Second Word: Forgetting (Verse 13 - "Forgetting Those Things Which Are Behind")
- Paul didn't say "forget" or "having forgotten" but "forgetting" - present participle verb
- This means continuous action: do it now and keep doing it
- Must keep forgetting because we keep remembering
- We remember hard times, bitter times, failures, victories, ups and downs, joys and defeats
- Must put it all behind us over and over again
- Not about being unable to have memories - memories are precious
- Must learn from the past or we repeat it
- But don't dwell in the past or live there
- Life is going forward, not backward
- Forgetting is absolutely necessary to run the race with joy and win the prize

### Paul's Personal Example of Forgetting
- Paul wasn't saved until later in life (pastor's testimony: saved at 21, been saved almost 50 years)
- Pastor's experience: must keep forgetting the "portraits hung in the hallway of my mind" - garbage from living without God
- Memories keep coming up, but won't let them cause defeat because of the cross
- Christ has forgiven all of it
- Must keep forgetting it

### Paul's Catalog of Sufferings (2 Corinthians 11:23-27)
- "Are they ministers of Christ? I speak as a fool, I am more"
- In labors more abundant
- In stripes above measure (beaten beyond the legal limit of 39 lashes)
- In prisons more frequent
- In deaths often (almost died many times)
- Five times received 40 stripes save one (39 lashes, 5 times)
- Three times beaten with rods
- Once stoned
- Three times shipwrecked
- A night and day in the deep
- Journeyings often
- Perils of waters, robbers, own countrymen, heathen, city, wilderness, sea, false brethren
- In weariness and painfulness
- In watchings often
- In hunger and thirst
- In fastings often
- In cold and nakedness
- Paul's response to all this: "forgetting those things which are behind"

### Christian Life Is Not Easy
- When you got saved, you picked up enemies you didn't have before: devil, flesh, world
- It doesn't get easier; it gets tougher
- Grace's comment: "It can't be a sissy to get old"
- Can't be a sissy to be a Christian
- Must be a man or woman of God, of faith
- Must have focus: life is not my own anymore, it's about Christ
- Must clear the mind of junk and forget it
- Must press onward
- Can't do anything with the past anyway

### Third Word: Future (Verse 13-14 - "Reaching Forth" and "Press Toward the Mark")
- Tied in with forgetting
- Verse 13: "I count not myself to have apprehended, but this one thing I do, forgetting those things which are behind, and reaching forth unto those things which are before"
- Verse 14: "I press toward the mark for the prize of the high calling of God in Christ Jesus"
- God apprehended Paul to make him like Jesus Christ
- God apprehended every believer for the same purpose
- Questions: How's that going in your life? Do people see the Lord in you? Are you like Christ?
- Paul looked to the future prize: the high calling of God in Christ Jesus

### The High Calling Explained
- Three callings in a believer's life:
  1. Calling of the gospel - what saved us (Romans 8:28-29 - "the called")
  2. Calling to serve Him - everyone called for a purpose, to do something, to serve
  3. Future call - the high calling in Christ

### The High Calling Is Geographical
- Ephesians 6:12: "We wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places"
- These are real, literal spiritual enemies
- Spiritual wickedness in "high places" - a geographical location
- Paul looked forward to attaining a position that's high, way up there

### God Is Building in the Heavens
- Amos 9:6: "It is he that buildeth his stories in the heaven"
- Stories = floors of a building
- The church is God's building
- God is building something in the heavens with the body of Christ
- There will be an attaining of places for God's people to achieve as a prize in high places
- Paul wanted that high place, not a low position
- Illustration: Skyscrapers put minions on lower stories, executives move higher up
- This concept came from the mind of God, put into humanity as His creation

### Differences in Glory (1 Corinthians 15:35-44)
- Verse 35-37: Question about resurrection bodies - "Thou fool"
- Note: Paul could call them fools; we must rightly divide to understand why (not Jews living for the kingdom, but people in the body of Christ)
- He that winneth souls is wise; not winning souls is foolish living
- Verse 39: All flesh is not the same flesh (men, beasts, fish, birds; also flesh of woman and man)
- Verse 40-41: "There is one glory of the sun, and another glory of the moon, and another glory of the stars: for one star differeth from another star in glory"
- Sun has more glory than moon; moon gets its light/glory from the sun
- Stars differ from one another in glory - some twinkle brighter, some closer, some farther
- Stars picture believers
- Verse 42: "So also is the resurrection of the dead"
- In the resurrection, believers will not all have the same glory
- Different glory based on focus on Christ, forgetting things behind, pressing forward

### Glory Is Physical and Has Weight
- For all have sinned and come short of the glory of God
- Glory is not just something esoteric or floating in the air
- Glory is physical, literal, and real
- Colossians 3:4: "When Christ, who is our life, shall appear, then shall ye also appear with him in glory"
- 2 Corinthians 4:17-18: "For our light affliction, which is but for a moment, worketh for us a far more exceeding and eternal weight of glory"
- Glory has substance and weight to it
- Light affliction, fellowship of His sufferings, being like Christ - moves us toward an eternal weight of glory
- 2 Timothy 2:10: "Therefore I endure all things for the elect's sakes, that they may also obtain the salvation which is in Christ Jesus with eternal glory"
- Not just salvation, but salvation with eternal glory
- 1 Corinthians 2:7: "But we speak the wisdom of God in a mystery, even the hidden wisdom, which God ordained before the world unto our glory"

### Challenge Against False Humility
- If you're saved, you're going to heaven - that's not the issue
- The issue is going up into heavenly places with different kinds of glory
- Don't say "I just want to be a street sweeper in heaven" - "Thou fool"
- Evidence: We all have nice things in our homes (hardwood floors, carpet, air conditioning, washer, dryer, dishwasher, garbage disposal, gas fireplace, cars in garage)
- We want stuff and stuff means something to us
- Don't be content with low position when others enjoy the glory of God in their positions
- The glory we receive, we give right back to Him
- Want to attain all the glory possible to give the Savior the glory He's worthy of

### Conclusion: Simple Strategy for Life
- Need focus (knowing Christ)
- Need to forget (the past is done)
- Need to look to the future (it's coming)
- Paul's philosophy/ideology: "I want to know him, I'm going to forget, and I'm going to press toward the mark for the prize of the high calling of God in Christ"
- Don't have to make it hard
- Challenge: Refocus on being more like Jesus every day
- Reference to old fad: WWJD (What Would Jesus Do) - consider this to be more like Him
- Forget the things behind, press toward the mark
