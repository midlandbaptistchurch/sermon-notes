## Summary
Jim VanBuskirk explores Romans 8, focusing on the contrast between living according to the flesh versus living according to the Spirit. He discusses how God's Word illuminates different facets of Scripture at different times in our lives, and emphasizes the spiritual truths found in Paul's letter about freedom from sin and the peace that comes from being spiritually minded.

## Scripture
Romans 8:1-10 — Paul's foundational teaching on the freedom believers have in Christ and the life-giving power of the Holy Spirit versus the death that comes from the carnal mind.

## Key Themes
- Walking after the Spirit versus the flesh
- Freedom from sin and death through Christ
- The carnal mind versus the spiritual mind
- Spiritual mindedness as life and peace
- God's continuous illumination of Scripture
