## Summary
Pastor Mark Simpson teaches on the critical need for believers to assess their own value under grace. Unlike living under law with external motivation through judgment, grace requires internal motivation that comes from understanding one's significance, relevance, and importance to God. He emphasizes three key words that are not necessarily biblical terms but are critical concepts for living effectively under grace: believers must see themselves as significant (having meaning), relevant (being connected to God's work), and important (having value to what God is doing).

## Scripture
**Ephesians 4:16** — Describes the whole body fitly joined together and compacted by that which every joint supplies, according to the effectual working in the measure of every part, making increase of the body unto the edifying of itself in love.

**Galatians 6:1** — Instructs those who are spiritual to restore one who is overtaken in a fault in the spirit of meekness, while considering yourself lest you also be tempted.

## Key Themes
- **Grace Motivation vs. Law Motivation**: Under law, people had motivation through coming judgment and consequences, but under grace there is very little judgment until the end, requiring different motivation through understanding grace.
- **Assessing Value**: Believers must assess value to certain things when it comes to grace in order to be motivated to live according to it.
- **The Body of Christ**: Every believer is described as part of the body of Christ, the temple of God, and the building of God, making each person's contribution vital.
- **Self-Worth in God's Economy**: Understanding what Christ paid for us and recognizing our value after salvation in terms of how God wants to use us during our earthly life.

## Notes

### The Problem with Grace and Motivation
- Grace doesn't make you do anything, unlike the law which had judgment as motivation
- Under the law, God brought specific judgments (enemies taking over, making children slaves) as consequences
- Under grace, there's very little judgment until the end
- A person can get saved and still live however they want with few immediate repercussions
- This creates a need for what we call "grace motivation"
- One of the greatest motivators of grace is your understanding of it
- You have to be able to get it and see it

### Assessing Value - The Foundation
- You must assess value to certain things when it comes to grace
- Example given: The speaker's father-in-law keeps what the speaker calls "junk" but the father-in-law sees as "supply of ministry"
- Different people assess different values to the same things based on their perspective
- When it comes to grace and the things of God, it's critical to assess value
- The speaker has been preaching to bring up the fear of God that ought to be at the judgment seat
- We will be judged for things after we get saved - things done in our body whether good or bad
- Many people emphasize only the good and think since they're forgiven and going to heaven, the bad doesn't matter
- If a person allows sin to have dominion their entire saved life, God will not simply say "you're forgiven so it doesn't matter"

### Three Descriptions of Believers
- We are the body of Christ
- We are the temple of God
- We are the building of God

### Assessing Our Value After Salvation
- We can look at what Christ paid for us (His death, bearing our sin, separation from the Father, humiliation, embarrassment)
- Generally, if you pay a lot for something, it has good value
- But what's our value during the time He leaves us here on earth?
- How important are we? How valuable are we?

### The Comparison Trap
- We look at people like the Apostle Paul - valuable, used by God as apostle to Gentiles, inspired books of the Bible
- We look at Pastor Payne - served the Lord all these years, valuable
- We look at soul winners like Jerry Clute - led many people to the Lord, valuable to the work of the Lord
- We look at people who have ministries, done it a long time, see the fruit and impact
- Then we look at ourselves and wonder: How valuable am I? What difference do I make? Does it really matter?
- Does it matter if I pray? How much I study my Bible? How faithful I am to church?

### The Priority Test
- If you can't assess your own value, you won't have much investment in it
- You won't put a whole lot into it or see it as important
- Look at your life and prioritize: What do I give myself to? What has my heart? My mind? My material possessions?
- What do I think about going to bed, waking up in the morning, what do I dream about?
- If none of those things has to do with how the Lord's going to use you, you probably think you're not going to make that much of a difference
- Under grace, if you think that way, that's what you're going to reap - that's the product that will come

### Three Critical Words for Understanding Your Value

#### 1. Significance
- Not a Bible word but critical for seeing yourself right under grace
- Definition: Having meaning
- You have significance, you have meaning
- You are significant to the work of God
- You are significant to the age of grace
- You are significant to what God is trying to do

### Understanding the Body (Ephesians 4:16)
- "From whom the whole body fitly joined together and compacted by that which every joint supplieth, according to the effectual working in the measure of every part, maketh increase of the body unto the edifying of itself in love"
- This is a hard verse to read - many short statements coming together
- The whole body fitly joined together
- Have you considered what body part you want to do without?
- You can take your appendix out, maybe gall bladder, but for the most part there's no part you'd choose to lose
- We should be thankful for all our parts
- The other parts are glad they're there so they have something to fit alongside
- That's why He calls us His body
- We're supposed to think of it that way - it's not just about us, it's about how we fit amongst others

### The Steward Principle
- The Bible talks about stewards who are supposed to be found faithful
- What if you make your study and knowledge of the Word of God the thing you're most passionate about?
- Give yourself to being in the Word, studying it, memorizing it, understanding it, hearing it, going to Bible studies, reading books
- Do this for your entire life
- How significant is that to God? What does that look like at the judgment?

### The Value of Knowing God's Word
- It's His Word - what He gave to us
- If I make much of that, it's something that will bring Him glory
- You might say "I'm not going to be a preacher and preach the Bible to everybody"
- But you're "just" going to study to show yourself approved
- How significant is that?
- When you study the Bible, it allows you to think things that most people never think about

#### 2. Relevance
- Definition: To have bearing upon or to be connected with the matter at hand
- Similar to significance but a little different
- To carry some weight, to be connected to it
- How connected do you see yourself to the work of the Lord and to what He's doing?
- Do you feel connected enough and relevant enough and significant enough to make sure you're in the Word?
- To see what He wants you to do and how He would want you to do it?

### The Excuses We Make
- Sometimes we say: "I don't know if it matters," "I don't know if I have time," "I don't know if I feel like it"
- But what if everybody acts like that?
- When you understand your relevance to the body of Christ, to the time period you get to live, to the God that died for you, to the heaven that is to come
- When you see yourself having significance and relevance, you feel connected

- And he's His

- You don't see the importance of you doing your part
- "Well, somebody else will do it" - they might, then again they might not
- It might just not get done
