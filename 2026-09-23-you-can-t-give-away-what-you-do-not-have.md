## Summary
Willie Wackerly teaches that Christians cannot share what they don't possess spiritually. Through an analysis of Ephesians 4-6, he demonstrates that Scripture emphasizes "dos" (62 verses) twice as much as "don'ts" (29 verses), applying this principle to parenting and Christian living. He stresses the necessity of studying Scripture and walking in the Spirit so believers have spiritual resources to give others when opportunities arise, illustrated through personal testimonies of evangelism success and failure.

## Scripture
Ephesians 4-6 — The duty section of Ephesians containing instructions on how believers should walk, with emphasis on positive commands over negative prohibitions.

2 Timothy 2:15 — "Study to show thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth."

2 Timothy 3:16-17 — "All scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness: That the man of God may be perfect, thoroughly furnished unto all good works."

## Key Themes
- **Rightly Dividing Scripture**: The Book of Ephesians divides into doctrine (chapters 1-3) and duty (chapters 4-6), with duty emphasizing dos (62 verses) over don'ts (29 verses)
- **Walking in the Spirit**: Seven uses of "walk" in Ephesians (worthy, in love, circumspectly, as children of light) require Holy Spirit empowerment, not flesh
- **Parenting Application**: Parents should emphasize positive instruction (dos) twice as much as negative correction (don'ts), speaking truth in love to avoid provoking children to anger
- **You Cannot Give What You Don't Have**: Without studying Scripture and walking in the Spirit, believers lack spiritual resources to share with others in evangelistic opportunities
- **Memorization and Preparation**: Storing God's Word enables believers to answer objections and share the gospel effectively when opportunities arise

## Notes

### Dividing Ephesians by Doctrine and Duty
- Ephesians 1-3: Primarily doctrine
- Ephesians 4-6: Primarily duty
- Total verses in chapters 4-6: 89 (chapter 4 has 32, chapter 5 has 33, chapter 6 has 24)
- Of these 89 verses: 62 are "dos" (approximately 2/3), 29 are "don'ts" (approximately 1/3)
- God emphasizes positive commands more than negative prohibitions

### Seven "Walks" in Ephesians
- Ephesians 2:10 - Walk in good works
- Ephesians 4:1 - Walk worthy of your calling
- Ephesians 5:2 - Walk in love as Christ loved us
- Ephesians 5:8 - Walk as children of light
- Ephesians 5:15 - Walk circumspectly, not as fools but as wise
- Paul uses "walk" 37 times in his epistles, 7 times in Ephesians alone

### Parenting According to Scripture
- Ephesians 6:4 - "Fathers, provoke not your children to wrath, but bring them up in the nurture and admonition of the Lord"
- Colossians 3:21 - "Fathers, provoke not your children to anger, lest they be discouraged"
- "Nurture" appears only once in the Bible (Ephesians 6:4), related to speaking truth in love
- Proverbs 15:1 - "A soft answer turneth away wrath: but grievous words stir up anger"
- Proverbs 22:6 - "Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it"
- Apply 2/3 dos and 1/3 don'ts ratio: tell children what to do twice as much as what not to do
- Example: Instead of just "Don't run," say "Walk" (positive instruction)

### Truth and Love Must Go Together
- "Love without truth is hypocrisy; truth without love is brutality"
- Speaking truth in love connects to "nurture" in Ephesians 6:4
- Children need positive feedback, not only correction
- Application extends beyond parenting to all Christian interaction, including social media

### The Fruit of the Spirit Connection
- Ephesians 5:9 - "For the fruit of the Spirit is in all goodness and righteousness and truth"
- Galatians 5:22-23 - "But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith, meekness, temperance"
- All goodness, righteousness, and truth comes from the Holy Spirit, not the flesh
- Cannot have Ephesians 5:9 qualities without Galatians 5:22-23 fruit

### You Cannot Give Away What You Do Not Have
- Acts 3:6 - Peter said "Silver and gold have I none; but such as I have give I thee"
- If walking in the flesh, cannot give away the Spirit
- If walking in the Spirit, cannot give away the flesh
- James 3:10-12 - Same fountain cannot yield both sweet and bitter water
- When angry or comparing self to others, walking in flesh not Spirit

### Personal Testimonies
- **Pastor Ramseier** - Founder of Midland Street People's Church in Bass City; demonstrated love while preaching truth; always worked alongside others; made lasting impression on young Willie
- **Hospital encounter** - Former employee dying of cancer, angry at God; Willie didn't have gospel knowledge to share, man likely died without Christ
- **Kitchen table encounter** - 10 years later, distant relative objected that God isn't love because He condemned whole human race for one sin; Willie had studied Romans 5 and showed how one man's sin required one Savior's righteousness; woman received Christ two minutes later
- **Vietnam War draft** - Drafted during Tet Offensive, sent to Fort Leonard Wood for AIT; received orders for Korea instead of Vietnam (needed visa); avoided combat and POW camps

### Paul's Progressive Revelation
- Acts 9:20 - First sermon: "He is the Son of God" (no mention of body of Christ or justification)
- 1 Corinthians 15:3-4 - "For I delivered unto you first of all that which I also received, how that Christ died for our sins according to the scriptures; And that he was buried, and that he rose again the third day according to the scriptures"
- 2 Corinthians 12:1 - "I will come to visions and revelations of the Lord"
- Paul received progressive revelation; wrote prison epistles containing deeper doctrine
- Paul couldn't give away what he hadn't yet received

### POW Camp Gospel Sharing
- North Vietnam POW camps practiced daily torture
- Prisoners kept in solitary confinement
- Christians shared gospel through walls using Morse code and whispers
- Message: "Jesus Christ died to save sinners. You can know for sure you're going to heaven"
- Many prisoners got saved through this witness
- Question posed: Could you lead someone to Christ through a wall with limited communication if you were in that situation?

### Call to Study and Memorize Scripture
- 2 Timothy 2:15 - "Study to show thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth"
- Key verses to memorize:
  - 2 Timothy 3:16-17 (all Scripture inspired and profitable)
  - 2 Timothy 2:15 (study to be approved)
  - 1 Corinthians 15:3-4 (death, burial, resurrection)
  - Galatians 5:22-23 (fruit of the Spirit)
- Psalm 1:1 - "Blessed is the man that walketh not in the counsel of the ungodly"
- Without Scripture in your heart, cannot give it away to others
- Need assurance of salvation, conviction about God's Word (KJV), and biblical answers to objections
- Proverbs references: pure words as silver tried in furnace, purified seven times; God preserves them forever
