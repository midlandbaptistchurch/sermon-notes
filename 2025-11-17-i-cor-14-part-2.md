## Summary
Pastor Simpson examines 1 Corinthians 14 in the context of the transition from the kingdom gospel to the grace gospel. He explains why Paul emphasizes prophecy and edification over unknown tongues, traces the historical progression of tongues through Acts, and clarifies how sign gifts were temporary provisions during a transitional period in God's program.

## Scripture
1 Corinthians 14 — Paul's instructions regarding spiritual gifts, particularly tongues and prophecy, in the context of church edification and understanding.

## Key Themes
- The temporary nature of sign gifts during a transitional period
- Prophecy and edification as priorities over unknown tongues
- The shift from the kingdom gospel to the grace gospel
- Understanding vs. supernatural manifestation in church
- The role of charity (love) in evaluating spiritual gifts
- The Gentile transition through Peter and Cornelius

## Notes

### Tongues vs. Prophecy
- Unknown tongues speak to God but don't edify others; prophecy edifies, exhorts, and comforts the church
- Paul prioritizes understanding and teaching over supernatural gifts
- Speaking in tongues requires interpretation to benefit the congregation
- Paul is not condemning tongues but contextualizing their proper use

### The Transitional Period
- Sign gifts (miracles, healings, tongues) validated Christ as Messiah to Israel
- After Israel rejected the Messiah, God initiated a transitional period through Paul
- The grace gospel (faith without works) replaced the kingdom gospel (faith plus works/endurance)
- Sign gifts gradually phased out as the new program under grace was established

### Historical Progression
- Acts 2 (Pentecost): Tongues manifested as "other tongues" — everyone heard in their own language
- Acts 10 (Cornelius): Gentiles received the Holy Ghost and spoke in tongues, signifying their inclusion
- Acts 19: Paul encountered believers with only John's baptism; they received the Holy Ghost
- 1 Corinthians 14: The last biblical mention of tongues in scripture

### The Two Gospel Programs
- **Kingdom Gospel**: Believers had to endure to the end; salvation tied to works and perseverance
- **Grace Gospel**: Believers are sealed by the Holy Ghost; eternal security through faith alone
- The transitional period bridged these two programs through apostolic sign gifts

### Practical Applications
- Clarity and understanding in church teaching should supersede the demonstration of supernatural gifts
- Love (charity) must govern the use of all spiritual gifts
- The supernatural sign gifts are not the basis for modern church practice
