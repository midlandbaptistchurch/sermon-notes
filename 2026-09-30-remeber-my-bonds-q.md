## Summary
Hugh Taylor explores the three-word catchphrase "remember my bonds" from Colossians 4:18, examining it as more than just Paul's reminder of his imprisonment. Through examining Paul's extensive suffering detailed in 2 Corinthians 11:23-28, Taylor presents three meanings: a request for prayer, a recognition of the price Paul paid for ministry to Gentiles, and a rallying cry to participate in the cause of Christ. He challenges believers to remember those currently suffering persecution and to remain faithful in their own circumstances.

## Scripture
Colossians 4:18 — Paul's personal handwritten salutation closes with "remember my bonds, grace be with you, amen." This is the only epistle where Paul uses this specific phrase in his signature.

## Key Themes
- **Biblical Catchphrases**: Short memorable statements in Scripture that can have multiple meanings and applications for believers today
- **Paul's Suffering**: The extensive persecution and hardship Paul endured for his ministry to the Gentiles, detailed in 2 Corinthians 11:23-28
- **Intercessory Prayer**: The importance of praying for those in bonds and suffering persecution, both in Paul's time and today
- **Recognition of Price Paid**: Acknowledging the sacrifices made by Paul and Christ for our spiritual liberty and knowledge of truth
- **Faithfulness**: A rallying cry for believers to continue participating in the cause of Christ despite difficulties

## Notes

### Introduction to Catchphrases
- A catchphrase is an attention-getting word or phrase used to publicize something like a campaign or product
- Examples from culture: "I'll be back" (Terminator), "What's in your wallet?" (Capital One)
- Biblical catchphrases can have multiple meanings and applications depending on reader's situation
- Scripture is "quick and powerful and sharper than any two-edged sword"

### Biblical Catchphrase Examples
- **Handfuls of Purpose** (Ruth 2:16): Brother William Jeffcoat preached on this recently, referring to blessings God graciously bestows
- **A Man After God's Own Heart** (1 Samuel 13:14, Acts 13:22): God's description of David; urges believers to become that man or woman after God's own heart
- **Opening Hearts** (Acts 16:14): God opened Lydia's heart to attend to Paul's preaching; speaker prays this for unsaved and for believers to understand right division
- Speaker prays for Brother Bill's heart to be opened to attend to hospice workers' words

### Studying Catchphrases
Three steps to studying biblical catchphrases:
1. Define the meaning - what is God telling us?
2. Derive principles - what do we believe and value in relation to it?
3. Develop practices - how do we behave accordingly? It becomes our doctrine

### Discovery of "Remember My Bonds"
- Speaker noticed this statement a few weeks ago while sitting in church going through Colossians
- Located in Paul's personal handwritten salutation in Colossians 4:18
- Paul would typically have a scribe write (Onesimus, Titus, or Timothy) but would sign personally to validate authorship
- This is the only time Paul uses this specific phrase in any epistle
- Intriguing that God gave Paul liberty and inspiration to include this very personal statement

### Meaning 1: Remember My Bonds as a Request for Prayer
- Face value: Paul saying "don't forget that I'm in prison"
- When you tell someone to remember a need, it's typically so they'll pray for it
- Paul believed in and practiced prayer:
  - Romans 1:9: "without ceasing I make mention of you always in my prayers"
  - Ephesians 1:16: "making mention of you in my prayers"
  - Colossians 1:9: "we do not cease to pray for you"
- Paul requested prayer from others:
  - Colossians 4:2-3: "continue in prayer...with all praying also for us"
  - 1 Thessalonians 5:25: "brethren, pray for us"
  - 2 Thessalonians 3:1: "finally, brethren, pray for us that the Word of the Lord may have free course"
- God wants us to care about each other like He cares about us
- Speaker's wife tells children: if someone bothers you, pray for them - hard to have bitterness toward someone you pray for
- Application today: believers being persecuted in places like Nigeria and China
  - Anywhere you have Islamism or Communism, you have domination and persecution
  - "Peaceful Islam is established enough to have Sharia law"
  - "Communism is the Sharia law of socialism"
  - When socialism establishes communism, you have domination and persecution
- Prayer needed for Brother Biz and his family and ministry in Haiti and Dominican Republic (facing economism and corruption)

### Meaning 2: Remember My Bonds as Recognition of the Price
- Paul paid a price for his ministry to Gentiles
- Paul's resume from 2 Corinthians 11:23-28:
  - In labors more abundant
  - In stripes (lashes of whip) above measure
  - In prisons more frequent
  - In deaths oft
  - Five times received 40 stripes save one from the Jews
  - Three times beaten with rods
  - Once stoned
  - Three times suffered shipwreck
  - A night and day in the deep
  - In journeyings often
  - In perils of waters, robbers, own countrymen, heathen, city, wilderness, sea, false brethren
  - In weariness and painfulness
  - In watchings often
  - In hunger and thirst
  - In fastings often
  - In cold and nakedness
- Ephesians 3:1: "For this cause I Paul the prisoner of Jesus Christ for you Gentiles"
- No one suffered for human race as much as Christ, but can't think of any man or woman who suffered as much as Paul for the cause of Christ
- Paul suffered for us Gentiles
- His offense/crime was simply preaching the gospel of the grace of God
- Colossians 4:3: "praying also for us that God would open unto us a door of utterance to speak the mystery of Christ for which I am also in bonds"
- "Truth sure is a dangerous criminal thing, isn't it?"
- Paul fought good fight, finished his course, kept the faith
- Without Paul's willingness to suffer, Gentiles would have no hope - speaker has no Jewish blood/heritage
- Paul's bonds were on our behalf and for our benefit

### Meaning 3: Remember My Bonds as a Rallying Cry to Participate
- Rallying cry: a word or phrase used to make people join together to support an idea or cause
- Historical example: "Remember the Alamo"
  - March 6, 1836: 1,500-man Mexican army under Santa Anna defeated 200-ish Texans at the Alamo
  - Santa Anna killed even survivors who surrendered - no mercy
  - Only spared some to spread message of defeat
  - April 21, 1836 (six and a half weeks later): Texan army under Sam Houston defeated Santa Anna at San Jacinto
  - Battle lasted only 18 minutes
  - Texan soldiers repeatedly cried "Remember the Alamo!" - they were fired up
- For believers: "Remember my bonds" is rallying cry to identify and involve ourselves with cause of Christ
- Following Paul as he followed Christ
- Hebrews 13:3: "Remember them that are in bonds, as bound with them, and them which suffer adversity as being yourselves also in the body"
- Speaker read book about Reese Howells, known for intercessory prayer
  - Howells did as much as he could to take in his heart the plight of person he was praying for
  - He didn't sympathize, he empathized
- Application: When driving to church, think what blessing/privilege it is to:
  - Get in a car (don't have to walk)
  - Drive to church
  - No roadblocks
  - No guards at door
  - No fear of anyone marching in and dragging preacher out
- If we were mindful of what folks in China, Nigeria, Dominican Republic, Haiti are going through, we might become intercessory prayers
- We might find we have something to pray for beyond football season or opening day of hunting season
- Need to remember what's been done and why, then do our part
- For those present on Wednesday evening: this is affirmation, not guilt trip
  - Third Wednesday without senior pastor
  - Drove through miserable weather
  - Had good teaching while pastor gone, but "it's just not the same when he's not here"
  - Pastor returning Sunday morning
  - "Remember my bonds" for Wednesday night attendees is rallying cry for affirmation
  - To carry on, keep on keeping on
  - Keep pressing toward the mark for the prize, the high calling of God in Christ Jesus
- "Beloved, don't faint. Don't faint. Keep fighting, keep pressing. Remember my bonds."
