## Summary
Associate Pastor Mark Simpson teaches on living the resurrected life by setting our affections on heavenly things and mortifying earthly desires. He emphasizes the importance of choosing to focus on eternal rewards and roles in God's kingdom rather than being controlled by fleshly temptations like sexual sin and anger.

## Scripture
Colossians 3:4-9 — Paul instructs believers on practical holy living, focusing on heavenly priorities and putting off sinful behaviors.

## Key Themes
- Setting affections on things above rather than earthly things
- Understanding our resurrected life and security in Christ
- Mortifying sexual sins and anger as acts of the flesh
- The connection between present faithfulness and future rewards/roles
- Practical application of putting off the old man and putting on the new
- Eternal perspective on temporal trials and relationships

## Notes

### The Resurrected Life (vv. 1-4)
- "If ye then be risen with Christ" - believers must examine their spiritual condition
- We are commanded to "seek those things which are above" and "set your affection on things above"
- This is a deliberate choice, not just a feeling - we must decide what to love
- Our life is "hid with Christ in God" - speaks to our security and eternal perspective
- Future relationships in heaven with angels and participation in God's kingdom work

### Mortifying Earthly Members (v. 5)
- "Mortify therefore your members which are upon the earth"
- Focus on sexual sins: fornication (Greek: porneia), uncleanness, covetousness
- Cross-reference with Ephesians 5:3-5 - same themes of sexual purity
- Warning about losing inheritance in the kingdom (not salvation, but rewards)

### Dealing with Anger (v. 8)
- "Put off all these: anger, wrath, malice, blasphemy, filthy communication"
- Cross-reference with Ephesians 4:26-32 - "Be angry and sin not"
- Anger is a choice - we "allow" people to make us angry
- When angry, we are not walking in the Spirit or living resurrected
- Solution: forgiveness, forbearance, and overcoming evil with good (Romans 12:17-21)

### Practical Application
- Daily battle is fought in the mind - choosing spiritual vs. fleshly responses
- Trials often come before opportunities for ministry or spiritual growth
- Our mouth can be either a tool for edification or destruction (like fire)
- Present faithfulness determines future roles, ranks, and rewards in God's kingdom

### Eternal Perspective
- This life is temporal - a training ground for the next life
- There will be "principalities, powers, might, and dominion" in heavenly places
- God will show "exceeding riches of his grace in his kindness" throughout eternity
- What we do with trials and temptations affects our eternal inheritance
