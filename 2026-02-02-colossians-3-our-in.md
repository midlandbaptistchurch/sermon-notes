## Summary
Pastor Simpson explores the challenging passages about God's wrath coming upon "children of disobedience" and losing inheritance in the kingdom, clarifying that these refer to believers who can lose rewards and inheritance at the judgment seat of Christ, not their salvation. He emphasizes that God desires to work through believers to qualify them for their full inheritance.

## Scripture
Colossians 3:5-6 — Paul's instruction to mortify sinful members and warning about God's wrath on disobedience.

## Key Themes
- The difference between guaranteed salvation and losable inheritance
- God's wrath manifested at the judgment seat of Christ through loss of rewards
- The importance of allowing God to work in and through believers
- Understanding the value of the inheritance we can gain or lose

## Notes
- **Context matters**: All the parallel passages (Ephesians 5:3-5, 1 Corinthians 6:9-11, Galatians 5:19-21) are addressed to believers, not unbelievers
- **Two types of inheritance**: 
  - What we cannot lose: salvation, forgiveness, glorified body, rapture hope
  - What we can lose: rewards, works, inheritance based on faithful service
- **The judgment seat of Christ**: Where believers' works will be tested by fire (1 Corinthians 3:15)
- **God working through us**: Not just doing good works, but allowing God to operate through us as His temple (1 Corinthians 15:10, Galatians 2:20, Philippians 2:12-13)
- **Practical application**: Acknowledging God in all service, whether ministry tasks or daily work, maintaining right heart attitude
- **The value of inheritance**: What we bring before God in eternity for positions of rank and authority
