## Summary
This lesson examines Robert Stephanus's contribution to the Greek New Testament, particularly his introduction of verse divisions in 1551, and explores the history of chapter and verse divisions in the Bible. Taylor emphasizes that the King James Bible represents the culmination of God's purification process of His Word in English, comparing it to silver purified seven times. He challenges believers to appreciate their complete Bible while recognizing they are still part of God's ongoing work in progress until Christ's return.

## Scripture
Psalm 12:6-7 — "The words of the Lord are pure words, as silver tried in a furnace of fire purified seven times. Thou shalt keep them, O Lord. Thou shalt preserve them from this generation forever."

## Key Themes
- **Divine Preservation Through Human Instruments**: God used various scholars and translators as part of an iterative purification process to give us His perfect Word in English.
- **The Value of Chapter and Verse Divisions**: These divisions, introduced by Stephen Langton (chapters, 1227) and Robert Stephanus (verses, 1551), make Scripture accessible and memorable.
- **Living as Part of God's Work in Progress**: Believers should live urgently and purposefully, recognizing they are part of God's ongoing plan even while possessing the complete Word of God.

## Notes

### Robert Stephanus (Etienne) Background
- Also known as Robert Etienne or Stephanus
- Lived 1503-1559
- French printer who fled to Geneva, Switzerland due to Roman Catholic persecution
- Produced four editions of the Greek New Testament
- His third and fourth editions were essentially the same text, but the fourth (1551) introduced verse divisions

### History of Chapter and Verse Divisions

#### Chapter Divisions
- Created by Stephen Langton, Archbishop of Canterbury, around AD 1227
- First used in the Wycliffe English Bible (1382), translated from Latin into Middle English
- Nearly all Bible divisions since have followed Langton's chapter pattern

#### Old Testament Verse Divisions
- Created by Jewish Rabbi Isaac Nathan (Rabbi Nathan), around AD 1448
- Stephanus used Nathan's verse divisions for the Old Testament

#### New Testament Verse Divisions
- Created by Robert Stephanus in his 1551 Greek New Testament (fourth edition)
- Reportedly made many divisions while riding horseback from town to town
- This may account for some awkward verse breaks
- First English Bible to use these verse divisions was the Geneva Bible (1560)

### Modern Preservation of Verse Numbering
- Modern versions that remove verses still preserve the original verse numbering system
- Example: A passage might skip from verse 6 to verse 8, maintaining the traditional numbering even where verses are omitted
- This demonstrates the enduring nature of Stephanus's system even among those who question the underlying text

### The Purification Process (Psalm 12:6-7)
- Each English Bible version represented "silver" (the Word of God) in its own right
- God orchestrated an iterative purification process through multiple versions
- The process moved from Middle English (Wycliffe) to Modern English translations
- The development paralleled the maturation of the English language itself
- The King James Bible represents the culmination of this seven-fold purification

### Biblical Examples of Work in Progress
- Moses had no written Scripture when he began
- Samuel only had what was written before him (no Psalms or Proverbs)
- David didn't have the later prophets
- Apostle Paul was a "work in progress" - from Pharisee of Pharisees to Apostle to the Gentiles
- God held each accountable for what they had, even as the revelation was incomplete

### Living in Light of Completion
- Believers today have the advantage of a complete Bible
- This doesn't mean God's work is complete - prophecy shows much is yet to come
- Statistically, most believers won't leave in the Rapture, yet we should live as if it could happen today
- Comparison to the day before vacation - most productive day because deadline is imminent
- Should live with same urgency regarding eternal matters

### Personal Application
- Don't become bored or take for granted the complete Word of God
- Recognize we're still part of God's ongoing process
- Those who have gone before (loved ones who have died) are in a "postgraduate status"
- Believers still here are in "kindergarten" comparatively, but here for God's purpose
- Should make "full proof of the ministry" God has called us to
- Use the complete Word to glorify Christ and benefit others
