## Summary
Pastor Simpson teaches about the importance of understanding our personal value and worth to God under grace. He emphasizes that believers must assess their significance, relevance, and importance to God's work in order to be properly motivated for Christian living and service.

## Scripture
Ephesians 4:16 — Paul describes how the whole body of Christ is "fitly joined together" with each part contributing to the growth and edification of the body.

## Key Themes
- Understanding personal significance in God's work
- Grace motivation versus law motivation
- Self-assessment of spiritual value
- Individual importance in the body of Christ

## Notes

### Guidelines of Grace Introduction
- This teaching is part of a series called "Guidelines of Grace"
- Grace doesn't force obedience like the law did through fear of judgment
- Grace motivation requires understanding one's value and significance
- Under grace, there are fewer immediate consequences, so believers need different motivation

### The Need for Value Assessment
- We must assess value to certain things under grace
- If we don't see something as valuable, we won't invest much in it
- Many believers struggle to see their own importance to God's work
- Without proper self-assessment, we may think our prayers, Bible study, and faithfulness don't matter much

### Three Critical Concepts

**1. Significance**
- Having meaning and purpose
- Every believer is significant to God's work in this age
- Reference to Ephesians 4:16 - the body needs every part
- Example: Being faithful in Bible study matters greatly to God, even if no one else notices

**2. Relevance**
- Having bearing upon or being connected to what God is doing
- Feeling connected to the work of the Lord
- Taking ownership and responsibility ("it's mine because it's His and I'm His")
- Understanding that your part may not get done if you don't do it

**3. Importance**
- Being valuable to what God is accomplishing
- Every believer is important whether in visible ministry or not
- Personal Bible study, prayer, and faithfulness all matter deeply to God
- God notices what others don't see

### Practical Applications
- Choose to study God's Word passionately as the most important thing in life
- Recognize that God values when we notice things about Him that others miss
- Understand that choosing holiness over sin brings God joy
- See yourself as connected to and responsible for God's work
- Don't wait for traumatic events to motivate spiritual growth

### Personal Relationship Analogy
- Just as we work at our marriage relationships because they're significant, relevant, and important
- Our relationship with God should receive even greater attention and investment
- God left us here in sin-cursed bodies so we could choose to honor Him
- Taking care of ourselves spiritually and physically shows love and brings security to those who care about us
