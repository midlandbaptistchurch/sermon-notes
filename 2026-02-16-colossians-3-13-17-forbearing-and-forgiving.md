## Summary
Pastor Simpson continues the study of Colossians 3, focusing on the Christian's daily practice of forbearing and forgiving one another as Christ forgave us. He emphasizes that forgiveness is not a statement but a state of the heart, and explores how the peace of God and the Word of Christ must rule in our hearts, including practical applications to music choices.

## Scripture
Colossians 3:13-17 — Continuing the "put-offs and put-ons" theme, focusing on forbearance, forgiveness, charity, and letting God's peace and Word dwell within.

## Key Themes
- Forbearance vs. forgiveness - putting up with people vs. forgiving wrongs
- Forgiveness as a state, not just a statement
- Letting the peace of God rule our hearts through prayer and trust
- Allowing the Word of Christ to dwell richly and guide decisions
- Music standards and separation from worldly influences
- Eternal inheritance and rewards based on faithful service

## Notes
### Daily Practice of Forbearance and Forgiveness
- Forbearing = putting up with people's faults and weaknesses
- Forgiveness doesn't mean the wrong was okay, but we choose to release it
- Both are daily practices, not one-time decisions
- Our standard: "even as Christ forgave you, so also do ye"

### The Nature of Forgiveness
- Forgiveness is a state of heart, not just words
- Requires tenderness of heart, not bitterness
- Meekness = strength under control, deciding how circumstances affect you
- Reference to Romans 12:17-21 - overcome evil with good

### Peace of God and Word of Christ
- Let the peace of God rule - connected to Philippians 4:6-7
- Peace comes through prayer and trusting God's sovereignty
- Let the Word dwell richly - both knowing and allowing Scripture to guide
- Both require our active "letting" - allowing God's influence

### Music and Separation
- Psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs should teach truth
- Music choices reflect our spiritual condition
- Historical perspective on rap music's degrading influence
- Question: Does this bring glory to God?

### Eternal Perspective
- Verse 24: "receive the reward of the inheritance"
- Verse 25: "he that doeth wrong shall receive for the wrong"
- Our service and walk affects our eternal rewards
- Some things in life should be "not for sale"
