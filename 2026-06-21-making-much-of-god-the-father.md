## Summary
Preached on Father's Day 2026, Assoc Pastor Mark Simpson calls the congregation to intentionally honor and recognize God the Father — not merely as a distant deity, but as a personal, present, and exalted Father. Drawing from Romans 8, Galatians 4, 1 Corinthians 15, and Ephesians 1–4, he highlights the spirit of adoption, the spiritual blessings God freely gives, and the example of Christ's own submission to the Father as a model for how believers should relate to God.

## Scripture
- **Romans 8:15** — The spirit of adoption whereby we cry "Abba Father," expressing personal dependence and submission to God.
- **Galatians 4** — Further context on adoption and the cry of "Abba Father" sent into believers' hearts.
- **Mark 14:36** — Christ in the Garden of Gethsemane crying "Abba Father" and submitting his will to the Father's.
- **1 Corinthians 15:21-28** — Christ's ultimate submission to God the Father, that God may be all in all.
- **Ephesians 1:3** — God the Father has blessed believers with all spiritual blessings in heavenly places in Christ.
- **Ephesians 1:17** — Paul's prayer that the Father of glory would give believers the spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of him.
- **Ephesians 2:18-22** — Believers built together as a holy temple, a place for the inhabitation of God through the Spirit.
- **Ephesians 3:14** — Paul bowing his knees unto the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.
- **Ephesians 4:4-6** — One God and Father of all, who is above all, through all, and in you all.
- **Ephesians 5:20** — Giving thanks always for all things unto God and the Father in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.

## Key Themes
- Honoring and making much of God the Father, especially as he is often overlooked
- The spirit of adoption — crying "Abba Father" as an expression of personal dependence and submission
- Christ as the model of submission to God the Father
- Spiritual blessings the Father has given to believers
- The spirit of wisdom and revelation as a gift from God the Father
- Being a place where God inhabits — in heart, mind, and life
- God the Father as above all, through all, and in all — his omnipresence and intimacy
- Giving thanks to God the Father as a practical act of honor

## Notes
**Father's Day Context**
- Pastor Simpson acknowledges the mixed emotions Father's Day can bring — joy, loss, difficult memories — and calls the body to be sensitive to one another.
- The role of fatherhood is described as something God gave humanity that reflects his own character, though it has been attacked and distorted.

**"Abba Father" — Romans 8:15, Galatians 4, Mark 14:36**
- The term "Abba" carries the meaning of Father/Dad but also the idea of crying out in submission through difficulty.
- Christ himself used this term in Gethsemane, choosing to submit to the Father even at the cost of the cross.
- God does not want believers to see him only as a far-off deity but as a personal Father toward whom they have dependence, submission, and exaltation.

**1 Corinthians 15:21-28 — Christ's Submission and God Being All in All**
- At the end of the age, Christ will deliver up the kingdom to God the Father, with all enemies put under his feet.
- Verse 28: "That God may be all in all" — God the Father occupies the highest position of authority even within the Godhead.
- Christ is currently seated at the right hand of the Father in a position of subjection; this is reflected in Paul's consistent pattern of naming God the Father first in his greetings.
- Believers are called to mirror this attitude of reverence and submission in their own lives.

**Ephesians 1:3 — Spiritual Blessings**
- God the Father has blessed believers with all spiritual blessings in heavenly places in Christ.
- Pastor Simpson encourages the congregation to pause and take stock of these blessings — including a future glorified body, eternal purpose, the Word of God, sound preaching, and the security of knowing God holds the future.
- Just as children can take earthly fathers for granted, believers can become neglectful of the blessings God the Father provides.

**Ephesians 1:17 — Spirit of Wisdom and Revelation**
- Paul prays that the Father of glory would give believers the spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of him.
- A teachable spirit — never saying "I know" to what God is trying to reveal — is one of the greatest gifts a believer can offer back to the Father.
- Pastor Simpson reflects personally on 35 years of walking with the Lord and his continued desire for God to reveal truth to him.

**Ephesians 2:22 — Inhabitation of God**
- Believers together are being built as a holy temple for the inhabitation of God through the Spirit.
- The call is for each believer's heart, mind, and life to be a place where God the Father would want to dwell — free from complaining, bitterness, and sin.

**Ephesians 4:4-6 — Above All, Through All, In You All**
- God the Father is above all (sovereign), through all (omniscient — nothing is hidden from him), and in you all (personally present in every aspect of life).
- Believers are called to live transparently before God, acknowledging he knows everything, and to invite him into every part of life rather than hiding from him as Adam did.

**Practical Application**
- Acknowledge God as Father through giving thanks (Ephesians 5:20).
- Bow the knee — both literally and in spirit — in reverence to God the Father.
- Cultivate a teachable, humble spirit that desires ongoing revelation from God.
- Be a dwelling place worthy of God's inhabitation.
- Recognize Christ's submission to the Father as the model for the believer's submission to Christ.
