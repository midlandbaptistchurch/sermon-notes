## Summary
Pastor Jim Payne preaches from Romans 5:1-4, focusing on the word "experience" as used by the Apostle Paul. He presents five essential experiences every Christian should have before leaving this earth, covering two in depth — regeneration and redemption — with the remaining three to be addressed the following week. Using personal testimony and Scripture, Pastor Payne emphasizes that salvation is not merely an intellectual agreement but a genuine, life-changing encounter with God.

## Scripture
Romans 5:1-4 — Paul's progression from justification by faith through tribulation, patience, and experience to hope; also Titus 3:4-5, Acts 26:16-18, Romans 3:23-24, and 2 Timothy 2:19-21.

## Key Themes
- Experience as a real, personal encounter — not mere emotion or spectacle
- Justification by faith, not by works
- Regeneration: being raised from spiritual death to life
- Redemption: being purchased by God and made fit for His use
- Caution against experience-centered Christianity that can be counterfeited by the devil
- The reality and transformative evidence of genuine salvation

## Notes

### Romans 5:1-4 — The Word "Experience"
- Paul uses the word "experience" twice in verse 4: "patience [works] experience, and experience hope."
- Experience appears only a handful of times in all of Scripture, making Paul's use here significant.
- Defined as: *a particular instance of personally encountering or undergoing something*; also, *knowledge and wisdom gained from what you have observed, encountered, or undergone.*

### Caution About Experience-Focused Christianity
- Many Christians seek constant emotional highs and supernatural feelings as the measure of their faith.
- Warning: the devil is also in the miracle-working business (2 Thessalonians 2:9 — the Antichrist comes with "power, signs, and lying wonders").
- True Christian experience is grounded in the Word of God and the Holy Spirit, not manufactured feelings.

### Five Experiences Christians Should Not Leave Earth Without
Pastor Payne introduced five experiences, all beginning with the letter R. Two were covered in this sermon:

#### 1. Regeneration (Titus 3:4-5; Acts 26:16-18)
- We were once foolish, disobedient, and serving sin (Titus 3:3).
- God saved us "not by works of righteousness which we have done, but according to his mercy...by the washing of regeneration and renewing of the Holy Ghost" (Titus 3:5).
- Regeneration = being given life again; a spiritual resurrection from the death brought by Adam's sin.
- Acts 26:16-18: Paul's commission to turn people from darkness to light, from the power of Satan to God, to receive forgiveness of sins and an inheritance among the sanctified.
- All physical miracles Jesus performed (giving sight, restoring hearing, healing the lame) were pictures of what happens spiritually at salvation.
- Regeneration is an experience: eyes opened, ears unstopped, freedom from Satan's bondage, sins forgiven, spiritual riches received.
- **Personal testimony:** 47 years ago on an Air Force base, Pastor Payne was led to Christ behind a building by a man with an open Bible. He immediately went to his fiancée Tracy and urged her to hear the Gospel. The following Sunday he attended church twice and went to work Monday with a Bible, eager to share his faith.
- Concern: Many people pray a prayer or agree with the Gospel but show no evidence of change — no life from the dead, no opened eyes, no freedom, no desire for God.
- You do not *grow into* becoming a Christian; there must be a particular moment of encountering and undergoing regeneration.

#### 2. Redemption (Romans 3:23-24; 2 Timothy 2:19-21; 1 Corinthians 6:20)
- To be redeemed is to be bought with a price; we are not our own (1 Corinthians 6:19-20).
- Romans 3:24 — justified freely by His grace "through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus."
- 2 Timothy 2:20-21 — in a great house there are vessels of honor and dishonor; if a man purges himself, he becomes "a vessel unto honor, sanctified, and fit for the master's use."
- Redemption is not just the fact of being saved, but growing in the understanding that God redeemed us to clean us up and make us usable for His purposes.
- God wants to use every believer — at home, at work, and in the local church.
- The experience of redemption involves realizing that everything before Christ was wrong, and beginning to see all of life through the lens of Christ and the Word of God.
- Ahead lies the Judgment Seat of Christ — all five experiences are framed as preparation for that day.
- **Personal testimony:** After getting saved, Pastor Payne eagerly got involved in church ministry — even attending fellowship events he wasn't directly invited to — simply wanting to be used by God. He mopped floors and served wherever he could.

### Three Remaining Experiences (Preview)
- The third R is **Release** — to be covered fully the following week.
- The fourth and fifth experiences were not named in this sermon.

### Closing Challenge
- 2 Corinthians 13:5 — "Examine yourselves, whether ye be in the faith."
- Not an attempt to make people doubt their salvation, but an encouragement to look honestly at their salvation testimony.
- Key questions: What have you trusted? What has God done in you? Did you go through an experience?
