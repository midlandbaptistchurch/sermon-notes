## Summary
Pastor Simpson teaches on living according to grace through biblical principles rather than specific rules. He emphasizes that grace requires believers to think scripturally and make godly decisions within biblical boundaries, using purpose as the foundation for understanding how to apply these principles in daily Christian living.

## Scripture
Romans 12:9-21 — Paul's instructions on Christian conduct and relationships within the body of Christ.

## Key Themes
- Living by principles rather than specific commands under grace
- The importance of purpose in the Christian life
- Renewing the mind through Scripture
- Liberty with responsibility in grace-based living

## Notes

### Purpose as Foundation
- Primary purpose after salvation: to glorify God
- Ministry of reconciliation (2 Corinthians 5:18) - winning souls and the word of reconciliation
- Being approved of God through faithful service

### Understanding Principles vs. Rules
- Grace doesn't give specific instructions but provides guidelines and boundaries
- Believers must "figure it out" using biblical principles
- Romans 12:2 - "be transformed by the renewing of your mind"
- God wants believers to do the right thing for the right reasons while being right

### Key Principles from Romans 12:
- Let love be without hypocrisy (v. 9)
- Abhor evil, cleave to good (v. 9)
- Be kindly affectioned with brotherly love (v. 10)
- Not slothful in business, fervent in spirit (v. 11)
- Rejoicing in hope (v. 12)
- Patient in tribulation, continuing in prayer (v. 12)
- Recompense no man evil for evil (v. 17)
- Overcome evil with good (v. 21)

### Mental Renewal and Spiritual Vigilance
- Ephesians 4:23 - "be renewed in the spirit of your mind"
- Don't give place to the devil (Ephesians 4:27)
- Grieve not the Holy Spirit (Ephesians 4:30)
- Walk circumspectly - with 360-degree awareness (Ephesians 5:15)

### Grace-Based Living vs. Legalistic Control
- Grace gives liberty but requires responsibility
- Some churches use pressure and guilt instead of teaching principles
- Liberty can be misused by the flesh if principles aren't understood
- Believers must make the faith "their own" rather than following others

### The Process of Spiritual Maturity
- Knowledge → Understanding → Wisdom
- Principles help believers "think the Word of God"
- Consistency develops character
- Purpose → Principles → Priorities → Passion
