## Summary
Tim Faulk examines the nature of the gospel message that drives God's mission to reach the world. He emphasizes that the gospel is biblical (based on Scripture, not human invention), powerful (able to transform lives), universal (for all people everywhere), and essential (necessary for salvation). Using examples from missionary work in the Philippines and Myanmar, Faulk challenges believers to recognize that people cannot be saved without hearing and understanding the gospel, and that God has chosen the method of sending preachers from local churches to proclaim this message.

## Scripture
**Romans 1:16** — Paul declares he is not ashamed of the gospel of Christ because it is the power of God unto salvation for everyone who believes, both Jew and Greek.

**1 Corinthians 15:3-4** — The gospel message is that Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures, was buried, and rose again the third day according to the Scriptures.

**1 Timothy 2:4** — God's will is that all men be saved and come to the knowledge of the truth.

**Romans 10:14-15** — How shall they call on Him in whom they have not believed? How shall they believe in Him of whom they have not heard? How shall they hear without a preacher? How shall they preach except they be sent?

## Key Themes
- **The Gospel is Biblical**: The message is rooted in Scripture, not human invention—it is the gospel of Christ according to the Scriptures
- **The Gospel is Powerful**: God's message has power to transform lives; Faulk testified of being transformed from pursuing worldly things at age 25 to being obsessed with the gospel since March 1985
- **The Gospel is Universal**: The message has gone into all the world and is for every creature; modern technology and transportation make the world smaller than ever
- **The Gospel is Essential**: People must hear, understand, and believe the gospel to be saved—there is no salvation without hearing the message
- **Sending is Required**: God's method involves local churches sending preachers; missionaries referenced churches in the Philippines that had not sent anyone out in 24-27 years

### God's Magnificent Obsession
- Term comes from 1954 movie starring Rock Hudson and Jane Wyman
- God has a magnificent obsession with His Son, the Lord Jesus Christ
- Missions brings honor and glory to Christ

### The Foolishness of Preaching
- God chose the method of sinful men preaching to sinful, dying men
- Not the foolishness of the message, but of the method
- Faulk saved March 1985, acknowledges ongoing struggle with sin
- Contrasted with preacher who claimed not to have sinned since the 1960s
- The holy aspect is the message from Scripture, not the messenger

### Salvation Message
- All have sinned and come short of the glory of God
- Penalty for sin is death—eternal separation from God in the lake of fire
- Hell is a place of fire prepared for the devil and his angels
- Cannot save yourself through works or religious activity (baptism, confirmation)
- God sent His Son as a substitute to die on the cross
- Christ died for our sins, was buried, rose again the third day according to Scriptures
- Faulk's testimony: Convicted for a week, couldn't sleep, feared waking up in hell
- Must trust Christ—acknowledge sin, recognize penalty, believe Christ paid it

### The Message We Proclaim is Biblical
- Based on the gospel of Christ according to the Scriptures
- Not invented by man but given by God
- Many mention "gospel" but when pressed cannot define the biblical gospel

### The Message is Powerful
- It is the power of God unto salvation
- We don't make it powerful; God already has
- Transformed Faulk's life at age 25 from pursuing worldly "toys" to serving Christ
- Changes thought life, thinking about others instead of self
- Not all believers change as dramatically, but something must change in thought life

### The Message is Universal
- Paul preached to every creature (Colossians 1:23)
- Gospel gone into all the world (Colossians 1:6)
- Gospel gone into all nations (Romans 16:25)
- World is smaller now than in 1985—no internet or social media then
- Modern mass transportation makes travel faster (plane vs. car: 4 hours vs. 14 hours)
- 108 million people in Philippines—most religious but lost
- 60 million in Myanmar—very religious Buddhists but lost
- Story of Buddha's tooth in Burma—people viewing a tooth in a case for forgiveness

### The Message is Essential
- People must hear, understand, and believe to be saved
- Not an elective but a requirement
- Met people in jungles of Myanmar who never heard the name Jesus Christ
- If they die without hearing, they go to hell
- God set it up this way—no salvation without hearing the gospel
- Islands in Philippines waiting to hear after 150 years of missionary activity
- Regions in Myanmar waiting to hear

### The Sending Method (Romans 10:14-15)
- How shall they call without believing?
- How shall they believe without hearing?
- How shall they hear without a preacher?
- How shall they preach except they be sent?
- Beautiful are the feet of those who preach the gospel

### Missionary Examples from the Philippines
- **Sonny Boy Elbita, Saved by Grace Fundamental Baptist Church, Gimbul**
  - From first batch of Bible school graduates in Oton
  - Sent out to plant church from scratch 27 years ago
  - Church now thriving after 27 years
  - Has not sent out any preachers in 27 years (zero)

- **Pastor Mario, Grace Gospel Baptist Church**
  - 24th anniversary in 2026 (started 2004)
  - Currently has no one being sent out to plant churches
  - Training someone in Suwon, 2.5 hours north
  - Convicted about not having anyone to send

### God's Method of Evangelism
- Faithful men teach faithful men
- Ordain elders in every city
- Plant churches everywhere
- Main thing: sending people to preach the gospel
- Local churches send the preachers

### Personal Mission Story
- In 1989, Hugh Taylor asked Faulk to pray about starting Bible study in Fort Walton Beach, Florida
- Felt something inside needed to get out beyond jail ministry, nursing home ministry, and visitation
- Resulted in planting a church
