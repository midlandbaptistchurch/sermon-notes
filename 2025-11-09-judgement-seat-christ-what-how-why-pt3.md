## Summary
Pastor Payne continues his series on the Judgment Seat of Christ, examining what believers will give account for. He emphasizes that every believer will stand before Christ to give account of their life and service, covering points four through seven: giving God glory rather than seeking human praise, godliness (fealty), charity within the body of Christ, and spiritual fitness in running the race for an incorruptible crown.

## Scripture
Romans 14:10-12 — Paul reminds believers that we shall all stand before the judgment seat of Christ to give account of ourselves to God.

## Key Themes
- The certainty of standing before Christ's judgment seat
- Giving God glory instead of seeking human praise
- Living with godliness and devotion (fealty)
- Practicing charity as the bond of unity in the body of Christ
- Spiritual fitness and self-discipline for eternal rewards
- The incorruptible crown awaiting faithful believers

## Notes

### Review: Foundation, Faith, and Faithfulness
- Previously covered: proper doctrinal foundation, life of faith, and faithfulness in service
- The judgment seat is for believers only - a place of reward, not condemnation
- Paul uses "shall" - indicating God's certain promise and intent

### Point Four: Glory to God (Not Seeking Human Praise)
**1 Corinthians 10:31** - "Whether therefore ye eat, or drink, or whatsoever ye do, do all to the glory of God."

- **Matthew 6:1-6** - Giving alms and praying for human recognition brings earthly reward only
- **1 Corinthians 4:5** - Judge nothing before the time; God will reveal heart motives
- Check your heart motivation - are you serving for God's glory or human approval?
- **Proverbs 27:2** - "Let another man praise thee, and not thine own mouth"

### Point Five: Fealty - Deep Inner Devotion and Godliness
**1 Timothy 4:7-8** - "Exercise thyself rather unto godliness. For bodily exercise profiteth little: but godliness is profitable unto all things."

- Fealty = deep inner devotion to someone you owe (like vassals to lords)
- Godliness is profitable for this life and the next
- **1 Timothy 3:16** - The mystery of godliness in the church context
- **2 Corinthians 4:11** - The life of Jesus made manifest in our mortal flesh
- **Colossians 1:27** - "Christ in you, the hope of glory"
- Godly living brings happiness and blessing in this life

### Point Six: Charity - The Bond of Unity
**1 Corinthians 13:1-3** - Without charity, all service profits nothing

- Charity is more than love - it's compassionate love with action
- **Colossians 3:12-14** - Charity is the bond of perfectness that unites the body
- How we treat fellow believers matters greatly
- Forbearing one another, forgiving as Christ forgave us
- Many stay away from church due to lack of charity
- Unity in the body of Christ requires charitable spirits

### Point Seven: Fitness - Running for an Incorruptible Crown
**1 Corinthians 9:24-27** - "So run, that ye may obtain... they do it to obtain a corruptible crown; but we an incorruptible."

- Run to win, not just to participate
- Athletes are temperate in all things to win a prize
- We run for an incorruptible crown that will never fade
- Paul ran with certainty, not uncertainty
- **Verse 27** - "I keep under my body, and bring it into subjection"
- **Galatians 6:8** - Sowing to flesh reaps corruption; sowing to Spirit reaps eternal life
- **Proverbs 14:24** - "The crown of the wise is their riches"
- Self-discipline and victory over fleshly desires are essential
