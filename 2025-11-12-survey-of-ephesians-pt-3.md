## Summary
Pastor Jim Payne continues his survey of Ephesians, emphasizing the book's central truth: the body of Christ as a specific, revelatory doctrine given through Paul. He explores how Ephesians teaches us who we are in Christ through the lens of three time periods (time past, but now, and ages to come), examines Paul's elevated understanding after his imprisonment, and traces the Trinity's work in forming and blessing the church with heavenly spiritual blessings.

## Scripture
Ephesians 1-2 — The foundational passage for understanding the mystery of the body of Christ and our access to the Father through Christ by one Spirit.

## Key Themes
- The body of Christ as a revelatory truth unique to Paul's ministry
- Rightly dividing the Word through time periods: time past, but now, and ages to come
- The shift from Acts 7 as a turning point in God's dispensation
- Paul's elevation to higher revelation (epignosis) during his imprisonment
- The Trinity working together to form the body of Christ
- Spiritual blessings in heavenly places
- Our identity and position in Christ
- Access to the Father through Christ by one Spirit

## Notes

### The Core Truth of Ephesians
- Ephesians deals specifically with the body of Christ, a revelatory truth found only in Paul's letters (Romans through Philemon)
- The book must be studied carefully to understand this central theme

### Time Divisions in Scripture
- **Time Past**: Before Acts 7, when God dealt specifically with Israel and the circumcised
- **But Now**: From Acts 7 onward, the dispensation of grace where Jew and Gentile are united in one body
- **Ages to Come**: The future hope believers have

### Paul's Elevated Understanding
- During his Acts ministry (about 25 years), Paul used his Jewish heritage as an advantage to the Jews
- After his imprisonment (Acts 28), God elevated Paul's understanding to "the excellency of the knowledge of Christ Jesus" (Philippians 3:8)
- This higher knowledge (epignosis) appears in Ephesians, Philippians, and Colossians

### The Structure of Paul's Letters
- **Romans-Galatians**: How you got in (foundation, law, correction)
- **Ephesians-Colossians**: Who you are (doctrine about the body)
- **1 Timothy-Titus**: Instruction in righteousness for assembly
- **Philemon**: The perfect man in Christ

### The Mystery and the Body
- Ephesians repeatedly emphasizes "one body":
  - Ephesians 1:22-23 — the church as His body
  - Ephesians 2:11 — both reconciled in one body
  - Ephesians 3:5-6 — the mystery of the body
  - Ephesians 4:4 — one body, one spirit
- We are bone of His bone and flesh of His flesh (Ephesians 5:30)
- God's purpose is manifested on earth through the church

### Eternal Life Promised Before the World Began
- Titus 1:2 promises eternal life "which God that cannot lie promised before the world began"
- This is distinct from Israel's hope (earthly kingdom from foundation of world)
- Believers have a promise made in eternity past by the Trinity

### The Trinity in Ephesians 1
- **The Father**: Blessed us with spiritual blessings, made us accepted in the beloved (verses 3-8)
- **The Son**: Our redemption through His blood, forgiveness of sins (verses 7-12)
- **The Holy Spirit**: Sealed us, earnest of our inheritance (verses 13-14)
- All three working toward forming the body of Christ and bringing us to heavenly places

### Access to the Father
- Ephesians 2:18 — "For through him [Christ] we both have access by one spirit unto the Father"
- This is the privilege of the body of Christ, showing our exalted position

### Characteristics of Ephesians Recipients
- The book opens addressing "saints and faithful in Christ" (Ephesians 1:1)
- Only the faithful receive higher revelation; the carnal (like the Corinthians) cannot comprehend body truth
- Believers are to grow in knowledge and make spiritual judgment about excellent things (Philippians 1:9-10)
