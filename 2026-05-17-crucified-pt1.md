## Summary
This sermon begins a series on the crucifixion by examining Old Testament prophecies that foretold Christ's suffering and death. Bill Baughman walks through 28 specific prophecies from Isaiah 53 and Psalm 22, demonstrating how each was precisely fulfilled at the cross, emphasizing the supernatural nature of these predictions made 700-1000 years before Christ.

## Scripture
Isaiah 53:5 — A key messianic prophecy describing Christ's suffering for our transgressions and healing through His stripes.

## Key Themes
- Old Testament prophecies of the crucifixion
- God's eternal purpose of reconciliation
- The supernatural fulfillment of 28 specific prophecies
- Christ's voluntary suffering in obedience

## Notes

### God's Eternal Purpose
- The fall of Lucifer from heaven
- The fall of Adam and Eve in Eden
- God's plan for reconciliation through the cross (Ephesians 1:9-10, Colossians 1:20-21)
- The gospel: Christ died, was buried, and rose again

### 28 Old Testament Prophecies Fulfilled
The sermon details 28 specific prophecies and their fulfillment:

1. **Serpent would bruise the seed of woman** (Genesis 3:15 → John 12:31-33)
2. **Messiah cut off, not for himself** (Daniel 9:26 → John 11:50-52)
3. **Betrayal by close friend** (Psalm 41:9 → Mark 14:10-11)
4. **Forsaken by disciples** (Zechariah 13:7 → Matthew 26:56)
5. **Betrayed for thirty pieces of silver** (Zechariah 11:12 → Matthew 26:15)
6. **Betrayal money thrown to potter** (Zechariah 11:13 → Matthew 27:5-7)
7. **Sacrifice as Passover lamb** (Isaiah 53:7 → 1 Corinthians 5:7)
8. **Scourging and mocking** (Isaiah 50:6 → Matthew 27:26-31)
9. **Body mutilated and disfigured** (Isaiah 52:14 → Psalm 22:17)
10. **Shame as criminal** (Psalm 69:9,19 → between two thieves)
11. **False witnesses** (Psalm 35:11 → temple destruction claim)
12. **Silent before accusers** (Isaiah 53:7 → before Pilate)
13. **Numbered with transgressors** (Isaiah 53:12 → crucified with criminals)
14. **Hands and feet pierced** (Psalm 22:16 → crucifixion wounds)
15. **Garments divided by lots** (Psalm 22:18 → soldiers gambling)
16. **Given vinegar to drink** (Psalm 69:21 → offered but refused)
17. **Onlookers staring** (Psalm 22:17 → guards and crowds watching)
18. **Family standing afar** (Psalm 38:11 → Luke's account)
19. **Head shaking mockers** (Psalm 22:7 → Matthew's description)
20. **Words of mockers** (Psalm 22:8 → "let God deliver him")
21. **Intercession for sinners** (Isaiah 53:12 → "Father, forgive them")
22. **Cry of abandonment** (Psalm 22:1 → "My God, why have you forsaken me?")
23. **Side pierced with spear** (Zechariah 12:10 → soldier's spear)
24. **Committing spirit to God** (Psalm 31:5 → final words)
25. **No bones broken** (Psalm 34:20 → unlike the thieves)
26. **Buried in rich man's tomb** (Isaiah 53:9 → Joseph of Arimathea)
27. **Overall victory through cross** (Genesis 3:15 → John 12:31-33)
28. **Resurrection hope** (Psalm 22:22-31 → "Your heart shall live forever")

### Psalm 22: David's Vision
- Written 1,077 years before the crucifixion
- Vivid description of crucifixion experience
- Details the physical and emotional suffering
- Ends with praise and hope

### Mathematical Impossibility
- Scientist calculated 8 prophecies: 1 in 10^17 chance
- 28 prophecies: approximately 1 in 10^50 chance
- Demonstrates supernatural origin of Scripture
