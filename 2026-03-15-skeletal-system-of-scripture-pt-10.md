## Summary
Pastor Jim Payne continues the series on the skeletal structure of Scripture by examining God's creation of heaven and earth, the nature of God's throne as a place of righteousness and judgment, and the fall of Lucifer. He explores Lucifer's original creation as a beautiful and wise angelic being before his rebellion through pride, showing how his iniquity and violence led to his downfall and the establishment of the lake of fire.

## Scripture
Isaiah 14:12 — "How art thou fallen from heaven, O Lucifer, son of the morning?"
Ezekiel 28:12-17 — God's description of Lucifer's original beauty, wisdom, and fall through pride and rebellion.
Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth."
Hebrews 1:2 — Christ created the worlds (plural).
Revelation 4:1-2 — John's vision of the throne of God in heaven.
Psalm 97:2 — "Righteousness and judgment are the habitation of his throne."

## Key Themes
- The structural foundation of Scripture: creation, worlds, and God's throne
- God's throne as the seat of righteousness and judgment
- The distinction between spiritual knowledge and shallow faith
- Lucifer's creation as the anointed cherub covering the throne
- Pride and rebellion as the original sins
- Lucifer's corruption through merchandising and deception of a third of the angels
- God's response to rebellion: the lake of fire and the separation of light from darkness
- The role of music and religion as tools of satanic deception
- Living by faith in the current dispensation of grace

## Notes
- God's skeleton of Scripture includes understanding creation (physical and spiritual), the different worlds, and God's governmental throne
- In the beginning refers to the beginning of creation, not of God, who is eternal
- The heaven (singular) in Genesis 1:1 refers to God's throne; the heavens (plural) refer to the created heavens
- God's throne is literal and physical, not ethereal or spiritual
- Lucifer was created perfect in wisdom and beauty, adorned with precious stones representing his religious leadership role
- The "workmanship of thy pipes" indicates Lucifer had great musical and worship capabilities
- Lucifer's rebellion involved actual physical violence and insurrection against God's throne
- Lucifer attempted to get angels to follow him by merchandising falsehood; he gained a third of the angels
- The three great angels (Michael, Gabriel, Lucifer) likely each controlled a third of God's angelic forces
- Michael ("who is like God") represents Christ's authority; Gabriel ("God is mighty") represents the Father's authority
- Lucifer's five "I will" statements (ascending to heaven, exalting his throne, etc.) are answered by God's five "I will" statements of judgment
- God created the lake of fire as a response to this rebellion
- The current dispensation is one of grace where believers walk by faith, not by sight of miracles and supernatural manifestation
- Charismatic Christianity falsely claims supernatural gifts not meant for the current church age
- Religion and music are the devil's primary tools of deception in the modern world
- Believers should live in reverent awe of God's throne and grow in knowledge of His will
