## Summary
Tim Faulk distinguishes between the Great Commission (Matthew 28, Mark 16) given to the twelve apostles and the great commitment given to believers today through Paul's ministry. While traditional missions focuses on the Great Commission, believers in this dispensation are called to embrace the ministry of reconciliation committed to us in 2 Corinthians 5. True missions begins with transformation in Christ and manifests through committed ambassadorship for Christ to a lost world.

## Scripture
2 Corinthians 5:10-21 — Paul describes believers as new creatures in Christ who have been given the ministry of reconciliation and serve as ambassadors for Christ, pleading with the world to be reconciled to God.

## Key Themes
- **Commitment vs. Commission**: We are not under the Great Commission (Matthew 28, Mark 16) but have been given a great commitment to the ministry of reconciliation
- **The Ministry of Reconciliation**: God has reconciled us to Himself and committed unto us the ministry of sharing this reconciliation with others
- **Ambassadorship**: Believers serve as Christ's ambassadors, representing Him and pleading with the world on His behalf
- **Transformation**: Missions effectiveness begins with personal transformation - becoming a new creature in Christ with a new position, new person, and new perspective
- **Accountability**: All believers will appear before the judgment seat of Christ to give account for their level of commitment

### The Great Commission vs. Great Commitment
- **Matthew 28:19-20**: "Go ye therefore and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost: Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you"
- Matthew 23:1-3 shows Jesus commanded disciples to observe what scribes and Pharisees taught (the Law, including Sabbath keeping)
- **Mark 16:15-18**: "Go ye into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature. He that believeth and is baptized shall be saved...And these signs shall follow them that believe; In my name shall they cast out devils; they shall speak with new tongues; They shall take up serpents; and if they drink any deadly thing, it shall not hurt them"
- People quote "preach the gospel to every creature" as a "full stop" but ignore the rest of the passage
- We don't baptize in the name of Father, Son, and Holy Ghost specifically; we don't cast out devils, speak in new tongues, handle serpents, or drink deadly things

### Paul's Commission
- Paul went into all the world and preached the gospel (Colossians 1:6)
- Paul preached the gospel to every creature (Colossians 1:23)
- Paul preached the gospel to all nations (Romans 16:25)
- Paul said "Be followers of me, even as I also am of Christ"
- We follow Paul's example, not the twelve's commission

### The Transformation (2 Corinthians 5:17)
- "Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new"
- **New Position**: We are in Christ, seated together with Him in heavenly places now
- **New Person**: We are a new creature with a new man to live for, not the flesh
- **New Perspective**: Old things passed away, all things become new
- Speaker saved at age 25, Brother Bill at 50, Heath at 32 - coming to Christ with "a heavy sack of rocks" from years of sin
- Must forget those things which are behind
- No longer view the world through human eyes but see people as souls for whom Christ died

### The Ministry of Reconciliation (2 Corinthians 5:18-19)
- "All things are of God, who hath reconciled us to himself by Jesus Christ, and hath given to us the ministry of reconciliation"
- "God was in Christ, reconciling the world unto himself, not imputing their trespasses unto them, and hath committed unto us the word of reconciliation"
- God didn't save us simply to sit and soak and savor in rich doctrine
- We've been reconciled to God and now entrusted with sharing this reconciliation
- The ministry belongs to God but the responsibility has been entrusted to us
- We are God's hands, feet, and fingers
- God has provided forgiveness, not imputing trespasses - giving everyone a chance to hear

### Ambassadorship (2 Corinthians 5:20)
- "Now then we are ambassadors for Christ, as though God did beseech you by us: we pray you in Christ's stead, be ye reconciled to God"
- An ambassador doesn't represent himself but someone else
- We represent Christ, His church, His person, His work, His message
- God pleads with the world through His people
- Example: Brother Mario in the jungles of Cebu standing in Christ's stead pleading with folks to be reconciled to God

### The Nature of Commitment
- Story of young country couple: Man would visit his girlfriend but say "I'll see you next Sunday afternoon if it ain't raining" - didn't want to drive on muddy red clay road
- This proves lack of commitment
- Some Christians treat church membership this way: "I'll see you next Sunday preacher if it ain't raining"
- Commitment tested when it's hard: no money in bank, bad weather, difficult circumstances
- Marriage commitment (speaker married at age 20): "I do" meant commitment for better or worse, riches and poverty, sickness and health
- Christ's commitment: Left heaven's glory, made Himself of no reputation, took form of servant, obedient unto death of the cross, enduring shame
- We've been given this ministry based on Christ's greatest commitment in history

### Living in a Non-Committal Age
- People today don't want to commit to anything
- They say "maybe" when asked to attend church events but really mean no
- We're living in a day where no one's committed to anything
- God has committed unto us the ministry of reconciliation - how are we doing on our commitments?

### The Impact of Commitment
- When we get our heart right about the ministry of reconciliation and understand the commitment involved, all other peripheral commitments are automatically fulfilled
- Focus on this central commitment and God will help with everything else
- God will manifest Himself mightily in giving when we take this commitment seriously
- God will enable fulfillment of all other commitments when we focus on this one
- Will be amazed at what God does in finances when committed to getting this ministry around the world

### The Judgment Seat of Christ (2 Corinthians 5:10-11)
- "We must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ; that every one may receive the things done in his body, according to that he hath done, whether it be good or bad"
- "Knowing therefore the terror of the Lord, we persuade men"
- Will give account for things good or bad based on level of commitment
- Though more casual than Great White Throne judgment, there will be terror
- Paul says "work out your own salvation with fear and trembling" in light of judgment seat
- The issue will be our level of commitment to the cause of Christ and the ministry of reconciliation

### Call to Action
- Question: Not simply "Do we obey the great commission?" but "Will we embrace the great commitment?"
- Challenge to evaluate all commitments: Christian life, family life, all of life
- For the unsaved: Understand sin's penalty (wages of sin is death), that all have sinned, can't save yourself through works, trust Christ as substitute
- For the saved: Re-examine all commitments, especially commitment to the ministry of reconciliation
- Prayer that "the needle be moved to the more serious approach to that commitment"
